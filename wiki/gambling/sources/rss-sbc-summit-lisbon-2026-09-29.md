## Narrative

### Keynote — "Leadership in Sports Business"

The opening keynote on Tuesday 2026-09-29 put **Michael Jordan** on stage with **Sportradar** founder/CEO **Carsten Koerl** and **DraftKings** co-founder/CEO **Jason Robins**. Broadcaster **Kirsty Gallacher** moderated.

Jordan's contribution was the athlete's counter-position to the analytics industry built around him. He said he never tracked the market:

> "I can't worry about what people are betting on, I can't worry about what the stats are, I never worried about stats. All I care about is if we win."

He framed the athlete's control as narrow — "ultimately as an athlete, I can only influence one thing, what I do on the basketball court" — and contrasted his era with today's, where "you got to build a brand, you got to worry about what your numbers are." On the sportsbook's task of finding a player's weakness, he joked that the problem with analyzing Michael Jordan is locating the weakness.

Robins pushed back on the idea that betting pressure is new: fans were already vocal before betting existed; betting "may add a new dimension to that."

### DraftKings on prediction markets

Robins said the classification question — prediction markets as **financial instruments or sports betting** — is part of a broader legal and regulatory debate. DraftKings is not trying to forecast the outcome. It is focused on **maintaining flexibility** to operate under either scenario.

He added that DraftKings **cannot ignore** prediction markets if they become a large and sustainable US industry, but must also weigh the possibility that the sector "doesn't end up surviving the regulatory process."

Jordan noted the three had discussed the topic privately, then joked: "We should not share that."

### Koerl on AI

Koerl called **AI customisation** the biggest trend affecting the industry's future. The panel closed by unanimously naming Jordan the most competitive of the trio.

### Brazil

The parallel track at Lisbon was **Brazil**. President **Lula** pledged an outright **online betting ban**, which drove the conference discourse and drew backlash from large bookmakers. One panelist described Brazil's current situation in a word: "confusion."
