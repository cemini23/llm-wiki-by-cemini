The Week 6 board is driven by **David Montgomery's groin injury** (exited Week 5 in the second quarter vs. Tennessee) and **Saquon Barkley's hamstring**.

**Top priorities:**

### The two reads worth keeping

**1. The trade-conditional stash.** **Tyjae Spears** caught **nine passes** in Week 5 after only five in the first four weeks. The column's reasoning is explicit: if that receiving role holds he is a desperation PPR start, **but the real case is that Tony Pollard could be traded** — Pollard is in the final year of his contract and the Titans are **0-5**. That is a stash driven by a **roster event**, not by current usage.

**2. Handcuff value is team-quality-dependent.** Brian Robinson Jr. and Tyler Allgeier are both handcuffs behind lead backs, but the column distinguishes them: Robinson is **startable when Atlanta is favoured**, while Allgeier's value is **decreasing** because "the Cardinals just aren't a very good football team, so those situations are few and far between." **The same role in a worse offense is worth less.**

**Depth notes:** Chris Rodriguez Jr. (JAX, 35%) · Austin Ekeler (WAS, 5%) · Sione Vaki (DET, 1%). Rostered over 60% but worth adding anywhere available: **Braelon Allen (NYJ, 84%)**, **Emanuel Wilson (SEA, 84%)**, **Ollie Gordon II (MIA, 78%)**, **Alvin Kamara (NO, 71%)**.
