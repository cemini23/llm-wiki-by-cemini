## Narrative

### 1 — Lawmakers demand every FanDuel VIP video

**Senator Richard Blumenthal** (CT) and Representatives **Paul Tonko** (NY) and **Valerie Foushee** (NC) wrote to FanDuel President **Christian Genetski**, responding to a 2026-09-24 letter from FanDuel SVP **Cory Fox**.

FanDuel's own 2026-09-24 letter said it had sent VIP bettors **"approximately 30 such videos of athletes and entertainers over the last two years"** as loyalty rewards. The lawmakers say that **contradicts FanDuel's claim that it does not encourage continued gambling**.

**The named case:** **Terry Thompson** allegedly lost **$1.5M** and received a personalized **Bryce Harper** video. Harper said he "did not know what the purpose of the video he created was." Thompson is suing FanDuel **and** DraftKings, claiming they "preyed on him despite a clear gambling addiction."

**What the letter demands, by 2026-10-20:**

- Copies of **all** personalized VIP videos
- For each: bet amounts, any problem-gambling resource requests, why the recipient was chosen, and any VIP-host steps taken to avoid problem gambling
- How aware the athletes and entertainers were of how the videos would be used
- FanDuel's problem-gambling screening tools

**Why it matters to this wiki:** it is the clearest instance yet of **VIP-host practice** being treated as evidence rather than marketing. The demand is for the **decision records** behind each video — the same "show your model" pattern as the DK AI reviews below.

### 2 — Three states turn to AI after the DraftKings report

Following the **New York Times** investigation of 2026-09-19 — "How DraftKings Uses AI to Target the Gamblers Likelies to Lose" — three states moved:

| State | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **New York** | The Gaming Commission met **Friday 2026-10-09 at noon** on two proposals: **"Use of artificial intelligence for wagering purposes"** and **"Safeguards for at-risk mobile sports wagering patrons."** The first would **bar operators from using AI for personalized bonuses, promotions, or bet suggestions**; the second would require flagging bettors who cross set thresholds such as deposit totals |
| **Massachusetts** | Commission chair **Jordan Maynard** asked Executive Director Dean Serpa to investigate how AI and machine learning are used by in-state sportsbooks |
| **Maine** | The Gambling Control Unit is **monitoring** AI usage by online sportsbooks |

**The NYT's findings:** DraftKings used AI/ML to identify bettors most likely to lose money and sent them more promotions. Similar in-house models **built to spot problem gamblers were never used**. DraftKings "rejects any implication that its marketing practices are unfair or improperly targets customers."

**The NY proposals predate the exposé** but track Governor **Kathy Hochul's** stated goal of reducing gambling addiction.

**Penalty exposure:** if commissions find DraftKings targeted bettors who likely needed problem-gambling programs, punishments include **fines, suspensions, or even revocation of its sports betting licence**.

**Note the sequence:** @sources/rss-lsr-pm-industry-2026-09-29-30.md recorded Michigan and Massachusetts reviewing in late September. The list has now grown to **New York, Massachusetts, and Maine**, with New York moving to **rulemaking** rather than review.

### 3 — Macquarie trims gaming targets

**Chad Beynon** of Macquarie expects "cautiously optimistic" commentary in the Q3 preview, which he says should be a positive catalyst — but trimmed seven targets for Las Vegas and Brazil market updates, lower sports betting hold, and **higher predictions investment**.

**Gaming stocks are down 12% over the last month and 22% over the last quarter.**

| Company | New target | Change | Implied upside |
|---------|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Rush Street Interactive | $33 | −$2 | 61% |
| **DraftKings** | **$34** | **−$4** | 72% |
| **Flutter** (FanDuel) | **$128** | **−$22** | 68% |
| Genius Sports | $11 | −$1 | 77% |
| Inspired | $7 | −$2 | 76% |
| MGM | $48 | −$7 | 57% |
| Sportradar | $19 | −$1 | 56% |

**Unchanged:** Caesars ($31) and Penn Entertainment ($25). Beynon rates Caesars and Inspired **neutral**, the other seven **outperform**. Flutter, Genius Sports, and Sportradar are named preferred.

**Q3 expectations:** online sportsbook GGR **+6%**, helped by a strong July (World Cup) and improved September volume, offset by a soft August (handle −1%). iGaming expected **+18%**. Hold should be "a wash" despite New York signalling a year-over-year decline.

**Why it is recorded:** the **higher predictions investment** line is the tell — the books are spending to compete with prediction markets while simultaneously arguing states should regulate them.
