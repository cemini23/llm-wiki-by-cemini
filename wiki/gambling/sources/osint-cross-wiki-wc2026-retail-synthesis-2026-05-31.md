## Narrative

First **World Cup 2026** batch for gambling-wiki. Source material already deep-read in private `@osint-wiki`; this page records provenance and retail-facing takeaways only. Bot/LP/module specs remain on osint side.

**Retail themes extracted:**

1. **48-team format** — top two per group + eight best third-place teams → Round of 32; changes base rates vs 32-team eras.
2. **Contract menu** — advance / group winner / outright / match moneyline / to-advance are **different bets** with different resolution rules.
3. **Books vs PM/Kalshi gaps** — host-nation and brand-name favorites often **juiced on sportsbooks** vs event-contract prices (USA, Mexico, Switzerland, England called out in research).
4. **Third-place route** — points + GD tiebreaks create bubble teams; Ivory Coast / Scotland / DR Congo examples in research (verify live before betting).
5. **Knockout pitfalls** — 90-minute three-way moneyline ≠ to-advance (ET/penalties); tied at 90′ can settle Draw on ML while knockout continues.
