## Narrative

**Texas vs Oklahoma (Red River Rivalry), in Dallas.** Top-ranked **Texas** (undefeated, off a road win at Tennessee) faces unranked **Oklahoma (2-2)**.

**Market:** Texas **−7.5** (−110), ML **−320**; Oklahoma +7.5 (−110), ML +260; total **39** (−110 both sides).

### Best bet: Oklahoma first-half under 7.5 points (−165)

The article calls this "the best bet of the game."

**The support:** Oklahoma's first-half offense has been nearly absent. Over its last three games it scored a **combined seven first-half points — all against New Mexico**. It has scored **no first-half points against a Power Four team** this season.

Full-game scoring: **13** vs Georgia, **14** vs New Mexico, **10** vs Michigan.

**The other side of the check:** Texas has allowed more than seven first-half points only once this season (vs Ohio State). Ohio State ranks **14th** nationally in points per game; Oklahoma ranks **110th**.

### Second bet: Texas −7.5

Texas has beaten Oklahoma by **at least 16 points in three of the past four** meetings — 2025 **23-6**, 2024 **34-3**, 2022 **49-0** (2023 was a 34-30 Oklahoma win). In those Texas wins, **Oklahoma scored no more than six points**.

Texas is the top-ranked team in the AP Poll with **61 of 70** first-place votes.

**Alternative noted:** Oklahoma full-game under **16.5 (−145)** — the article prefers the first-half version.
