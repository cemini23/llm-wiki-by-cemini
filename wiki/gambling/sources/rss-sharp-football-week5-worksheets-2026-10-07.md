## Narrative

### 49ers @ Seahawks

**Market:** Seattle **−3.0**; implied totals **SF 21.75 / SEA 24.75**.

| | SF | SEA |
|---|-----|-----|
| Points/game | **30.5 (2nd)** | 26.3 (10th) |
| Points allowed | 16.0 (3rd) | **18.3 (5th)** |
| Points per drive allowed | 1.73 (5th) | **1.49 (3rd)** |

SF leads the league in **offensive EPA (56.82)**; Seattle is **second in defensive EPA (38.62)**. SF converts a **league-high 84.6%** of red-zone trips into touchdowns. The article frames it as **"strength-on-strength."**

- **Brock Purdy** — 2nd in yards per attempt (**8.9**), **1st in TD rate (9.7%)**. Against an elite Seattle pass defense, called a **"fringe QB1"** — not benched for a streamer.
- **Sam Darnold** — "more of a **QB2**." The split that defines him: **league-worst 16.8 rating under pressure** versus **138.0 when clean**.

### Broncos @ Chargers

**Market:** Denver **−3.5**; implied totals **DEN 23.0 / LAC 19.5**.

| | DEN | LAC |
|---|-----|-----|
| Points/game | 18.5 (27th) | 16.8 (29th) |
| Points allowed | 23.5 | 26.5 |

Both teams "run an offensive play when trailing by double digits in all four games." Denver has been **outscored 63-35 through three quarters** and scores the **fewest first-half points per drive (0.65)**. The Chargers rank **26th in passing success rate** and are "allowing 5.5 sacks plus turnovers per game."

- **Bo Nix** — 60% completions at **6.1 yards/attempt**, limited downfield success. Called "a **floor-based QB2**."
- **Justin Herbert** — declining yards/attempt, **no multi-touchdown games**, and **interceptions in all four games**. "Things don't look promising for him as more than a back-end QB2 here," citing offensive-line injuries and pressure rate.

### Lions @ Cardinals

**Market:** Detroit **−5.5**; implied totals **DET 30.0 / ARI 24.5**.

**The environment finding:** Detroit games average a **league-high 61.5 combined points**. **40.5% of drives against Detroit end in a touchdown — the top rate in the NFL** (Arizona 33.3%, 30th). Detroit has allowed a **league-most 25 plays of 20+ yards**. Arizona has the **lowest rate of 10+ yard plays (13.3%)** and the **fewest 20+ yard plays (5)**.

The article's labels are explicit — both quarterbacks are marked **TRUST**:

- **Jared Goff — TRUST.** Fourth-highest scoring QB through four games, but with a **split**: **QB15 in first halves, QB3 in second halves**. He threw a season-high **52 times** Sunday, **31 after halftime**. The matchup is favorable — Arizona allows the **most passing points per attempt (0.71)**, a **league-high 9.1 yards per attempt**, a **league-high 10.1% TD rate**, and pressures on only 22nd-most. Against two-high looks Goff completes **71.8% for 8.7 Y/A**.
- **Jacoby Brissett — TRUST.** Averaging a **league-low 5.4 Y/A** and **6.1 air yards per attempt** (27th); only **27.5%** of throws go 10+ yards (26th) and **15.9%** go 15+ (28th). But he was **QB4 two weeks ago versus San Francisco**, and Detroit has allowed **Tyler Shough, Josh Allen, Geno Smith, and Bryce Young** to post QB8-or-better weeks, giving up a **league-high 23.6 passing points per game**. **The risk:** Detroit ranks **7th in pressure rate (41.1%)** and **2nd in sacks (15)**, and Brissett struggles under pressure — **38.2 rating, 41.7% completions, 3.7 Y/A**.

**Injury note:** Arizona's **Paris Johnson is out for the season** (biceps).
