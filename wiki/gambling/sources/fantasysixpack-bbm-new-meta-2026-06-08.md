## Narrative

### Macro thesis [TENTATIVE — environment claim]

NFL scoring has **compressed** vs the 2021–2024 passing boom. Drivers cited:

- More **two-high / split-safety** coverage limiting explosives
- Heavier **12/13 personnel** → fewer routes for ambiguous WR3s
- QBs forced **underneath** rather than chunk-play dependent

**Implication for best ball:** drafters still building for the old passing environment leak roster capital. “New Meta” = **structural** response (where premium capital goes), not a single player list.

### Position-by-position claims

| Position | F6P thesis | Confidence |
|----------|------------|------------|
| **QB** | Elite rushing QB **edge thinner** at price; flatter QB1–QB12 scoring → early QB = RB/WR/TE opportunity cost; **3-QB builds** as structural answer; pay up only in **value pockets** | Aligns with 4for4 (fade Allen R3, R8–10 window) [CONFIRMED directional] |
| **WR** | **Blind WR hoarding** is a leak; WR3 roles fragile in heavy personnel; still draft alphas + verified route earners; mid ADP (25–60) can hold value if top-heavy | **Tension** with 4for4 “4–5 WR by R7” — reconcile as **count + quality** |
| **RB** | Market **overcorrected** on Zero RB fear; premium **volume + RZ** backs (18–22 touches) deserve early clicks in lower-scoring environment | Aligns with 4for4 BBMVI early-RB advance data [CONFIRMED directional] |
| **TE** | **Elite or wait** — avoid middle-class TE trap; 12/13 personnel may create **late TE dart** value | Aligns with 4for4 (fade Bowers/McBride R2, R11–13 cluster) [CONFIRMED directional] |

### Roster blueprint checklist (structural)

1. Bankable early **touch volume** (RB/RZ equity)?
2. QB spend only when **price** justified?
3. WRs earning **routes/targets** in heavy personnel, not vibes-only WR3?
4. Avoided **TE middle tier**?
5. Late picks have **contingency / spike paths**?
6. Drafting **2026 NFL**, not a memory of 2022?
