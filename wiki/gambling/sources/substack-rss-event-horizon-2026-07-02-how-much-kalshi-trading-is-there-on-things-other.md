    People keep  pushing the narrative  that trading on non-sports events is growing at Kalshi.  But exactly how much volume is there on “everything else?”

 In the trailing seven days, 12.7% of volume came from categories other than sports and parlays (which are almost entirely sports). That rises to 14.3% for June as a whole (data per    Ticker Tracker   ).

 Here is how that breaks down for June:

   The vast majority of that non-sports/parlay volume was crypto markets, which accounted for 12.2% of all Kalshi volume.

   Almost all of that volume — more than 91% — came from just two types of Bitcoin markets. And 60% of crypto volume came from Bitcoin up-down 15-minute markets.

     That left just 2.1% of volume for everything else at Kalshi, or about $700 million out of $33 billion. For comparison, sports events (excluding parlays) are generally eclipsing $700 million  in a day  in recent weeks.

   Elections were the biggest category of the rest, at more than $200 million.

   Mention markets are the second biggest at $140 million.
