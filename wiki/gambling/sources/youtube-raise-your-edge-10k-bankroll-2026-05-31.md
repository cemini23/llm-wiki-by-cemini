### 10-step path (synthesized)

| Step | Theme | Retail rule |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Variance + BRM + EV | **100 buy-ins minimum** ($100 → $1 MTTs; $200 → $2); ~200 BI if cautious; ~300 for hyper formats |
| 2 | Preflop | **See, understand, apply** — don't blind chart-copy; drill 2–3 weeks before "real" stakes |
| 3 | Micro execution | Start **lowest stakes** even with $500; cap ~$0.10 MTTs; review sessions |

| 9 | Advanced ICM | HRC / paid trainers when bankroll justifies |
| 10 | Behavior | River **curiosity calls** = gambling; revisit pot odds / EV |
