## Narrative

The **National Council on Problem Gambling (NCPG)** declared that prediction markets are **"functionally gambling"** regardless of how they are "currently legally defined." Board President **Derek Longmeier** issued the statement on Tuesday, 2026-09-23. It lands about two weeks after the **Massachusetts Gaming Commission** discussed leaving the NCPG over its **Kalshi** partnership, announced in May 2026.

### The Kalshi arrangement

Kalshi **donated $2 million** and became the **first member** of a new NCPG "Financial Services & Trading" membership subcategory. Many NCPG members are state regulators actively litigating Kalshi's sports contracts, which turned the donation into a conflict.

### Member and affiliate exits

| Body | Action | Date / note |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Ohio Casino Control Commission** | Left first | June 2026 — Interim ED Andromeda Morrison |
| **Michigan Gaming Control Board** | Left | ED Henry Williams: "not associated with organizations that are affiliated with companies engaged in illegal gambling" |
| **Nevada Council on Problem Gambling** | Ended affiliation | August 2026 — ED Trey Delap cited a "fundamental difference in how we believe a problem gambling organization should respond to emerging gambling risks" |
| **Evergreen Council on Problem Gambling** | Ended 35-year affiliation | Claimed Longmeier accepted the Kalshi donation without board approval |
| **Massachusetts Gaming Commission** | Considered leaving; stayed | Will "continue to monitor the situation" |
| **Aristocrat** (slot manufacturer) | "Stepping back" | Cited NCPG's "acceptance of substantial funding" from a prediction-market operator |

Illinois officials voiced support for the regulators who cut ties. The Pennsylvania Gaming Control Board said it respected those decisions but has not left.

### Staff departures

- **Jaime Costello**, NCPG director of programs, resigned immediately after Longmeier's statement. She said her exit was **not** about Kalshi.
- **Heather Maurer**, Government Director, resigned after fewer than nine months in the role (started 2027-01-07, succeeding Keith Whyte, who led the group for more than 20 years). She remains through **2026-10-16** to assist the transition. The NCPG began a search for a new executive director.

### The poll the NCPG leans on

A **Harris Poll** commissioned by the NCPG found:

- **85%** of Americans agree people can develop unhealthy or addictive behaviors related to prediction-market platforms.
- **84%** believe these platforms should be treated similarly to gambling on consumer protections.
