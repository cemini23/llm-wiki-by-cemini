## Narrative

### Stake's Brazil reprieve lasted one day

**The ban.** President **Lula da Silva's** emergency decree — **Provisional Measure 1,394/2026**, effective **2026-09-25** — banned all online betting and casino games overnight, giving operators **10 days** to remove their digital presence and **30 days** for federal authorisations to terminate.

**The injunction.** On **2026-10-08**, Judge **Cristiane Farias Rodrigues dos Santos** (9th Federal Civil Court of São Paulo) granted **Stake** a preliminary injunction preserving its authorisation until **2026-10-25**. Her reasoning was textual: the decree sets **two different deadlines** — 10 days to go dark, 30 days for licences to lapse — and the second is evidence the government itself contemplated a transition period. Stake had asked to operate to **2029-12-31**; that was refused.

**Conditions attached:** Stake had to keep **AML controls, identity and age verification, responsible-gambling tools, self-exclusion, deposit/loss/session limits** running, block legally prohibited and programme-excluded persons, and prevent social-welfare funds being used for gambling. It could not **onboard new customers, promote sign-up bonuses, or create new marketing material**, and had to keep withdrawals and refunds unobstructed.

**The reversal.** One day later — **2026-10-09** — Judge **Luis Antonio Johonsom di Salvo** of the **Federal Regional Court of the 3rd Region** suspended the injunction on the Attorney General's (AGU) appeal. His reason: letting one company stay licensed while every rival complied was **unequal**, and would undermine the ban's policy. Stake went dark again from **2026-10-10**.

**The stake (literally):** Stake told the court it paid **R$30M (~US$6M)** for a licence meant to run to 2029.

**What is still open.** Supreme Court Justice **Luiz Fux** will decide whether to maintain the provisional measure, and has indicated he will not rule until **after the election concludes on 2026-10-25** — to avoid favouring a candidate. The runoff is on that date; Lula trailed Bolsonaro 45.16% to 47.03% after round one.

**Why it is recorded:** the ban has already shut **Flutter's** Brazilian business (~US$70M of 2026 revenue at risk), and Stake's injunction was the **first judicial limit** on the measure. It also establishes the shape of any temporary relief: **equal treatment across operators**, not relief for one.

The prior period ran **78 weeks** (£1.5bn), so the year-over-year fall is not comparable. Betfred says the online spend drove a **£23.3M like-for-like revenue increase** on an adjusted 12-month basis.

**Duties paid:** £147.4M betting duty, £54.5M machine gaming duty, £18.6M statutory betting duty, £55.1M commission.

**The retail retreat is the story.** Employee costs rose **£19.2M** like-for-like despite headcount falling **9,622 → 9,463** (National Minimum Wage and employer NI). A further **132 shops** close this year, up to **600 jobs**. Betfred is cutting its **Super League** title sponsorship, and Fred Done has said the five British Classics sponsorships go if the MGD rate doubles.

### Sky Bet — new MD, Flutter's scale

**Neil Banbury** took over as Managing Director of **Sky Betting and Gaming** on **2026-09-29**, joining from **Kindred** (Financial Analyst 2010 → Chief Commercial and Marketing Officer 2024). He oversees **Sky Bet, Sky Casino, Sky Poker, Sky Bingo**.

> "Sky Betting & Gaming is one of the strongest brands in the UK market… I'm excited to lead the team in its next phase: delivering a great experience for our customers, with safer gambling at the core, and working with colleagues across Flutter to make the most of the group's scale."

**Context:** this is the third senior change at Flutter this year — **Dan Taylor** replaced **Peter Jackson** as Group CEO on **2026-10-01**, and **Amy Howe** left the FanDuel CEO role earlier. **Flutter's share price is down over 63% year over year.** The UK market faces restructuring after the **April 2026 tax rise**; Flutter (Paddy Power), Entain (Ladbrokes), and evoke (William Hill) have all announced shop closures.
