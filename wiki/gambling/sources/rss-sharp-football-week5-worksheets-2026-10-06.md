**Matchup data:** Jets favored **−2.5**, implied totals **Jets 21.25 / Cleveland 18.75**. Both teams rank in the **bottom third in plays per game**.

**Team notes:**

- The Jets have produced **no run of 15+ yards** this season and sit **last** in converting completions into first downs or touchdowns.
- Cleveland's pass defense ranks **7th in success rate** but **30th against the run**.
- The Browns have led on only **27.6%** of offensive snaps.
