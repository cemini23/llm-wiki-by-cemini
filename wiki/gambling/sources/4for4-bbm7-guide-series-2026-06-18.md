## Narrative

### Umbrella guide structure [TENTATIVE — paywall]

Five sections per author intro: **Roster Construction, Stacking, ADP Value, Player Takes, Diversification/Uniqueness** — backed by 24 BBMVI graphs/tables.

### QB (BBMVI → BBMVII) [CONFIRMED]

- **3-QB builds:** 4 of 5 best-performing constructions; 3-QB outperformed 2-QB in **4 of 6** BBM years
- **BBMVI timing:** optimal to have **zero QBs through R13**; elite QBs (ADP R1–4) had **14.3%** advance vs **16.7%** avg
- **2026 shift:** only Josh Allen elite (R1–4); Hurts/Daniels cheap in **R6**; late QBs expensive — Stroud **R12** last confident starter; **~40%** of ADP 85–116 are QBs
- **2026 play:** avoid Allen R3 (RB cliff); target R6 tier + aggressive R8–10 QB window for 3-QB builds
- **Stacking:** 3 team stacks → **2×** finals rate vs 0; cap same-team at **4–5** players
- **Week 17:** more game stacks = higher ceiling; BBMVI winner stacked **4** W17 matchups
- **Byes:** same-bye QB room → **14.5%** advance vs **16.7%**; never draft 1 QB only

### RB (BBMVI → BBMVII) [CONFIRMED]

- **R1 RB:** doubles playoff advance vs avoiding; **2 straight RBs R1–2:** ~**30%** advance vs ~**8%** for zero RB early
- **RB count:** constructions sorted by RB total — **4 RB** best, then 5, 6, 7 (early-RB teams take fewer total)
- **2025 context:** RB3–7 set/near 5-year PPG highs; early RB health + goal-line run rate up
- **Zero RB salvage:** prior-season **routes/game** correlates with team score — targets: Stevenson, Swift, Pollard, Skattebo
- **2026:** RB cliff after **R3**; weak rookie class; author leans **min 2 RB through R5**, not viable zero-RB in early drafts

### WR (BBMVI → BBMVII) [CONFIRMED]

- **Avoid R1 WR** doubled advance vs taking R1 WR — but **4–5 WR by R7** optimal
- **Winning pattern:** ~**3 RB early**, then **4 consecutive WRs**; best WR1 in **R3–5**, immediate WR2
- **WR6–20:** lowest scoring in 5 years; R1–3 WRs mostly below avg advance (except Nacua, JSN)
- **2026:** elite WRs (Chase, Nacua, ARSB, Lamb) OK R1; otherwise RB R1; **R3–5 WR window**
- **ADP value:** top CLV bucket → **3×** finals rate vs worst; closing line = late Aug / early Sep

### TE (BBMVI → BBMVII) [CONFIRMED]

- **3-TE builds** outscored 2-TE in regular-season points — **6th consecutive year**
- **Elite TE (R4 or earlier):** **13.9%** advance (below avg); McBride only elite hit
- **BBMVI optimal:** zero TE through **R11**, then rapid to **3 TEs** in R11–13 cluster
- **Trend:** TE7–12 PPG at 5-year highs; multi-TE sets ↑ at expense of slot WRs
- **2026:** fade Bowers/McBride R2 opportunity cost; attack **R11+ TE window** — Ferguson, Goedert, Strange, Gadsden; goal **3 TEs** stacked with QBs

### Schedule & byes [CONFIRMED]

- **~84%** of BBMVII prize pool paid in **Week 17**; 1st place = **533×** last-place W17 payout
- **Game stacks:** tiebreaker after early-round bets, not primary R1–4 driver; Ladd → target Worthy example
- **Byes:** 2nd vs 3rd in pod often **~45 pts** — one QB/TE week can decide advance
- **3 QB same bye** → below-average advance; **3 TE distinct byes** → edge (2nd consecutive year)
- **Shared bye cap:** fine through **7** players; **10+** same bye → **15.6%** advance (−1.1%)

### Draft timing (4for4 view) [CONFIRMED]

Reconciles partially with ETR Manifesto (@sources/etr-best-ball-mania-manifesto-draft-timing-2026-06-18.md):

| Phase | Calendar | 4for4 guidance |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| Post-NFL Draft | May | Lean structure; 3-QB/3-TE; conservative; target rookies before ADP rises |
| Minicamp | Jun–Jul | ADP fragile — monitor news; avoid stale picks |
| Preseason | Aug–Sep | **Pad portfolio**; sharpest stands; injury clarity; highest ceiling outliers |

BBMVI playoff teams scored **~10 PPG more** in Jul–Sep drafts vs Feb–draft window. High-score outliers mostly **late Aug/Sep**; low-score outliers **Jul–early Aug**.
