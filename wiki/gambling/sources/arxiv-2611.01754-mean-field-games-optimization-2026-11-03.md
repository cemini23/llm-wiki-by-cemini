## Narrative

A 125-page survey of discrete-time mean field games — the equilibrium framework where many small agents each act in their own interest and the aggregate distribution is what each one reacts to.

**Why it is filed in the gambling wiki at all:** it arrived in that inbox, and mean field games are the formal language for **crowd equilibrium**. A prediction market is a population of agents whose payoffs depend on what the population does. That is the MFG setting, stated informally.

**What it is not:** a trading paper. There is no market here, no calibration, no empirical result. It is mathematics with an optimization and learning emphasis.

**Caveat.** Only the title page was read. Nothing about the specific frameworks or algorithms is established on this page.

**What would make it useful later.** If a future page needs to reason about equilibrium in a crowded market — why a popular strategy decays, why an obvious trade is already priced — this is the reference for the formalism. Until then it is a shelf item.
