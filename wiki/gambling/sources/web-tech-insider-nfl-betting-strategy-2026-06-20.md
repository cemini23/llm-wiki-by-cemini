## Narrative

Process-first NFL primer aligned with wiki +EV discipline: **key numbers → line shop → flat units → CLV**, not outcome chasing.

### Key numbers [CONFIRMED — standard NFL literature]

Margins cluster on **3** (most common), **7**, then 10, 6, 14. Half-points around 3 and 7 carry disproportionate EV vs other half-points.

| Margin | Shopping note |
|--------|---------------|
| 3 | Often worth buying +2.5/+3 or -3/-2.5 |
| 7 | Valuable at +6.5/+7 |
| 10 | Situational |
| 1, 4, 5 | Rare — rarely worth buying |

### Line shopping + CLV [CONFIRMED]

- Closing line = market consensus; **positive CLV** (beat close) = strongest long-term edge signal
- Multi-book accounts mandatory; half-point spread differences compound over a season

### Situational angles [TENTATIVE — verify weekly]

- Rest/travel: bye weeks, short-week road trips
- Division games: tend closer than spread implies
- Weather: **wind > ~15 mph** biggest total factor (more than rain/cold)
- Backup QB: market often misprices week-one reaction
- Lookahead/letdown spots: motivation edges (game-specific)

### Bankroll [CONFIRMED — matches @concepts/bankroll-management.md]

- **1–2% flat units** per play; no chase sizing
- Parlays as core strategy = -EV (hold compounds per leg)

### Live betting hygiene

- Overreaction on quick TDs; slow run-heavy starts can improve live under prices
- Pre-set live bankroll rules before kickoff
