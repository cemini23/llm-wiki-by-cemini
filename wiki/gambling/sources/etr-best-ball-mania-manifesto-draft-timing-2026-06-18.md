## Raw Concept

Extract from Mike Leone's **Best Ball Mania Manifesto** (Establish The Run), Part 2 — ADP Value, live players, and draft timing. Based on BBM3 (2022 season) pick-by-pick data.

### Key timing findings

1. **July–August optimal** — best combined odds of strong closing-line ADP Value + 13+ live players at playoff entry
2. **May high variance** — top AND bottom ADP Capital buckets overrepresented; live-player count suffers
3. **August health boost** — 47.2% of 18-live-player teams drafted in August vs 41.2% expected
4. **Bye weeks** — accounting for byes did not materially change stacking/advance conclusions
5. **Kerrane drafted July 18** — BBM3 $2M winner; 15/18 live in Week 17

### ADP value impact (same source)

- Top 20% ADP value/capital: advance rates +~25%, playoff win rates +~5–10%
- Top 10% vs bottom 10%: 328-pick equivalent gap (116 gained vs 212 lost)
- Real-time ADP value nearly as impactful as closing-line value

## Snippets

> "Drafting somewhere between mid-July and mid-August seems to provide contestants with the best combined odds of achieving strong closing line ADP Value and having 13-plus live players on a roster when entering the playoff stages."

> "The earlier you draft, the less likely you are to make it to the playoff stages with 13+ live players."

> "The data did not change in any meaningful way after accounting for either bye weeks or early-season weeks."

## Dead Ends

- Manifesto uses BBM3 data — confirm patterns hold in BBM4–6 before treating as immutable law
- Slow-draft timing confound acknowledged by author; not fully isolated
