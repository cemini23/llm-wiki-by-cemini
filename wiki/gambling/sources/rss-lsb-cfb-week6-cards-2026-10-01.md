## Narrative

### A — No. 4 Miami @ Clemson, Sat 19:30 ET (ABC)

**Pick: Clemson +17 (−110).** Both sides −110; the spread is posted at both **16.5 and 17**, so the higher number carries the value — a 17-point Miami win pushes at +17 and loses at +16.5.

The hook: this is **the first time Clemson is a double-digit home underdog in Dabo Swinney's 245 games**. His five prior double-digit-dog spots (at Florida State 2012 and 2014, vs. Georgia in Atlanta, at Texas 2024, at LSU this year) were **all away from home and all losses**.

Supporting numbers: Clemson's defense has allowed **12.3 points per game since the LSU opener** and exceeded its ~15.5 implied mark in all three wins (22, 28, 24), missing only at LSU (10). Miami beat Wake Forest by just **13 as a 20.5-point favorite** — the kind of closer game the cover needs. Miami's defense is elite: **9.0 points per game** (4th FBS), **158 rushing yards on 85 carries (1.9 YPA)**, a nation-best **39.5 rushing yards per game**, against an offense averaging **51.8**. Risk is a blowout, as at LSU.

Moneyline +575 is framed as a "sprinkle" — $5 returns $28.75.

Full market: Miami −17 (−110) / Clemson +17 (−110); Clemson ML +575 (14.8%), Miami ML −900 (90.0%); total 48 (−110 both sides).

### B — No. 7 Alabama @ Mississippi State, Sat 12:00 ET (ABC)

**Kamario Taylor (MSU QB) props** — the market is ahead of the usage.

| Market | Price | Implied |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Anytime TD | −117 | 53.9% |
| First TD | +900 | 10.0% |
| Heisman | +700 | 12.5% (from +1300) |

Taylor: **1,192 passing yards, 14 TD, 2 INT, 83-of-120 (69.2%)**, 298 yards per game at **9.9 YPA**. Game log: 354 vs. ULM, 227 at Minnesota, 251 at South Carolina, 360 vs. Missouri.

**The conflict:** the anytime-TD price implies he scores **53.9%** of the time, but he has run for **185 yards and one score on 37 attempts**, and that lone rushing TD came **2026-09-05 vs. ULM** — none in three straight games (**25%, one of four**).

Game: Alabama −5.5 (−115), Alabama ML −225 (69.2%), MSU ML +185 (35.1%), total 60 (−110). Keelon Russell anytime TD −162 (61.8%); Russell Heisman +1200. Both teams 4-0, 2-0 SEC. Alabama has won the series **16 straight, the last four by 23+**.

### C — Washington @ No. 18 USC, Sat night at the Coliseum

**Pick: Under 59 (−110).** Both teams enter off their first losses. **USC is 1-4 ATS; Washington is 0-4.**

The arithmetic: the midpoint of the two teams' scoring averages is **55.5** — three and a half points under the 59, which is the cushion.

Washington's defense allows **16.8 points per game and 4.6 yards per play**; opponents convert **29% on third down (15 of 52)**, including Washington State's **0-for-11**. It allows **3.2 yards per rush and 6.4 per pass attempt** — roughly three yards below Jayden Maiava's **9.5 per attempt (1,499 yards, nation-leading)**.

The caveat, stated in the article: three of four Washington opponents were Washington State, Utah State, and FCS Eastern Washington; Minnesota scored 27 (21 in the first half).

USC's five games average **66.2 combined points** (four topped 59); Washington's four average **44.8**. USC gained 446 yards vs. Oregon but at **5.9 per play** (below its 6.9 season average), converted **2 of 11** third downs, and scored a season-low **27** after 39+ in every prior game. Against that, USC's defense allowed **30, 35, and 41** in its last three — and the Oregon game carried a 61.5 total and finished with **68** points.

The author's frame: the Under needs the Oregon game, "not USC's first four, to be the template."

Injuries: USC WR **Corey Simms out**; freshman **Trent Mosley** (13 rec, 255 yds, 4 TD) and TE **Mark Bowman** (21 rec) both questionable. The Big Ten's final availability report lands **two hours before the 19:30 ET kickoff**.

Market: USC −9.5 (−115) / Washington +9.5 (−105); total 59 (−110 both); USC ML −390 (79.6%), Washington ML +295 (25.3%).
