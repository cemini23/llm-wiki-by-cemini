## Narrative

### The situation

**Tampa Bay swept the Yankees** in three games, clinching **4-3 at Yankee Stadium** on a Victor Mesa Jr. two-run homer in the sixth — the franchise's **first best-of-five sweep**. The Rays awaited the **Guardians–White Sox Game 5** (Saturday, 2026-10-10, Progressive Field), with the **ALCS opening Monday 2026-10-12 at Tropicana Field**.

### The repricing

| Team | Before | After | Implied |
|------|--------|-------|---------|
| **Rays** | −105 | **−180** | **64.3%** |
| **Guardians** | +1100 | **+330** | **23.3%** |
| **White Sox** | +170 | **+500** | **16.7%** |

Cleveland's move added **14.9 points** of implied probability; Chicago's slide removed **20.4 points**. A Rays ticket at −180 risks $180 to win $100.

### The two mechanisms

**1. The price tracks October form, not the season.** The Rays won **98 games** with a **+86** run differential — *below* the Yankees' **+138**. The article's framing: the price reflects "October form more than the season's margin." A sweep repriced the market away from the regular-season rating.

**2. The road team was 4-0 in the Guardians–White Sox series.** Home teams went **0-4**. Cleveland's shorter price over Chicago rests on **Progressive Field and two straight wins**, despite the White Sox having the **superior season run differential (56 vs. Cleveland's 11)**.

**Game 4 detail:** Cleveland's **9-5 win at Rate Field** turned on a sixth inning in which **10 Guardians batted and six scored**. Reliever **Grant Taylor faced six hitters and retired none** — the night after Chicago allowed nine runs in Game 3. Expected starters: **Gavin Williams** (Cleveland, despite closing out Game 4) and **Sean Burke** (Chicago).

> The White Sox went from the Rays' "closest pursuer to the longest shot on the board" after losing Games 3 and 4 by a combined **18-8**.

## Dead Ends

- **MLB is not an operator lane.** Recorded for the pricing mechanism only.
- **The White Sox's run differential was better than Cleveland's** yet its price was far longer — a reminder that **futures price the remaining path**, not the season aggregate.
- **A 0-4 home record in one series is noise**, not a pattern. Do not carry it forward.
