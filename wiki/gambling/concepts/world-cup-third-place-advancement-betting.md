## Raw Concept

Betting the **third-place advancement** path in the 48-team World Cup — points thresholds, GD tiebreaks, and bubble teams.

## Narrative

### Rule reminder

12 group thirds compete for **8 spots**. Ranking uses FIFA tiebreakers (points → GD → goals scored → …). A team can finish **3rd in group** and still reach Round of 32.

### Points heuristics (research simulation) [TENTATIVE]

| Third-place points | Advancement outlook |
|--------------------|---------------------|
| **4** | Near-certain among thirds |
| **3** | GD / goals-scored dogfight — **bubble tier** |
| **2** | Effectively dead for third-place route |

### Bubble vs safe profiles

**Borderline thirds (GD-dependent):** Czechia, Ghana, Iran, Bosnia — outcomes swing on margin of defeat vs group winner [TENTATIVE research list].

**Defensive / low-event profiles** that protect GD: Sweden, Ivory Coast, Paraguay, Scotland — research "safe third" bucket [TENTATIVE].

**Always re-fetch live prices** — these are pre-tournament research anchors only.

### How to bet it

1. Model **three group results** (W/D/L) and GD — not just "strong team"
2. Prefer **advance / reach knockout** contracts over group-winner if third-place path is the thesis
3. Watch **final group-day** simultaneous kickoffs — live advance markets reprice fast
4. Cross-check `@concepts/world-cup-books-vs-pm-divergence.md` before sizing

### Common mistake

Assuming a big nation **must win the group** — in 2026 they may target **4 points + GD** as third and still advance.
