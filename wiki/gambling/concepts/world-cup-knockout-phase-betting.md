## Raw Concept

Knockout-round **betting mechanics** for World Cup 2026 — calendar, contract types, and resolution traps for retail bettors.

## Narrative

### Phase calendar (betting rhythm)

| Phase | Window | Retail note |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Group stage | 2026-06-11 → 06-27 | Advance markets resolve on elimination |
| **Transition** | ~2026-06-27 | Round of 32 listings surge; bracket permutations |
| Round of 32 | 06-28 → 07-03 | `Reach R16` ≈ match **To Advance** (1:1) |
| Round of 16 | 07-04 → 07-07 | Milestone markets reprice on elimination |
| Quarterfinals | 07-09 → 07-11 | Longer rest → more stable pre-kickoff lines |
| Semifinals | 07-14 → 07-15 | Peak match liquidity |
| Final | 2026-07-19 | Outright winner resolves |

### Resolution classes (read the rules)

| Bet type | Settles on | Trap |
|----------|------------|------|
| **90-min moneyline (3-way)** | Regulation + stoppage only | **Draw** if tied at whistle — even if favorite wins in ET |
| **To Advance / To Qualify** | Winner after ET + pens | Different from 90-min ML |
| **Reach milestone** (e.g. Reach QF) | No if eliminated before stage | Immediate No on KO exit |
| **Tournament winner** | No on any KO loss | Not the same as "Reach Final" |

### Repricing thesis (multi-source)

Advance and milestone markets **move during the tournament** as standings and bracket paths update — some PM creators frame edge as **betting repricing**, not picking the champion upfront [Source: @osint-wiki YouTube compilation — SportsGrid].

Retail implication: you can **exit** before resolution if price moves favorably (subject to liquidity and fees).

### Match betting tips

1. Confirm whether your book/PM market includes **ET** before celebrating a tied 90′ lead
2. **Postponement** — regulated markets may resume within 48h; check void rules
3. Semifinal **Reach Final** merges with To Advance logic — don't double-expose unknowingly

### Kalshi vs Polymarket naming

Kalshi may list **stage-of-elimination** (e.g. "eliminated IN R16") as inverse of **Reach QF** — read ticker + rules; don't assume label parity [TENTATIVE — @osint-wiki knockout map].
