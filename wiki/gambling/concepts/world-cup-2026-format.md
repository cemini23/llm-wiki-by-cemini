## Raw Concept

How the **2026 expanded World Cup** structure changes betting base rates and market types vs the 32-team era.

## Narrative

### Structure [CONFIRMED FIFA 2026 rules via @osint-wiki sources]

- **48 teams**, **12 groups of 4**
- **Top two** in each group qualify automatically (24 teams)
- **Eight best third-place** teams also advance → **32 teams** in Round of 32
- **104 total matches** (72 group, 32 knockout)

### Why bettors care

| 32-team era habit | 2026 adjustment |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| "Favorite must win group" | Third-place escape hatch for strong teams in tough groups |
| Advance ≈ top-2 only | Model **third-place GD** scenarios explicitly |
| Group of death = elimination | More paths to Round of 32 — can **inflate** favorite advance prices |
