## Raw Concept

Best ball / draft-and-hold fantasy — roster construction across large entry portfolios (Underdog Best Ball Mania, DraftKings Best Ball, etc.). **Not** sportsbook betting; shares NFL research stack with DFS and props.

## Narrative

### Mechanics

- Draft full roster once; **optimal lineup auto-selected** each week
- No waivers, no trades — pure **draft skill + portfolio variance**
- Underdog BBM7: 12-team snake, **18 rounds**, $25 entry, up to **150 entries**, $2M first `[TENTATIVE — verify live]` [Source: Hpo51KrYKPI]

### Strategy themes

1. **Portfolio drafting** — many entries diversify build types; treat fees like GPP bankroll (`@concepts/bankroll-management.md`)
2. **ADP vs projection delta** — early May–June drafts show widest mispricings; compare rank tools to Underdog ADP [Source: AdVSOvT79tA, LgDz2jAfGjU]
3. **TE mispricing cluster** — multiple analysts flagged **40–50 spot ADP deltas** on mid-tier TEs in BBM7 prep [TENTATIVE]
4. **Stacks / correlation** — QB+WR (+ bring-back); debate whether **overstacking** (e.g. Burrow+Chase+Higgins) caps ceiling [Source: Hpo51KrYKPI]
5. **Board-driven drafting** — “optimal strategy = take what the board gives you” vs forcing zero-RB [Source: tJ_Oh18AAKo]
6. **Bye week construction** — playoff weeks (15–17) determine tournament survival; avoid roster cliffs
7. **Barbell timing** — pros draft heavily at launch when casual drafters use “horrific rankings” [Source: AdVSOvT79tA]

### BBM7 vs sports betting

Same NFL knowledge (injuries, pace, game environment) informs stacks — but best ball is **against the field**, not the spread. Player prop angles overlap; line shopping does not.
