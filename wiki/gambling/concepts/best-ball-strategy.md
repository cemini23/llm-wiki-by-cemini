## Raw Concept

Best ball / draft-and-hold fantasy — roster construction across large entry portfolios (Underdog Best Ball Mania, DraftKings Best Ball, etc.). **Not** sportsbook betting; shares NFL research stack with DFS and props.

## Narrative

### Mechanics

- Draft full roster once; **optimal lineup auto-selected** each week
- No waivers, no trades — pure **draft skill + portfolio variance**
- Underdog BBM7: 12-team snake, **18 rounds**, half-PPR, $25 entry, up to **150 entries**, $15M pool, $2M first [CONFIRMED — @entities/tournaments/best-ball-mania-vii.md]

### Winner roster archetypes (BBM1–BBM6) [CONFIRMED]

Full dossier: @concepts/best-ball-mania-winners.md. Modal shell and evolution:

| Position | Typical count | Pattern |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| QB | 3 | Late-round QBs (R13–R18) repeatedly decisive |
| RB | 4–6 | Draft capital **migrated later** each year (Hyperfragile → Hero RB) |
| WR | 6–9 | **Early WR picks dominate** modern winners (BBM4–BBM6) |
| TE | 2–3 | 2 TEs standard; late-round TE ADP mispricing trend |

**Archetypes that have won:** Hyperfragile RB (BBM1), Double-Anchor (BBM2–3), Zero RB (BBM4), WR-first stacks (BBM5), Hero RB (BBM6). **Portfolio diversification across archetypes is rational.**

**Stacking:** Near-universal but **skinny** (≤3 per team). Overstacking caps ceiling [Source: ChessLiam, BBM2 winner]. 4for4 BBMVI: **3 team stacks → 2× finals rate**; cap same-team at 4–5 players [Source: @sources/4for4-bbm7-guide-series-2026-06-18.md].

### BBM7 2026 positional playbook (4for4 / BBMVI advance data) [CONFIRMED]

From @sources/4for4-bbm7-guide-series-2026-06-18.md — optimizes **regular-season advance**, not identical to historical **winner** paths (@concepts/best-ball-mania-winners.md). Reconcile both in portfolio.

**Modal shell for 2026:** **3 QB / 4–5 RB / 6–7 WR / 3 TE** (4for4 leans 3 TE; winners often 2 TE — diversify)

| Position | BBMVI advance meta | 2026 BBM7 application |
|----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **QB** | 3-QB builds dominate (4 of 5 top constructions) | R6 tier (Hurts, Daniels) + aggressive **R8–10 QB window** (~40% of ADP 85–116); avoid Allen R3 vs RB cliff; Stroud ~R12 last trusted starter |
| **RB** | R1 RB doubles advance; 2 RBs R1–2 triples vs zero-RB early | **Min 2 RB through R5**; RB cliff after R3; 4-RB builds optimal 2 yrs running; zero-RB only with high routes/game (Stevenson, Swift, Pollard, Skattebo) |
| **WR** | Avoid R1 WR (2× advance) BUT 4–5 WR by R7 | Pattern: **3 RB early → 4 straight WRs**; elite WRs (Chase, Nacua, ARSB, Lamb) OK R1; **R3–5 WR window**; WR6–20 depressed |
| **TE** | 3-TE > 2-TE (6th straight year); zero TE until R11 | Attack **R11–13 TE cluster** (Ferguson, Goedert, Strange, Gadsden); fade Bowers/McBride R2 opportunity cost |

**Week 17 / schedule:**
- **84%** of BBM7 pool paid in W17; game stacks increase ceiling (BBMVI winner: 4 W17 game stacks)
- Use W17 projected totals as **tiebreaker**, not primary R1–4 driver
- **Distinct QB/TE byes** — same-bye QB room = 14.5% advance vs 16.7%; 3 TE distinct byes = edge
- Cap **10+ players** on same bye (−1.1% advance)

**ADP value:** Top closing-line CLV bucket → **3×** finals rate vs worst [Source: 4for4 WR article]. Non-negotiable skill for 150-entry portfolios.

### 2026 scoring-environment thesis (Fantasy Six Pack) [TENTATIVE — macro]

From @sources/fantasysixpack-bbm-new-meta-2026-06-08.md — **framework**, not a player list. Claims NFL scoring has **compressed** (fewer free explosives, more 12/13 personnel, flatter QB scoring). Sharp 2026 drafting = **structure for this environment**, not 2022 autopilot.

| Position | F6P shift | Reconcile with 4for4 / winners |
|----------|-----------|--------------------------------|
| **QB** | Early elite QB **thinner edge** at price; **3-QB** as structural answer | Same as 4for4: fade Allen R3, attack R8–10; elite QB only on CLV |
| **WR** | **Selective**, not blind hoard — fragile WR3 roles in heavy personnel | Still hit **4–5 WR by R7** shell; prioritize alphas + verified routes (Chase, Nacua, ARSB, JSN) over ambiguous depth |
| **RB** | Market **overcorrected** on Zero RB; volume backs (18–22 touches) ↑ value | Supports 4for4 early-RB advance meta + portfolio RB-forward tranche (~35%) |
| **TE** | **Elite or wait** — middle tier trap; late darts in multi-TE offenses | Same as 4for4: fade Bowers/McBride R2, cluster R11–13 |

**Structural checklist before locking a build:** early touch volume · QB only at price · route-earning WRs · no TE dead zone · late spike paths · drafting 2026 not 2022.

### Strategy themes

1. **Portfolio drafting** — many entries diversify build types; treat fees like GPP bankroll (`@concepts/bankroll-management.md`)
2. **ADP vs projection delta** — early May–June drafts show widest mispricings; compare rank tools to Underdog ADP [Source: AdVSOvT79tA, LgDz2jAfGjU]
3. **TE mispricing cluster** — multiple analysts flagged **40–50 spot ADP deltas** on mid-tier TEs in BBM7 prep [TENTATIVE]
4. **Stacks / correlation** — QB+WR (+ bring-back); debate whether **overstacking** (e.g. Burrow+Chase+Higgins) caps ceiling [Source: Hpo51KrYKPI]
5. **Board-driven drafting** — “optimal strategy = take what the board gives you” vs forcing zero-RB [Source: tJ_Oh18AAKo]
6. **Bye week construction** — playoff weeks (15–17) determine tournament survival; avoid roster cliffs (byes known from May schedule)
7. **Draft timing** — ETR BBM3 data: **mid-July through mid-August** balances CLV and live players; split 150 entries across windows — @concepts/best-ball-draft-timing.md
8. **Early-window CLV** — May–June still valuable for rookie mispricings (~20–30% of portfolio), but avoid drafting entire portfolio early [Source: AdVSOvT79tA, ETR Manifesto]
9. **Advance vs winner tension** — 4for4 BBMVI data favors early RB + late TE; BBM4/BBM6 champions used Zero/Hero RB — portfolio both
10. **2026 environment filter** — F6P: avoid WR3 lottery hoarding and priced-in elite QB; still run diversified archetypes [@sources/fantasysixpack-bbm-new-meta-2026-06-08.md]

### BBM7 vs sports betting

Same NFL knowledge (injuries, pace, game environment) informs stacks — but best ball is **against the field**, not the spread. Player prop angles overlap; line shopping does not.

### Phase-0 before max-entering

1. Confirm payout structure and max entries on platform
2. Use projection source with documented track record (not promo rankings)
3. Cap total entry fees as fixed **entertainment/GPP budget**
