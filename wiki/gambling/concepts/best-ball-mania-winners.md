### Cross-winner patterns

#### 1. Modal roster shell [CONFIRMED]

**2–3 QB / 5–6 RB / 6–8 WR / 2–3 TE** — every winner fits this envelope. BBM5 is the outlier (2 QB, 3 TE, 8 WR). RB count (4–6) varies by archetype; WR floor is 6+.

#### 2. RB draft capital migration [CONFIRMED]

Chronological shift in when RBs are drafted:

```
BBM1 (2020): Hyperfragile — 3 RBs in first 3 rounds
BBM2–3 (2021–22): Double-Anchor — 2 elite RBs early
BBM4 (2023): Zero RB — all RBs mid/late
BBM5 (2024): WR-first + stack-heavy
BBM6 (2025): Hero RB — single elite back (Henry R2), then WR-heavy
```

**Every major archetype has won.** No single "correct" build — portfolio diversification across archetypes is rational.

#### 3. Stacking rules [CONFIRMED]

- **Near-universal** but deliberately **light** ("skinny stacks," ≤3 per team)
- **Don't overstack** — caps weekly ceiling (ChessLiam principle)
- **QB not required in stack** — secondary pass-catchers + RB/TE stacks work
- **Week 17 game stacks** treated as freeroll micro-edge (Penny+ARSB in BBM2)
- BBM5 exception: **5-man Bengals stack** won — proves aggressive correlation can work when paired with closing-line value (Conner, Thielen, Brown discounts)

#### 4. Late-round league-winners [CONFIRMED]

Decisive value repeatedly from **final-round RBs** and **R15–R18 QBs**:
- Kyren Williams (R18, BBM4)
- C.J. Stroud (R17, BBM4)
- Bryce Young (R13) + Tyler Shough (R15, BBM6)
- Chase Brown (late, BBM5)

#### 5. TE strategy [CONFIRMED / TENTATIVE blend]

- **2 TEs standard** across winners
- BBM1: pay up for elite (Kelce + Gronk)
- BBM4: 3 TEs, value-tier (LaPorta breakout)
- BBM6: mid TE (Kittle 5.09) + very late bridge (Johnson 16.04)
- Trend: shift from early-elite toward **late-round TE ADP mispricing**

#### 6. Draft slot [CONFIRMED partial]

BBM3 seat **7**, BBM5 seat **4**, BBM6 seat **9** — no evidence that slot position systematically predicts winners.

### BBM7 portfolio implications

1. **Diversify archetypes** across 150 entries — Hero RB, Zero RB, stack-heavy, balanced
2. **Anchor on WR early** — consistent across BBM4–BBM6 winners
3. **Limit RB draft capital** — 0–1 early RB unless board forces Double-Anchor
4. **3 QBs, 2 TEs** — non-negotiable shell
5. **Skinny stacks** — 2–3 players per team; avoid 4+ unless high-conviction environment (BBM5 path)
6. **Reserve late capital** for R15–R18 QB/RB lottery tickets
7. **Week 17 correlation** — prioritize players in meaningful late-season games
