Sharp/low-hold sportsbook often used as **CLV benchmark** and model **devig** reference. Access varies by jurisdiction.

## Narrative

Pinnacle is widely cited as a **sharp** book: lower hold, efficient lines, quick moves on sharp action. Limits winning players. US access restricted — many bettors use Pinnacle **closing line** via aggregators as skill benchmark without executing there.
