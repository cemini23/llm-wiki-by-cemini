## Steal bullets (cite these)

**K325:** SLR over architecture, perception, memory, planning, action space, orchestration, self-improvement, applications, and assessment. Synthesis: agents can act; **bounded authority + auditable trajectories** are the gap. CCC: keep HITL on irreversible tools; eval = evidence tree not scalar (K289).

**K326:** Average execution under novel spoofs is low (~1.21%) but **concentrated in reproducible config cells** and shifts across deployment windows (up to 47pp). Restrictive policy can eliminate execution on the same weights. External monitor rejects forged/tampered/replayed/unsigned requests by construction; remaining gap is **semantic authorization** of authenticated user/deputy messages (pairs K310 pre-auth intent). **Zero attack text in wiki.**

**K327:** Formal composability calculus assumed one process = one failure domain. Logos: ROS-like bus; 80 sessions resume with no repeated effect after kills at tool-call cycle boundaries. Steal: isolate plugins; durable state in an append-only transcript; do not re-execute a retained prefix (pairs K282 recovery-boundary honesty).
