## Raw Concept

What competencies should an **agentic engineer** develop — beyond prompt crafting — to operate autonomous systems responsibly?

## Narrative

K237 (arXiv **2607.29610**, Mamdouh Alenezi / SDAIA) frames **ACCEL** — five pillars for educating agentic engineers:

| Pillar | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| **A** — Intent specification | Problem framing, requirements, success criteria |
| **C** — Collaboration / orchestration | Delegation, multi-agent coordination |
| **C** — Critical evaluation | Verification, validation, skepticism over agent output |
| **E** — Ethical governance | Accountability, audit trails, risk awareness |
| **L** — Learning | Adaptive self-directed skill growth |

Delivery: curricula, collaboration, continuous learning. **Risks:** automation bias, deskilling, superficial engagement, diffuse accountability. Evidence: AI tooling can slow work while users *feel* faster.
