## Narrative

These harness facts are worth keeping.

1. **Evolve the interface, not the route.** Agent S4 runs Qwen3.8-27B against an evolved interface repository. It reaches **21.3%** binary score on OSWorld V2. That is **3.8 times** the same model with fixed interfaces. It is above GPT-5.5 at **13.0%** and Claude Opus 4.8 at **20.6%**, at about **4.3%** and **1.5%** of their costs. ScienceBoard goes from **41.4%** to **58.6%** with the same model. @sources/arxiv-agent-s4-evolving-interfaces-2610.28601-2026-10-23.md **No route swap. No clone.**
2. **A family of harness classes can beat one global harness.** OO-Harness gains up to **15.79 percentage points** over the base harness. HGM-Dual gains up to **14.45%** relative case accuracy over the strongest baseline, and it matches HGM final performance with **2x-4x** fewer evaluations. Budget-aware rewards can teach a model to stay inside a stated budget without an external allocator. The abstract gives no single winning percent. A sigmoid penalty often wins within-budget success.
