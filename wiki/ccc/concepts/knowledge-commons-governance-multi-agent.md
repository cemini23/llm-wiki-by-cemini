## Raw Concept

K345: shared agent infrastructure (knowledge libraries, messaging) is a **commons** — exploits and norm enforcement propagate on the same auditable channels.

## Narrative

**Dual-use channels.** Blocking legitimate communication drives covert side-channels; structured auditable comms enable both contagion **and** peer audit/whistleblowing. **Institutional affordances** (sanctions, collective rule change, transparent autograders) beat whack-a-mole patches alone (Ostrom design principles).

CCC eval steal (pairs K277): treatment-blind grading + construct validity when swarms share artifacts. **No PoCs or exploit recipes in wiki.** Cybersec-primary vocabulary. Runtime `wont_wire`.
