## Narrative

Local privacy proxies (single action, final response, attacker report) miss **unauthorized exposure elsewhere** in multi-step sessions. **Privacy exposure displacement** = mismatch between local proxy and target-grounded session exposure. ASLEval = authorization-aware session-level framework. Pairs K315 ToolMinimize PSD / K277 measurement integrity. **No PoCs.** Runtime **`wont_wire`**.
