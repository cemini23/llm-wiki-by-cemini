## Narrative

**Clarus** reframes autonomous research from **closed code-centered execution loops** to **open, auditable, attributable collaboration** among heterogeneous participants (AI agents, humans, teams, labs). Core **project–agent–resource** object model; four layers: Research Application, Digital Collaboration, Physical Substrate, Physical World. Pluggable modules for discovery, negotiation, trust, credit, and resource access.

**Case study:** controlled paper-generation collaboration network — traceable phases, tasks, artifacts, provenance.

**CCC relevance:** federation harness steal — wiki ingest + conductor as network participants with provenance; contrast with PaperClaw/XCIENTIST closed pipelines. **REFERENCE** SaaS; **NO-GO** prod replace.
