## Narrative

OOD for CCC (health HCI) — **stub only**, no concept depth, **`wont_wire`**. Pairs K247 (voice-vs-keyboard input robustness): both show the **interface is not neutral** — modality moves trust and behavior independent of content quality.

Study 1 (N=21, within-subjects, general/symptom/treatment health questions): **significantly higher trust in ChatGPT than Google** for identical health information. Study 2 (N=20, within-subjects, counterbalanced): **text vs speech vs embodied UI changes trust ratings** for the same dissemination; semi-structured interviews throughout.

Operator takeaway: when surfacing agent output to humans (prod briefs, GuruWatcher alerts), the chosen channel shifts perceived trustworthiness — do not treat channel choice as content-neutral.
