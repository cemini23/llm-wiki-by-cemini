## Narrative

A summary of a ~350-page S-1 filing. The headline figures, as the newsletter states them:

- 1400% growth rate.
- 48% of revenue through cloud partners.
- 85% margin.
- $1B operating profit in Q2 — the first profitable quarter in the company's history when adjusted for $1B per quarter of stock-based compensation.
- $134B cash in bank.
- $10B lifetime burn.

The newsletter frames the company's efficiency as two loops, "improvement" and "investment".

**Positioning.** The filing describes the product as "Virtual Collaborators" rather than agents, aimed at more than one billion knowledge workers by 2028. The filing's language: these systems "carry the access rights, system connections, and context that their organizations grant them", can "navigate and use internal tools", and "execute workflows that span multiple steps that require sustained reasoning".

**Why it matters.** The 48% cloud-partner share and the "virtual collaborator" framing are the two structural claims worth tracking. The first ties revenue to hyperscaler channel; the second sets the product frame the company will defend publicly.

All figures are as reported by the newsletter from the filing. They are single-source here and not independently checked.
