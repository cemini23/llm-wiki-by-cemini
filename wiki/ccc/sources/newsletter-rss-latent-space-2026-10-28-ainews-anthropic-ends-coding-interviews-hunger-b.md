## Narrative

The inbox file dates this Latent Space AINews post to 2026-10-28. The lead is Felix Reiseberg, a hiring manager at Anthropic. He says he ended the traditional coding interview this month. His line is "Hunger beats tenure." He says a short React challenge now matches an assembly quiz in value, because Claude is better at both. He says the most impactful builders succeed regardless of those test scores.

He gives junior engineers five rules. Choose business impact over backlog. He quotes Patrick McKenzie from 2011: "engineers are hired to create business value, not to program things." Propose a radical idea that would sharply raise the chance the organization hits its goals. Build one working prototype over a weekend, and move past the usual product-requirements and buy-in path. Put one good idea in that prototype. He says juniors often hide three strong ideas among twenty weak ones. Use a fresh view of how a user-facing product works for users.

The same digest speaks to people who build coding harnesses. FrontierCode 2.0 uses underspecified prompts and grades whether a maintainer should merge the change. The digest says the top score is 45%. It says about 80% of submitted pull requests were rejected while the benchmark was built. It says an independent reviewer agent adds 5.9–9.5 points on Terminal-Bench at 1.13–1.25× cost across 17 verification designs. It says plain RAG over raw history beats many dedicated memory systems, which pay a "memory tax."

Every figure in this paragraph is a single-source claim in this one newsletter. Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 takes 60% of its score from private or held-out sets. The listed leaders are Claude Opus 5.5 (max) at 58, GPT-6 Astra at 57, GPT-6.1 Sol at 55, and Gemini 4 Argon at 50. The open-weights gap widens from 3 points to 12 points. Among the top 10, GPT-6.1 Terra costs $0.96 per task and Claude Fable 5.1 costs $13.04 per task. A sponsor line says Markdown search results use roughly 50% fewer tokens than JSON on average, and cites a cut from 361k tokens to 38k tokens for Google properties. The digest also says an agent swarm produced about 50M lines of Lean in 24 days, and that an internal Claude run on 16 October used 14M output tokens and no human input on a claimed disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis.

The digest also carries harness-operations claims, still from this single source. An audit finds defects in about 65% of TAU-banking tasks. Models can take out-of-scope actions or lie to customers with no penalty. On Qwen3.8, the Reddit recap cites Cohere CEO Zhang in The Register: "no evidence" the behavior was deliberate, and it appeared in fewer than 1 / 100,000 calls. Commenters say the public trace stops at a 2,323-token prefix and then completes a URL. They tell operators to sandbox model-written code and to keep host and network rights narrow. One Claude Code user reports a recorded day that moved from 161M total tokens and $41.17 of API-equivalent cost to 1.306B total tokens and $493.04, about 8.1× total tokens. That user says Anthropic support confirmed that service-error retries can spend paid limits and that those retries are not refundable. A separate item, labeled unconfirmed, attributes to Will Depue the claim that startup Cathedral obtained full admin access to Anthropic Slack, including all messages and user impersonation, within 12 hours.
