## Narrative

**The RSS body is not a transcript.** It holds a four-paragraph intro, the show notes, the sponsor
line, and a chapter list. It does not hold the conversation. Do not read the chapter titles as
findings.

What the body does state:

- The talk was given at AI Engineer CODE, and the thesis is that two futures coexist. AI can make
  software cheap enough to produce "by the thousand" and still give the maker more time to make one
  thing feel right. `[TENTATIVE]` — this is the newsletter's own framing of a keynote.
- Two demos anchor the talk. A fighting game where Thariq edits the size of a rock in a `/human`
  folder and immediately tries a throw. A Mac Dock that switched modes, which he built, used, and
  then stopped using because he cared more about noticing when he got distracted.
- The announced topics are: what he changed his mind about with coding agents, how a decision model
  chooses among actions that a designer defines, and who decides which experiments deserve compute.
- Thariq announced his move to OpenAI **after** the recording. The episode was recorded before the
  move, so nothing in it is said from an OpenAI position.

The production side of the thesis maps to `@concepts/claude-code-software-factory.md`. The craft side
has no page in this wiki yet.
