## Narrative

ARC-AGI-3 as interactive skill acquisition. Tycho builds executable game models (rendered deterministic Moore machines). Key result: **transition-match ≠ useful model** — auto-repair after verify failures → better sims but worse RHAE (83.07) than actor-requested delegation (88.49). Frontier runs hit **100 RHAE**. **Active abstraction** = decide when to construct/repair/use/bypass the model. **ADOPT** decision policy; **GO** Apache clone. Game-dev cross-wiki stub for ARC coding-agent eval.
