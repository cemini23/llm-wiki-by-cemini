## Narrative

OO-Harness treats the harness as a class hierarchy. An evolution agent grows subclasses from execution records. A router picks a subclass at test time.

The method is the best of the compared methods. The absolute gain is up to **15.79 percentage points** over the base harness, on business workflows, workspace tasks, and tool use.

The lesson is that one global harness is not the only shape. A family of classes can carry more of the work. **No install. No route swap.**
