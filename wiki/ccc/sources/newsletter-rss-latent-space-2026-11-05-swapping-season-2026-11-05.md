## Narrative

Four movement items in one day, three of them between direct competitors.

| Item | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| **Cat Wu** — Head of Product on the Claude Code team since inception | **Anthropic → OpenAI**, to lead Core Products PM |
| **Sharon Zhou** — CEO of Lamini, acquired by AMD, later VP of AI to Lisa Su | **AMD → Anthropic** |
| **Factory** | **acquires HumanLayer** |
| **Meta** | **acquires Conductor** — described as "the pioneers of the form factor that everyone copied this year" |

### Why the Conductor line matters here

Conductor is the reference to the agent-orchestration form factor this workspace already tracks. Meta buying the originator is a statement that the pattern is infrastructure rather than a product feature.

It also means the original is now inside a large company, which historically changes both the roadmap and the licence posture of the open parts.

### The Cat Wu item

The person who led product for Claude Code from inception moving to lead Core Products PM at OpenAI is a specific kind of signal. Claude Code's product decisions — the harness shape, the skills model, the permission surface — are the ones this workspace builds on daily. A product lead moving is not a code change, but it is a statement about where the next generation of those decisions is likely to be made.

### What this is not

Four personnel and M&A items. No benchmark, no capability claim, no code. It is industry motion, useful for context and not for a decision.

**Caveat.** Newsletter summary; several items are single-sourced to a social post.
