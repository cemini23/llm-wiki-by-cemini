## Narrative

Four-module memory-centric system for end-to-end scientific research: **SciMem** (structured persistent memory), plus literature, experiment, and manuscript modules. CCC maps SciMem patterns to **wiki git + claude-mem + ingest gates** — not automated lab science on prod.
