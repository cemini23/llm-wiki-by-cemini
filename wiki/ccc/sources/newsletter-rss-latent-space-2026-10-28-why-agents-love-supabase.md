## Narrative

The post is a podcast with Paul Copplestone and Ant Wilson, the Supabase co-founders. It
is their first podcast together, after taking an open-source, fully remote company from zero
to a $10 billion valuation.

The author asks why agents pick Supabase as a backend. He notes that managed databases are
competitive, and that model familiarity is useful distribution but does not explain the
work of turning a generated application into one that runs. He says his frontier AEO tracker
showed Supabase as an almost complete sweep for first-choice app backend across every
frontier model he tested.

Supabase grouped its Select announcements into **Build**, **Operate** and **Scale**. The
author singles out three practical items:

- Start a local backend without Docker.
- Keep the desired database schema in Git.
- Run compute beside the database.

His reading is that each item removes work developers have done for years, and that agents
make that friction harder to ignore.

This page records the newsletter text. The episode itself is audio, and the post describes
it rather than transcribing it.
