## Narrative

This is the fuller record of the episode already filed as
`@sources/newsletter-rss-latent-space-2026-11-19-scaling-linears-gtm-to-100m-arr-cristina-cordova.md`.
That page holds the go-to-market summary. This page holds the product and pricing detail.

**The agent surface.** Linear shipped its MCP server in May 2025. Cristina Cordova says it was
basically the first in its category. A year and a half later, more than half of the issues created
in Linear come from agents. A year before, the share was 3%.

**The two-line test.** Cordova watches agent usage and app usage together. If agent usage rises and
app usage does not, the product becomes a commodity. She names this as the mistake that SaaS
companies make when they put an MCP server on a database and stop. At Linear, she says both lines
rise. See `@concepts/agent-usage-vs-app-usage.md`.

**"Issue tracking is dead."** CEO Karri Saarinen published the post in March 2026. Customers agreed
and then asked what they were buying. Linear's answer is a product development system built around
agent orchestration. Frontier AI labs run on Linear and pushed for faster agent access. Companies
such as Ramp and Coinbase built internal agents and needed an API.

**The roadmap changed under the company.** Linear declared an enterprise push for 2025. Then agents
arrived. The team shipped an MCP server, an agents platform, and Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot
integrations. Much of the planned enterprise work was never done. Cordova chose to race ahead.

**Sales shape.** Linear has never hired a sales development representative (SDR). Account executives
do their own outbound. Linear does no forward deployed engineering; solutions engineers sell beside
enterprise account executives. Support stays in house. An LLM drafts replies, and a human decides
what is sent.

**Pricing.** Cordova wants outcome-based pricing, like Intercom Fin at $0.99 per resolved
conversation. She says Linear cannot define the outcome, so seats stay. Linear Agent chat is free
even on frontier models. Coding sessions pass through the published API price plus a small markup.
Loops bill on AI credits. New AI features start free and later gain a price.

**Why ARR moves fast.** Companies carved out AI budgets for tools that did not exist. A product that
fits that budget skips the usual fight for spend. Buyers now sign one-year deals and paid pilots
instead of three-year contracts. Cordova warns that some $100M ARR figures are a few $30–40M
contracts with defense firms or large law firms. Renewals will show whose ARR was real.

**Headcount.** Linear runs on about half the headcount of companies at its stage and revenue. Cordova
brought a rule from Stripe: at least 40% of the company should be engineering, product, design and
data. Hiring is slow on purpose, and candidates do a work trial on a real problem.

All figures are vendor-reported and single-source. Treat them as `[TENTATIVE]`.
