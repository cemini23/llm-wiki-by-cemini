## Narrative

**PaperClaw** — harnessed multi-agent system: **Domain → Idea → Hypothesis → Paper** autonomously.

1. Auto domain curation from live literature/datasets/code (open scholarly indexes, not model memory)
2. Brainstorm → living idea spec with pre-registered main-result contract
3. **Stoppable hypothesis map** — propose → test → reflect loop; grows only from measured verdicts
4. **Full-lifecycle memory** — single living record; pause/resume without context loss
5. Venue-compliant paper generation with validated citations + genuinely-run results
6. **In-cycle research assistant** — same tools/skills at every stage; human can step in anytime (web/desktop/CLI)
