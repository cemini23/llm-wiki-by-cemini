## Narrative

Argues the next bottleneck in agentic AI is **system scaling** — auditable, persistent, modular, verifiable architecture around foundation models — not model-parameter scaling alone. Evaluation should score memory, retrieval, tools, orchestration, verification, and governance as first-class harness dimensions.
