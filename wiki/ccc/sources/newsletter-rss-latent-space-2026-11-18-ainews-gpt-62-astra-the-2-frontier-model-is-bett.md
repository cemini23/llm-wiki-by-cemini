## Narrative

OpenAI shipped GPT-6.2 **Astra** and **Sol** two days before Anthropic's first trading day.

Per Artificial Analysis, Sol ties September's Astra at about 15% of the cost per task. New Astra scores 62 on the Intelligence Index, behind Fable 5.2 at 65. The summary line is that Anthropic still owns the highest score and OpenAI owns the best intelligence per dollar.

API prices per million tokens: Astra is $8 input, $0.40 cached input and $40 output. Sol keeps the older pricing at $2, $0.10 and $10. Both have a 1M context window and a 128K output limit.

Two details are harness-relevant:

- **Preparedness rating.** OpenAI rated Astra High for AI Self-Improvement, a first for an externally released model.
- **Mixed reviews.** Influencer feedback was strongly negative, and the newsletter notes visible RL artefacts.

For Cemini: the price table is directly usable in lane cost calculations. The mixed-review note is a reminder that a benchmark rank and an engineering experience can diverge. [TENTATIVE] for the qualitative claims.
