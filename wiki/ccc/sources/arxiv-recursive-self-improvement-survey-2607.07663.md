## Narrative

Two-axis taxonomy: **what improves** (deployment behavior / training policy / evaluator / research process) × **loop closure** (human-in / human-on / closed). Separates industrial **bounded self-refinement** from open-ended **RSI**. Dedicated **self-evaluation** category with a **verification hierarchy** (formal verifiers → execution feedback → PRMs/judges → intrinsic self-assessment). Frames Anthropic Institute RSI essay as motivating continuum, not evidence.
