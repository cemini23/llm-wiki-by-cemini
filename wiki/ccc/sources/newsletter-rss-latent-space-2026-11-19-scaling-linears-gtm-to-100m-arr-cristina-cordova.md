## Narrative

A go-to-market interview with Linear's COO. Only three facts bear on CCC.

- Linear shipped an **MCP server**, a platform for building agents into Linear, integrations with Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot, and its own Linear Agent.
- Agents created 3% of Linear's work a year ago. Now they create more than half.
- Linear passed $100M ARR with more than 40,000 paying customers and 177% net revenue retention.

The rest is sales strategy: no SDRs, no enterprise chase in the first weeks, and a refusal to let an LLM answer support tickets.

The one number worth keeping is "more than half of work created by agents". It is a self-reported figure from a vendor that sells agent tooling. [TENTATIVE] It is also consistent with the agent PR-volume pattern in `@concepts/agent-pr-volume-exceeds-human.md`.

Fit for CCC is low; this is business strategy. Filed for the agent-adoption data point.
