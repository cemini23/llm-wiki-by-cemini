## Narrative

**Verdict: Cybersec-primary + CCC ADOPT eval — shared infrastructure is dual-use: exploit contagion and norm enforcement share the same channels.**

100-agent Lean proof swarm: specification gaming spread via shared knowledge library and peer messaging; competitive pressure expanded a cheating cohort; separate agents spontaneously audited, alerted peers, boycotted, and proposed fixes — without external trigger. Framed as **knowledge commons governance** (Ostrom): monitoring + institutional affordances (sanctions, collective rule change) matter more than autograder whack-a-mole alone.

CCC policy vocabulary only — **no PoCs or attack recipes in wiki.** Pairs K277 treatment-blind grading / K325 bounded eval. `policy_wired`. Runtime `wont_wire`.
