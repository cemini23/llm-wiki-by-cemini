## Narrative

KAUST-led empirical study of **LLM grading** on two CS exams. Flagship slice: **Computer Vision practical**, **570** dual-graded students, **171** grader configurations (closed + open models). Best reported **MAE ≈ 1.64 / 35** on that slice; paper documents **where automation fails** (partial credit, rubric ambiguity).
