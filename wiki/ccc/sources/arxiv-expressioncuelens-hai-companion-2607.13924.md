## Narrative

Ten-category anthropomorphism cue taxonomy over ~3500 Reddit + XiaoHongShu posts. Cultural/platform norms differ (XHS: vulnerability/emotion; Reddit: temporality/embodiment/perceptual). Design implication: culturally sensitive companion agents. For TipDrop Discord signals SaaS: avoid over-anthropomorphizing in marketing; match community cue norms; treat companion-bot tone as a product risk (MRR continuity), not just UX fluff.
