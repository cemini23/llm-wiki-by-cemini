## Narrative

Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 per share. The stock closed at $97.17 on the first day, up 17%, then drifted to $92.60 after hours. The $81.3B offering overtook SpaceX's June IPO as the largest in history.

Two governance details are reported. Class F shares carry 50.1% of voting power on most shareholder matters, so control stays with the founders. Dario and Daniela Amodei said the company would pace training and deployment on whether it can keep systems safe and under human control.

The newsletter also flags a risk: about a trillion dollars of shares unlock over the next year, which can weigh on the price, while the company has to keep the top model.

Fit for CCC is low. It is corporate news. Filed because the model-supply lane depends on which labs can fund frontier training, and because the safety-pacing statement is a policy signal.
