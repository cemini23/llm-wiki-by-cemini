## Narrative

Cloudflare launched two **System One** models and called itself a neolab. The relevant claim is that one of them, **Clef**, is a decision model inspired by TypeSafe AI's Jev model. The other, **Clairic**, is an "extract" model.

In an interview, Michelle Chen (Cloudflare AI Platform, product) said more models will follow, possibly reasoning models, and that the team mostly does post-training rather than pre-training from scratch. The stated aim is to make existing resources more efficient, not to build a frontier model.

The "neolab" label is contested in the piece itself: the term usually means a research-first startup with elite researchers and no revenue traction, and Cloudflare is none of those.

Fit for CCC is indirect. A small, cheap **decision model** that runs close to the infrastructure is the same idea as a router that sends easy work to a cheap model. That connects to `@concepts/decision-model-hybrid-agent-routing.md`. Treat the capability claims as [TENTATIVE]; no benchmark numbers are given.
