## Narrative

**Goal.** Convert microscopy images (SEM, TEM, SHG, brightfield) into **tactile lithographs** preserving structure and **length scale** for blind/low-vision audiences — not decorative lithophanes for Etsy, but accessibility-centered science communication.

**Workflow [CONFIRMED].**

1. Import image in **ImageJ** → grayscale; optional **Gaussian blur r=1.0 px** for 1–2 px detail.
2. Export PNG/JPG → upload to **Bambu Maker Lab** for heightfield STL (external or inverse-LUT internal views).
3. Iterate brightness/shading to shrink files (~**150 MB → 75 MB** reported).
4. Print on consumer FDM or higher-end resin.

**Cost / hardware benchmark (10×10 cm lithograph).**

| Printer | Layer | Material | Time | ~Cost |
|---------|-------|----------|------|-------|
| **Bambu X1E (FDM)** | 0.12 mm, 0.4 mm nozzle | 37 g | 2 h | **$0.75** |
| Stratasys J835 (PolyJet) | 0.027 mm | 82 g | 52 min | $16 |
| Carbon M2 (SLA) | 0.1 mm | 45 mL | 3.5 h | $11 |

Paper cites **~$350** entry printer class; Bambu FDM succeeded on **all** generated files across modalities tested.

**Reader fit [CONFIRMED].** Strong **post-calibration maker project** for @concepts/novice-cad-workflows.md — no custom CAD required; skills are ImageJ + Maker Lab + slicer. Good outreach/education angle; not store-ops SKU material unless accessibility niche.

**Caveats.** Tactile translation requires design choices (what to simplify vs preserve); workflow is **UDL/accessibility** oriented, not automatic alt-text replacement.
