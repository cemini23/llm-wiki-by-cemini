## Narrative

Repurpose a **low-cost 3D printer** as automated probing rig to generate labeled calibration data for vision-based tactile sensors (DIGIT, GelSight Mini). Open-source library + data ablation guidelines. Practical **research tool** use of desktop FDM beyond printing parts.
