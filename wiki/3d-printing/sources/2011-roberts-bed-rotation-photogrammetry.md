## Narrative

Custom **research FDM testbed** with **rotating bed** for in-situ photogrammetric reconstruction — response to closed-source consumer machines lacking research flexibility. Historical context for open-hardware movement.

## Snippets

> "Consumer grade machines are cheap and plentiful, they lack many of the features desired in a machine used for research purposes and are often closed-source platforms." [Source: 2011-roberts-bed-rotation-photogrammetry.pdf p.1]
