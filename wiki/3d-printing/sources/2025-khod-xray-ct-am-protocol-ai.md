## Narrative

AI pipeline predicts **MEX/FDM process parameters** (layer height, nozzle speed) from **X-ray CT** quality inspection — non-iterative, self-training. Three commercial soft-material extrusion printers; segmentation on NDT data drives protocol selection. Research-grade QA loop; not a hobbyist workflow.
