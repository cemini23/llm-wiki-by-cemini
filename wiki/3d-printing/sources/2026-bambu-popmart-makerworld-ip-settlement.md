## Narrative

36Kr reports that **Bambu Lab (拓竹)** settled with **Pop Mart (泡泡玛特)** over unauthorized **Labubu** and related models on **MakerWorld**: apology statement, unauthorized models delisted, litigation that had been heading to court (reported target ~April) ended in a fast settlement. Article frames this as the industry’s first loud copyright “gray rhino” as hardware vendors bet on model communities for lock-in.

### Platform scale claims [TENTATIVE — press figures]

MakerWorld described as global #1 monthly-active 3D model community: ~tens of millions MAU, &gt;200k active creators, &gt;1M models. Closed-loop incentive (points → filament/coupons) + **print-ready 3MF** with machine/filament presets vs bare STL — raises both usability **and** platform duty-of-care for infringing uploads.

### Adjacent / open threads [TENTATIVE]

- Separate **Luoxiahei (罗小黑)** information-network-transmission case against Bambu cited as still active (hearing ~2026-03-20).
- Lawyer quoted: hardware-vendor model communities carry high notice duty; “Standard Digital File License” on a listing does not cure missing upstream rights.
- Disney/Marvel/Pokémon-style IP already searchable across MakerWorld / Creality Cloud / Printables; farm-scale monetization raises rights-holder attention [TENTATIVE narrative].
