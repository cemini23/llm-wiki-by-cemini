## Narrative

**Background-only for this wiki.** Describes **direct ink write (DIW)** of **near-ambient transition temperature liquid crystal elastomers (NAT-LCEs)** with a **hybrid cooling strategy**: low-temperature nozzle + cooled build platform + UV crosslinking arranged to preserve mesogen alignment during extrusion.

**Headline claims (abstract).** Nematic order **~3000% higher** than room-temperature DIW NAT-LCE; printed sheets **spontaneously form 3D shapes** after release due to TNI shift during printing; **bidirectional** deformation with heat/cool; graded properties via nozzle/plate temperature; demo wristband for heart-rate monitoring.

**Why low priority here.** Not FFF/filament FDM—requires **custom oligomer ink**, DIW printer modifications, and UV cure. No overlap with Bambu Studio / Orca-Flashforge / PLA-PETG day-1 workflows. Included in cluster ingest only as **adjacent shape-morphing modality** for completeness.

**Reader action.** Skip unless investing in soft-actuator materials R&D; see @concepts/shape-changing-fdm-interfaces.md for how this relates to FDM cluster papers.
