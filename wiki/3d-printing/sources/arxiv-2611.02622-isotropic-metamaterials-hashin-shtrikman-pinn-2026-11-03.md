## Narrative

A plate-lattice metamaterial study from the Singapore Centre for 3D Printing. Keywords on the title page: plate lattice, metamaterial, Hashin-Shtrikman, machine learning, physics-informed neural networks, LAPIS.

**The claim in the title is a bound.** The Hashin-Shtrikman bounds are the tightest possible stiffness range for a two-phase composite at a given volume fraction. Reaching the upper bound with an *isotropic* lattice is the hard part — most stiff lattices achieve their stiffness in one direction and pay for it in the others. A design that reaches the limit isotropically would be a genuine result.

The method is a physics-informed neural network used as the design generator rather than as a surrogate for simulation. That is the notable technique choice: the physics constraint is in the loss, so candidate geometries are pushed toward admissible solutions rather than filtered afterward.

**Caveat.** Only the title page was read. No stiffness value, volume fraction, print material, or experimental validation figure is established here. Do not cite a number from this page.

**Relevance.** Plate lattices are printable on FDM at the mesoscale and this is a materials-design paper from a 3D-printing centre. It is a candidate for the design side of this wiki once read properly.
