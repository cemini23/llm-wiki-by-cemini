## Narrative

**15-DOF** hand with thumb opposition + four-finger flexion; **four parts** total — mostly one flexible **3D-printed skin-skeleton** unit. Same JSK lineage as MEVITA/MEVIUS/M3D-skin. Advanced multi-part soft robotics; not day-1 FDM.
