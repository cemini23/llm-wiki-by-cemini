## Narrative

Tomographic VAM suffers from narrow exposure windows — overexposure cures out-of-part voxels. Correct exposure time can vary **~40%** with resin history; human timing is subjective. Proposes **real-time light-scattering** processing for robust auto-exposure. Parallel to closed-loop quality themes in @concepts/fault-detection.md but on vat-photopolymer volumetric modality.
