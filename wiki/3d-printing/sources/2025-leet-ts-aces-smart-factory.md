## Narrative

**Smart Factory Embedding (SFE)** — assign manufacturing processes to machines + program transport agents between them. **TS-ACES** solver scales to **100+ machines** (vs prior ~dozen). Formal complement to heuristic scheduling in @concepts/print-job-scheduling.md.
