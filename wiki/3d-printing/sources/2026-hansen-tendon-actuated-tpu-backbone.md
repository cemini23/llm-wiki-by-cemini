## Narrative

Low-cost **3D-printed TPU** continuum robots with **tapered backbone** for distal compliance; integrated base with tendon tension sensing via load cells. Forward kinetostatic model (Cosserat rod + spatially varying cross-section). Uses TPU meaningfully — see @entities/materials/tpu.md.
