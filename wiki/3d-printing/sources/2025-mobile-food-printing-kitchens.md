## Narrative

Qualitative inquiry of **3D food printing (3DFP)** potential in professional kitchens — labor, waste, creativity. Notes 3DFP inherits axial/nozzle idioms from FDM but needs food-specific formulations. Background for food AM, not polymer FDM.
