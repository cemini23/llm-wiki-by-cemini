## Narrative

Hybrid **single-photon Tomographic VAM (TVAM)** for cm-scale fast prints + **two-photon polymerization (2PP)** for sub-µm features on the same platform. TVAM builds mm-scale volumes on a glass rod; 2PP adds high-resolution detail. [TENTATIVE] Not consumer FFF — background for where photopolymer AM goes beyond layer-wise FDM.
