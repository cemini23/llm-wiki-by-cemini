## Narrative

A software paper on visualizing and quantifying 4D flow and other high-dimensional magnetic resonance images. The author list joins Queen's University's Laboratory for Percutaneous Surgery, Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, Ebatinca, Lausanne, and Pennsylvania. The framework builds on 3D Slicer, the open-source medical imaging platform.

**Relevance to this wiki.** This is a weak fit. The subject is medical image analysis, not additive manufacturing. It is filed because the arXiv batch carried it under the 3D-printing inbox. The only transferable element is the pattern of building a domain extension on 3D Slicer, which already has mesh and surface tooling.

**Caveat.** Only the first page was read. The page above is largely a title-and-author record rather than a synthesis. The abstract is not reproduced here, and no claim from this paper should be cited beyond its existence and subject.
