## Narrative

SparkToro is an audience-research product, founded by Rand Fishkin. It reports where an audience spends attention: which accounts, publications, podcasts, subreddits, and websites they follow.

**Main products.**

- **Audience profiles.** A list of sources an audience follows, built from a clickstream panel.
- **Share of Influence** (added 2026-11-24). Ranks channels by their contribution to a buyer journey, split across Awareness, Interest, Consideration, and Conversion. See @concepts/share-of-influence-channel-measurement.md.
- **Prompt and behaviour studies.** Occasional public research, such as the ChatGPT prompt study in @concepts/ai-assistant-usage-buyer-journey-share.md.

**Data basis.** A clickstream panel, layered with crawled data from YouTube, Reddit, social platforms, Google keyword volume, AI prompts, and podcasts. All outputs are modelled estimates, not direct measurements.

**Fit for this workspace.** Useful for channel hypotheses before budget moves. Not a GEO or local-pack tool. It does not measure citations, local rankings, or AI visibility.

**Limit.** The vendor publishes no panel size or formula. Treat the numbers as directional. `[TENTATIVE]`
