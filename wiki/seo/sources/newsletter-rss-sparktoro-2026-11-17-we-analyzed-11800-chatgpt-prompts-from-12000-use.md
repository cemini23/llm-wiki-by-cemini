## Narrative

The RSS body in the inbox truncates after two paragraphs. The headline numbers below come from the full published page, fetched 2026-11-22. Treat every number as a single-source claim from SparkToro and Verso.

SparkToro analyzed ChatGPT use with a Verso opt-in panel that gives anonymized access to OpenAI connections. The dataset has 118,163 prompts from 41,961 conversations, covering 1 January to 29 October 2026. It has 12,321 panel users in the raw data. The authors removed 131 heavy users, who averaged over 33 prompts a month but produced 21% of all prompts. 9,675 panelists had demographic records and were weighted to 2024 US Census targets.

**Use categories, as per-user shares:** images and visual design 16.1%, information and entertainment 14.0%, everyday life and admin 12.2%, writing and communication 11.9%, health and wellness 10.3%, money/work/business 8.5%, schoolwork and learning 7.6%, shopping/brands/local services 5.7%, technology support and coding 4.4%, relationships and emotional support 3.4%, chit-chat and other 3.0%, creative writing and roleplay 2.9%.

**The SEO-relevant numbers.** 12.1% of responses contain one or more brands, products, or companies. Only 6.9% of prompts relate to researching a potential brand to buy from. The comparable Google figure is above 15% of queries. Fishkin estimates Google gets purchase-journey queries at more than 16 times the rate of ChatGPT. He treats ChatGPT as a heavily consumer application, near the size of Bing on this metric, and not near Google.

**Caveats the author gives.** API requests are not captured, and Fishkin estimates they are 50% to 75% of all usage. The panel skews younger, female, and lower-income, and mobile use is overrepresented. Census weighting moved no category by more than 1.4 percentage points. Fishkin argues that AI visibility matters most through Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, not through ChatGPT alone.

**Why it matters here.** The concrete number is 6.9%: a small share of ChatGPT use is brand purchase research. This limits how much a small business should expect from ChatGPT visibility alone, and it supports a focus on Google AI surfaces.
