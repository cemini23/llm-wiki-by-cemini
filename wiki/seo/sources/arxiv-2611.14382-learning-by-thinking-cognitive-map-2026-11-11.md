## Narrative

**Learning by Thinking (LbT)** — acquiring new insight through internal processes such as explanation and reasoning, **without new external input**.

LLMs increasingly show behaviour that resembles it, and there is no systematic account of the mechanisms behind it or of their limits.

The paper proposes a cognitive map of the mechanisms.

### The distinction worth having

LbT is the difference between a model that gets better because it was shown something and a model that gets better because it **reorganised what it already had**. The second is what "reasoning" claims to be, and it is rarely separated from the first in evaluation.

That separation matters for this workspace directly: an agent that improves a plan on its second attempt may have learned from feedback (external) or restructured (internal). Those are different capabilities with different failure modes — overfitting to feedback versus confabulating structure.

### The caution

A cognitive map is a framework paper. It proposes a vocabulary and a structure; it does not demonstrate a mechanism. Treat the vocabulary as useful and the claims as unverified.

**Caveat.** Abstract only. Single-source framework; no empirical result reproduced.
