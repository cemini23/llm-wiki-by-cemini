## Narrative

**No SEO link.** "RTL" here means register-transfer-level hardware design, not right-to-left text. This paper has no connection to search engines, retrieval, citation, or local ranking. The page exists for inventory honesty and links from @concepts/corpus-overflow-out-of-scope.md.

The authors study AI agents that optimize chip designs described in RTL. These agents use electronic design automation (EDA) feedback, but their search procedures are mostly hand-built. Meta-RTL evolves the agent harness from a minimal loop while it keeps the optimization model, the EDA flow, and the budget per design fixed.

Compared with a prior system, Dr. RTL, Meta-RTL reduces aggregate timing violations by 7.8% and 11.0% across 20 development designs, with area reductions on 70% of them. Without more evolution, it transfers to 10 unseen designs and reduces the same violations by 20.5% and 14.0%, and reduces total synthesized area by 12.6%. The authors then describe three search mechanisms in the evolved harness. The percentages are single-source claims.
