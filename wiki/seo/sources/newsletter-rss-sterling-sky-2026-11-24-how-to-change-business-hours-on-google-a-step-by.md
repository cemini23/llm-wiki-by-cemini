## Narrative

A business owner edits Google Business Profile (GBP) hours in the GBP dashboard or in the Maps app. The article gives the click path, the hours types, and the ranking effect of each type [Source: https://www.sterlingsky.ca/edit-your-business-hours-on-gbp/ (retrieved 2026-11-26)].

**Edit the main hours (short path).**

1. Sign in to the Google account that manages the profile.
2. Click **Edit Profile** for the location.
3. Open the **Info** tab and find the **Business hours** section.
4. Click the **pencil icon** next to "Business hours".
5. Pick the hours from the dropdowns. Mark a day as **closed** when needed.
6. Click **Save**.

**Edit the main hours (full path).** Go to the GBP dashboard. Click **Edit Profile**. Scroll to "Business hours" and click the pencil icon. Choose **"Open with main hours"** (the default). Set the hours and mark closed days. Click **save**.

**Breaks inside one day.** Enter the morning hours in the first two dropdowns. Click the **plus sign**. Enter the afternoon hours in the second set of boxes. Repeat the plus sign for each extra break. The article sets no limit on the number of breaks.

**Hours modes.** Three modes are separate and do not convert into each other:

| Mode | What the listing shows | Note |
|------|------------------------|------|
| Open with main hours | The hours you set | Default mode |
| Open with no main hours | No hours at all | The listing stays live. This is not 24/7 |
| 24 hours | Open at all times | Set "24 hours" in both the open and close dropdowns |

A reader comment says "open with no main hours" can prompt Google to ask users for hours. Treat that as `[TENTATIVE]`.

**Closed status.** The hours section also holds **temporarily closed** and **permanently closed**. Both show in Maps and Search. Use them only when they are true.

**Category hours.** Under the main hours, Google may show hours that depend on the primary category. Examples are access hours, delivery hours, takeout hours, and happy hours.

**Extra hours.** Some categories offer more types, such as online service hours. They appear when a user clicks **More hours**.

**Special hours.** These sit below the normal hours in the dashboard. Use them for holidays, vacations, and one-off days.

**Ranking effect.** Sterling Sky tested "Online service hours" on service-area and storefront listings. The extra hours did not change rankings. Only the **main hours** change rankings, because they set the open or closed label [TENTATIVE — single agency test, no published positions].

**The extra hours do not repair a stale main hours field.** If the main hours are wrong, the listing still shows as closed. Correct the main hours first. Then add the category or extra hours.

**Risks and checks.**

- Google may call to confirm profile details. Not every hours-update call is from Google. Treat unsolicited calls with care.
- After an update, Google adds a note to the profile that the business changed its hours.
- If Google suspects the hours are wrong, it shows **"hours may be incorrect"** and invites user corrections. An approved user edit can leave wrong hours live.
- Google sometimes changes hours on its own. Check the profile on a regular schedule.

**What the article does not give.** It states no limit on the number of special hours, breaks, or category-hour entries. It states no character limits. It states no limit on how often hours can be changed in a year.
