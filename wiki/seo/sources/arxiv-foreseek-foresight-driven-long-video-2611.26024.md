## Narrative

**No SEO link.** This paper studies long-form video understanding. It has no connection to search engines, web retrieval visibility, citation, or local ranking. The page exists for inventory honesty and links from @concepts/corpus-overflow-out-of-scope.md.

The authors study video agents built on multimodal large language models. These agents retrieve clips in a reactive way, based on local saliency, and have no global temporal plan. ForeSeek instead forms visually grounded expectations over unseen time regions, checks them with targeted observations, and refines its search as evidence arrives. Failed expectations become signals for backtracking.

The authors add a two-stage training framework for foresight-guided reasoning and efficient decisions. On five long-video benchmarks, ForeSeek matches or beats the strongest search-based agent while looking at 2.5 to 4.4 times fewer frames. The numbers are single-source claims.
