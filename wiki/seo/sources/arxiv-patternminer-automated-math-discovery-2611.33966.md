## Narrative

The paper sits between two styles of AI-driven mathematical discovery. One uses large language models to prove well-specified conjectures by text reasoning. The other uses narrow models plus interpretability for open-ended, human-in-the-loop exploration of object-based data. The authors argue the second is limited to simple empirical pattern detection and the first is poorly suited to open-ended exploration of large datasets. PatternMiner combines them: a large model guides the training of a small one, and the small one is interpreted.
