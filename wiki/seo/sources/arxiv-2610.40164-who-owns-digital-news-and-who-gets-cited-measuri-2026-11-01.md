## Narrative

Digital news outlets compete for audience attention. Search engines with generative AI, such as Google AI Overviews, increasingly decide where that attention goes and where the revenue goes. National regulators lack a shared data-collection system. The paper says this gap blocks cross-country comparison of media concentration. It also blocks assessment of how these intermediaries reshape digital news markets.

Carragher and Carley compile an ownership dataset for 9,761 digital news outlets from Wikipedia and Wikidata. They report high agreement with independently compiled US and UK news directories. They derive traffic-weighted concentration indices for 77 countries. The title applies the same concentration question to seven AI systems.

The paper states these results from this source alone. Journalists report more owner pressure in countries with higher concentration (𝜌=+0.318). Outlet-level measures understate concentration. Owner-level indices are a median 1.30× higher. Citation distributions from major AI systems are up to 2.08× more concentrated than traffic. If Google AI Overviews mediated all organic search, the resulting rise in US concentration would be several times that associated with highly regulated media mergers and acquisitions. The analyses indicate that AI systems preferentially cite state-controlled media outlets when prompted in that country's native language.

The introduction uses a US merger to show the measurement gap. On October 6, 2026, Paramount Skydance acquired Warner Bros. Discovery in a deal worth over $110bn. Twelve state attorneys general and the Writers Guild of America challenged the deal. The deal places CBS News and CNN under one owner. Figure 1 maps ownership concentration by country as HHI on traffic shares by owner. Higher values mean fewer owners hold the traffic. Each country's state-controlled media are grouped under one state entity. State-sponsored media are not grouped that way.

For a local-SEO and digital-marketing reader, the paper ties search citation to attention and revenue. It claims AI citation is more concentrated than traffic, by up to 2.08×. It claims a full shift of organic search to Google AI Overviews would raise US concentration by several times the rise associated with highly regulated media mergers. It also claims prompt language changes who gets cited, with a preference for state-controlled outlets in the native language. The authors release the dataset so others can track these effects as they emerge.
