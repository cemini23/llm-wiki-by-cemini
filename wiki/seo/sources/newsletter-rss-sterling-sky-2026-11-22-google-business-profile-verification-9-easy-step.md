## Narrative

Google can require video verification for a Google Business Profile. The video must prove three things at once: the **location**, the **existence of the business**, and the **management** of it. Sterling Sky gives nine steps, grouped under those three headings [Source: sterlingsky.ca/google-business-profile-video-verification/, retrieved 2026-11-25].

**Video rules.** The clip must run at least 30 seconds. It must be one continuous, unedited take, filmed live on a mobile device. Do not upload a pre-recorded file. Upload through the Google Maps app, signed in to the account that manages the profile. Allow camera, location, and microphone permissions. Do not show faces, sensitive data, or private information. Narration is not needed. A 60–90 second clip often works better than a longer one when uploads fail.

**The nine steps.** The source marks each step for storefronts, service area businesses (SABs), or both.

| # | Group | Step | Applies to |
|---|-------|------|------------|
| 1 | Location | Show the area around the business: street signs, intersections, nearby businesses, building numbers | Both |
| 2 | Location | Show permanent signage that matches the profile name. If indoors, point the camera out of a window at a landmark or street sign | Both |
| 3 | Operations | Show the workspace or the place where the work happens: office, shop, job site, home workspace | Both |
| 4 | Operations | Show tools, equipment, or inventory: chairs, kitchen, desks, work tools | Both |
| 5 | Operations | Show branded assets: uniforms, vehicles, equipment, business cards, brochures | Mostly SABs |
| 6 | Operations | Open the vehicle or equipment and show the tools inside | SABs |
| 7 | Affiliation | Show that you can access the location: unlock and enter, or unlock a vehicle | Both |
| 8 | Affiliation | Show official business documents, such as a business licence or tax registration. Hold them steady and readable | Both |
| 9 | Affiliation | Show access to business systems: CRM, accounting software, or MLS software for realtors | Both |

Step 9 is an extra signal for affiliation. Sterling Sky quotes Brad Wetherall, who calls it a "massive 'Extra Credit' signal for affiliation" [Source: sterlingsky.ca/google-business-profile-video-verification/, retrieved 2026-11-25].

**After upload.** Google review can take up to five business days. Do not upload a second video to speed it up. Google may not tell you when you fail. Check the Maps app each day:

- A **"Review issues"** warning means the video was rejected, and Google shows the reason.
- **"Get verified"** with the option to video verify again means it failed, with no reason given.
- **"Get verified"** without that option, but with other methods, means it passed.

If review runs more than one week, the process is broken. Use the verification status tool to reach support. A blue tick appears on the dashboard after verification.

**Common failure causes.** The source lists ten:

1. Name, address, or phone does not match across the website, listings, and documents.
2. The address is incomplete, inaccurate, or not the type Google expects.
3. The name carries added keywords or promotional words, or does not match the real-world name.
4. The video does not clearly show location, signage, operations, or management proof.
5. Clear photos of the office, signage, team, and real work are missing.
6. Duplicate profiles exist.
7. The name, address, or key details change often.
8. The business uses a PO box, virtual office, or mailbox store.
9. The business address on trusted sites such as Yelp or Facebook differs.
10. Minor policy violations exist.

**One internal tension.** The source says video is "the only method" of verification in one place. It then lists phone, text, email, live video call, and postcard as other methods. Google's help centre says a profile may need more than one method. Treat the "only method" line as a slip, not a rule [Source: sterlingsky.ca/google-business-profile-video-verification/, retrieved 2026-11-25].
