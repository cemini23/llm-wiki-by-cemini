## Raw Concept

This page answers one question: how do you raise clicks on a page that already ranks? The source is @sources/newsletter-rss-sterling-sky-2026-11-15-optimization-of-title-tags-for-ctr.md, a Sterling Sky test series.

## Narrative

A page can rank well and still get few clicks. The title tag is the first thing a searcher reads. A title that matches intent earns more clicks than a title that only repeats a keyword.

**How to find candidates.** Open Google Search Console. Find pages with good average position but low click-through rate. These pages already have rank, so a click gain is a cheap win.

**How to write the new title.** Keep the top keyword that drives traffic. Then state the outcome or the answer the user wants. Lead with the benefit. Make the H1 match the start of the title tag, because Google rewrites display titles when the two disagree.

**Evidence.** Sterling Sky reported three gains: 65%, 63%, and 400% more clicks [TENTATIVE]. The 400% case moved from a question-style title to a benefit-first title.

**Where it fails.**
- Do not remove the main keyword. One test lost clicks when the keyword was dropped.
- Fix the title only when the title is the cause. In one case a stronger title did worse because Reddit and Google-owned results ranked above it.
- Revert a change that lowers performance.

**Why it matters for local SEO.** Local businesses often have large libraries of older articles that still rank. A title rewrite needs no new content and no ranking gain. It is a fast, low-risk tuning step inside the content work. Always test, because a title that helps one page can hurt another.
