## Raw Concept

This page answers one question: how much of AI-assistant use is marketing-relevant? The source is @sources/newsletter-rss-sparktoro-2026-11-17-we-analyzed-11800-chatgpt-prompts-from-12000-use.md. The data is a SparkToro study on a Verso panel.

## Narrative

AI visibility work has lacked a measure of how much assistant conversation is relevant to marketers. The SparkToro and Verso study gives one estimate for ChatGPT in the US. It analyzes 118,163 prompts from 41,961 conversations, from 1 January to 29 October 2026, with 12,321 panel users weighted to the US Census [TENTATIVE].

**What people use ChatGPT for.** Images and visual design is the largest category at 16.1% of per-user prompts. Information and entertainment is 14.0%. Everyday life and admin is 12.2%. Writing and communication is 11.9%. Shopping, brands, and local services is only 5.7% [TENTATIVE].

**The marketing-relevant number.** 12.1% of responses contain one or more brands, products, or companies. Only 6.9% of prompts relate to researching a brand to buy from. On Google, the comparable share is above 15% [TENTATIVE].

**What this implies.** Fishkin argues two points. First, ChatGPT is a consumer application, close to Bing on this metric, not close to Google. Second, a brand should expect more from Google AI Overviews and AI Mode than from ChatGPT alone. He estimates Google gets purchase-journey queries at more than 16 times the rate of ChatGPT [TENTATIVE].

**Limits.** The panel excludes API requests, which Fishkin estimates are 50% to 75% of total use. The panel skews younger, female, lower-income, and mobile. The study measures one assistant in one country. The share is an estimate, not a census.

**Use in this wiki.** This page sets expectations for GEO planning. A small share of ChatGPT use is purchase research, so a small local business should not build its AI strategy on ChatGPT alone. The better target is the Google AI surface, where purchase intent is higher. This transfer to local strategy is this wiki's inference and is tagged `[TENTATIVE]`.
