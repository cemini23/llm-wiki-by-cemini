## Raw Concept

This page answers one question: how does a business set and keep correct Google Business Profile (GBP) hours? The source is @sources/newsletter-rss-sterling-sky-2026-11-24-how-to-change-business-hours-on-google-a-step-by.md, a Sterling Sky practitioner article fetched 2026-11-26.

## Narrative

Hours drive the **open or closed** label on a GBP listing. That label shows in Maps, in the local pack, and in the Knowledge Panel. A wrong label sends a customer to a closed door. It also sends a false signal to Google about the business [TENTATIVE — single agency source, consistent with GBP help text].

**Set the hours.**

1. Sign in to the account that manages the profile.
2. Click **Edit Profile**.
3. Open **Info** and find **Business hours**.
4. Click the **pencil icon**.
5. Pick the hours from the dropdowns, and mark closed days.
6. Click **Save**.

**The three opening modes.** These are separate. The listing does not convert from one to another:

| Mode | Result |
|------|--------|
| Open with main hours | Shows the hours you set. This is the default |
| Open with no main hours | Shows no hours. The listing stays live. Not the same as 24/7 |
| 24 hours | Open at all times. Set "24 hours" in both the open and close dropdowns |

**Breaks.** Put the morning hours in the first two dropdowns. Click the plus sign. Put the afternoon hours in the second set. Repeat for each break. The article sets no limit on the number of breaks.

**The hour layers.** A listing can carry more than one set of hours. Each layer has a different job:

- **Main hours** — the hours that set the open or closed label. These are the only hours that change rankings.
- **Category hours** — access, delivery, takeout, or happy hours, set by the primary category. They show under the main hours.
- **Extra hours** — for some categories, such as online service hours. They show when a user clicks **More hours**.
- **Special hours** — holiday and one-off days. They sit below the normal hours in the dashboard.

**The ranking finding.** Sterling Sky tested "Online service hours" on service-area and storefront listings. The extra hours did not change rankings. Only the main hours change rankings, because they set the open or closed label [TENTATIVE — single agency test, no published positions]. `[OPEN QUESTION 2026-11-26]`: does the same hold for category hours such as delivery hours? The article does not test them.

**A stale main hours field beats every extra layer.** If the main hours are wrong, the listing still shows as closed, even when the category or extra hours are correct. Fix the main hours first.

**Watch the hours.**

- If Google suspects the hours are wrong, it shows **"hours may be incorrect"** and invites user corrections. An approved user edit can leave wrong hours live.
- Google sometimes changes hours on its own. Check the profile on a regular schedule.
- After an owner update, Google adds a note to the profile that the business changed its hours.
- Google may call to confirm profile details. Not every hours call is from Google. Treat unsolicited calls with care.
- Closed-when-listed-open is a quality flag on the listing. See @concepts/google-business-profile.md and @concepts/local-pack-rankings.md.

**Operator rule.** Correct the main hours after every schedule change. Add special hours before each holiday. Then check the profile again in a week, because a user edit or a Google auto-change can undo the fix.
