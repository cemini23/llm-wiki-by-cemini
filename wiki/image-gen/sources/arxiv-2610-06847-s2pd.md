## Narrative

**The problem.** Bidirectional video diffusion violates physical and symbolic rules even when trained on effectively unlimited in-distribution data — a ball passes through a wall, a puzzle piece teleports. Serial (autoregressive, block-causal) generation fixes validity but is slow and drifts over long horizons. S2PD takes both: it denoises **serially at high noise, then in parallel at low noise**.

**What is serial vs parallel.** This is a **sampling-schedule restructuring**, not an architecture change — enabled by a block-causal *training* regime. Video tokens are grouped into blocks of size b. Sampling denoises blocks autoregressively from t=1 down to a transition noise tau (default 0.6, so 4 of 10 steps are serial), each conditioned on a KV cache of prior blocks. Then the whole video is one block, denoised in parallel from tau to 0 (the remaining 6 steps). Training mixes block sizes b in {4, 8, ..., Nf, Nv} per batch with per-block independent noise and block-causal attention.

**Results.** Conway invalid-transitions per rollout goes **9.4 (bidirectional) to 0.0**. Chess 51.0 to 12.3; Fifteenth Puzzle 59.1 to 12.7. Pong dynamics error 159.7 to 1.3. With the Wan-5B-LoRA variant, CD-FVD on Kubric MOVi-A drops 252.8 to 137.0 and on Rubik's Cube Real 698.7 to 64.4. It is also roughly **2x faster** than the causal baselines (6.8 s vs 13.8 s).

**Implementations.** A pixel-space DiT-B (132.7M, from scratch) and a **Wan-5B-LoRA** (rank-32, 80.6M trainable against 5.00B frozen), using Wan's frozen UMT5-XXL text encoder. Output is 256x256, 10 s at 24 fps.

**Phase-0 (2026-10-07).** **Code, datasets and weights are released** — rare in this batch and the reason this is the strongest item. The licence is not stated, so confirm rights before commercial use. Training was 12 h on 4xH100 (DiT-B) and 48 h on 16xH100 (Wan-5B-LoRA). Note the resolution: 256x256 research output, not production video.

**Why it matters locally.** The schedule is **architecture-agnostic and cheap**. An operator can apply two-phase sampling to a Wan 2.x pipeline already running locally, without training anything, if a schedule-compatible checkpoint exists. That is a materially different proposition from the wiki's other restructuring entries: DMD, DMD2 and DMAD are *distillation* (they change the model), while this restructures *when* the model is called. See `@concepts/serial-to-parallel-diffusion-schedule.md`.

**Basgiath hook — yes.** Two distinct hooks, both from the *evaluation* method rather than the generator: parse output into symbolic states and count invalid transitions per rollout, which drops straight into an unattended world-gen test bench; and the serial-coarse / parallel-fine idea as an inference strategy for autoregressive terrain synthesis. See `../dragon-rider-map/briefs/2026-10-07_s2pd-transition-validity-bench.md`.
