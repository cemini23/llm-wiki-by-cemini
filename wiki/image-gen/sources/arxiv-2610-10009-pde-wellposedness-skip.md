## Narrative

**What it is.** Pure functional analysis. It proves strong well-posedness — existence, uniqueness, regularity and weak-strong uniqueness — for a coupled diffuse-interface system: incompressible Navier-Stokes with variable density, a Cahn-Hilliard equation carrying a soluble-surfactant concentration field, concentration-dependent mobilities and singular Flory-Huggins potentials. Local-in-time strong solutions in 3D; global plus strict phase separation in 2D.

**Method.** Semi-Galerkin approximation, viscous regularisation, weighted Laplace operators, fixed-point and compactness arguments. **No numerical computation.**

**The word "diffusion" here is a trap worth recording.** It refers to physical mass diffusion (Cahn-Hilliard), not diffusion models. Anyone triaging this by keyword would mis-file it.

**Phase-0 (2026-10-09).** No code, no repository, no licence, and no model evaluation. The paper's own data statement reads that no datasets were generated or analysed.
