## Narrative

The paper describes a participatory design study for Deaf-centric text-to-speech (TTS). The team ran two focus groups, three co-design sessions, and four one-on-one early-stage design evaluation sessions with deaf and hard of hearing (DHH) participants. The work names two main gaps: DHH users cannot easily verify speech output without listening to it, and generic voices do not reflect personal identity. The paper appears at ASSETS 2026. The work is design research, so it gives requirements and user criteria, not a model. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
