## Narrative

~4k manually verified clips with rich annotations (speech content, speaker ID, music attrs, sound events, temporal relations) + voice-cloning and timestamp-conditioned subsets. Protocol covers acoustic fidelity, speech quality, semantic alignment, temporal accuracy. Benchmarks agentic orchestrators, unified AV generators, and native mixed-audio models — **no model wins across all axes**.

**Phase-0: WATCH / REFERENCE eval.** High value for persona-audio stack (Fish-Speech + ACE-Step + Stable Audio Open mux) once dataset+code land. Do not claim installable. Phase-1: `deferred`.
