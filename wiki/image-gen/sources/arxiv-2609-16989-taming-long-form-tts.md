## Narrative

LACI detects and repairs long-form TTS failures (skip/hallination) during multi-minute autoregressive generation with models like Qwen3-TTS and VoxCPM2. Relevant to persona voice pipelines that need stable multi-turn calls. Argmax affiliation. No public SPDX repo at Phase-0. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
