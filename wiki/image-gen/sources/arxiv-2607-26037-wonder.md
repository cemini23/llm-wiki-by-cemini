## Narrative

Interactive world model: image or video → persistent navigable world with 6-DoF camera, sparse-attention memory over full-fidelity history, rectified self-forcing distillation. Claims minute-scale @ 16 FPS / ~0.5s constant latency; I2V + V2V re-shoot.
