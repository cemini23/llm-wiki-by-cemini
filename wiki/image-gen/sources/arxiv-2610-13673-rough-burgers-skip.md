## Narrative

**Pure probability theory.** It studies parameter estimation for a singular Burgers-type stochastic PDE on the one-dimensional torus driven by space-time white noise. It proves the solution's remainder term is smoother than its Gaussian part, computes exact spatial quadratic and temporal quartic variations, and builds strongly consistent pathwise estimators of the diffusivity parameter.

**Method — theory only.** Rough-path analysis, Holder regularity and Gaussian analysis. No experiment, no numerical simulation.
