## Narrative

BooM-VVT is a mask-free video virtual try-on (VVT) framework that builds on the keyframe-driven paradigm. It uses a multi-stage training strategy with image-level pseudo data to learn mask-free localization. It adds Garment-Sensitive Keyframe Sampling and Frame-Shared 3D-RoPE for garment consistency. The authors also construct OmniView, a large-scale multi-view try-on dataset. The work appears at ACM MM 2026. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
