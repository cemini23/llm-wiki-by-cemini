## Narrative

Vidu S2 from Tsinghua University and Shengshu Technology has two parts. Vidu S2-Avatar generates a real-time interactive digital character at 720p and 25 to 42 FPS. Vidu S2-Editing edits a video stream in real time for style transfer, virtual try-on, character replacement, and background replacement. The paper also studies real-time spatial video for left-eye and right-eye views. The work is a closed product paper with a playable online demo at vidu.com/vidu-stream. The text gives no public weights and no repository. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
