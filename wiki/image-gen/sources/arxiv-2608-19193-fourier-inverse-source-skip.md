## Narrative

**Phase-0: SKIP math.NA.** Reconstructs a piecewise-smooth source in a Poisson equation on a semi-infinite strip from noisy interior-line measurements: a Dirichlet-operator reduction confines instability to second-order differentiation, then staggered detection partitions + GTSVD-regularized derivative coefficients + a mollified conjugate-Fourier breakpoint detector recover the source. Numerical inverse-problems analysis (heat/pollutant/crack identification, photo-acoustic imaging).
