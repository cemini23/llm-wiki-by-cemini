## Narrative

GeoLAM learns geometry-grounded latent actions from unlabeled human manipulation videos. It combines future-frame reconstruction with geometry constraints so motion is not entangled with appearance or camera shift. Useful as a world-model / latent-action pattern for video stacks. No public GitHub found at Phase-0. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
