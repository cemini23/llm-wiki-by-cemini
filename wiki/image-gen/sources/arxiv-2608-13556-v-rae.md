## Narrative

**Follow-up/sibling of @entities/models/videorae.md (arXiv:2607.14088)** — same thesis: latent spaces are optimized for pixel-level reconstruction, not generative modeling; a reconstruction-optimal latent need not suit generation. **V-RAE** builds compact generative latents on top of **frozen vision foundation model representations** (4 encoders evaluated); lightweight **temporal pooling** removes temporal redundancy while preserving semantic structure; video decoder reconstructs continuous motion.

Results [TENTATIVE]: **2.13 rFVD on K600** — outperforms all evaluated large-scale pretrained video VAEs; latents retain substantially more semantic info than conventional video-tokenizer latents; best variant gFVD **117.86 (UCF101) / 19.16 (K600)**, up to **6× faster convergence**; improves future video prediction on Cityscapes over **Wan 2.2 VAE** under matched settings. Introduces **tFVD**, a temporal-coherence diagnostic that correlates more reliably with downstream generation quality than recon alone.
