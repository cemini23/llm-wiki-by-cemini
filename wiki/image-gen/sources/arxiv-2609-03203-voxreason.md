## Narrative

VoxReason measures the pre-synthesis delivery decision in expressive speech systems. A planner outputs a source-cited speaking plan for affect, pitch, energy, rate, pause, emphasis, and stance. A deterministic verifier then checks citation legality, slot agreement, unsupported state, schema validity, and one-cue counterfactual locality. The work matters for this wiki because it tests source-use before any waveform exists. Rendered waveform quality stays outside the present evaluation. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
