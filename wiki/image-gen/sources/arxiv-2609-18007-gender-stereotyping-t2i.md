## Narrative

Evaluates gender representation across 20 occupations and 4 Stable Diffusion generations (SD 1.5, 2.1, SDXL, SD 3 Medium); 8,000 images. Finding: newer models are not fairer (76.4% male-presenting in open-source set). Thin WATCH for persona/legal risk awareness — not a build-track model. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
