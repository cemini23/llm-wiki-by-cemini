## Narrative

Trajectory-based distillation: predict multiple denoising steps per NFE (parallel decoder / fused linear). Avoids brittle VSD+adversarial recipes; claims better diversity. SOTA 4–8 NFE on LTX-2.3 T2V/Audio, Wan 14B T2V, Qwen-Image T2I.

**Phase-0: WATCH → CONDITIONAL-GO when PDD lands in FastGen** — FastGen Apache-2.0 (~12 MB) is the intended home; do not clone laptop until PDD configs/checkpoints appear. TipDrop CUDA when code ships — high-value Wan/LTX latency path.
