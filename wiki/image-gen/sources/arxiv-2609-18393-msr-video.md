## Narrative

MSR (Multiple Subject Reference) is slot-aware conditioning for LTX-based video generation: each reference image gets independent encoding, numeric slot embedding, and slot-dependent temporal offsets. Pairs with subject-aware attention / persistent identity themes. Licon Studio. No public repo at Phase-0. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
