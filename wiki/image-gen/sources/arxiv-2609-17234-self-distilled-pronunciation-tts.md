## Narrative

ICASSP 2027 submission. Controls pronunciation and accent without a lexicon or per-word exemplars: a frozen backbone reads a sentence with a common word it already pronounces correctly, then self-distills to a tagged replacement word. Relevant to persona voice pipelines that need rare-word / accent fixes without retraining. No code found. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
