## Narrative

The paper introduces Principia, a benchmark that tests Newtonian physics in generated video through relational consistency between paired objects. It spans eight phenomena and uses real-world scenes recorded under controlled protocols. The authors also give a calibration-independent consistency score that measures physical violation in image space. Six video generators score at most 0.42, although each scores near 0.8 on VBench.
