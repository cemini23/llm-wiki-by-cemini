## Narrative

KAIROS is a dataset for fine-grained video-language modeling. It organizes annotations over three axes: space (what is where), time (when does it happen), and dynamics (how does it change). The source set holds 19,004 long-form videos with 5,420 total hours. Each clip runs 10 to 30 minutes, with an average of 17 minutes. This is a dataset, not the Kairos 3.0 world model.
