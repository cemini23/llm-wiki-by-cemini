## Narrative

**A title that reads like a security paper and is not.** "SSH" here is the **Su-Schrieffer-Heeger model** — a dimerized tight-binding chain from topological condensed-matter physics — not Secure Shell. "Cavity" is a superconducting microwave resonator (cavity QED), not a storage structure. Both were checked explicitly rather than assumed.

**What it does.** Recodes a generalized quantum Rabi (cavity-atom) Hamiltonian beyond the rotating-wave approximation into an effective 1D tight-binding lattice in the excitation-number basis. Large photon number yields a dimerized SSH chain under a linear Wannier-Stark gradient, giving Bloch and Bloch-Zener oscillations; small photon number deforms the hopping in a photon-number-dependent way, reshaping the density of states and producing anomalous diffusion.

**Method.** Analytical quantum mechanics — spectral analysis, perturbation theory, Bessel-function identities, wave-packet dynamics. "Simulation" in the text means **analog quantum simulation on superconducting circuits**, which is theory, not computation.
