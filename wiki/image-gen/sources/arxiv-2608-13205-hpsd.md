## Narrative

TI2V models get much better visual quality from a high-quality first frame (I2V mode) than from text alone (T2V mode). Question: can that privileged-condition capability be **internalized into the model's own base generation ability** (self-distillation)? Naive supervised fine-tuning is **off-policy**: supervision confined to teacher-generated endpoints from a fixed offline distribution. HPSD is a hybrid-policy self-distillation that addresses the off-policy gap.
