## Narrative

Real-time **streaming** video with **online 4D control**: decomposed camera motion, human motion, and background; motion transfer + long-term generation. Complements Stream4D (4D consistency *reward* for AR rollouts). **WATCH HIGH.** No public training code at Phase-0. Image-gen Phase-1: none.
