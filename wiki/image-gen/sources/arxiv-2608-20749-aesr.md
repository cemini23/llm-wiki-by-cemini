## Narrative

Training-free loop around **closed commercial** identity-preserving T2V (Seedance2 / Ark APIs). **Agentic Enhancement** learns model-specific prompting from docs + human-interaction priors and writes a reusable playbook. **Semantic Repair** uses a VLM to locate bad segments, edits frames into visual references, and drives a video editor. Lightweight MoE picks among generation/repair paths. MIPL_Video ranked first on ACM MM 2026 IPVG Track 1.

**WATCH HIGH** for persona identity T2V, but it is a **cloud-API orchestrator**, not a local Wan/ComfyUI graph. Null SPDX → no GO clone. Image-gen Phase-1: none (`deferred`).
