## Narrative

Diffusion lip-sync for **complex scenarios** (Quwan / Playmate111). **Dual-stream joint training** reduces reference-frame information leakage; **single-step distillation** targets real-time inference; **relational alignment loss** improves sync quality vs latency tradeoffs. Persona stack comparator to LatentSync / EfficientSync for hard lighting, profile, or motion cases.
