## Narrative

VIDHALLOC is an evaluation harness for video-hallucination detectors. It tests fifteen methods for the localisation of hallucinations in video. The dedicated video-hallucination detectors peak at 34.63 percent overall. This result shows limited reliability across video hallucination types. Do not trust a single detector.
