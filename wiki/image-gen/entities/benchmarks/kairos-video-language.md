## Narrative

KAIROS is a fine-grained video-language dataset. It labels long-form video over space, time, and dynamics. The source set holds 19,004 videos with 5,420 total hours, at 10 to 30 minutes per clip. The result is a benchmark target for video understanding and captioning work. Name collision note: this KAIROS is a fine-grained video-language dataset.
