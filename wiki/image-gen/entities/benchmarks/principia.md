## Narrative

Principia is a benchmark that tests Newtonian physics in video generators and vision-language models. It compares the relative motion of two objects that obey the same physical law. The method measures a relational invariance in image space, so it needs no camera calibration or metric scale. The benchmark holds about 500 real-world scenes across eight phenomena.
