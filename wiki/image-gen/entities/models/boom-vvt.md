## Narrative

BooM-VVT is a mask-free video virtual try-on framework. The method uses image-level pseudo data in a multi-stage training strategy instead of costly video-level pseudo data. Garment-Sensitive Keyframe Sampling picks keyframes from garment-relevant body regions. Frame-Shared 3D-RoPE links keyframes to target video frames for garment-detail transfer. The paper reports better temporal consistency and garment fidelity than existing methods. No repository was found, so do not clone it. Image-gen Phase-1: none. wire_status: deferred.
