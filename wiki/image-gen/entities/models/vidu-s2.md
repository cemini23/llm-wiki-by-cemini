## Narrative

Vidu S2 is a real-time interactive video system from Tsinghua University and Shengshu Technology. It has two models. Vidu S2-Avatar generates a digital character at 720p and 25 to 42 FPS, and accepts a new reference image at any moment. Vidu S2-Editing edits a live video stream for style transfer, virtual try-on, character replacement, and background replacement. The editing method uses frame-aligned attention, so each target frame reads only the source frame at the same time step.
