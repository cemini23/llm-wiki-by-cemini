## Narrative

"Seeing the Voice" is a participatory design study for Deaf-centric text-to-speech (TTS), not a released model. The method is co-design with deaf and hard of hearing (DHH) users: two focus groups, three co-design sessions, and four early-stage design evaluations. The authors ask for non-auditory verification of speech output, control of tone and emotion, and identity-centered voice cloning. The paper states that voice cloning must stay user-driven, and it must not "correct" deaf speech. The code is none found, and there is no clone.
