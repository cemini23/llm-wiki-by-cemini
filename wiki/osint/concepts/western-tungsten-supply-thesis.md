## Narrative

**Thesis (operator position):** China-tight tungsten is still live. Almonty is the liquid US ticker for that bottleneck (Sangdong processing, GTP offtake, Panasqueira cash, Gentung as the US hook). The stock at **$17 / ~$4.47B** (262.8M shares; UW $4.25B is the $16.18 prior close) has already discounted a lot of the strategic story. Grants are a **sector** tailwind; **NO_EVIDENCE** of an ALM-specific award as of 2026-08-20.

The 20 Aug EhrmantrautCap tweet is **EQ Resources / China→Japan 0.0 kg**, not an ALM leak. Same-complex cousin tape. The **0.0 kg** is the tweet's wording; ZeroHedge same week said Chinese **APT** to Japan has reportedly been zero since January, not every tungsten form. Do not flatten EQR into ALM.

Q2 NI is mostly **C$173.1M** non-cash. Ops improved (Adj. EBITDA C$17.6M) on **US$3,075/MTU** APT as the primary revenue driver, plus Panasqueira. Sangdong sales are a **Q3** test (UW-estimated earnings **2 Nov**). Convert **US$800M closed 9 Jun** (priced $700M + $100M option). Buyback authorization is not execution. Aug 17 dark pool **$80.5M** UW-classified sell-lean (possible duplicate $31M block).
