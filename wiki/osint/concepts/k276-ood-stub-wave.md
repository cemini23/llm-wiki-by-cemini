## Narrative

Seven items. Two arXiv papers, two engineering newsletters, two crypto newsletters, and one certification paper. One repo is MIT. Zero clones.

E-WAVE and ReSight-SMC are vision and inference names. The Shopify item reports that AI coding agents removed a language barrier and flipped a settled platform decision. The CockroachDB item supports the same review-fading thesis. The Bitget item is a hack headline with no exploit steps. The CZ item gives a sizing rule, not a level.

RSD-Poker is the one with a reusable pattern. It freezes a candidate bank and a group partition, then joins the oracle labels last. That is the same commit-authority order we filed on the K275 hub. Poker as a product is retired, so the paper is a name and a pattern only.

The Bitget page records facts only. No exploit path, no address list, no laundering method. **No PoC. No xsp or pm scp.**
