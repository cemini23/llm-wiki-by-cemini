### Academic / REIT precedents (not corrections-specific)

Campbell et al., *Real Estate Economics* — major REIT property sales **1992–2002**:

- Abnormal returns **positive on average** for opportunistic sales.
- Returns **lower** when proceeds retire **long-term debt** (information content: distress or lack of reinvestment option).
- Returns **higher** when proceeds fund **share repurchases**.
- Discount / liquidation sales → **~zero** abnormal return.

**Read-through for GEO/CXW:** ICE turnkey sales at **premium to book** are "opportunistic," but if the market hears **"deleveraging first"** (Zoley Q1 language; CXW 8-K waterfall), academic literature predicts **muted** announcement returns even when the headline $ is large.

**Buy-the-rumor / sell-the-news:** Well-documented when expectations are pre-built (earnings, central bank, M&A). CXW Jul 2026 fits: **positive news + negative day**.

### Post-event convergence watch (CXW, Jul 2026)

Reframes the trade from **"gap and sustain on print"** (failed Jul 6) to **"does price converge toward fair value within ~10 trading days"** — a **liquidity + positioning + catalyst** bet, not a fundamental one. What would confirm convergence vs a stuck/failed drift:

| Signal | Convergence (bull) | Stuck / failed (bear) |
|--------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Daily tape post-Jul 6 | Higher lows, holds >$31 | Fade back toward/below $30.42 |
| Buyback evidence | Volume + 10b5-1 / accelerated repo disclosed | "Considering" only; no prints |
| Analyst follow-through | More $40+ PTs, upgrades | PT cluster stalls at $36 |
| Volume / short cover | Rising vol on up days | Short **volume** stays elevated on down days |
| Macro / sector | Neutral-to-risk-on | Risk-off (Jul 7: oil spike, AI wobble, VIX +5.5%) pressures small caps |

**Base rate caution:** post-sale mispricings can persist until a **hard catalyst** forces recognition. For CXW that catalyst is **Q2 earnings (Aug 5/6)** — likely **outside a 10-day window** from Jul 6. A pure 10-day bet therefore leans on **fund repositioning + repurchase flow**, not on the print. "Fundamentally right, path-dependent" is the operative risk.

### Working conclusion

**GEO 5–8 facility sale at ~$300k/bed is a real binary** with **larger dollar headline than CXW**, but **default expectation should be CXW-shaped: positive headline, negative or flat reaction** unless surprise + capital-return stack breaks the pattern. The market is not trading **$/bed × total portfolio**; it is trading **(surprise − leak) × (cash to equity − debt) × remaining catalysts**.
