### Academic / REIT precedents (not corrections-specific)

Campbell et al., *Real Estate Economics* — major REIT property sales **1992–2002**:

- Abnormal returns **positive on average** for opportunistic sales.
- Returns **lower** when proceeds retire **long-term debt** (information content: distress or lack of reinvestment option).
- Returns **higher** when proceeds fund **share repurchases**.
- Discount / liquidation sales → **~zero** abnormal return.

**Read-through for GEO/CXW:** ICE turnkey sales at **premium to book** are "opportunistic," but if the market hears **"deleveraging first"** (Zoley Q1 language; CXW 8-K waterfall), academic literature predicts **muted** announcement returns even when the headline $ is large.

**Buy-the-rumor / sell-the-news:** Well-documented when expectations are pre-built (earnings, central bank, M&A). CXW Jul 2026 fits: **positive news + negative day**.
