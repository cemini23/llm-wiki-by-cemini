## Raw Concept

How this wiki files a 1000+ URL public OSINT directory without a reject-stub farm, without visiting crimeware, and without pretending ToS-gated sites are un-catalogable.

## Narrative

A public OSINT directory is a **locator set**, not a product install list. Cemini writes one hub source, one hub entity, and a handful of section catalog pages. Every URL gets a table row: KEEP (public investigative tool worth remembering), DEAD-OUTDATED (ignore), CONTEXT (know it exists; do not operationalize), NESTED-HUB (another board), or CAUTION (leak dumps, darknet markets, ransomware sites, unauthorized cameras, malware corpora). CAUTION rows still exist so the operator can see what the board contained; agents do not open those sites and do not write exploits or dump-search playbooks.

Freedom-of-information cataloging of **public search, archives, maps, people locators, and privacy tools** is in scope for an authorized OSINT company. Interacting with criminal leak forums, buying dumps, or writing implant/PoC material is not. Intent is judged on tool effect, not wrapper phrasing. Mass scraping of third-party sites is out; bookmarking the locator is in.

Shadow libraries (Sci-Hub, Libgen, Z-Library) stay CONTEXT: do not ingest copyrighted corpora. Fake-identity generators stay KEEP as tradecraft locators with a dual-use caveat — they are not a crime how-to. Genealogy-relevant public records (FamilySearch collections, SSDI locators, cemetery directories, OpenCorporates, Canadian corporate registries) KEEP toward Atto methodology pages, never private Barone vitals.
