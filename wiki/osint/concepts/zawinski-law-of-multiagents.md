## Narrative

Every agent expands until it can message other agents; non-messaging agents get replaced. Ops implication: treat **inter-agent channels, shared stores, and externalized memory** as first-class security/monitoring surfaces (pairs AgentRadio inbox / K232). Harness choice dominates model choice on some benches — keep HarnessOpt-Bench framing.
