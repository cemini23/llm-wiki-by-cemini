What prompted this page: SMB *Top 10 Gap Trading Mistakes* — five **expensive errors** (countdown 10→6) plus five **lap trades** (5→1) that fall into your seat when you wait for information (2026-01-28).

## Narrative

### Five expensive gap mistakes [TENTATIVE]

| # | Mistake | Fix / reframe |
|---|---------|---------------|
| **10** | Immediate gap-and-fail chase | Urgency = alert, not action; wait for first pull-in / rest without falling apart |
| **9** | Gap into HTF resistance | Ask "what problem does this gap run into?"; expect negotiation, not continuation |
| **8** | Low-participation gap | Require **≥3% ADV** in pre-market + defined PM trend; don't fight PM downtrend on positive catalyst |
| **7** | Opening-drive exhaustion | Speed ≠ control; fake strength burns out — prepare for fade when no base after rip |
| **6** | Ignoring VWAP verdict | VWAP = consensus of larger players; sustained below = stop hoping, listen |

**PM gap filter (mistake #8):** gap must trade ≥3% of average daily volume; avoid gaps sold from 4am open with no participation.

### Five lap trades (wait for these) [TENTATIVE]

| # | Pattern | Signal |
|---|---------|--------|
| **5** | High-and-tight flag | Stillness near highs = strength (shallow pullbacks, volume drying up) — not boredom |
| **4** | Gap-up VWAP build | Repeated defense at VWAP → campaign not scalps; trend day may come late |
| **3** | Failed flush reclaims level | Weakness clears field; flush + aggressive reclaim = favorite long setup |
| **2** | Tight range + volume compression | Shrinking volume stores volatility; **≥50% volume bar expansion** = OR break cue |
| **1** | Absorption at highs | Massive sells hit tape but price won't drop → iceberg bid; tape-reading breakthrough |
