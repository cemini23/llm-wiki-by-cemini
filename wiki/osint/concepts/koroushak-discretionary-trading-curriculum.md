## Raw Concept

Synthesizes @KoroushAK's 2026 X curriculum (19 posts, K67): a **regime-first discretionary stack** for crypto spot/perps — pick environment (breakout vs reversal), select coins (Orion-style liquidity/attention), execute with shared risk/exit/bias modules.

## Narrative

### Core thesis

Two opposing strategies, each valid only in matching environment:

| Regime | Strategy | Retail mistake |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| Breakout | Long resistance / short support | Buy support, sell resistance |
| Reversal | Fade extremes back to mean | Chase breakouts in chop |

**Environment filters** (reversal post): four-filter checklist before trading — low TRD + mixed movers → reversal; high TRD + directional leaders → breakout. Aligns with @concepts/altcoin-screener-orion-workflow.md column semantics.
