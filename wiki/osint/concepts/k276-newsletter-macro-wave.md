## Narrative

Three items. No level to arm.

Two Klement posts summarize academic work. The first says the rise of passive funds made active managers worse, through flow pressure against the active tilt, with the alpha regime shift around 2010. The second says a corporate tax rise selects for productive firms and pushes weak domestic firms toward creative destruction, while multinationals move production instead.

Capital Flows is a paywalled teaser. Its one reusable line: hedging the last regime's risk is a form of pattern capture.

None of the three names a price, a strike, or a date to watch. Do not arm a watch. Do not write `watches.json`. **No level to arm.**
