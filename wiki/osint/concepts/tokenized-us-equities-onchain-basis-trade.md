## Narrative

K67 Post 23 is a promotional flag, not a venue notebook: **~$1B TVL** in tokenized US stocks (~8 months) remains `[TENTATIVE]` single-thread. The steal is the *trade class* — onchain basis (token vs perp / borrow / bridge) for crypto-native flow — not directional equity picking on CeminiSuite.

Do not treat a tokenized-stock “discount to the tape” as a yield or alpha signal until the K118 pipeline is applied: (1) **measurement reconciliation** (day-count, fees, NAV lag, maturity mismatch can be tens of bps by themselves), then (2) **price-discovery admissibility** (serial dependence + idiosyncratic dispersion — not obviously an accounting republish). Tokenized Treasuries: of four products with history, **exactly one** passed admissibility. Equities wrappers inherit the same failure mode.
