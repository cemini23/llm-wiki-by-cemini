**Liquidity Provision and Rebate Design in Option Markets (26606)** — a nested optimisation model for market making plus exchange rebate design. The market maker trades multiple European calls in a local-stochastic volatility market with make (continuous) and take (impulse) controls. The objective maximises portfolio value plus cumulative rebate revenue, under a penalty on residual delta and vega. The exchange side is a three-step rebate design scheme that can target a specified liquidity level. Solution technique: Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman quasi-variational inequality.
