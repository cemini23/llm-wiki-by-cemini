## Raw Concept
When the end state is not knowable at t=0, do not write one giant spec. Orchestrate child sessions against a **map** (decisions already made) and a **ticket** (this session’s task). Reveal the next ring of the map after each decision (fog of war). Use `/grill-me` when the path fits in one session.

## Narrative
Pocock’s leading words (map / ticket / session; grill / prototype / research / human-task) are the skill. Inconsistent synonyms are misevolution.

Osmani relocates human judgment onto the factory’s *edges*: Warp labels as queue+lock (ready-to-implement / ready-to-spec / needs-info / wait-to-implement); Vercel success/flawed/blocked/manual; verification budget; persist trajectory to fight comprehension debt. Wayfinder fills the *unknown-unknown* planning hole those factories still have.

CCC steal (propose-only, do not edit `.cursor/skills` this wave):
- Gate: grill-me vs wayfinder.
- Artifacts: map.md + ticket.md merged before AFK.
- Factory: labels as lock; draft PRs; only `success` ships.
- Pair with BPS so the AFK run does not top-k a bloated skill pack into the overnight context.
