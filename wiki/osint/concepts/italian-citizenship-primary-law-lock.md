### Two-clock method (do not merge in product copy)

1. **Clock A — historical transmission (24045):** Was the next-in-line a birth dual national (*ius sanguinis* + foreign *ius soli*)? If yes, parent naturalization during minority should not cut the Italian line under Art. 7. Prove foreign nationality **title** (birth vs derivative/collective effect).
2. **Clock B — recognition filter (L74 / 63 / 147):** Can the applicant still clear art. 3-bis exceptions, pre-27 Mar 2025 filing/appt, or pending CJEU relief? Separate from Minor Issue.
