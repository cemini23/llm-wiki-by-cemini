## Narrative

### Setup grading [TENTATIVE]

- **B setups** — several per week possible; baseline risk unit.
- **A+ setups** — at most ~1/month for top traders; if more frequent, grading too loose.
- **Risk differential** — A+ sized ~**5×** B risk when every nuance hits; backed by multi-year stats.

### Sliding-scale risk chart (Garrett / ep. 10) [TENTATIVE]

Playbook-agnostic grading — opportunity profile mental cues + **logarithmic risk % of daily max stop**:

| Grade | Opportunity cue | Risk % of daily stop |
|-------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **A+** | Trade of the quarter | **80%** |
| **A** | Trade of the month | **40%** |
| **A−** | Trade of the week (undisputed edge) | **20%** |
| **B** | Situational filler (common — dangerous) | **5%** (collapsed with C) |
| **C** | Experimental / learning | **5%** |

Frequency triangle: take every A+ when it appears (rare); A− is normal high-edge playbook trade; **pass most B/C** — overtrading B trades clouds A− visibility.

### Full expression vs paralyzing sizing [TENTATIVE]

A+ world ≠ single perfect entry at 80% on open. **Bring your A+ trader** — trade freely with smaller probes (e.g. 20% against solid stop), add as tape confirms; market must **give** the full-size entry (Gold/Silver parabolic + FSLY examples). Avoid review-induced perfectionism on one spot.

### Daily stop % ladder (Jackie / level 3) [TENTATIVE]

From @sources/smb-capital-youtube-week-2026-02-28.md — market earns risk, default **zero**:
- C: 5% · B: 15% · A: 30% · A+: 80% of daily stop
- B-sized risk requires ≥3:1 R:R; filters: technical criteria + see/feel opportunity

### Five themes from dinner

1. **Extreme selectivity** — A+ frequency low by design.
2. **Dynamic risk** — size up best ideas responsibly; under-sizing A+ is an error.
3. **Mental capital** — focus on few large opportunities vs chasing every scalp.
4. **Systematic risk scaling** — iterative career growth without blow-up.
5. **Tool adaptation** — humility as markets change (2022 slow period lessons).
