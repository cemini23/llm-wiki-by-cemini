## Narrative

### Eight drawdown lessons [TENTATIVE]

| # | Lesson |
|---|--------|
| 1 | **Don't miss what you do best** — can't skip core playbook (breakouts) while learning new product |
| 2 | **Separate playbooks** — commodities/crypto ≠ equities; silver ATR can 3× in vol clusters |
| 3 | **Earn your risk** — new product starts at reduced size; add risk from success only |
| 4 | **Don't play for the grand slam** — lock **≥half** on huge winners; three solid trades > one $10M swing |
| 5 | **Don't seed structural edge** — active traders exit/re-enter easier than institutions; book profits |
| 6 | **Past success doesn't continue** — build business inside market principles every regime; humility |
| 7 | **Stay in seat for easy markets** — 10-year cycle: ~10 great years, 18–24 hard months; must survive to compound |
| 8 | **Push risk when hot** — Shark / Druckenmiller cite: scale up when trading well (not when in hole) |

### The cure: consistency [TENTATIVE]

When in drawdown, **one focus only** — consistency on your best trades:
- Best setups, entries, exits, time frame from your playbook
- Set intraday alerts to take **easy money** setups only
- Sit in the pain (don't pretend session didn't happen) — pain forces laser focus
- Drawdown while learning new product → expanded playbook + reinforced guardrails if you adapt
