## Narrative

**Thesis:** three objects are being welded. Only the third is a BioNTech clock, and it is not a vaccine approval.

1. **19 Aug 2026.** Moderna + Merck INTerpath-001. Melanoma vaccine + Keytruda. Phase 3 met RFS and DMFS. Not BioNTech.
2. **28 Aug 2026.** BioNTech + Genentech terminated Phase 2 BNT122-01 (NCT04486378) in ctDNA-positive resected CRC. Futility boundary crossed October 2025. Company: numerical OS imbalance, no direction published, no new safety signals. IMcode003 in PDAC continues.
3. **12–15 Sep 2026.** WCLC Seoul. Official table has six BioNTech slots. None on Saturday. Sunday meso is a **bispecific**, US cash closed. Monday has the only official mRNA slot (BNT116 Phase 1). Tuesday OA14.01 is pumitamig + ADC, Phase 1/2. No Phase 3 win.

COVID revenue is shrinking. Cash is large. Late-stage weight is bispecifics and ADCs, plus BNT113 AHEAD-MERIT still in 2026 guidance. No oncology product sales expected in 2026.

Retail flow is buying WCLC as an MRNA lottery ticket. That analogy is the error.
