## Raw Concept

Does a company change what it says when a government can take its revenue? Prompted by the Lopes and Goncalves study summarised in the Klement post.

## Narrative

### The finding

Ana Isabel Lopes and Joao Goncalves (2026) read the social media feeds of the **20 largest US and 20 largest European companies**. They classified each post and measured the DEI share, comparing 2024 with 2025.

The DEI post share falls in both regions. The article shows the chart but gives no numeric values in text. [Source: substack-rss-klement-2026-11-09-dei-is-dead.md]

### The mechanism

The proposed channel is **revenue exposure to government**. Klement's example: Alphabet has 191,000 employees. A federal lawsuit or a lost contract could cost thousands of jobs. On that arithmetic, a public stance on a contested issue carries a measurable price.

The mechanism is not unique to one policy area. It generalises: **where a government is a large customer or a large litigant, public communication is a priced decision**.

### What the study cannot separate

The study measures **speech**, not programmes.

A fall in DEI post share is consistent with:

1. the programmes were cut;
2. the programmes continue and the firm went quiet.

The data cannot distinguish them. This is the standard limit of disclosure-based measurement: silence is not absence.

`[TENTATIVE]` for the causal claim. One working paper, 40 firms, no stated control group.

### What would make it testable

- A measure of programme activity that is not self-reported: job postings, supplier contracts, filings.
- A split by the firm's federal revenue share. If the mechanism holds, the fall should be larger at high-exposure firms.

### Why it matters here

This is a governance input, not a market signal. Two uses:

1. **A precedent for disclosure as a choice variable.** Any dataset built from corporate speech has a selection problem that moves with political conditions.
2. **A political-risk factor.** Revenue concentration in government contracts is measurable and it predicts speech changes.

## Dead Ends

- **Reading post share as programme activity.** Silence and termination are indistinguishable in the data.
- **Extrapolating from 40 large firms to the market.** The sample is the largest firms in two regions.
