## Raw Concept

Operator asked for a **1-page lean conversion brief** after Gemini DR pentad ingest (K192). Full multi-page marketing site was deferred — this page is the shippable spine for youratto.com, not a brand magazine. Opus fact-check applied 2026-07-23.

## Narrative

### Audience

English-speaking DIY **jure sanguinis** applicants (and privacy-sensitive genealogists assisting them) who already accept they will gather documents themselves — drowning in PDFs, apostilles, translations, and name discrepancies — and will not pay full-service retainers. macOS-first. High PII anxiety; hostile to cloud auto-merge.

### One-sentence promise

**A local-first Mac vault for organizing your own Italian civil records — you own the files, you make the calls; Atto never files, judges eligibility, or leaves your Mac.**

Avoid “bank-grade / zero-knowledge” unless controls match @concepts/atto-local-first-threat-model.md.

### Must-have sections (lean page only)

1. **Hero** — product UI; promise; CTA `Buy the DIY kit — $299`; macOS · pay once · local · soft-gate
2. **Silent demo strip** — statics + GIF tiles (no autoplay narrated hero for v1)
3. **Stakes (neutral)** — discrepancy / messy-packet cost; do not claim Atto prevents rejection
4. **This is / isn’t** — organizer ≠ attorney; suggest ≠ auto-merge; soft-flag ≠ verdict
5. **What you get** — Apple-notarized `.dmg`, vault, checklists, claim `license.json`, setup PDF
6. **Price** — single $299 SKU; soft anchor vs full-service (“thousands”); no decoy tier; no dead Screen Studio OTP comp
7. **Trust** — Apple-notarized · local-first/open formats · **30-day refund** (match T&Cs) · not gov-affiliated
8. **Short FAQ** — eligibility no; Prenot@Mi no; Ancestry contrast; migrate vault; **macOS only**
9. **Footer disclaimer** — not a law firm; laws change; not government-endorsed

### Demo asset list (priority)

| Priority | Asset | Notes |

| P3 | 60–90s narrated demo | After page converts |

### Non-goals (why full marketing site stayed deferred)

- Multi-page brand magazine / blog / podcast / team-address hub as launch blockers
- Two-tier decoy pricing, physical USB merch, interactive Guideflow demos
- Paid Meta/Google “citizenship” ads before claim-fence harden
- Facebook group hard-sell; Prenot@Mi bots; eligibility quizzes
- Windows availability claims; MoR as day-one default; SaaS onboarding
- “AI lawyer,” “automated lineage validation,” citizenship guarantees, document notarization services

### Open decisions (do not silently invent)

- Confirm “1 year of updates” product policy before FAQ
- Whether “kit” includes downloadable template letters (only if legally reviewed / non-advice)
- Exact encryption / telemetry claims must match @concepts/atto-local-first-threat-model.md
