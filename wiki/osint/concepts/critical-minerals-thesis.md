## Narrative

### Universe

"Critical minerals" in this concept's usage = the set the US Geological Survey defines as critical (2022 list, periodically updated): rare earth elements, lithium, cobalt, nickel, uranium, copper, graphite, manganese, plus tactical adjacencies.

### Thesis

1. **Supply concentration**: 60-90% of supply chains for most critical minerals concentrated in 1-2 countries (typically China + one partner: Myanmar for REEs, DRC for cobalt, Indonesia for nickel)
2. **Geopolitical export restrictions** (post-2022): China April 2025 REE export curbs, Indonesian nickel-ore ban evolution, US Inflation Reduction Act + EU Critical Raw Materials Act demanding alternative sources
3. **Demand acceleration**: EV transition + grid storage + humanoid-robotics (REEs in motors) + datacenter buildout (copper) + nuclear restart (uranium)
4. **Capacity lag** (per @concepts/capital-cycle-theory.md): 5-15 years to bring a new mine online; current capex underspend 2014-2020 limits 2025-2030 production response
5. **Investment leverage**: real-asset equities (per @concepts/real-assets-investing-framework.md) capture the supply-deficit price expansion via operating leverage

### Specific sub-theses (Cemini-tracked)

- **Rare earths (heavy: Dy, Tb)**: @concepts/rare-earth-supply-deficit-thesis.md — Adamas forecasts 116× Lynas-Mt-Weld-equivalent supply gap by 2040
- **Uranium**: nuclear restart (Powell-to-Warsh transition policy backdrop per WW Issue #176) + datacenter demand growth + post-Fukushima supply collapse + Russia/Kazakh geopolitical fragility. UEC +140% Donny.
- **Lithium**: EV demand vs price collapse 2023-2024; bottom-of-cycle entry; ELVR +264% Donny / WW $20→$88 thesis.
- **Copper**: structural deficit thesis on grid + EV + datacenter convergence; not in Donny watchlist but Massif-adjacent.
