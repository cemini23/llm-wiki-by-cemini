## Raw Concept

What prompted this page: SMB Capital YouTube *The 5 Day Trading Mistakes Robbing Your Account* (Jeff Holden, 2026-05-12). SMB claims 19/20 stuck traders made ≥3 of these; 60-day focus on mistakes (not strategy) recovered 16/20.

## Narrative

SMB frames **execution discipline** — not strategy or stock selection — as the bottleneck for stuck intraday traders.

### The five mistakes + fixes [TENTATIVE — SMB desk data, single source]

| # | Mistake | Fix (summary) |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 1 | **Holding runners past ~10:30** | Respect intraday momentum window; take profits before chop |
| 2 | **Averaging down** | (video details add-on sizing rules — don't add to losers without plan) |
| 3 | **Trading without tape confirmation** | Require L2/T&S confirmation before entry/hold — see tape-reading concept |
| 4 | **Ignoring three red-bar momentum shift** | Exit when momentum structure breaks (three consecutive adverse bars) |
| 5 | **Not taking profits at resistance** | Scale out before level; don't hope for first-test breakout |

### SMB cited statistics [TENTATIVE]

- **First-hour runners (>5%):** 73% make HOD before 10:30; 89% before 11:00; only 11% new HOD after 11:00 (5,000-trade sample, 12 months).
- **Resistance first test:** holds ~68%; second test ~54%; third ~42% — take partial profits approaching resistance (50% off within 10¢ of level claimed +40–60% per-trade profit improvement over 100 trades).

### Implementation protocol

- Fix **one** mistake for **60 trades**; track before adding others.
- SMB claims +$300–500/month from fixing one mistake; more if all five [TENTATIVE].

### Cemini relevance

- Intraday equity sleeve: hard **time-of-day exit rules** (10:30/11:00) are testable gates for momentum strategies.
- Resistance partial-take rule maps to execution layer, not signal generation.
- Complements @concepts/tape-reading-order-flow-day-trading.md for mistake #3.
