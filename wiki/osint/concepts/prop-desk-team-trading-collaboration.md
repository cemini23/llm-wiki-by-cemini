## Raw Concept

What prompted this page: SMB Capital YouTube *The Secret Structure of 8-Figure Prop Desks* (2026-05-05) — team collaboration vs lone-wolf myth; shared workspace for playbooks, themes, and catalyst research.

## Narrative

SMB argues elite prop desks win through **collaboration and shared infrastructure**, not isolated “maverick trader” mythology.

### Desk structure themes [TENTATIVE]

1. **Specialization by strength** — idea generation, execution, quant/automation, risk, bandwidth (multi-position traders), narrative/theme tracking.
2. **Pre-market volume filter** — flag names doing **>20% of average daily volume** in pre-market/after-hours; exceptional signals (e.g. Fastly **200% ADV**) trigger team alert and deep-dive.
3. **Shared playbook / catalyst catalog** — research attached to tickers and themes; avoids “Google Doc disappears after the open” loss of institutional memory. Parallel to Lance’s note-app-per-name pattern cited in video.
4. **Theme widget** — running sector narratives (software/SaaS disruption, metals, crypto, energy, etc.) so stock selection aligns with market regime.
5. **Idea grading + table pounding** — specialist (e.g. earnings inflection-quarter process) rates setup A/A+; team listens when domain expert signals.

### Trading Floor Podcast — pod formation (2026-03-14) [TENTATIVE]

Garrett Dyrden / Tim / Kurt — *The Missing Piece That Made Trading 5× Easier*:

1. **Similar playbooks + differing strengths** — shared setups/watchlists; each member leads their specialty (idea gen, tape, narrative, execution, risk, bandwidth).
2. **You need not be elite at everything** — NBA role-player analogy (Jordan/Pippen/Rodman); pod covers cognitive gaps.
3. **Communication is non-optional** — explicit pod norms; remote traders must invest effort (FinTwit/X, communities, deliberate outreach).
4. **Rodman rule** — allow "going rogue" if role returns to team function (rebounder rebounds).
5. **Signal → catalyst → tape confirmation** — collaboration chain turns small ideas into full trades none would run alone.

### Fastly case study (video) [TENTATIVE]

PM volume spike → team chat → earnings specialist validates inflection quarter → theme context (software selloff) → coordinated open preparation — **stock selection before tape confirmation**.
