## Raw Concept

Concept synthesized from Donny's market research note (2026-05-09). Addresses the structural supply-demand imbalance in heavy rare earth elements (dysprosium, terbium) and its investment implications.

## Narrative

### Supply Concentration

**China + Myanmar** control over 90% of global DyTb mined production. China's heavy rare earth production quotas have been **flat since 2018** at approximately 19.15k tonnes per annum. In **April 2025**, China restricted exports of Dy and Tb in response to US tariffs.

### US Government Response

The US Department of War provided a **$150M loan** and a **$400M preferred equity investment** into MP Materials between July and August 2025, demonstrating clear willingness to fund domestic and allied critical minerals supply chains.

### Demand Acceleration

- **Humanoid robotics**: Projected as the largest single demand category for rare earth magnets by 2040, requiring potentially over **70,000 tonnes per annum** of REO for robots alone.
- **EVs**: Each uses approximately 2–3kg of RE magnets.
- **Wind turbines**: Require ~2,000kg each.
- **Advanced air mobility and defence**: All require high-DyTb-content permanent magnets.

### Forecast

**Adamas Intelligence** forecasts:
- A supply gap for DyTb by **2040**, equivalent to ~116x the annual DyTb production of Lynas' Mt. Weld mine.
- Forecast price increases in magnetic rare earths of **over 100% by 2040**.

### Strategic Implications

The combination of **supply concentration risk**, **demand acceleration**, and **government intervention** creates a structural tailwind for critical minerals that is difficult to replicate in most other commodities. REA's positioning at the intersection of US government support and high-grade discovery makes it a key beneficiary of this thesis.
