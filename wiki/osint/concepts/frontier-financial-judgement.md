## Raw Concept

Can agents tell what might move a stock — and at what false-positive cost?

## Narrative

FFJ (2607.20645) shows frontier agents are far from reliable equity news triage: best all-label match **52.4%**, FP rates ~1–32% across labs. For Cemini XSP/intel: never auto-escalate agent news scores without an FP budget; prefer conservative (low-FP) models for overnight regime alerts.
