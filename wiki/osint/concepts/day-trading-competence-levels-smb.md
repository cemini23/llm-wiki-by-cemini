## Raw Concept

What prompted this page: SMB *The 5 Levels of Day Trading Competence* (2026-03-05). Development ladder with mindset / behavior / P&amp;L pattern per level; **level 3 is the danger zone** where careers stall.

## Narrative

### Level summary [TENTATIVE — SMB framework]

| Level | Name | Mindset | Graduate via |
|-------|------|---------|--------------|
| 1 | Gambler | Gut feel | Write entry+exit rules; follow 30 days |
| 2 | System seeker | "Need the right strategy" | Pick **one** strategy; **20 focused reps** (no switching) |
| 3 | Break-even grinder | "Doing everything right" | **Quality filter** + market-earned sizing (Jackie case) |
| 4 | Consistent earner | Trust process | ~2–3 months; **earned right** to raise daily stop |
| 5 | Master trader | Execution is everything | Scale into A+ clusters; 70–80% daily stop on best setups |

**Cannot skip levels.** Desk mantra: **brutal honesty** about current level.

### Level 3 breakthrough — Jackie case

Following rules but taking every qualifying setup (many C setups at B risk) → flat P&amp;L.

**Rules vs standards:** rules = when to enter/exit; standards = which setups deserve capital.

Three filters before sizing:
1. Meets technical criteria?
2. Risk:reward ≥ **3:1** (required for B-sized risk; C can be smaller)
3. Clearly see **and feel** the opportunity?

**Daily stop allocation** (market earns risk, not recent P&amp;L):
- Default: **zero risk**
- C: 5% of daily stop
- B: 15%
- A: 30%
- A+: 80%

Result: 11 trades vs 43 in first month; ~$42k [TENTATIVE single anecdote].

### Level 5 note

Trades "less" by concentrating gradient into A+ — not fewer executions, but most days &lt;1% account risk until cluster appears.
