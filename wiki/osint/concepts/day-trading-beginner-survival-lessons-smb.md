## Narrative

### Life skills vs trading skills [TENTATIVE]

| Life success | Trading equivalent |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Confidence | Healthy skepticism |
| Persistence | Cut losses fast |
| Strong opinions | Flexibility |
| Taking action | Waiting |

### Seven survival lessons [TENTATIVE]

| # | Lesson | Core rule |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Paper trading lies | Start **small real money** (50–100 shares) so fear/feedback is real |
| 2 | Screen time ≠ results | **5 focused hours** > 12h spectating; quality over quantity |
| 3 | First strategy will fail | Strategy is vehicle for **execution** (sizing, exits, emotion) — fix execution before switching |
| 4 | Sizing > entry | Same setup, different size → opposite P&amp;L; edge = setup + size for your emotional infrastructure |
| 5 | Budget tuition | **12–18 month runway**; monthly max loss budget (example: $1k/mo × 18 mo); losses = feedback not failure |
| 6 | Style finds you | Stop copying adjacent trader; listen to market feedback for **your** playbook fit |
| 7 | Community matters | Normalize struggle; daily trader feedback accelerates learning |
| 8 (bonus) | Consistency beats heroics | Boring repeatable compounding > big swing months that shut off feedback |

### Lesson 5 detail — tuition budgeting [TENTATIVE]

- Treat first year as **skill development**, not a pass/fail test.
- If you cannot afford 12 months of structured tuition, do not trade full-time yet — one setup, one opportunity, let A+ fall into lap.
- Market is a **feedback-generating machine**; chasing opportunity without listening extends the curve.
