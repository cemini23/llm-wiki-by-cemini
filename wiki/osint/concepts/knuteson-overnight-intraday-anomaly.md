## Raw Concept

Bruce Knuteson SSRN paper (K103 ingest) documents persistent **overnight-positive / intraday-negative** return splits across global equities, framed as satire on regulatory and media dismissal tactics.

## Narrative

Methodology: split daily returns into overnight (close→open) and intraday (open→close) components; plot on log scale for hundreds of names and indices. Author argues similar shapes recur for decades with limited public escalation.

For Cemini equity-signal work: use as **skepticism prior** when evaluating overnight-only factors or session-split alphas — verify on ArcticDB with corporate-action hygiene before trusting Yahoo-derived session splits [NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-06-07 for replication on Cemini ticker universe].

Not a trading signal; satirical sections on "how to say nothing to see here" are methodology about institutional incentives, not trade instructions.
