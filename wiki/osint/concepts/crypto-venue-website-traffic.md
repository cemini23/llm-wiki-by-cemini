## Raw Concept

What this page answers: monthly crypto-exchange website-traffic trends (WuBlockchain series) as a retail-flow / venue-activity proxy, distinct from derivatives notional volume.

## Narrative

July 2026: **~134.31M visits across 12 exchanges, −2.35% MoM** (5 rose, 7 fell). Top: Binance 36.04M (26.8%), OKX 25.62M (19.1%), Coinbase 20.60M (15.3%); top-3 = 61.2% concentration. MoM: Bitfinex +3.7%, KuCoin +3.2%, Bybit +2.8%; Deribit −44.7%, HTX −23.0%, Upbit −16.0%.

Reading: (1) **Deribit's website-traffic collapse (−44.7%) tracks its derivatives-volume collapse (−44.4%)** — options-venue contraction is real activity, not sampling noise; (2) top-3 web concentration 61.2% vs top-3 derivatives 74.9% — same venue-concentration story, slightly looser on the retail-facing side; (3) geographic split — Binance top origin India, OKX Japan, Coinbase USA — maps the regional retail flow.

As a proxy, website traffic is a leading-ish indicator for retail onboarding and app-download intent, but is confounded by bot traffic and marketing campaigns; treat as context, not signal.
