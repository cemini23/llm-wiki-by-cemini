## Raw Concept

Should Atto treat **Argentina / Brazil / Venezuela / Dominican Republic** as first-class audiences alongside Americans? What civil-registry, nationality-title, language, and consular-system facts must the product encode before shipping locale packs?

**Not legal advice.** Diaspora headcounts are secondary estimates; demand signal is ISTAT recognition mix. Product may collect docs / checklist — never auto-eligibility.

### Country system cards

#### Brazil

- **Nationality:** Constitutional *jus soli* (CF art. 12) — born in Brazil = *brasileiro nato* (diplomat exception). Strong Clock A bipolide pattern when paired with Italian *sanguinis*.
- **Civil registry:** **Cartório do Registro Civil** issues **certidão de nascimento / casamento / óbito**. Province/municipality of registration matters for re-issue.
- **Apostille:** Hague (in force Aug 2016). Apostille at **authorized cartórios** (CNJ list) — not Italian consulate.
- **ID stack:** CPF, RG, Brazilian passport as corroboration — birth cert remains primary soil-title proof.
- **Italian channel:** Consolato Generale **São Paulo** historically the poster-child backlog (Prenot@Mi waitlists years deep); other seats (Rio, Curitiba, Porto Alegre, BH, Recife, etc.) vary. Judicial recognition volume also high (ISTAT: abroad JS split consular vs judicial roughly 53k / 61k in 2024 overall).
- **Atto hooks:** PT-BR UI strings; `DOC_FOREIGN_IUS_SOLI_TITLE` = apostilled certidão + IT sworn translation; label naturalização separately; soft-flag São Paulo appointment archaeology for `SF_APPT_IMPOSSIBLE` evidence packs.

#### Argentina

- **Nationality:** Strong *ius soli* — *argentinos nativos* by birth in territory. Distinguish *nativo* vs *carta de ciudadanía* / naturalización / *opción*.
- **Civil registry:** Provincial **Registro Civil** + **RENAPER** ecosystem for DNI. **Partida de nacimiento** (and marriage/death) requested from the province of event (not via Argentine consulates abroad as primary channel).
- **Apostille:** Cancillería **TAD** (Trámites a Distancia) digital apostille for public docs including partidas; CABA partidas often two PDFs (acta scan + “certificado de partida entregada”). **Seat×province overlay** (7 IT seats) — Rosario/La Plata/BA acceptance rules differ [Source: argentina-consular-rules-deepening-2026-07-29].
- **Naturalization path:** **[RETRACTED]** DNU 366/2025 Migraciones *carta* live UX — courts nullified; competence remains **Juzgados Federales**.
- **Italian channel:** Buenos Aires + other Argentine seats; BA Prenot@Mi new JS suspended post **28 Mar 2025**; same L74 constraints.
- **Atto hooks:** ES-AR copy; partida + seat-aware apostille checklist; CNE F003/F052 fee **5,000 ARS**; DNI language check (*nativo* vs naturalized).

#### Venezuela

- **Nationality:** *Jus soli* at birth (constitution + nationality statutes); dual nationality permitted in modern frame. **Monasterios (24045):** Gustavo born VE 1946 (*ius soli* + Italian blood); mother naturalized VE **3 Mar 1954** while he was minor → SS.UU. Art. **7** continuity.
- **Civil registry / apostille:** **Partida / acta de nacimiento** from Registro Civil; apostille via Venezuelan competent authority (SAREN / MPPRE e-apostille path — operators must verify live portal; secondary guides note electronic expansion). Document access for diaspora abroad can be **operationally hard** (gestores, power of attorney) — product should expect friction, not pretend one-click.
- **Italian channel:** Caracas + other VE seats; many applicants now reside **outside** VE (US/ES/PT/BR) — venue follows **residence**, not birth country.
- **Atto hooks:** Reuse Monasterios as worked example in Research UX (never claim auto-win); require exact parent naturalization **date** + child’s age; VE birth title + naturalization packet as separate vault items.

#### Dominican Republic

- **Nationality:** Constitution art. 18 — birth in territory confers Dominican nationality **except** children of diplomats, persons **in transit**, or **irregular residence**. Dual nationality recognized. **Not** US-style unrestricted *ius soli*.
- **Civil registry:** **Junta Central Electoral (JCE)** / Oficinas del Estado Civil — **acta de nacimiento**. Post-2000s practice: foreign-mother / irregular-status births may land in **Libro de Extranjería** (does **not** confer Dominican nationality) — Clock A false-positive risk if operator assumes “born in DO = Dominican.”
- **Apostille:** Hague member; apostille/legalization path via foreign ministry / competent authority after JCE extract.
- **Italian channel:** Smaller seat (Santo Domingo); lower volume than BR/AR — good for a **locale stub**, not GTM priority #1.
- **Atto hooks:** Explicit Research branch: “Dominican **from birth**?” → acta in ordinary civil book vs Libro de Extranjería / later naturalization. Do **not** auto-tag `DOC_FOREIGN_IUS_SOLI_TITLE` from birthplace alone.
