## Raw Concept

Should Atto treat **Argentina / Brazil / Venezuela / Dominican Republic** as first-class audiences alongside Americans? What civil-registry, nationality-title, language, and consular-system facts must the product encode before shipping locale packs?

**Not legal advice.** Diaspora headcounts are secondary estimates; demand signal is ISTAT recognition mix. Product may collect docs / checklist — never auto-eligibility.

### Country system cards

#### Brazil

- **Nationality:** Constitutional *jus soli* (CF art. 12) — born in Brazil = *brasileiro nato* (diplomat exception). Strong Clock A bipolide pattern when paired with Italian *sanguinis*.
- **Civil registry:** **Cartório do Registro Civil** issues **certidão de nascimento / casamento / óbito**. Province/municipality of registration matters for re-issue. National CRC / Busca Nacional sells **breve relato** only — consulates need **inteiro teor** from the holding cartório. CRC **excludes** inteiro teor (LGPD); diaspora path is usually **direct cartório**, not CRC intermediation [Source: brazil-jure-sanguinis-deepening-2026-07-29].
- **Apostille:** Hague (in force Aug 2016). Apostille at **authorized cartórios** (CNJ list) — not Italian consulate. **São Paulo / Belo Horizonte reject digital docs / e-Apostilles** — physical chain dominates.
- **ID stack:** CPF, RG, Brazilian passport as corroboration — birth cert remains primary soil-title proof. CNN via `deest.mj.gov.br/ecertidao/` with all name variants. Grande Naturalização + Certidão Negativa remain first-class DOC types.
- **Italian channel:** Consolato Generale **São Paulo** historically the poster-child backlog (Prenot@Mi waitlists years deep); other seats (Rio, Curitiba, Porto Alegre, BH, Recife, etc.) vary. Judicial venue: Cartabia Art. **19-bis** → 26 Corti d’Appello (ancestor’s comune). Watch **L. 11/2026** postal / 2029 centralization.
- **Atto hooks:** PT-BR UI strings; `DOC_FOREIGN_IUS_SOLI_TITLE` = apostilled **inteiro teor** + IT sworn translation; never promise CRC→filing-grade; soft-flag São Paulo appointment archaeology for `SF_APPT_IMPOSSIBLE` evidence packs.

#### Argentina

- **Nationality:** Strong *ius soli* — *argentinos nativos* by birth in territory. Distinguish *nativo* vs *carta de ciudadanía* / naturalización / *opción*.
- **Civil registry:** Provincial **Registro Civil** + **RENAPER** ecosystem for DNI. **Partida de nacimiento** (and marriage/death) requested from the province of event (not via Argentine consulates abroad as primary channel).
- **Apostille:** Cancillería **TAD** (Trámites a Distancia) digital apostille for public docs including partidas; CABA partidas often two PDFs (acta scan + “certificado de partida entregada”). **Seat×province overlay** (7 IT seats) — Rosario/La Plata/BA acceptance rules differ [Source: argentina-consular-rules-deepening-2026-07-29].
- **Naturalization path:** **[RETRACTED]** DNU 366/2025 Migraciones *carta* live UX — courts nullified; competence remains **Juzgados Federales**.
- **Italian channel:** Buenos Aires + other Argentine seats; BA Prenot@Mi new JS suspended post **28 Mar 2025**; same L74 constraints.
- **Atto hooks:** ES-AR copy; partida + seat-aware apostille checklist; CNE F003/F052 fee **5,000 ARS**; DNI language check (*nativo* vs naturalized).

#### Venezuela

- **Nationality:** *Jus soli* at birth (constitution + nationality statutes); dual nationality generally **No** until **1999** Const. **Monasterios (24045):** Gustavo born VE 1946 (*ius soli* + Italian blood); mother naturalized VE **3 Mar 1954** while he was minor → SS.UU. Art. **7** continuity. Mid-century Ley Nat. **1940/1955** minor-umbrella paradox + married-woman automatism traps remain open.
- **Civil registry / apostille:** **Partida / acta** from Registro Civil; since **11 Jun 2026** SAREN e-apostille for **153** types (in-person SLAE voided for those). Pre-1960 / non-digitized → poder → Registro Principal. **Payment:** PUB + domestic VE bank + SMS to VE mobile — foreign cards fail [Source: venezuela-italian-citizenship-deepening-2026-07-29].
- **Naturalization proof:** **SAIME** `datosfiliatorios.saime.gob.ve` = named datos filiatorios product (Gaceta nº + date).
- **Italian channel:** Caracas + Maracaibo; many applicants reside **outside** VE — venue follows **residence**. US VE consulates reported **closed** / no reopen plan (hedge). Caracas: L.74 + **beneficio di legge** calendar **31 May 2026** for affected minors.
- **Atto hooks:** Monasterios as worked example (never auto-win); SAIME datos as DOC type; soft-flag payment firewall + US-consular-dark.

#### Dominican Republic

- **Nationality:** Constitution art. 18 — birth in territory confers Dominican nationality **except** children of diplomats, persons **in transit**, or **irregular residence**. Dual nationality recognized. **Not** US-style unrestricted *ius soli*.
- **Civil registry:** **JCE** / Oficinas del Estado Civil — **acta de nacimiento**. **Libro de Extranjería** confers **no** nationality. Ley **169-14**: **Group A** = original Acta = at-birth; **Group B** = naturalization → **Libro de Transcripción** (Res. **13-2024**) = **not** birthright `[RETRACTED prior A→Transcripción map]` [Source: dominican-nationality-document-deepening-2026-07-29]. **Acta Única** (Jul 2025) may mask book type — soft-require QR / *copia in extensa*.
- **Apostille:** MIREX e-apostille (~RD$620 / ~24h); translations need **PGR** then MIREX. JCE online hard-requires **cédula**.
- **Italian channel:** Smaller seat (Santo Domingo); lower volume than BR/AR — locale stub, not GTM #1. Checklists silent on Extranjería/Transcripción — silence ≠ trap absence.
- **Atto hooks:** Research branch: book type first (ordinary vs Extranjería vs Transcripción Group B). Do **not** auto-tag `DOC_FOREIGN_IUS_SOLI_TITLE` from birthplace alone.
