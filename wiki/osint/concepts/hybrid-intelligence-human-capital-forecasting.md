## Raw Concept

Synthesis of Ming (2026) pilot: human–AI forecasting value is **trimodal**, moderated by collaborative human capital (perspective-taking, curiosity, intellectual humility), not raw **g** or model benchmark scores.

## Narrative

Pooling hybrid users into one average hides failure modes:

- **Automators** — copy model; floor above unaided human but below AI-only.
- **Validators** — use AI to confirm priors; can underperform **both** human-only and AI-only `[TENTATIVE]` small n=9.
- **Cyborgs** — iterative complementary reasoning; only group beating market and solo models in pilot.

For PM/Kalshi bots and Poker Arena: instrument **how** the operator/agent uses tools (style tags), not just model tier. Maps to `@concepts/poker-arena-multi-axis-cognitive-profile.md` as a prospective **M₈ collaboration-style** or trait-proxy axis. Do not deploy prod gates from pilot alone — replication pre-registered.

Socratic-withholding model (supplement): raised Cyborg rate at cost of satisfaction — design tradeoff for training curricula, not UX default.
