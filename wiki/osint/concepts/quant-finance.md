## Narrative

The quant-finance cluster covers (non-exhaustively):

- volatility forecasting and regime detection
- VaR / CVaR / drawdown risk measures
- backtesting methodology and look-ahead bias
- factor models and signal generation
- alpha decay and capacity constraints

Cluster membership is established by the corpus-bootstrap router's regex matching against `quant-finance` keywords (see `scripts/bootstrap_extract.py` and `scripts/bootstrap_inbox.py`). False-positives are expected: first-keyword-wins routing matches surface-level vocabulary, not topical intent. Manual re-cluster on deep read is acceptable.

Cross-cluster overlap with other hubs is explicit (a single source can be tagged into multiple of: options-derivatives, market-microstructure, quant-finance, portfolio-strategy). Each hub lists every member regardless of overlap — the hubs are discovery aids, not partitioning categories.
