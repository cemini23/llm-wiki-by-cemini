## Narrative

### Universe definition

"Real assets" in Massif's usage = equities providing claim on **physical resource production**, not the resources themselves:

- **Mining equities** (gold, silver, copper, lithium, REEs, uranium)
- **Energy equities** (oil & gas producers, integrated energy, renewables-with-grid-attachment)
- **Materials equities** (industrial metals processors, specialty chemicals)
- **Infrastructure** (pipelines, ports, rail) — context-dependent inclusion

Excludes: physical metals, commodity futures, broad commodity ETFs. The thesis is **operational leverage on capacity-constrained production**, which only equities give you.

### Why equities not commodities

- **Operating leverage**: equities are levered to the spread between selling price and all-in sustaining cost (AISC), which can expand 5-10x in tight supply conditions
- **Optionality on reserves**: miners with proven-but-undeveloped reserves are call options on future commodity prices
- **Capital allocation decisions matter**: well-run miners compound; poorly run ones don't, even at the same commodity price

### Position-sizing discipline (per Q4 2025 letter)

Massif maintains concentration: typically 15-25 positions, with top 5-10 representing >60% of NAV.

**Position taxonomy (Q4 2025 disclosed)**:
- **Equinox Gold (EQX)**: 10% position. Newly producing Greenstone mine in Ontario; capex behind them, FCF inflection 2026-2027
- **G Mining Ventures (GMIN)**: exited October 2025 after thesis played out (Tocantinzinho went into production on time/budget)
- **Lundin (presumably LUN.TO)**: detractor in Q4 2025
- **Enovix (ENVX)**: detractor in Q4 2025 — battery-tech crossover position, regime-change exposure

### Thesis-test discipline

Per @concepts/wicked-problems-capital-allocation.md: every real-asset position has a documented:
- Load-bearing assumption
- Falsification signal (capex acceleration; demand collapse; political event)
- Re-examination cadence (typically quarterly)
