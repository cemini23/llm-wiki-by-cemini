## Narrative

**Checks in favor** = pre-trade mantras that quiet noise and justify risk — not generic textbook entries. SMB grades each as must-have, adds conviction, or extra credit.

### Seven must-haves (breakouts) [TENTATIVE]

| # | Check | Meaning |
|---|-------|---------|
| 1 | **A+ pink line** | Significant breakout level (often prior failures + volume at level); avoids mid-range chop |
| 2 | **Higher-timeframe squeeze** | Weeks/months consolidation compression — energy for trend day |
| 3 | **Blue sky** | 52-week high, ATH, or no overhead resistance — "explode into space" |
| 4 | **Narrative** | Theme, short interest, fund growth, macro story — reason for re-rating |
| 5 | **Volume moment** | Volume surge at breakout (distinct from background liquidity) |
| 6 | **Clean tape** | Order flow confirms (lifts, no hidden seller) — ties to tape-reading concept |
| 7 | **Hidden relative strength** | Name strong vs market/sector when tape looks mixed |

### Three question buckets

- **Setup** — Does this belong on the watchlist? (pink line, squeeze, …)
- **Context** — Does it have reason to go? (narrative, RS, momentum name)
- **Timing** — Is it happening now? (opening proximity to level, not half-ATR spray into resistance)
