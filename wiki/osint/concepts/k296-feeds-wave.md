## Raw Concept

Wave hub for the 2026-11-10 ingest. Nineteen sources: sixteen newsletters and three arXiv papers. The question this page answers: what is in this batch, and which items change a decision.

## Narrative

### What the wave is about

Three threads run through the batch.

1. **The midterm aftermath, in three markets.** Prediction-market regulation, crypto risk assets, and AI-infrastructure credit all moved on the same Democratic sweep. See @concepts/prediction-market-impartial-access-dcm.md and @entities/companies/coreweave.md.
2. **Prediction-market structure.** Volume records, the parlay share of volume, and the argument that the durable moat is trade-out quality. See @concepts/prediction-market-trade-out-moat.md.
3. **Reading instruments correctly.** An options-flow package that most feeds misread, and a VIX term-structure model with a fitted memory parameter. See @concepts/options-flow-package-reading.md and @concepts/vix-term-structure-fractional-memory.md.

### What is weak

- Two Capital Flows posts are teasers with almost no content.
- The Klement pieces are working-paper summaries with 40-company and regional samples.
- The two probability papers (branching, LCM) carry no financial content. They are method references.
- The Moontower essay is philosophy, not market content.

### What changed a view

Only one item changed a working assumption. The Event Horizon volume tables show that **about three quarters of Kalshi volume is parlay product**. Any model that prices Kalshi as a venue of single binary contracts is sizing against the wrong book.
