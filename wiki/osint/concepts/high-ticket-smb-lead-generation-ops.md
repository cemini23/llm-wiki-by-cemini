## Raw Concept

K57 newsletter Part 1 — operational playbook for solo operators selling **lead qualification + follow-up + review lift** to legacy local trades (HVAC, remodeling, cosmetic surgery). Primary distribution target is **seo-wiki** (GBP/reviews/local SEO); OSINT page records the model for cross-domain search.

## Narrative

### Value stack

1. **Lead filter** — urgency + project size (80–90% of unqualified tire-kickers removed).
2. **Follow-up automation** — SMS/email on missed calls; CRM notes → patterns.
3. **Dead-pipeline recovery** — re-engage cancelled quotes with separate messaging.
4. **Reputation manager** — route happy customers to Google/Yelp reviews; star-rating moat for local search.

### Economics

- Start **performance-based** (closed-sale cut) → migrate to **recurring** once playbooks stabilize.
