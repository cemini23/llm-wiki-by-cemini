## Raw Concept

What prompted this page: Jeff Holden (SMB head of trader development) at SMB Trading Summit — three trader **personality archetypes** and playbook alignment (2026-03-19 video).

## Narrative

SMB classifies traders by **where in the move they extract edge**, then insists stats + personality drive playbook design — not copying another archetype's best trades.

| Archetype | Edge | SMB examples cited |
|-----------|------|---------------------|
| **Player** | Catching turns — tops/bottoms | Reversal specialists |
| **Runner** | Momentum continuation | Nano, Man on desk |
| **Finisher** | Follow-through after initial move | Raff (~$19M single-year firm record) |

**Process:** review personal stats → identify which trade types dominate P&amp;L → overweight those setups in playbook; avoid forcing archetype mismatch (e.g. finisher chasing player entries).
