## Raw Concept

What prompted this page: SMB monthly review after historic gold/silver move — **P&amp;L disappointment is wrong metric** when team gained six career assets trading a new product for the first time (2026-02-07).

## Narrative

### Process over scoreboard [TENTATIVE]

Missed profit on a commodity surge ≠ failure if traders: (1) traded a new asset class, (2) reviewed A+ setups, (3) collaborated under volatility, (4) expanded playbook, (5) controlled risk, (6) built tools for next time. **Good trading experience = growth, not just green.**

Basketball analogy (10-year-old daughter): practice after practice matters; goal is greatness at 18, not making every shot at 10.

### Six career assets from first commodity cycle [TENTATIVE]

1. **Commodity mastery** — beyond equities
2. **Deep analysis** — extensive A+ setup reviews
3. **Collaboration** — navigate historic vol as a pod
4. **Playbook expansion** — separate commodity playbook (≠ equity rules)
5. **Risk control** — survive historically volatile move
6. **Tech growth** — tooling to capture more next cycle

### Pattern 1 — Daily overextension + intraday breakdown (GLD example) [TENTATIVE]

**HTF overextension filters:**
- Volume expansion **>150%**
- Price expansion **>5 ATR in <5 sessions**
- **>1 ATR** above upper Bollinger Band / key MAs; gap-up; traded outside upper band

**Intraday alignment:** lower highs, support at key level (e.g. 505), hold below VWAP, opening range tightens into 10:00, clear 5-min structure break → short below support with HTF alignment.

**Exit:** cover into sharp down moves on volume spikes; exit when product turns back up.

### Pattern 2 — Textbook backside (silver example) [TENTATIVE]

- Daily overbought (indicator extreme **>80**)
- Wait for intraday alignment — e.g. ~1:25 ET (legacy pit close / mark window) first significant red candles
- 5-min support floor; short below lows when backside confirms
- Don't press front-side; **let trade present itself**

### Trading principle [TENTATIVE]

**A+ parabolic shorts almost always fall into your lap** — in review, obvious support breaks; live, traders short too early, stress about top. Be patient; don't make things happen.
