## Raw Concept

What this page answers: the 2026 AI-industry pulse — where capability unlocks happened and what "good enough to trust" changed. From Latent Space BioAI + AINews digests.

## Narrative

Two 2026 signals: (1) **BioAI step change** — structural models became binding models, making AI-for-pharma tools "good enough to trust" (Chai Discovery, $4B, 2yo); tools now unlock things labs can't do — the unlock is capability, not efficiency. (2) **Open local agent workforce trend** — NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning ("local agent workforce": smaller, faster models tuned for high-volume tool use), Meta Muse Glimmer 30B (Apache-2.0, always-on local agents, ~4-bit <20GB). Both point to the same arc: cheap local executors + strong planners.

[Source: Latent Space 2026-08-11/12]

## K284 additions — the discovery turn and the capex problem (2026-10-07)

Three October signals:

- **AI as a discovery engine.** OpenAI published **722 math manuscripts** (~3 hours of compute each) from an unreleased model, including a "Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis" placed near a Fields-Medal result. Claims, not verified, ~20% disproofs. The arc: models move from *assistants* to *discoverers* in verifiable domains. [Source: @sources/substack-rss-latent-space-2026-10-07-quasi-riemann-openai-math.md]
- **The capex math problem.** **Anthropic 2025: revenue $4.6 B, compute spend $7.3 B, operating loss $8.06 B** (Klement/Reuters). Revenue grows fast; compute grows faster. [Source: @sources/substack-rss-klement-2026-10-07-ai-labs-profit.md]
- **The industry reorganises around agents.** Agent-authored PRs **exceeded human-authored PRs on GitHub in August 2026**; 5–10 parallel agents is normal; the IDE is fading. Old practices (tests, tracer bullets, planning) return. [Source: @sources/substack-rss-pragmatic-engineer-2026-10-06-state-of-tech-2026.md]

Net: capability keeps rising, the economics stay unresolved, and the *workflow* has already changed.
