## Narrative

Two items. Product maturity and capital, not prices.

**Polymarket launched responsible-trading tools.** Self-exclusion from 30 days to lifetime, deposit limits by day, week, or month, and mental-health resources through a national telehealth partner. It is also staffing a Trust and Safety team and publishing moderation standards. This is a maturity signal for the venue class.

**Capital followed.** Wu Blockchain and RootData report that Polymarket raised at a **$21B post-money valuation**, up about 40% from roughly $15B, in a round led by a firm where Donald Trump Jr. is a partner. Kalshi is reported to be raising toward a $40B valuation, from $11B in December and $22B in March.

Volumes reported: Kalshi on 29 September did about $2.2B, split 61.1% parlays, 18.3% sports, 16.1% crypto. Trailing seven-day: Kalshi $16.7B, Polymarket US $2.95B, DKeX $608M, and a long tail.
