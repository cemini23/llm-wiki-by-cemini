## Raw Concept

Question this page answers: when a large company adopts a net-zero target, do its emissions measurably fall?

Synthesised from one secondary source that reports a 2026 primary study. No independent measurement is recorded here.

## Narrative

Target adoption is now near-universal among large caps. About 70 of roughly 2,000 global large-caps had a long-term target at the end of 2019; more than 1,200 did by the end of 2024, about two-thirds of the sample's market capitalisation.

Measured progress is smaller than adoption. Three years after adoption, absolute emissions are about 15% lower and emissions intensity about 10% lower on average. The reported result is not statistically significant.

The distinction that matters for any downstream use: a pledge is a stated intention, and the measured effect is a separate, weaker quantity. Do not treat target coverage as an emissions outcome.

[TENTATIVE] This page rests on one newsletter's reading of one study. The primary paper (Dietz and Hastreiter, 2026) has not been read here.

## Snippets

> "the adoption of net zero targets has significantly reduced greenhouse gas emissions" — the claim the reported evidence does **not** support.
