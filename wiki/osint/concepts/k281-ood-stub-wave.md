### Japan and Korea — a split worth recording

Two Asia-Pacific signals in the same week, pulling opposite ways.

**Japan is tightening enforcement and studying its own debt.** The tax agency launched a new core system on 24 September to detect undeclared crypto assets. The Ministry of Finance formed a group to study **on-chain settlement of government bonds**, first meeting 8 October. A sovereign studying on-chain settlement of its own issuance is a different order of commitment from a bank pilot.

**South Korea is shrinking while its regulator expands the product.** Market cap fell **33%** in six months, to about $43.4B, with daily volume down from 5.4T to 3.1T won and KRW deposits down 35%. In the same window the FSC proposed extending **tokenized securities to stocks, bonds, and funds**, effective February 2027.

Retail volume collapsing while the product surface widens is the shape to remember. It is the opposite of what a "crypto is over" read would predict.
