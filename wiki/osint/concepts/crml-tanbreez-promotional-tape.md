## Raw Concept

What this page answers: is NASDAQ:CRML a Western rare-earth mine or a Greenland geopolitics option? Operator desk 1 Sep 2026. Observational. CFTC Rule 4.41.

## Narrative

**Thesis:** the tape pays for Greenland and the chance Washington writes a cheque. It does not pay for a built mine.

Facts that have to stay attached:

1. **MIN 2020-54** is a 30-year exclusive licence (8 Sep 2020 → 7 Sep 2050). No revocation notice found as of 1 Sep. Plan and financial-security dates remain unverified.
2. Classified resource is **44.87 Mt at 0.38% TREO**. **4.7 billion tonnes** is kakortokite host rock. The technical report says not to lean on it.
3. Host mineral is **eudialyte**. No commercial eudialyte rare-earth mine at scale. Silica gelation is the binding constraint.
4. Production speech walked 2026 → 2028–29 in thirteen months. Q4 2025 bankable study did not show up.
5. EXIM is a letter of interest. Ucore is non-binding. REalloys is binding for **15% of Phase 1** only. "75% pre-sold" is management speech with mixed scopes.
6. EUR all-stock scheme issues more CRML paper. It is not a reverse split. At $7.21 the indicated ratio sits at the **0.045** cap.

Do not short a 22M-share crowded name that rips on cousin USAR headlines. Optionality, if any, sits in listed puts with dead bids — treat last as a print.

### Falsifiers

- MRA-approved exploitation plan and posted bond appear on the Greenland record.
- A funded US cheque equivalent to the USAR package, named to CRML.
- Binding offtake coverage that is actually 75% of Phase 1 concentrate, with no five-year long-stop.
- EUR scheme fails the A$330M net-cash test at the second court date and the equity is recapitalised without the float doubling.
