## Raw Concept

What this page answers: is the US rearmament cycle an investable multi-year trade, and where does the growth sit? Operator desk 8 Oct 2026. Observational. CFTC Rule 4.41.

## Narrative

**Thesis:** the FY2027 budget and the depleted interceptor stockpile fund a multi-year rebuild. The primes get paid first and are already large, so the growth sits in the sub-tier and the drone complex.

Three legs. First, policy: the FY2027 defense request is $1.45 trillion, and the missile and munitions pool is about $95 billion, of which CRS attributes about $52.9 billion to the named programs. Second, demand: the Patriot interceptor stockpile is down about 65 to 67 percent, and the rebuild runs to the end of the decade. Third, escalation: Iran runs past the November midterms, and a reported European intelligence assessment puts a Russian Article 5 test in a possible 2027 window.

The trade. Eight names, chosen for growth room over the mega-cap primes.

1. **LHX** — Aerojet Rocketdyne solid rocket motors for PAC-3 MSE and THAAD. The deliberate size exception, at about $44 billion. A Missile Solutions spinoff is planned for mid 2027.
2. **MRCY** — defense microelectronics and signal processing. Record Q4 bookings, backlog over $1.9 billion.
3. **MP** — rare-earth magnets and NdPr. Rubio named "minerals"; a reported executive order sets a start of 2027 deadline to remove non-allied critical minerals.
4. **AVAV** — Switchblade loitering munitions, the largest pure play. Funded backlog $1.5 billion at Q1 FY2027.
5. **KTOS** — Valkyrie collaborative combat aircraft, airframe partner on the Northrop CCA team.
6. **RCAT** — Black Widow, winner of the Army Small Reconnaissance Requirement. Acquisition objective 5,880 systems.
7. **ONDS** — counter-UAS roll-up, eight acquisitions, Palantir command-system partnership.
8. **UMAC** — NDAA-compliant drone components, near breakeven.

Structure: hold AVAV, KTOS, LHX, MRCY and MP for years, lever with January 2028 or January 2029 LEAPS. Treat RCAT, ONDS and UMAC as speculative equity.

Confidence tags: budget figures and the depletion data are `[TENTATIVE]`. The 2027 escalation window is a scenario, not a base-case date. Framework agreements are production goals, not funded orders. A request is not an appropriation. An appropriation is not an obligation. An obligation is not revenue.

### Falsifiers

- A durable Iran ceasefire holds and the naval blockade lifts.
- The FY2027 request is cut in conference, or the Iran supplemental dies.
- The inventory dispute resolves low, and the true stockpile is higher.
- Valuation runs ahead of earnings, or dilution eats the revenue line.
