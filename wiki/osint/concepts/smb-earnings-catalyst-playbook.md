## Narrative

SMB **earnings catalyst play** = swing from day-one reaction (days to months hold) when report + tape + theme align. Catalyst is highest-weight check; not sufficient alone.

### Three core buckets (+ exception) [TENTATIVE]

| Bucket | Definition | Typical reaction |
|--------|------------|------------------|
| **1. First clean profitable beat** | First quarter proving business model profitability | Strong day-one; not full inflection unless sustained |
| **2. Acceleration / inflection quarter** | Episodic pivot — huge EPS/revenue acceleration after bucket 1 | Major re-rating candidate (Qualmaggie-style) |
| **3. Strong company still crushing** | Post-inflection; still great beats but lower rate-of-change vs inflection | Continuation, smaller relative surprise |
| **4. Theme exception** | Pre-profit name in **hot running theme** when street rewards whole group; high SI / smaller float squeeze potential | AAOI-style — doesn't fit bucket 2 strictly but theme + short squeeze |

### Non-catalyst checks (grading) [TENTATIVE]

Theme alignment, overnight/pre-market volume, HTF breakout vs overhead resistance, AH/PM range holding, open-volume surge (2+ σ bars), day-one price confirmation.

### Theme sequencing (4th amendment core)

Track **earnings order within theme** — first reporter sets street re-rating narrative for peers. Example: fiber optics LITE (inflection) → COHR → AAOI (exception bucket) → CIEN. Use earlier reports in sequence as **leading indicator** for later names [TENTATIVE].
