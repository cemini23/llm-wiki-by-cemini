## Raw Concept

SIA (Hexo Labs) merges harness-update meta-agents and test-time weight training into one Feedback-Agent loop — addressing the bottleneck that humans still write/tune both models and scaffolds.

Long-horizon alignment with Sakana conductor-RL and AHE harness evolution papers — watch for reproducible open implementations before adoption.
