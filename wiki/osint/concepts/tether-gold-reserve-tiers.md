What this page answers: is Tether's gold one pool, and does it back USDT the same way XAU₮ is backed? Single-source 2026-09 forensic. `[TENTATIVE]` pending CRR/KPMG re-read.

## Narrative

**Three tiers, three legal objects.** Do not add them into one "Tether gold" headline.

| Tier | What it is | Who has title | In USDT reserves? |
|------|------------|---------------|-------------------|
| 1 | XAU₮ allocated LBMA bars (TG Commodities / El Salvador DAIL) | Token holders, bankruptcy-remote | No |
| 2 | Precious metals in general CRR ("Precious Metals") | Tether entities; unencumbered collateral | Yes |
| 3 | Surplus / SICAF / Gold.com equity | Corporate, no token refund liability | No |

Forensic implied stock: consolidated **146.13 t** by Q2 2026; XAU₮ float from ~7.67 t (early 2025) to ~16.17 t (mid-2026). 2025 general-reserve add **63.41 t**. Rank-among-sovereigns and "10.57% of OTC institutional demand" stay `[TENTATIVE]`.

Yield engine (non-yielding USDT liabilities vs T-bill / reverse-repo) funds the hard-asset pivot. Swiss vaulting is framed as OFAC/IEEPA insulation vs Cantor-custodied Treasuries. That is management rationale in the source, not a proof that a freeze is impossible.

**Conflicts:** MiCA excludes physical commodities from EMT/ART reserves. GENIUS Act transition language is the US onshore story in this file. Gold in a par-liability reserve marks to market — the source's own $6.814B → $4.11B buffer shrink is the live example.

### Falsifiers

- Tether sells physical bars to cover opex or dividends when policy rates fall.
- Later attestations replace allocated bars with unallocated / ETF / futures.
- Onshore access is bought by liquidating the metals sleeve.
