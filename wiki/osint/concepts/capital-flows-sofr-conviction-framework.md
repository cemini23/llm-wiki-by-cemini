## Narrative

### SOFR curve as anchor

- Start allocation reads from the **SOFR curve** (price of money), not narrative alone.
- Pair with Macro Weather GREEN/YELLOW/RED as **complementary** context — SOFR slope/level informs rates path assumptions; Macro Weather gates portfolio Kelly fraction.

### Evidence-weighted conviction

| Principle | Operator rule |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Coherence** | Thesis must align across rates, FX, equities, vol — not isolated hot take |
| **Evidence count** | Log number of independent confirms supporting the view |
| **Anti-pattern** | **Block size-up** when journal shows balanced pro/con lists without cross-asset confirms |
| **Vol edge** | Edge from time-horizon mismatch vs market — document hold horizon vs catalyst calendar |
