### Local / state oversight friction (2026)

**San Diego County** — first CA county to invoke **2024 state law** authorizing county public-health inspections of private immigration detention facilities `[CONFIRMED]`.

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **Feb 2026** | Supervisors Terra Lawson-Remer + Paloma Aguirre denied full inspection access; health officer partial access only |
| **Mar 10, 2026** | *County of San Diego v. DHS* filed — **3:26-cv-01520** (S.D. Cal.); defendants include DHS, ICE, **CoreCivic** |
| **Jun 4, 2026** | Judge James Simmons Jr. orders inspection by **Jun 17, 2026**; up to four health experts (not supervisors) |
| **Jun 26, 2026** | County completes **~8-hour** inspection; formal report expected **Jul 2026** `[CONFIRMED media]` |
| **Jul 2, 2026** | **Federal purchase closes** — six days after inspection |

### Turnkey / post-sale posture

WaPo draft **two-site CoreCivic California list** validated: Otay + California City `[CONFIRMED]`. **Higher $/bed** than Cal City on Jul 2026 close (**~$371k** vs **~$286k**) `[CONFIRMED media/8-K]`. Sale closed amid **active county litigation** and **pending county inspection report** — supports thesis that ICE pays premium for **blue-state, inspection-exposed** sites while retaining operator continuity.
