## Raw Concept

Created from the 2026-11-10 wave. Two newsletters in the same batch use CoreWeave as the reference name for AI-infrastructure credit stress. The purpose of this page is to hold the numbers, not the opinion.

## Narrative

### What the company is

A GPU cloud provider. It rents compute capacity, mostly for AI training and inference, and finances the hardware with debt. Its economics therefore combine a fast-depreciating asset with a fixed-rate liability.

### Reported figures

| Metric | Value | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $2.575bn, +112% y/y | Q2 2026 |
| Backlog | about $104bn | Q2 2026 |
| Net loss | $626m | Q2 2026 |
| Net interest expense | $640m | Q2 2026 |

[Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-09-wublockchain-weekly-outlook-us-cpi-and-ppi-test.md]

The interest expense is about **25% of revenue**. The net loss is larger than the interest expense, so operating cash generation did not cover the financing cost either.

### The bear case

Capital Flows calls CoreWeave "the highest-risk stock with the greatest sensitivity to the credit risk in AI" and reports it **down 77% from its high**. The same post argues that capex and demand do not pass through to profit, and cites a divergence between H100 rental prices and token prices as evidence. [Source: substack-rss-capital-flows-2026-11-05-market-status-market-bottom-or-beginning-of-the.md]

Both are `[TENTATIVE]`. The 77% figure is a single-source newsletter claim with no date.

### The bull counter

The backlog is the bull case: $104bn of contracted future revenue. The bear answer is that backlog conversion needs capacity delivery, and delivery needs more capital, which raises the interest line further.

`[OPEN QUESTION 2026-11-10]` — what share of the $104bn backlog is cancellable, and what is the average contract term?

### Ratios to watch

1. **Quarterly net interest expense / quarterly revenue.** Currently about 25%.
2. **Backlog conversion rate.** Revenue recognised against backlog added.
3. **Capacity utilisation** on delivered GPUs.

The equity price alone is a poor gauge. The credit line moves first, and the equity is a levered claim on the same asset.

### Relevance to the workspace

- **GuruWatcher** — a level watch on CoreWeave debt and on its earnings date, not on the stock.
- **Capital Flows thesis** — CoreWeave is the named transmission channel from AI capex to macro liquidity.
