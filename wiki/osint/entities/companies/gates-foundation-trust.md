## Raw Concept

Page prompted by the Q1 2026 13F ingest (2026-05-15). The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation Trust holds the endowment that funds the foundation's grant-making; its public-equity book has long been managed in association with Bill Gates' investment vehicle (Cascade Investment, historically run by Michael Larson). [Source: bill gates holdings.jpeg]

## Narrative

A low-turnover endowment book anchored by **Berkshire Hathaway** (26%, the legacy of Buffett's gifts to the foundation) and **Waste Management** (20%). Heavy in industrials and railroads (CNI, CAT, DE) and consumer staples (WMT, KOF, HRL). Q1 2026 trimmed the two anchors modestly — BRK.B −12%, WM −4.5%.
