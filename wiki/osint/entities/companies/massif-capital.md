### Investment philosophy (as expressed in 4Q2025 letter)

- **Wicked problems framework**: macro/financial systems exhibit characteristics of problems whose parameters shift in response to attempted solutions
- **Assumption triage**: in transitional regimes, returns accrue less to forecasting precision than to identifying which beliefs were conditional artifacts of the post-GFC accommodating environment
- **Rising real-rate regime**: durable rather than transitory; alters arithmetic of risk/return; duration may amplify drawdowns rather than cushion them
- **Capital cycle theory at sector level**: tech investors are not historically well-versed in capex-heavy businesses; harvesting stage > spending stage on classical capital cycle reasoning
- **Energy = addition, not transition**: prevailing demand-destruction narrative discounts decline rates as binding supply constraint
- **Materials sector**: Chinese demand policy-managed (not cyclical), regional premiums becoming common, cost curves fragmenting
