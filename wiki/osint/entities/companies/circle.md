## Narrative

### The business

Circle issues USDC, a dollar stablecoin. Revenue is mostly **reserve income**: interest earned on the cash and Treasury holdings backing the tokens. The business is therefore a spread business whose margin moves with short rates.

### Q3 2026 reported figures

| Metric | Value | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue and reserve income | $865m | +17% y/y |
| Net income, continuing operations | $226m | +5% y/y |
| Average USDC circulation | $73.3bn | +8% y/y |
| Restructuring charges | $9.145m | Q3 |
| Workforce reduction | about 5% | Q3 |

Other income included **$182m** of proceeds from an ARC token pre-sale. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-06-wublockchain-weekly-democratic-win-triggers-mark.md]

### The observation worth keeping

Revenue grew 17% and net income grew 5%. The gap is cost growth plus the mix effect of the pre-sale income. Circulation grew only 8%, so revenue per dollar of circulation rose. That is consistent with a higher reserve yield, which is rate-dependent rather than volume-dependent.

`[OPEN QUESTION 2026-11-10]` — what share of the 17% revenue growth came from reserve yield rather than circulation? The source does not split it.

### Corporate actions

- Genesis minting of **10bn ARC** tokens is complete.
- A definitive agreement to acquire the cross-border payments platform **Tazapay** is signed. The deal is expected to close in 2027, subject to approvals.

### Why it matters here

Circle is the cleanest listed read on stablecoin float. If a strategy depends on stablecoin supply or on short-rate carry in crypto, Circle's circulation and reserve yield are the observable inputs.

### Competitive map, November 2026

Two developments change the field around Circle.

**Project Faro.** Twenty-one financial institutions are building a bank-run stablecoin. Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Santander and Deutsche Bank committed about **$270m** to fund five years of operations. Wells Fargo, Fidelity, UBS and Lloyds are also participants. The plan is a US issuer and a dollar token in the first half of 2027, then other currencies. The project has no chief executive and no final name.

**Coinbase trust bank charter.** Coinbase received final OCC approval for a national trust bank charter. It can expand custody and payments, and may issue its own stablecoin or administer issuance for others. Coinbase is a distribution partner. A charter turns it into a potential competitor.

**Share context.** USDT and USDC together are roughly **85%** of the global stablecoin market.

The competitive threat from the banks is distribution and compliance, not product. The threat from Coinbase is that it already holds both.
