## Narrative

**Muddy Waters Research** is an activist short-seller founded in **2010** by **Carson Block**. It publishes due-diligence reports and runs a hedge fund. It became known for exposing accounting fraud at US-listed Chinese companies (Sino-Forest, 2011).

**Method.** Forensic accounting plus field investigation. Reports often turn on a specific verifiable practice — a financing structure, a licence, a compliance failure. Block presents findings on stage at Sohn conferences.

**Stated positioning (2026).** Block says he avoids mega-caps — he called shorting Nvidia "career suicide." He says he avoids crowded shorts. He runs a **mining short book**, shorting producing miners paired against junior miners of the same metal, and generally avoids shorting non-producing mines. His macro theme through 2026 is that **AI disruption** creates new short opportunities among "AI pretenders and wannabes."
