## Raw Concept

Created from the 2026-11-10 wave. Two Event Horizon issues carry Q3 figures and the market reaction. The page exists because DraftKings is the first listed incumbent to build a full prediction-market stack.

## Narrative

### The structure

DraftKings runs **brokerage, exchange, and market maker** for its Predictions product on DKeX. That vertical integration is the stated differentiator: it controls the product roadmap and keeps the economics across the stack. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-06-cftc-to-prediction-markets-stop-blocking-states.md]

### Q3 2026 figures

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| New Predictions customers in the quarter | more than 1m |
| Predictions customers year to date | more than 1.6m |
| October annualised consumer volume | $10.1bn, up nearly 3x from July |
| Annualised total notional volume | $56.8bn, up over 5x from July |
| DKeX take rate, Sep and Oct | 11.1% |
| Share of take kept by DraftKings | about two thirds |
| 2026 Predictions investment | raised to $650–700m |
| Expected 2026 cohort payback | about 20 months |
| Combos share of consumer volume | 27% in October |
| Customers trading combos | more than 70% |
| Non-sports share of customers | nearly 20% |

[Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-06-cftc-to-prediction-markets-stop-blocking-states.md]

The company calls itself the clear number three Predictions exchange by total volume, behind Kalshi and Polymarket. The newsletter notes it still trails Robinhood among all prediction-market operators. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-06-cftc-to-prediction-markets-stop-blocking-states.md]

### The market reaction

The stock fell more than **20%** after hours on the Q3 print. The company missed on adjusted EBITDA and lowered 2026 guidance. Three banks — JPMorgan, UBS, and Stifel — downgraded the stock over the near-$1bn prediction-market spend. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-10-the-moat-for-prediction-markets-is-trading-not-p.md]

### The disclosure worth reading twice

The company says that even if sports contracts ended in July 2028, the 2026 cohort would be paid back before that date. That is a contingency statement, and it tells you the company sees a real legal risk to the sports product. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-06-cftc-to-prediction-markets-stop-blocking-states.md]

### Why it matters here

DraftKings is the clean read on **prediction-market unit economics at scale**:

- take rate 11.1% across brokerage, exchange, and market making;
- a 20-month cohort payback;
- combos at 27% of volume, which is a parlay-shaped product inside an exchange.

If the trade-out moat thesis is right, a parlay-heavy mix is a weaker position. If the thesis is wrong, the combo mix is the growth engine.

## Dead Ends

- **Reading consumer volume and notional volume as the same measure.** Consumer volume is $10.1bn annualised; total notional is $56.8bn. The gap is market-making flow.
- **Treating the sports-contract contingency as boilerplate.** It gives a hard date and a payback test.
