## Raw Concept

Page prompted by the Q1 2026 13F ingest (2026-05-15). Fairfax Financial is the Canadian insurance-holding company led by **Prem Watsa**, often called "Canada's Warren Buffett" for its insurance-float-funded value-investing model. [Source: watsa holdings.jpeg]

## Narrative

A contrarian, deep-value book. The top position is **Orla Mining** (ORLA, 25.8%) — a gold miner — and the book carries the long-running **BlackBerry** (BB) stake (Watsa has chaired BlackBerry's board). Heavy in turnaround/cyclical names: Under Armour (held on both share classes; UA CL C +133%), Cleveland-Cliffs, Kennedy-Wilson. Note this 13F covers only US-listed holdings — Fairfax's larger Canadian and international book is not visible here.
