### The stated strategy

The CEO described an "Independence Stack": energy, Bitcoin mining, peer-to-peer communications, self-custody wallets, and local AI, all open source. The stated goal is that the open-source technology keeps running even if Tether ceases to exist. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-06-wublockchain-weekly-democratic-win-triggers-mark.md]

That is a governance claim, not a financial one. It affects the recovery value of USDT in a tail scenario.

### Why it matters here

Tether is the largest single holder of short US paper among crypto issuers. Its Treasury holdings are a direct link between crypto liquidity and the front end of the curve. When Tether's float grows, dollar funding demand from that channel grows with it.

### The ratio to watch

Excess reserves against total liabilities. A fall in that ratio, or a rise in the repo share of holdings, changes the liquidity profile without changing the headline number.
