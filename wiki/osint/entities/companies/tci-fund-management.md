## Narrative

The most concentrated 13F in this batch — **ten positions**, with the top four (GE Aerospace, Visa, Moody's, S&P Global) carrying ~77% of the book. The thesis is durable: aerospace duopoly economics plus the "toll-bridge" businesses of payment networks and ratings/index oligopolies. Q1 2026 added to the quality core (V +10%, MCO +8%, SPGI +19%) and cut Microsoft 84%.
