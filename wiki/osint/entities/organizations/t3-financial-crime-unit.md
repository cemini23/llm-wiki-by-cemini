## Narrative

The **T3 Financial Crime Unit** is a public-private partnership between **Tether** (USDT issuer), **TRON** (blockchain), and **TRM Labs** (blockchain-analytics firm) that cooperatively freezes illicit stablecoin assets in coordination with law enforcement. As of May 2026 it reported having frozen **>$450M** in illicit crypto across **23 jurisdictions**, with 2025 interceptions up ~44% year-over-year.

It is a notable institutional model for OSINT/threat-actor-finance purposes: the token issuer, the chain, and an analytics provider acting jointly gives stablecoin assets a centralized freeze capability that pure-crypto ideology would not predict. [Source: see related source page]
