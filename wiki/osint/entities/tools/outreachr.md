## Narrative

Automates **lead discovery, enrichment, and personalised cold-email sequences** for B2B acquisition.

### Why it is only Watch

Cold outreach to **jure sanguinis** law firms is a plausible Atto Concierge channel. But execution is **blocked on infrastructure, not code**: domain warmup, SPF/DKIM/DMARC setup, and deliverability management to protect the primary brand domain. **Not an engineering problem — an email-reputation prerequisite.**

### Boundary

High deliverability risk and **CAN-SPAM / GDPR exposure** if unvetted commercial email crosses international borders. Warm the domain and set up the compliance hooks first. Route: Atto + SEO wiki.
