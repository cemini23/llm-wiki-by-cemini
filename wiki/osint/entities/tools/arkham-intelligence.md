## Narrative

**Arkham Intelligence** is a blockchain-analytics platform that attributes on-chain wallet activity to real-world entities — making public blockchain data usable for investigation: wallet tracking, asset-flow tracing, transaction monitoring. In May 2026 it was reported tracing **$344M** held in Iran-linked TRON wallets, an example of on-chain analytics applied to geopolitical-finance / sanctions-evasion investigation.

OSINT relevance: Arkham is a primary-tier crypto-tracing tool for threat-actor financial profiling — turning the public ledger into an attribution surface. Sits alongside TRM Labs and Chainalysis in the blockchain-analytics category. [Source: see related source page]
