## Narrative

Educational climate data visualization platform from the Climate Change Institute. Aggregates output from 12+ global + regional reanalysis frameworks. Time-series, global overlays, regional temperature anomaly maps.

### Integration vector

Reference-only — manual web browsing for macroeconomic climate trend verification. No programmatic endpoints; cannot integrate into automated software.

### Risks

Manual visual interpretation only; not scalable for quant model inputs.
