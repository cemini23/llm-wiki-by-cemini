### Why no-license matters

Per `@concepts/cemini-license-posture.md`, no-license = no rights granted = legally indistinguishable from copyright infringement on first commit. Awesome-lists with no license are technically problematic to even maintain as forks — the curator owns the curation, and absent explicit grant, redistribution rights are not implied.

For **read-only browsing**, this is fine. For any other use, it isn't.

### Cemini relevance

Use the list as a periodic browsing source when seeding new strategy ideas. When a candidate emerges (e.g., a specific RL-for-trading repo, a momentum factor implementation, a backtesting framework), audit it independently.
