## Raw Concept

Free “One-Step” utilities by Stephen P. Morse translating street addresses + cross-streets into federal census **Enumeration Districts**. Unified finder: [stevemorse.org/census/unified.html](https://stevemorse.org/census/unified.html).

## Narrative

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Role** | Skip citywide census name-search noise when a known tenement address exists |
| **Fit** | Williamsburg / Jersey City Italian-American clusters with street + year |
| **Verdict** | **Use** — free, well-established; still requires reading the ED images |
| **Not for** | Proving Italian *comune* alone (birthplace columns help but need vitals) |

Workflow: State/county/city + primary street + cross streets → ED list → browse NARA/Ancestry/FS images for household ages, birthplaces, immigration year.
