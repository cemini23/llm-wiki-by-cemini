## Raw Concept

Free provincial baptism index for Trentino covering **1815–1923**, maintained with Archivio Diocesano Tridentino / MondoTrentino portals. Entry point: [natitrentino.mondotrentino.net](https://www.natitrentino.mondotrentino.net/).

## Narrative

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Role** | Lock immigrant-generation Trentino births to exact parish before ordering diocesan certificates |
| **Fit** | Odorizzi / Menapace / Dalpiaz-class Val di Non hypotheses |
| **Verdict** | **Watch / use** — free; spelling-sensitive; confirm hit against US long-form ages/birthplaces |
| **Not for** | Pre-1815 feudal claims; noble-origin stories without parish chain |

Query pattern from K154: surname + father’s given name (e.g. Odorizzi + Ottone) for ~1907 cohort. A hit yields parish (Tassullo, Sanzenone, etc.) and unlocks diocesan / FamilySearch Austrian Matrikel follow-ups.
