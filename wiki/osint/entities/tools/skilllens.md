## Raw Concept

arXiv 2608.10775 (Peking Univ / ByteDance / SJTU). Visual Skill Cards bind reusable GUI procedures with applicability cues, visual evidence, verification signals; CardDistill distills to a student acting without runtime retrieval. No public repo located at Phase-0 check.

## Narrative

**Verdict: WATCH / pattern-only.** VSC is the *visual* sibling of the text agent-skill family (SKILLER, K233). Potential Atto angle: distilled student for Antenati/portal GUI cassettes — but no artifact to adopt and OCR/HTR constraints still bind. Keep on the CCC skill-representation watch list.
