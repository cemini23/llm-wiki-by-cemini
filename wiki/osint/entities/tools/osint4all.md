## Raw Concept

Public OSINT bookmark board used as a locator catalog for people, maps, archives, and privacy tools. Not software to vendor.

## Narrative

Treat OSINT4ALL as an index. High-value tools that already have entity pages (CyberChef, IntelTechniques, sherlock) stay on those pages. Everything else is a KEEP/DEAD/CONTEXT row on a section catalog. Nested SOCMINT and GEOINT boards are follow-on hubs, not automatic mass scrapes.
