Maps directly to the Atto roadmap for dynamic, streamable lineage deliverables from strictly-gated GEDCOM/truth data — a new visual-asset vector for the concierge service and $8–20/mo access-share upgrades.

**Constraints:** PII handled during generation must stay strictly inside the Atto secure share perimeter; human-gate truth unchanged; Magika + PII strip unchanged. Cross-wiki: image-gen (SVG streaming pipelines). Do not vendor into Atto SaaS without GO.
