## Narrative

**Atlassian (TEAM)** — Strong Q1 2026 earnings beat drove a structural re-rating, with the stock surging +30% and approaching a first target price of $99. The thesis centers on TEAM's **Rovo product** (AI platform) growing +20% month-over-month via usage-based pricing through AI credits, which drives both top-line growth and profitability by enabling enterprise upsell.

The company was considered too cheap when trading at ~2-3x EV/Rev, given high-20s revenue growth and ~30% free cash flow margins (Rule of 50+). The stock has reportedly turned the corner and is expected to continue appreciating. Stop-loss moved from $55 to $69; trimming toward $99 TP.
