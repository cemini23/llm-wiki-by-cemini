## Equity Research Report: Strategy Inc. (MSTR)

### 1. Business Overview

Strategy Inc. (NASDAQ: MSTR), formerly known as MicroStrategy Incorporated, operates an unprecedented, dual-engine corporate structure that effectively functions as two distinct entities housed under a single ticker. While the firm maintains its foundational identity as an enterprise business intelligence (BI) and analytics software provider, the overwhelming majority of its enterprise value—exceeding 99%—is derived from its position as the world’s first and largest "Bitcoin Treasury Company".1 This bifurcation demands an analytical framework that independently evaluates the cash-generating software division and the capital-intensive, leveraged digital asset treasury, while recognizing the symbiotic, albeit asymmetrical, relationship between the two.

#### Core Business Operations and Revenue Segments

The legacy software division, operating under the "Strategy ONE" platform nomenclature, engineers AI-powered enterprise analytics, contextual insights, data governance protocols, and mobility software solutions for global enterprises.2 The operational architecture of this division is currently undergoing a structural transformation from legacy on-premise perpetual product licenses toward cloud-based subscription services.

For the fiscal year 2024, the enterprise analytics segment generated approximately $477.2 million in total operating revenue.3 The revenue base is highly diversified geographically, anchored by high-compliance developed markets. As of late 2024, North America accounted for 54.4% of total revenue ($259.6 million), while the EMEA region contributed 32.7% ($155.9 million), and other international regions comprised the remaining 10% ($48.0 million).5

The strategic pivot toward cloud-based architectures is evidenced by the robust acceleration in subscription services revenue. In the third quarter of 2025, subscription services surged by 65.4% year-over-year to $46.0 million, representing roughly 36% of total quarterly revenues.6 This transition to a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) architecture is critical; replacing lumpy, one-time license acquisitions with predictable, high-visibility recurring revenue streams insulates the software division against macroeconomic fluctuations. By the fourth quarter of 2025, total software revenue reached $123.0 million, representing a modest 1.9% year-over-year increase, driven by a 62.1% increase in subscription revenues ($51.8 million) that successfully outpaced a 16.9% decline in legacy product support revenues ($48.5 million).7

#### Treasury Operations and Unit Economics

While the software division provides the operational shell, SEC compliance framework, and baseline cash flows, Strategy Inc. is overwhelmingly valued by institutional markets for its treasury operations. The firm aggressively accumulates Bitcoin (BTC) utilizing cash flows from operations, at-the-market (ATM) equity offerings, convertible debt issuances, and, more recently, digital credit preferred stock sales.1 As of April 2026, Strategy Inc. had accumulated 815,061 BTC at an average cost basis of approximately $75,527 per bitcoin, representing a total cumulative capital deployment of $61.56 billion.9

The unit economics of this dual model present a stark dichotomy. The software division operates with highly robust gross margins, historically ranging between 66% and 74% (e.g., 66.1% in Q4 2025 and 70.5% in Q3 2025).7 However, the software business is aggressively reinvesting in its cloud pivot and did not generate positive cash flow from operations in 2024.12

Consequently, traditional unit economic analysis is inadequate for evaluating the firm's aggregate performance. To quantify the efficacy of the treasury, management relies on a proprietary performance indicator designated as "BTC Yield." This metric measures the percentage change in the ratio of Bitcoin holdings to assumed diluted shares outstanding.13 The underlying mechanism of the firm's unit economics relies on the equity's Net Asset Value (NAV) premium. By issuing shares at a market premium relative to the spot price of Bitcoin, the firm deploys that capital to purchase additional Bitcoin. This mathematically increases the amount of Bitcoin backing each outstanding share, despite absolute shareholder dilution. In fiscal 2025, the company achieved an extraordinary full-year BTC Yield of 22.8% 7, and by early 2026, the year-to-date yield had already accelerated to 13.7% 15, confirming the functionality of this highly unorthodox accretion mechanism.

#### Key Products and Market Positioning

The Strategy ONE platform integrates deeply with hyperscaler cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP) to deliver governed enterprise analytics.16 The platform's defining differentiator is its proprietary semantic metadata layer, which acts as a centralized governance nexus, ensuring that highly regulated entities—such as global financial institutions, healthcare providers, and federal government agencies—maintain strict data fidelity across disparate databases.2

Concurrently, the firm's market positioning has been intentionally rebranded. In 2024 and 2025, the company dropped the "Micro" from its primary operating identity to become Strategy Inc., explicitly positioning itself as the "world’s first Bitcoin Treasury Company" and a pioneer in the burgeoning "Digital Credit" market, aiming to bridge traditional fixed-income capital markets with decentralized digital assets.7

### 2. Competitive Landscape

#### The Enterprise Analytics and BI Market

In the global enterprise business intelligence and analytics sector—a market estimated to be worth $30 to $35 billion as of 2024—Strategy Inc. commands a minor, albeit deeply entrenched, market share of approximately 1.22%, positioning it 14th overall.16

The competitive landscape is aggressively dominated by hyperscale cloud infrastructure providers and heavily capitalized specialized analytics platforms. Microsoft’s Power BI is the undisputed market leader, holding a 19.91% market share, followed closely by Salesforce’s Tableau at 16.36%.18 Additional primary competitors include SAP, IBM, Oracle, Qlik, and emerging AI-native upstarts like Looker (Google) and Domo.16

Durable vs. Contested Moats in Software: The firm's software moat is highly contested. Microsoft and Salesforce utilize "freemium" and land-and-expand strategies, bundling BI capabilities deeply into their ubiquitous enterprise licensing agreements (e.g., Office 365, CRM suites). Strategy Inc. lacks this ecosystem leverage. Consequently, its moat relies entirely on product superiority in niche, high-compliance environments. The firm targets global enterprises where data governance, strict semantic layer control, and high-security mobile delivery are paramount over basic data visualization.16 While this results in higher average deal sizes and lower churn among legacy enterprise accounts, it structurally prevents the company from capturing the explosive, high-volume growth seen by its hyperscale rivals.

#### The Digital Asset Proxy Market

Strategy Inc.'s true competitive arena is the institutional digital asset exposure market. Following the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission’s approval of spot Bitcoin Exchange-Traded Funds (ETFs) in early 2024, Strategy Inc. lost its monopoly as the sole liquid avenue for public market investors to gain regulated, proxy exposure to Bitcoin.19

Primary Competitors in Digital Assets:

Spot Bitcoin ETFs: BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT), Fidelity Wise Origin Bitcoin Fund (FBTC), and others offer pure-play, frictionless spot exposure with exceptionally low management fees.

Public Bitcoin Miners: Companies like Riot Platforms and Marathon Digital provide operational leverage to the Bitcoin network.

Durable Moats in Digital Assets: Despite the proliferation of ETFs, Strategy Inc. maintains a highly durable, structurally differentiated moat. Unlike an ETF, which is legally constrained to holding spot assets and charging a fee (resulting in slow equity decay), Strategy Inc. is a corporate operating entity capable of utilizing intelligent leverage.13 The firm issues convertible debt, senior secured notes, and perpetual preferred equity to finance Bitcoin acquisitions.1 This functionally provides equity shareholders with convexity and leveraged exposure to Bitcoin's upside without the counterparty risks or margin calls associated with retail derivatives or leveraged ETFs.19

Furthermore, compared to Bitcoin miners, Strategy Inc. possesses an inherently superior risk profile. Miners face relentless capital expenditure requirements, hardware depreciation, surging energy costs, and the quadrennial "halving" of block rewards which compresses margins.19 Strategy Inc. avoids operational network risks entirely by functioning purely as an accumulator and custodian of the asset.19

Threats from Technology Shifts and Substitutes: The primary threat to this moat is financial, rather than technological. The firm relies on its stock trading at a premium to its Net Asset Value (NAV). If the overwhelming convenience and low cost of spot ETFs permanently siphon institutional capital away from Strategy's equity, the NAV premium could collapse. Without this premium, the firm’s ability to accretively issue equity to drive "BTC Yield" terminates, rendering the operational model stagnant.20 Additionally, theoretical technological threats, such as advancements in quantum computing capable of breaking SHA-256 cryptography, present long-tail existential risks to the entire business model, though sell-side analysts note the industry is actively developing quantum-resistant protocol upgrades.22

#### Capital Allocation

The firm's capital allocation philosophy is singular and absolute: the issuance of fiat-denominated debt and equity to accumulate Bitcoin. Strategy Inc. does not engage in mergers and acquisitions (M&A), does not pay dividends on its common stock, and does not conduct traditional stock buybacks.1

In 2025, the firm’s capital allocation scaled to historic proportions. Strategy Inc. raised a total of $25.3 billion, making it the largest equity issuer among all U.S. public companies for the second consecutive year.1 Furthermore, the company pioneered a new asset class of "Digital Credit" by launching five Initial Public Offerings (IPOs) of perpetual preferred stock, raising $5.5 billion in gross proceeds.7 The flagship instrument, the 8.00% Series A Perpetual Strike Preferred Stock (ticker: STRC), scaled to an aggregate stated amount of $3.4 billion, featuring a variable dividend mechanism currently set at 11.25%.7

#### Balance Sheet Health and Liquidity

The primary critique of highly leveraged holding companies is the threat of margin calls or debt maturity walls forcing asset liquidation at cyclical bottoms. Strategy Inc. has engineered its balance sheet to severely mitigate these risks.

Liquidity: The firm does not rely on software cash flows to service its massive debt load. Instead, management proactively established a dedicated $2.25 billion USD reserve in late 2025.2 This cash and cash equivalent reserve provides approximately 2.5 years (30 months) of unencumbered liquidity to fulfill dividend distributions for preferred stock and interest payments on outstanding convertible debt, completely insulating the Bitcoin treasury from forced liquidations during near-term market drawdowns.2

Debt Maturity Wall: The firm holds approximately $8.2 billion in senior convertible notes.1 These notes lack traditional financial maintenance covenants and are deeply subordinated. The maturity ladder is intentionally structured to provide a long-duration runway:

The structure of these convertibles dictates that they convert to equity at specified high-premium prices rather than requiring cash repayment. Because the firm’s average Bitcoin cost basis is roughly $75,527 10, management estimates that Bitcoin would have to decline by approximately 88%—to around $8,000 per coin—for the asset value to breach the debt threshold.40

### 4. Recent Events (Last 12 Months)

#### Extreme Earnings Volatility vs. Consensus

Strategy Inc. experienced historic earnings volatility throughout the trailing twelve months, culminating in a highly publicized Q4 2025 earnings release on February 5, 2026. The firm reported a GAAP EPS of -$42.93, catastrophically missing the Wall Street consensus estimate of $2.97—a negative surprise magnitude of 1,545%.29 This resulted in an unprecedented operating loss of $17.4 billion for the quarter.29 However, the entirety of this loss was attributable to a non-cash mark-to-market depreciation of its digital asset portfolio under ASU 2023-08 as spot Bitcoin prices retraced from late 2025 highs. Operationally, the core software division actually beat expectations, delivering $123.0 million in revenue against a $118.81 million forecast.28

Earlier in the year, the firm experienced similar accounting-driven surprises in the opposite direction. For example, in Q2 2025, the firm posted a massive EPS beat of $32.60 per share, driven by a $10.0 billion non-cash net income influx as Bitcoin prices surged during the quarter.25

#### Strategic Shifts, Financing, and Rebranding

Record Bitcoin Accumulation: Between mid-April 2026 and prior months, the firm executed the most aggressive accumulation phase in its history. In a single week ending April 19, 2026, Strategy Inc. acquired 34,164 BTC for $2.54 billion, funded by massive at-the-market equity and STRC preferred stock sales.9 This purchase vaulted the firm past BlackRock’s IBIT ETF, cementing Strategy Inc. as the largest single institutional holder of Bitcoin globally.9

Stock Split and Rebranding: In August 2024, the board of directors executed a 10-for-1 forward stock split of both Class A and Class B common stock to increase accessibility for retail investors following significant share price appreciation.33 Concurrently, the firm initiated a strategic rebranding, dropping the "Micro" from its name in early 2025 to operate solely as "Strategy Inc." across all capital market functions.1 This linguistic pivot formalized the transition from a software vendor into an institutional digital capital markets platform.

#### Management Changes

The executive leadership team has remained highly stable through this period of aggressive expansion. The strategic bifurcation initiated in 2022 remains in place: Michael Saylor operates as Executive Chairman, focusing entirely on capital allocation, treasury strategy, and macroeconomic advocacy, while Phong Le serves as President and Chief Executive Officer, managing the cloud transition of the software division and overall corporate operations.41 Andrew Kang remains the Chief Financial Officer, architecting the complex convertible debt and preferred stock structures.8

#### Regulatory and Legal Developments

The most profound regulatory development impacting the firm was the mandated transition to fair-value accounting for digital assets (ASU 2023-08), which the company adopted early on January 1, 2025.12 Prior to this, accounting rules required digital assets to be treated as indefinite-lived intangible assets, necessitating impairment charges if the asset price fell, but strictly forbidding upward revisions if the price recovered until the asset was sold. The new fair-value standard allows Strategy Inc. to accurately reflect the spot market value of its $61 billion treasury on its balance sheet, drastically improving apparent book value while introducing extreme net income volatility.12

The Accretive Flywheel: By trading at a persistent premium to its Net Asset Value (NAV), Strategy Inc. can issue equity to acquire Bitcoin, mathematically increasing the underlying Bitcoin backing per share and driving positive BTC Yield.13

Exclusive Leveraged Exposure: For institutional capital governed by mandates that prohibit the use of margin or the direct purchase of spot digital assets, MSTR remains the preeminent liquid equity instrument offering convexity and structured leverage to Bitcoin upside.1

If institutional preference permanently shifts toward low-fee spot ETFs, MSTR's NAV premium could compress to 1.0x or lower, permanently stalling the accretion engine and rendering future equity raises dilutive.20

Liquidation Risk in a Prolonged Bear Market: If Bitcoin enters a multi-year secular bear market that outlasts the 30-month fiat reserve runway, the firm may be forced to halt dividends, restructure debt at punitive rates, or catastrophically liquidate Bitcoin reserves to satisfy creditors.9

Extreme Single-Asset Concentration: The entirety of the firm's $60 billion+ market capitalization is tethered to an unregulated, highly volatile digital commodity, exposing shareholders to outsized geopolitical, regulatory, and protocol-level security risks.31

### 8. Risks

#### Company-Specific Risks

The MSTR Premium Compression: The entire capital accumulation flywheel operates on the premise that MSTR equity trades at a premium to its Bitcoin NAV. If this premium compresses due to market saturation from competing ETFs, the firm cannot accretively issue ATM equity.20

Debt Service Drain: Strategy Inc. operates a massively leveraged balance sheet with $8.2 billion in convertible notes and billions in preferred equity. The core software business does not generate sufficient free cash flow to service the $35.1 million in annual contractual interest or the $1.48 billion in annual preferred dividends.2 The firm is entirely reliant on a $2.25 billion fiat reserve. If this reserve is exhausted during a multi-year crypto winter, the firm faces a dire liquidity crisis.9

Customer Concentration & Software Execution: While secondary to the treasury, the software division is critical for maintaining the firm's status as an operating company (avoiding classification as an Investment Company under the 1940 Act). Failure to successfully migrate legacy, high-revenue enterprise clients to the cloud SaaS platform could damage base cash flows and invite regulatory scrutiny over the firm's corporate structure.6

#### Sector Risks

Regulatory Aggression: The digital asset sector remains highly vulnerable to aggressive jurisdictional regulations. Draconian tax policies targeting unrealized gains, bans on self-custody, or stringent capital requirements for financial institutions interacting with digital assets could severely impair Bitcoin's spot price and institutional adoption.31

Protocol and Technology Shifts: Bitcoin operates on the SHA-256 cryptographic algorithm. The theoretical advancement of quantum computing poses an existential, long-tail risk to the network's security architecture.22 Furthermore, software bugs or catastrophic forks in the Bitcoin protocol would immediately impair Strategy Inc.'s entire balance sheet.

#### Macro Sensitivities

Interest Rates and the DXY: Strategy Inc. equity is highly sensitive and inversely correlated to global macroeconomic tightening. Elevated yields on the US 10-Year Treasury bond increase the opportunity cost of holding non-yielding assets like Bitcoin. Furthermore, higher sovereign yields strengthen the US Dollar Index (DXY), which structurally suppresses the nominal fiat price of global commodities, including Bitcoin.45

Cost of Capital: The firm's ability to roll over its 2028-2032 debt maturity wall depends heavily on favorable credit market conditions. A persistent "higher-for-longer" interest rate environment would drastically increase the cost of capital for future debt refinancing, fundamentally breaking the unit economics of the leveraged accumulation strategy.1

Commodity Exposure (Oil/Energy): Geopolitical tensions causing spikes in global energy prices (e.g., crude oil) traditionally dampen aggregate market risk appetite, triggering sell-offs in high-beta tech and crypto equities, causing MSTR to suffer outsized drawdowns during macro shocks.63

### 9. Valuation & Peer Context

Traditional equity valuation frameworks—such as Price-to-Earnings (P/E), Enterprise Value to EBITDA (EV/EBITDA), and Price-to-Sales (P/S)—experience total breakdown when applied to Strategy Inc.

Due to the adoption of ASU 2023-08 fair-value accounting, the firm's income statement is distorted by multi-billion dollar non-cash adjustments. Consequently, as of early 2026, the company's trailing twelve-month EV/EBITDA stood at a structurally distorted and meaningless -60.3x.64 Furthermore, because the market cap reflects the $61 billion Bitcoin treasury rather than the $477 million software business, the Price-to-Sales (P/S) ratio has bloated to roughly 101.0x.3 For peer context, legacy software competitors like Salesforce or Microsoft trade between 7x and 15x sales, rendering any traditional SaaS benchmarking exercise futile.

The NAV Premium Framework (mNAV):

The market values Strategy Inc. almost exclusively as a leveraged holding company, utilizing a Net Asset Value (NAV) framework. By dividing the firm's enterprise value by the spot value of its 815,061 Bitcoin holdings, analysts derive the "mNAV" multiple.

As of April 2026, MSTR traded at an mNAV of approximately 1.24x (a 24% premium to the spot value of its reserves).2 Historically over the past five years, this premium has functioned as a sentiment oscillator—collapsing to near 0.8x during severe crypto bear markets and expanding to north of 2.0x during aggressive retail-driven bull markets.

This sustained premium over spot ETF peers (like IBIT, which trades at exactly 1.0x NAV) is structurally justified by three factors:

Convexity via Leverage: Strategy Inc. is the only vehicle that utilizes debt and preferred equity to amplify Bitcoin exposure without subjecting the equity holder to margin calls.1

Yield Generation: The firm utilizes its massive treasury to generate yield (e.g., writing covered calls or lending), whereas ETFs are passive.19

No Expense Ratios: Strategy Inc. subsidizes its corporate overhead through its software operations, eliminating the perpetual management fee decay inherent to ETFs.19

Ultimately, Strategy Inc. trades as a high-beta, leveraged proxy for global fiat liquidity. Its valuation remains inextricably correlated to the depreciation of the U.S. Dollar, the trajectory of sovereign bond yields, and the sustained institutional financialization of digital capital. So long as Bitcoin remains structurally integrated into institutional portfolios, Strategy Inc. is uniquely positioned to monetize its first-mover advantage as the foundational architect of the corporate Bitcoin standard.
