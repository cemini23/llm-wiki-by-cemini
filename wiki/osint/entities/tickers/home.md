### Setup framing

Sub-penny micro-cap setups carry distinct risk characteristics from mid/large cap:

- **Liquidity**: typically thin; slippage on entry and especially exit can eat target
- **Tier**: OTC / pink-sheet level — distinct from listed exchange
- **Halt risk**: micro-caps halt frequently; positions can be locked through resolution
- **Float dynamics**: small float means small flow moves price disproportionately
