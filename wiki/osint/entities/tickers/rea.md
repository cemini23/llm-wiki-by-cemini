## Narrative

**Rare Earths Americas (NYSE American: REA)** — Completed its IPO and began trading in New York in May 2026. At a market cap of ~$365M at listing, the company offers a differentiated critical minerals investment opportunity with exposure to a multi-asset portfolio of heavy rare earth projects across the **United States** and **Brazil**.

### Asset Portfolio

| Asset | Location | Resource (SK-1300) | Grade |
|-------|----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Shiloh Project** | Georgia, US | Preliminary (boulder assays) | Up to 39.7% TREO; trenching intercepts to 30.98% TREO (~5x MP Materials' Mountain Pass at 6%) |
| **Constellation** | Minas Gerais, Brazil | Inferred 266.2Mt | 2,637 ppm TREO, 1.0% DyTb |
| **Alpha** | Bahia, Brazil | Inferred 201.7Mt | 1,520 ppm TREO, 2.2% DyTb |
| **Homer** | Goiás, Brazil | Carbonatite target (niobium-REE) | Early stage; adjacent to St George Mining (SGQ) Araxa project |

The Shiloh project's grade profile (5x higher than the only currently producing US rare earth mine) is the standout differentiator, though these are preliminary boulder samples, not SK-1300-compliant resources.

### Shareholder Register

Backed by **Hancock Prospecting** (Gina Rinehart), **Whitehaven Coal**, Todd Hannigan & Bernardo da Veiga (IperionX / Brazilian Rare Earths), and Taso Arima (IperionX). Board includes Keith Phillips (former CEO Piedmont Lithium) and Reta Jo Lewis (former Chair US Export-Import Bank).

### Positioning

- **Entry range**: $19–25 (listing price ~$19)
- **Target Price**: $35 (~90–100% upside from mid-range)
- **Catalysts**: SK-1300-compliant technical report at Shiloh; scoping study advancement at Alpha/Constellation; incremental US government rare earth funding
- **Risk**: Early-stage execution risk across all assets
