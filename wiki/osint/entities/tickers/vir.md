## Narrative

### Business overview

Vir Biotechnology is a clinical-stage biotech pivoting market identity from infectious disease (COVID legacy, HBV/HDV) toward **oncology TCEs** built on **PRO-XTEN** dual-masking (Amunix → Sanofi heritage). Lead equity story under test: **VIR-5500** (aka AMX-500), a PSMA×CD3 T-cell engager, partnered with **Astellas**. Secondary sleeve: **tobevibart + elebsiran** in Phase 3 **ECLIPSE** for chronic hepatitis delta.

### VIR-5500 clinical truth set (use this cutoff)

### VIR-5500 commercial economics (scenario deep dive)

**Widely prescribed?** Not like oral **Xtandi** (~$6B, broad lines). More like **Pluvicto-class specialty** (PSMA+ metastatic). Q3W / community infusion is the wedge vs RLT logistics — still specialty, not mass market. **Commercial binary (v2):** outpatient without heme-TCE **REMS / 48h inpatient** and without **ophthalmology-mandate** label friction.

**Demand funnel (v2 working model — mostly Speculative):** US late-line ~15–20k/yr; mid-line pre-taxane ~35–45k; earlier combo pool larger; PSMA PET eligibility ~80–85% with ~15–20% access shrink; base peak penetration sketch ~6k US + ~6k EU5/JP.

**Competitive crowdedness (v2 High threats):** JANX007 · pasritamig (KLK2 Ph3) · Pluvicto · **ARX517** (PSMA ADC, no CRS) — plus med: xaluritamig, Ac-225 RLTs, **JANX014** dual-mask `[TENTATIVE]`. Clinical win → **share-fight**, not monopoly. PSMA TCE graveyard (AMG 160 etc.) = CRS/ADA lesson PRO-XTEN must clear.

**Very profitable?** *Franchise* yes if mid/early-line + community label ($2–4B base / $4–7B bull WW hyp.; v2 arithmetic base ~**$2.52B** WW → ~**$357M**/yr VIR peak CF after 50/50 + ~15% ex-US royalty + ~20% Sanofi drag — **Speculative**). *VIR* always **shared**. Model owned cash flows.
