## Narrative

**Edison International** is a US electric-utility holding company. Its main operating subsidiary is **Southern California Edison (SCE)**, which serves much of Southern California.

**The live issue is wildfire liability.** SCE equipment has been tied to several major California fires. The **Eaton Fire** (started 7 January 2025, Eaton Canyon, Altadena/Pasadena area) is the most recent large event. Fire investigators concluded that SCE's equipment started it (per the Muddy Waters report). That attribution is public and is the core of the stock's derating.

**Market state at filing (2026-10-13).** About **$53.83**, down 3.25% on the day. 52-week range **$51.10 – $81.62**. The stock sits near its 52-week low, so the wildfire liability is largely in the price.

**Liability channel to watch.** The **California Wildfire Fund** administrator's ruling decides whether the state fund covers claims or whether SCE must reimburse it. A **willful-disregard** finding is far more costly than an **imprudence** finding. This is the binary catalyst, not the short report itself. See @concepts/california-wildfire-utility-liability.md.
