## Narrative

**James H. Simons** (1938-2024) — founder of Renaissance Technologies LLC and the Medallion Fund (the highest-returning hedge fund vehicle on record). Mathematician, codebreaker, philanthropist.

### Career arc

- **MIT** — BS in mathematics.
- **UC Berkeley** — PhD in mathematics. Scientific research in geometry + topology.
- **MIT / Harvard** — taught mathematics.
- **Institute for Defense Analyses** (Princeton) — cryptanalyst, ~4 years. NSA built a special-purpose computer to implement an algorithm he developed (per his own account, the computer was still operating as of 2022).
- **Stony Brook** — chair of the Mathematics Department.
- **Renaissance Technologies** — founded; ran as CEO for 30+ years; stepped back 2009. Peter Brown has been sole CEO since.

### Mathematical contributions

- **1968**: *Minimal Varieties in Riemannian Manifolds* (Annals of Mathematics 88, no. 1). ~1,850 citations. Won the AMS Oswald Veblen Prize in Geometry (1976).
- **Chern-Simons theory** (with Shiing-Shen Chern): the Chern-Simons three-form. A 3D topological quantum field theory of Schwarz type, developed for QFT by Edward Witten. By Simons' own description in the 2023 interview, "every day an average of four papers come out in the physics journals, or in the archive, referencing and using Chern-Simons."

Simons explicitly said in 2023: "Chern-Simons was wonderful, it does not help at all with making money." The math was training; the application to finance was independent.

### Philanthropy

- **Simons Foundation**: large research-funding org. Combined with Mathematics + physical sciences + biology grant programs.
- **Flatiron Institute** (NYC, "the Flatiron District"): 5 groups of ~50 researchers each — computational biology, computational astrophysics, computational quantum physics, computational mathematics, computational neuroscience. Researchers paid salaries; do not have to apply for grants.
- **Math for America**: nonprofit recognizing + rewarding outstanding NYC public-school math + science teachers. $25M/year program; scaled nationwide via provision in CHIPS and Science Act 2022.
- **Sea Change Foundation** (son Nat Simons + Laura Baxter-Simons): climate-change mitigation + clean energy policy.
- **Quanta Magazine**: free, online; Pulitzer Prize for Explanatory Reporting (2022), National Magazine Award (2020).

### Operational philosophy (extracted from 2023 interview — see `@concepts/hiring-quant-talent-philosophy.md`)

- Hire mathematicians + scientists + statisticians; explicitly **not** finance-industry veterans.
- One model, continuously improved — not a portfolio of strategies.
- Data mining is endorsed: "thousands of computers scouring data."
- Math, not behavioral / fundamental: "We stick to the math."
- Slow thinkers > fast thinkers on hard problems. Simons describes himself as a slow thinker who works hard.
- Collaboration was extremely important at RenTech.
- Profit-sharing across the workforce.
- Machine learning is real and important at RenTech (his words, 2023).
