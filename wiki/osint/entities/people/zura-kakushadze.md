## Narrative

**Zura Kakushadze, PhD** — theoretical physicist (string theory background) turned quantitative-finance practitioner. Founded **Quantigic Solutions LLC** (Stamford, Connecticut). Affiliated with **Department of Physics, University of Connecticut**.

### Background

- Theoretical-physics PhD lineage (string theory / high-energy theoretical physics).
- Postdoctoral / faculty affiliations including Harvard and CERN-adjacent positions (per arXiv-listed institutional history).
- Faculty teaching at **C.N. Yang Institute for Theoretical Physics, Stony Brook University** — taught the Spring 2002 advanced PhD course that became the Phynance lecture notes.
- Founded Quantigic Solutions LLC — a research firm focused on statistical arbitrage, factor models, risk modeling for quantitative trading. Active publisher of working papers.

### arXiv publication record

Highly prolific on **arXiv q-fin** (quantitative finance). 50+ papers across the categories `q-fin.PM` (portfolio management), `q-fin.ST` (statistical finance), `q-fin.MF` (mathematical finance), `q-fin.TR` (trading + market microstructure). Topics include:
- Statistical arbitrage / mean reversion strategies
- Factor model construction
- Risk modeling for hedge fund portfolios
- Spectral methods in finance
- Phynance — the 111-page lecture notes that anchor `@sources/phynance-kakushadze-1405-1948v2-stochastic-calculus-2014.md`
