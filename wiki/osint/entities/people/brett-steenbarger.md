## Raw Concept

What prompted this page: SMB Capital YouTube interview/talk with **Dr. Brett Steenbarger** (2026-04-08) — *Surprising Advice From World's #1 Trading Psychologist*; book *Positive Trading Psychology: From Personal Strengths to Trading Strengths* referenced.

## Narrative

Steenbarger is a trading psychologist / coach known for working with prop desks and hedge fund managers. SMB talk emphasizes **positive psychology** — building on existing life strengths rather than only fixing tilt/fear/greed.

### Key ideas from SMB session [TENTATIVE]

- **Trading as self-expression** — edge must leverage talents you already excel at; imitating others drifts from authentic process.
- **Team/community** — virtual trading teams mirror SMB collaboration thesis.
- **"FU therapy"** — externalize negative self-talk to a disliked figure; aroused challenge response defuses rumination.
- **Spoken journal loop** — record verbal EOD review; listen next morning as self-coaching (hedge fund manager pattern cited).
- **Journaling risk** — written journals can cement negative framing unless structured for lessons.
