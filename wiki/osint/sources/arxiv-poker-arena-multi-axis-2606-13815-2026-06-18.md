## Narrative

NLHE **tournament platform** evaluating frontier LLMs with **three-layer memory** (within-hand, session, cross-session) and **nine-axis cognitive profile** (bet sizing, positional awareness, etc.). 50 sessions × 1,000 hands, seven seats — chip winner (Claude Opus 4.6) ≠ top axis-score model. Memory ablation: persistent memory helps some models, hurts others.

**Cemini steal:** multi-axis eval for sandbox iteration; memory architecture checklist vs our F6 showdown queue.
