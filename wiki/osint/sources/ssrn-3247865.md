## Narrative

`151 Trading Strategies` is the most comprehensive single-volume compendium of quantitative trading strategies in the academic literature. Kakushadze (Quantigic Solutions CEO) and Serur (NYU Courant) provide detailed descriptions and 550+ mathematical formulas for 150+ strategies spanning every major asset class: stocks, options, fixed income, futures, ETFs, indexes, commodities, foreign exchange, convertibles, structured assets, volatility, real estate, distressed assets, cash, cryptocurrencies, weather, energy, inflation, global macro, infrastructure, and tax arbitrage.

Each strategy includes mathematical formulation, economic rationale, and out-of-sample backtesting source code with explanatory notes. The book also contains ~2,000 bibliographic references and 900+ glossary/acronym/math definitions. Several strategies incorporate ML algorithms (neural networks, Bayes, k-nearest neighbors).

**Relevance to Cemini**: Direct strategy-development reference. The 151 strategies are a catalog of candidate alpha sources for the TorchRL MAPPO ensemble to evaluate and potentially incorporate. The `@sources/4-11-crypto-re.md` intelligence report cites the Trading Strategy Trainer (github.com/KellanFinney/Trading_Strategy), a FastAPI+React educational platform that implements these strategies with NumPy-vectorized PnL calculations and interactive payoff diagrams.

**Limitations**: As a reference compendium, the book catalogs strategies without ranking them by Sharpe ratio, capacity, or regime-sensitivity. The Cemini RL ensemble must perform its own out-of-sample evaluation on any candidate strategy before live deployment.
