## Narrative

Klement cites a KfW survey of German firms. 21 percent report an adverse climate impact. 19 percent of micro firms (under five employees) say so, against 73 percent of large firms (over €500 million turnover). The note says adaptation cost is the main obstacle for smaller firms. No trade. No level to arm. [TENTATIVE]
