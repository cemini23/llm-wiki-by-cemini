## Narrative

Announcement pointer to **Templates** feature: share a Bot configuration as a public `x.ai/bot/<id>` link; recipient **Add to Grok Bot** creates an independent copy. Preview states templates carry **skills, memories, and official @bot plugins** — not the creator's cloud computer, logins, or history.
