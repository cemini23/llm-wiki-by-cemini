## Narrative

### What it is

A read-only injury, reserve and weather packet for the FanDuel Sunday Million contest slate of Sunday 22 November 2026. It carries a T-90 pre-lock checklist. It contains no lineups, projections, ownership, salaries, spreads, totals or stacks. No logins and no contest actions were taken. [Source: 11.21dfs.docx]

The packet was compiled Saturday 21 November 2026 at 5:19 PM ET.

### Slate

Ten Sunday daytime games: seven at 1:00 PM ET, one at 4:05 PM ET, two at 4:25 PM ET. SNF and MNF are excluded, and the Thursday game was already final. Six clubs were on bye.

The packet flags a week-label conflict. The task text said Week 12, but NFL.com and ESPN both place 22 November in Week 11. The Week 12 page is Thanksgiving week. The injury hub used is the Week 11 page.

### Data coverage

- **Injuries.** 227 rows from the NFL.com Week 11 hub, cross-checked against club pages. Status totals: 5 Doubtful, 56 Questionable, 41 Out, 125 with no game status.
- **Hard exclude rule.** Out, IR, inactive, doubtful, NFI, PUP and suspended are hard excludes. Questionable, limited and DNP are flagged, not excluded. 261 unique players were hard-excluded across the hub and club reserve lists.
- **DFS-relevant rows.** 27 QB/RB/WR/TE rows are Out, Doubtful or Questionable.
- **Starting QB check.** Generated by the desk tool `qb_starters.py`. 0 of 20 confirmed from two sources, 20 of 20 CLAIMED from the ESPN depth chart only. Every listed backup is a hard exclude.
- **Rosters.** An active-roster whitelist of 193 players and an excluded list of 302 players were exported from the current ESPN slate.
- **Weather.** NWS hourly forecast at kickoff, with Open-Meteo as a second read, for weather-exposed sites only. Domes and SoFi are skipped by rule.

### Notable conflicts flagged, not fixed

- Arizona QB: the NFL.com hub says Carson Beck Questionable, the club page says Out, and the desk tool shows Josh Johnson as starter. Treated as a hard exclude on Beck until inactives.
- Minnesota at San Francisco is on the international slate; ESPN lists the venue as Estadio Banorte, Mexico City. Off-slate, so no effect.

### Reuse value

High, as a worked example of the packet structure. The sections are reproducible: slate construction from two schedule sources, roster and reserve diff, QB confirmation tiers, weather reads, and a timed pre-lock checklist. The T-90 checklist is the part worth stealing for the CeminiDFS pipeline. The injury rows themselves expire after the slate.

This document is the Week 11 companion to the Week 10 packet at `operator-dfs-slate-2026-11-15.md`. They are not duplicates; they cover different weeks with the same template.
