## Narrative

A calendar-and-numbers preview. It carries no argument and no crypto-specific claim. Its value is the earnings and macro baseline it records, which is the state of the inputs for the coming week.

### Earnings

| Company | Last reported | Detail |
|---------|---------------|--------|
| Apple | Q4 revenue **$109.4bn**, +16% | iPhone +22%, Services +12%, gross margin 50.1% — including an approximately **2 percentage point** benefit from tariff refunds |
| Palantir | Revenue **$1.935bn**, +93% | U.S. commercial revenue **+149%**; guided Q3 revenue $2.160–2.164bn |
| AMD | Q2 revenue **$11.5bn**, +50% | Data Center **$6.7bn, +107%**, about 58% of total; guided Q3 about $13bn at 56% non-GAAP gross margin |

**The Apple margin detail is the one to keep.** Two points of a 50.1% gross margin came from tariff refunds. That is a one-off inside a reported margin, and any comparison to a prior quarter that lacks it overstates the underlying improvement.

### Macro calendar

- **Tue 3 Nov** — U.S. midterms. All 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats, including two special elections. First polls close 6:00 p.m. ET in eastern Indiana and Kentucky. Republicans control both chambers going in. Contested races may take days.
- **Wed 4 Nov** — October ADP employment at 8:15 a.m. ET; ISM Services PMI at 10:00 a.m. ET.
- **Thu 5 Nov** — Bank of England decision and Monetary Policy Report, 7:00 a.m. ET.
- **Fri 6 Nov** — October employment report, 8:30 a.m. ET.

A Reuters survey on 15 October found most economists expected a hike to **4.00%** in November.

**The internal tension worth noting.** ISM Services employment sits at 50.1 — barely expanding — while its Prices Index is 74.0. A services survey showing near-zero hiring growth alongside strong price pressure is the combination that makes the Friday payroll print and the BOE decision more consequential than usual.

The newsletter itself warns ADP "should not be treated as a direct forecast of Friday's payroll report." That is correct and worth repeating.
