Event Horizon argues prediction markets used as sports gambling should inherit the common **21+** floor used for sports betting. A Texas doctors’ group is among advocates. Legal classification of PMs as gambling remains contested; age policy is a separate retail-protection track. Awareness for gambling wiki; **no pm scp**.
