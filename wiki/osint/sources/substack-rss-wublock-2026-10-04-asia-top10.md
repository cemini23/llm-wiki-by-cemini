## Narrative

A ten-item Asia-Pacific crypto regulation digest. **No level to arm.**

**Japan — tax enforcement.** The National Tax Agency launched its **KSK2** core tax system on 24 September to improve detection of undeclared crypto assets. Separately, the Ministry of Finance formed an **On-Chain Application Research Group** on 30 September to study real-time on-chain settlement of **government bonds**; first meeting 8 October. A government studying on-chain settlement of its own debt is a different order of commitment from a crypto pilot.

**South Korea — a shrinking market and an expanding product.** Virtual-asset market cap fell **33%** in half a year, to **58.9 trillion won** (about $43.4B), from 87.2 trillion. Daily volume dropped from 5.4T to 3.1T won, and KRW deposits fell 35%. Users were roughly flat at about 11.17 million. At the same time, the FSC proposed **expanding tokenized securities to stocks, bonds, and funds**, effective **4 February 2027**, with a retail cap of 100 million won per OTC platform per year and a 4 billion won issuer minimum. Public comment ran 2 October to 11 November.

That contrast is the item worth keeping: **retail volume collapsing while the regulator widens the product surface.**

**Corporate.** SBI completed full acquisition of exchange **Bitbank** for about 46.7 billion yen on 1 October. Shinhan Bank and the Solana Foundation finished a cross-border stablecoin remittance proof of concept. A Singapore brokerage subsidiary received in-principle MAS licence approval. A Dubai bank joined a German tokenized-deposit project. Malaysian police seized 30 mining machines in an electricity-theft case.
