## Narrative

### What the teaser states

The teaser describes a Friday livestream with four claims:

- Headline and core inflation are rising together.
- Nominal demand runs near **6%** against a **4%** policy rate. The spread amplifies rather than brakes price pressure.
- The inflation swap market still prices a deceleration that the data does not support.
- Factor flows show equities positioned for AI momentum, not for macro risk.

The post also promises a 60/40 purchasing-power study. The teaser claim is that more than half of this year's nominal return has been eaten by inflation, and that this setup preceded 2022. [TENTATIVE: teaser text only; no numbers or method are visible]

### What is usable

The one testable statement is the nominal-demand-minus-policy-rate spread. Measured as nominal GDP growth minus the policy rate, it is a simple regime indicator that does not need the paid deck.

The rest is unfalsifiable from the teaser. The claim that inflation swaps misprice deceleration needs the swap curve and a forecast. Neither is in the source.

### Why it is filed here

The item is a teaser, and the wiki treats it as a pointer, not evidence. The useful output is the spread monitor. See @concepts/inflation-risk-regime-2026.md.
