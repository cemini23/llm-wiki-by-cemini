## Narrative

### China: the Solana ad probe

The People's Bank of China found that Solana placed virtual-currency advertising through domestic entities in Lujiazui, Shanghai. The PBOC, with local financial, public-security, and market-supervision authorities, suspended the offline **Solana Accelerate China 2026** event and opened an investigation. The cited rule is the February 2026 circular on virtual-currency risk. The stated future focus is domestic traders and domestic affiliates of offshore exchanges. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-08-asias-weekly-top10-crypto-news-pboc-to-probe-sol.md]

This is the clearest statement in the wave that mainland China still treats promotion, not just trading, as the enforcement boundary.

### Russia opens the market

The Bank of Russia set the asset list for non-qualified investors: **BTC, ETH, USDT**. They must pass a risk test. Annual purchases through one intermediary are capped at **300,000 rubles**. The Moscow Exchange plans to start pilot trading on December 1.

### Hong Kong

Hong Kong will license virtual-asset trading, custody, advisory, and management, with amendment bills before the Legislative Council in 2026. SFC CEO Julia Leung said tokenisation should be integrated into market operations, and called for proportionate governance of agentic AI. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-08-asias-weekly-top10-crypto-news-pboc-to-probe-sol.md]

### Korea: tax, cold wallets, and an exchange halt

- **Tax delay.** The virtual-asset gains tax moves from 2027 to **2029**, the fourth delay. The rate is **22%** including local tax, on annual gains above **2.5m KRW**. The government will study a three-year loss carry-forward.
- **Cold-wallet rule.** The FSC is considering raising the minimum cold-wallet ratio from **80%** to about **99%**. Current ratios: Upbit above 99%, Bithumb about 94%, Coinone about 85%. Small exchanges sit just above 80%. The objection is that a higher cold ratio reduces hot-wallet liquidity and lengthens withdrawal times. Bithumb had a 13-hour withdrawal delay in August.
- **GOPAX halted.** The exchange moved all user assets to cold storage and suspended trading and transfers after threatening emails. It says no attack occurred.

[Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-08-asias-weekly-top10-crypto-news-pboc-to-probe-sol.md]

The cold-wallet item is the operational one. A **99% cold ratio** and instant withdrawals are in direct conflict. A rule that raises the ratio must also accept slower settlement.

### Policy and enforcement

- **MAS** said crypto trading is unsuitable for the general public and repeated that Binance is not licensed in Singapore.
- **Korea's president** called for a cross-government cybersecurity body after the Binance breach. The National Intelligence Service began emergency inspections of more than **2,100** public institutions.
- **U.S. Treasury** sanctioned four individuals and 41 entities linked to Cambodia's Prince Group, plus one Malaysian national tied to a Madagascar fraud ring.
- **Japan and the EU** put asset tokenisation into their joint financial-regulatory statement for the first time.

## Dead Ends

- **Treating China's ban as unchanged.** Enforcement now targets promotion and domestic affiliates, not only trading venues.
- **Reading the cold-wallet ratio as a pure safety gain.** It trades off against withdrawal speed.
