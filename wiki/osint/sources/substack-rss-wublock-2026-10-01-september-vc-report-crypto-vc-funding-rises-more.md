## Narrative

A monthly crypto venture-funding report from RootData. September 2026 had 61 disclosed deals, down 1.6% from August's 62 and down 35.1% from 94 a year earlier. Total disclosed funding rose 71.1% month over month to about $1.269B, from $742M. That is still down 79.2% from $6.096B a year earlier.

One line matters for this workspace. **Polymarket raised a round led by 1789 Capital, where Donald Trump Jr. is a partner, at a $21B post-money valuation.** That is up about 40% from roughly $15B.

Other large rounds named: Félix $200M Series B; Jeeves $110M Series C; Nasdaq Ventures $100M into Payward, the Kraken parent, at a $21B valuation; MoonPay and North Capital over $60M; Kaiko $57M; World Foundation $49M in an ONDO OTC deal; USD.AI $40M; HIFI $37M Series A; Latitude $35M Series A.

Sector split: DeFi 22.6%, CeFi 19.4%, real-world assets and DePIN 14.5%, AI 12.9%.

A valuation figure is not a price level. No level to arm. Do not arm a watch. FILE only.
