## Narrative

Failure attribution is essential for diagnosing and improving multi- agent systems (MAS), yet existing benchmarks and methods largely assume a single deterministic root cause for each failure. In prac- tice, MAS failures often admit multiple plausible attributions due to complex inter-agent dependencies and ambiguous execution trajec- tories.
