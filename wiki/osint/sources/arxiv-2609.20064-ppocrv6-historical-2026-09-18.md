## Narrative

Kiessling (Inria ALMAnaCH). Compact PP-OCRv6 recognizer vs CRNN and VLM HTR on historical Latin- and Arabic-script lines. Heterogeneous pretraining helps generalization; from-scratch does not consistently beat CRNN. Fine-tuned PP-OCRv6 can beat a Qwen3.5 Medusa VLM on historical Latin-script HTR. **Atto FILE only. Transkribus remains sole product HTR. Do not productize PaddleOCR / PP-OCR. 0 Integrate GO.**
