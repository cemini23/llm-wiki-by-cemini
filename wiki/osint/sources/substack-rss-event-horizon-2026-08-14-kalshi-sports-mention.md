## Narrative

Kalshi **sidelined sports mention markets** ahead of football season (Discord: "temporarily"; EH: short-term at least). NPR (Bobby Allyn, two unnamed sources): **federal regulators reviewing mention markets** (wagers on word choice of Trump through soccer sportscasters). NFL March letters asked venues not to list easily manipulated events (announcer words, celebrity attendance, draft). MLB raised similar CFTC concerns; player unions hostile.

Mention volume since Kalshi launched sports: **$1.2B**, more than a quarter on sports mentions; spiked in the World Cup. Non-sports/parlay/crypto volume is otherwise a rounding error except elections. Polymarket international still lists some sports mentions.

### Washington injunction (operational)

King County Superior Court **final order**: KalshiEX must cease many WA operations (sports, elections, politics, entertainment, culture, tech/science, **mentions**). IP+residency geofence by **Aug 19**; multi-source geofence by **Sep 2**. Advertising those products to WA consumers banned. Appeals stay denied. This is broader than sports — most of Kalshi's recent mix.
