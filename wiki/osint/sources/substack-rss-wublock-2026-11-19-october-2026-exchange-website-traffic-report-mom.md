## Narrative

October 2026 website traffic across nine major exchanges was about 139 million visits, down about 0.65% from 140 million in September. Three venues rose and six fell. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-19-october-2026-exchange-website-traffic-report-mom.md]

| Venue | Visits | Share |
|---|---|---|
| OKX | 35.69m | 25.7% |
| Binance | 34.57m | 24.9% |
| Coinbase | 22.14m | 15.9% |

OKX took first place from Binance. The top three held about 66.5%.

Movers: Bitfinex +10.9%, OKX +7.4%, Bitget +4.7% on the up side. HTX -22.5%, Upbit -16.0%, Bybit -6.0% on the down side.

Geography: OKX's top visitor origin was the USA, Binance's was Korea, Coinbase's was the USA.

Traffic is a retail-attention proxy, not a volume measure. It diverges from volume: OKX leads visits but holds 9.9% of spot volume and 17.4% of derivatives volume, while Binance holds 44.8% of spot. Read the two together.
