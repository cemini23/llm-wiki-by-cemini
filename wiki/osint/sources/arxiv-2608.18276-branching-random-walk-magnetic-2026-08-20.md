## Narrative

**Out of domain for Cemini product surfaces.** This is a mathematical spin-glass paper: adding a uniform magnetic field to a Gaussian binary branching random walk (BRW) does not require a new model-specific analysis, because the Hamiltonian remains a BRW with independent but non-identically distributed displacements. The author obtains ground state, free energy, 1-RSB, and Poisson–Dirichlet overlap statistics, then proves magnetization concentration at low temperature. A one-replica magnetization statement is **not** enough to control the Ising overlap of two independent samples; a two-replica large-deviation argument is required.
