## Narrative

A cluster of crypto illicit-finance developments with direct OSINT-tradecraft relevance:

- **T3 Financial Crime Unit crosses $450M frozen.** The T3 FCU — a public-private partnership of **Tether, TRON, and TRM Labs** — reported having frozen **>$450M in illicit USDT** (largely on TRON), working with law enforcement across **23 jurisdictions**; 2025 interceptions up **43.9%** year-over-year.
- **Arkham traces Iran-linked wallets.** Blockchain-analytics firm **Arkham** tracked **$344M** held in Iran-linked TRON wallets — an example of public on-chain analytics being used for geopolitical-finance investigation (wallet tracking, asset freezes, transaction monitoring).
- **FinCEN IRGC alert.** The US Treasury's FinCEN issued an alert to all US banks on Iran's IRGC using **crypto and front companies** for sanctions evasion. The UK separately sanctioned an Iran-linked money-laundering network tied to attack plotting.
- **Scale context.** Binance Research put illicit crypto on public blockchains at roughly **$75B** — still **under 1%** of total on-chain transaction volume.

**Why it matters here**: this is OSINT-core. Crypto-tracing — using public blockchain data plus analytics platforms (Arkham, TRM) to attribute funds to threat actors and sanctioned entities — is exactly the investigative tradecraft this wiki tracks. The T3 model (token issuer + chain + analytics firm freezing assets cooperatively) is a notable institutional pattern.
