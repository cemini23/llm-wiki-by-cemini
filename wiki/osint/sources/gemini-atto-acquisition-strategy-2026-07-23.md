## Narrative

Seven-channel plan: Reddit, Facebook Groups, YouTube, TikTok/Reels, X, SEO, podcasts. **Organic-first 90 days** due to Meta/Google government-document and personal-attribute ad risk.

**Prioritized rank for Atto:** YouTube → Reddit → Podcasts → SEO → X → TikTok/Reels → Facebook Groups (lowest — admin monetization, ban risk; prefer paid admin sponsorship if anything).

**Do-not-post:** “find out if you qualify,” citizenship guarantees, early hard-sell purchase links, “Are you Italian?” ad framing, public attacks on named counsel/consulates.

**Pre-PMF metrics:** demo watch %, refund rate (&lt;~5% warning), qualitative “replaced my spreadsheet.”

**Caveat:** Dual US-Italian FB dynamics and admin preferences are **community-reported** — treat as [TENTATIVE]; verify before paid sponsorship attempts.
