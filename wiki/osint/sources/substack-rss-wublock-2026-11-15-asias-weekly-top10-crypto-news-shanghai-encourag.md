## Narrative

Ten regional items, of which four carry structural information rather than price colour.

China's posture is unchanged and narrower than headlines suggest. Shanghai is promoting blockchain and privacy-preserving computation for scientific-data sharing under its Trial Measures, and the item states explicitly that this does not cover cryptocurrency trading or asset issuance. Separately, the CAC barred the words "virtual currency", "exchange" and "ICO" from blockchain service filing names.

Kazakhstan is building market plumbing. The National Bank completed a tokenised tranche of 91-day bills — 100bn of 500bn tenge at a 15.72% weighted average yield and 1.4x subscription — on a KASE platform reportedly built on Solana with BitGo custody, and plans tokenised government bonds from Q1 2027. Neither vendor has confirmed participation in the bond issue.

Russia set a date. The Central Bank says exchanges may trade crypto from 22 November 2026 once trading rules are agreed with regulators, with participant registration, trade monitoring and price calculation required.

Thailand went live with a sandbox. WORPT converts USDT/USDC to baht for tourists through PromptPay QR codes under the SEC's TouristDigiPay sandbox, co-built by Wipay and Waan Exchange.

Also recorded: Nomura weighing a sale of Laser Digital; BitRiver's founder facing a 773m-rouble bankruptcy claim; and a Korean dispute over whether non-banks may issue won stablecoins.
