## Narrative

Miners used to be priced on EH/s, BTC/day, and BTC on the balance sheet. The new units are **MW/GW**: energized capacity, contracted AI load, billable revenue. IEA: data-center electricity 485 TWh (2025) → ~950 TWh (2030). Hashprice ~$30.6/PH/s/day (VanEck, mid-July 2026).

Core Scientific Q2 2026: $164.2M rev (+109% YoY); high-density colo $136.7M (83% of rev, 59% GM) vs self-mining $21.54M (−66%, −56% GM). 437 MW billable; 1.1 GW contracted. TeraWulf 20-year Anthropic lease (~401 MW, ~$19B, Hawesville KY, 2H27–early 28). Hut 8 second 15-year $9.8B lease (352 MW, Beacon Point; campus 704 MW / $19.6B). VanEck EV/Gross Energized Power: signed AI/HPC leases >10× vs pipeline-only 2–6×.

**Wiki-only.** No named xsp rule. Observational power-as-asset note; pairs SPCX-as-landlord thesis.
