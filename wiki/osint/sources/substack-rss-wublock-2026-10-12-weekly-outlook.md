## Narrative

The week's macro calendar.

- **Mon Oct 12, 8:30/8:45 p.m. ET — Broadcom and NVIDIA keynotes at OCP.** Open AI networking and AI-factory infrastructure; sessions on optical interconnects, **800 VDC power**, and liquid cooling. Watch compatibility standards and deployment timelines, and **separate demos from customer deployments**.
- **Wed Oct 14, pre-open — ASML Q3.** Guided to **55–57% gross margin** and planned **~30% capacity increases in 2027** for low-NA EUV and DUV immersion. Orders and guidance test whether demand supports the expansion.
- **Wed Oct 14, 8:30 a.m. ET — US September CPI.** August was **3.4% YoY / 0.4% MoM**, with **gasoline >⅓ of the monthly rise**; core +0.3% MoM. Watch whether energy persists and whether shelter and core services accelerate.
- **Thu Oct 15, 8:30 a.m. ET — PPI, retail sales, jobless claims** together. Focus on the **four-week average of claims**.
- **Thu Oct 15, noon ET — EIA petroleum inventories** (delayed by Columbus Day).
- **Thu Oct 15, 11:30 p.m. ET — Fed Chair Kevin Warsh with IMF MD Kristalina Georgieva.** After the data, watch for any change in Warsh's read on inflation, growth, and the policy path. The agenda does not promise a rate discussion.

**The framing question:** do prices, spending, and employment point the same way? Persistent core inflation plus resilient consumption strengthens tightening expectations; weaker spending without easing prices makes the Fed's trade-off harder.
