## Narrative

Source-by-source map of US records that reveal an exact Italian *comune* (not “Italy” / port city). Passenger manifests (post-1906 birthplace; post-1907 page-2 nearest relative; last-residence fallacy pre-1906); Steve Morse White/Gold forms; naturalization (pre- vs post-1906 Basic Naturalization Act; Declaration vs Petition memory); WWI + WWII “Old Man’s Draft” (1942) place-of-birth fields; SS-5 / Numident FOIA; USCIS Genealogy; NYC/NJ vital indexes (IGG). Builds a **privacy-first deep-link ladder** for local assistants that open browsers only — zero family PII upload to external AI.

**Wiki scope:** ladder + failure modes + portal pointers. Living-person SSNs / private Barone facts stay off-wiki.

## Snippets

> Pre-1906 passenger “last residence” ≠ birthplace — rural Sicilian may list Palermo. [Source: Italian-American Genealogy Source Research.docx]

> Post-1907 manifests: comune often on **page 2** — digital viewers sometimes need “previous,” not “next.” [Source: Italian-American Genealogy Source Research.docx]

> Pre-1906 naturalization rarely names a town; post-1906 Declaration/Petition usually do — check Declaration first (sharper memory). [Source: Italian-American Genealogy Source Research.docx]

> WWII Fourth Registration (“Old Man’s Draft,” 1942) covers men born ~1877–1897 — often the only federal place-of-birth hit for non-naturalizers. [Source: Italian-American Genealogy Source Research.docx]
