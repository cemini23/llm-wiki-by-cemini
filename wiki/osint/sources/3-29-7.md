## Narrative

This paper proposes a privacy-aware collaborative DNN inference framework that adaptively partitions models across edge devices and servers, formulated as a Constrained MDP and solved with a hierarchical safe-RL algorithm (HC-MAPPO-L) using Lagrangian dual updates to enforce delay constraints. The contribution is an edge-computing inference-offloading method. This is **out-of-scope** for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda — edge-device DNN inference offloading has no application path to financial-market modeling or OSINT research.
