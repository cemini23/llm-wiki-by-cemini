## Narrative

Macro playbook takeaways from the Capital Flows livestream (free RSS teaser — member section paywalled):

- **Debt levels increasing is not bearish** — a crisis only occurs on an asset-liability mismatch. NVIDIA adding debt against appreciating GPU collateral = the company betting on itself.
- **Inflation modeling**: every source of nominal demand vs every source of real output. **M2 velocity is a worthless input**; real-time credit-card data, savings balances, and pricing power across major retailers are the real signals.
- **Core CPI decelerated 2.9 → 2.6 YoY**; at current deceleration speed, lands near 2% by year-end. Tomorrow's CPI + PPI are the regime-setting prints.
- **Bonds won't bid until AI CapEx ends or the credit behind it collapses.** Nowcast ~5.8% real GDP with fixed investment accelerating; ~40% of S&P 500 returns over the last year connected to AI.
- **Factor rotation already moving**: steels & metals +43% YTD, ags +16% YTD; short-end inflation swaps from Japan to the UK show where global pressure builds next.
- **Warning signs stacking**: inventory-to-sales ratios falling while government spending rises; real rates at new highs still not restrictive enough to slow the US economy.
