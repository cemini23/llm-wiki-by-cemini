## Narrative

CoRelNav is a multi-robot navigation paper. The goal is a target that is defined by a semantic class and by a relation to nearby objects. The paper couples task-conditioned exploration with a check that uses observations from more than one robot. A spatial-semantic field scores where to search. Robots then move toward the missing evidence.
