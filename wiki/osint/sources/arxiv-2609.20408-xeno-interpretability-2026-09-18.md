## Narrative

Xeno-interpretability: model-native representations that have no adequate human conceptual counterpart. Locate and manipulate internal structure even when it cannot be named in human terms. Safety implication: those representations may propagate across agents while remaining only partly visible in text. CCC FILE. Wiki-only. **Do not swap `/route`. 0 Integrate GO.**
