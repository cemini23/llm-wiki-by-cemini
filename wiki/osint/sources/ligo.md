## Narrative

This paper benchmarks classical and deep-learning models (gradient-boosted trees, MLPs, attention-based models, neural decision ensembles) for multiclass classification of LIGO gravitational-wave detector noise transients ("glitches"), using tabular numerical features from the Gravity Spy dataset. It is an astrophysics / detector-characterization machine-learning paper. It is out-of-scope for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda: while the tabular-classification methods are generic, the domain (gravitational-wave detector noise) and dataset have no transfer path to financial-market modeling or the CeminiSuite roadmap.
