## Narrative

SparkToro's weekly 5-Minute Whiteboard on the zero-click debate. Position: scholars are divided, but the website remains the **only permanent home** for content — the place to own and control the brand experience and to inform search, AI tools, and the occasional visitor. Underpins the "brand site as source-of-truth hub" argument for GEO/AEO strategy: AI answer engines need a canonical, well-structured site to cite.
