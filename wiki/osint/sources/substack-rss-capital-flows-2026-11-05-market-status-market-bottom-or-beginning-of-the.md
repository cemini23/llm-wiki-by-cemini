## Narrative

This is a teaser. The full post is behind the paywall, so the argument is visible only in outline.

The author says he turned less bullish on equities months ago and moved to neutral. The stated cause is built-up systemic risk that existed before the Democratic sweep. His phrase is that fragility waits for "a small prick to unwind it". [Source: substack-rss-capital-flows-2026-11-05-market-status-market-bottom-or-beginning-of-the.md]

He then gives a causal chain with three links.

1. **The flow starts with China.** Foreign holders must buy US assets to keep their current-account relationship with the US, so foreign money lifts US equity valuations.
2. **The capital has to land somewhere.** The AI build-out absorbed the flow, because the AI race between the US and China is one of the largest capex programmes in history.
3. **Capital inflow is not profit.** Usage does not convert into earnings on a horizon that sustains current valuations. CoreWeave is his example: down **77% from its high**, and the most credit-sensitive name in the complex. [Source: substack-rss-capital-flows-2026-11-05-market-status-market-bottom-or-beginning-of-the.md]

He also names a divergence between H100 rental prices and token prices as the evidence that capex and demand do not pass through to the bottom line.

### What is claim and what is argument

- **Claims with numbers:** CoreWeave down 77%. Both are single-source newsletter figures and are `[TENTATIVE]`.
- **The causal frame** — global liquidity driven by a managed Chinese currency, and AI capex as the sink for that liquidity — is an opinion. It is not testable from this teaser.
- **The prediction** — that a larger risk is still unrecognised — has no stated trigger, level, or date.

### Why it matters here

The tail-risk frame is the useful part for @concepts/guruwatcher-macro-watches.md. A watch needs a measurable trigger. This post gives a thesis but no level. The level to watch is CoreWeave's credit spread and its interest coverage, not the equity price alone.

The two-day follow-up post in the same wave adds the Anthropic IPO to the same frame.
