## Narrative

**The argument.** Kalshi co-founders Tarek Mansour and Luana Lopes Lara face criminal charges in Utah and must appear in person. The author says they should not go to jail, but that the charges are not an outrage. Both sides hold full conviction: states say the operators run illegal sportsbooks, and the operators say no state law applies. The industry took a large swing by launching sports event contracts; if it loses, states will seek a penalty. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-20-kalshi-utah-and-the-consequences-of-playing-chic.md]

**New York.** The bigger news is New York, where a shutdown would matter more than in almost any state except California, Texas and Florida. Chair Selig, who had called state attorneys general "rogue" and "naked lawfare", then offered "constructive dialogue".

**Congress.** The Senate passed a bill, sponsored by Senators Curtis and Schiff, to bar prediction markets from listing contracts on terrorism, assassination, war, military operations or an individual's death. It goes to the House. Such contracts are already not permitted on CFTC-regulated venues, which is the point being made.

**Courts.** The score line: the 9th, 6th and 4th Circuits rejected Kalshi's attempts to block state enforcement, each for different reasons; the 3rd Circuit held Kalshi exempt from state gambling laws. The split raises the odds of Supreme Court review.

**Industry.** Binance.US said the CFTC deemed its DCM and DCO applications materially complete, a step toward regulated perps for US customers. Coinbase is targeting in-house event contracts in the first half of 2027. Kalshi asked 35 states to share self-exclusion lists, together with Novig. Polymarket is now reachable through Talos, an institutional order and execution management system, for non-US clients. Kalshi stock-index volume reached $78.6m in October, up 96% from August, as Anthropic listed. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-20-kalshi-utah-and-the-consequences-of-playing-chic.md]

**Tax.** Pew reports at least 15 states considered prediction-market legislation in 2026, and three enacted tax laws: North Carolina, Kentucky and Illinois.

**Volume, Wednesday 18 November.** Kalshi $3.15bn: 63.6% parlays, 16.2% sports, 16.8% crypto. Seven-day: Kalshi $28.2bn, Polymarket US $3.73bn, DKeX $1.37bn.

For the PM/Kalshi bot, the lesson is that venue legality is a fast-moving input. A state action can remove a market, and a federal pause can remove a whole category.
