## Narrative

EH argues the “ban them and volume goes offshore” line is overstated. Sports-betting legalization **created new activity** rather than only shifting offshore books onshore. If US prediction markets (or sports event contracts) vanished, friction (payments, app stores, ads), crypto-only offshore UX, and **CFTC pursuit of US-serving offshore venues** (Polymarket 2022) would leave much of the activity **gone, not relocated**. Kalshi scaled as the legal option after Polymarket international already existed. Comp: post-UIGEA online poker — many players stopped rather than going offshore. Same-day ops: **Kalshi WA geofence 2026-08-19**. Mentions markets remain a tiny volume share under CFTC review. Gambling brief; **no pm scp** (no named bot rule).
