## Narrative

K187 lock pass against YouTube/soft-flag claims. Secondary counsel blogs used only as pointers; holdings below cite primary texts.

### Locked (CONFIRMED)

1. **Art. 3-bis / Law 74/2025** is in force (conversion of DL 36/2025; GU 23 May 2025). Automatic JS for many born abroad + other citizenship is barred unless a statutory exception applies (domanda / appuntamento by 27 Mar 2025 23:59 Rome; exclusive-Italian parent/grandparent; parent 2y Italy residency before birth/adoption — letters a–d as in Cost. 63 text).
2. **Costituzionale Sentenza 63/2026** (deposited 30 Apr 2026, Torino referral): challenges to art. 3-bis (words «anche prima…» + letters a, a-bis, b) largely **non fondate** / some **inammissibili**. Framing: **preclusione all’acquisto**, not mass revocation. Safe-harbor logic tied to domanda or **prescribed appointment** by cutoff.
3. **Cassazione Ordinanza 13818/2026** (I Sez. Civ.; camera di consiglio **4 Mar 2026**; R.G. 01944/2025; Pres. Acierno): **principio di diritto** — interest to sue for JS status ascertainment exists not only on denial/delay but when impediments prevent **even presenting** the request to the Administration. Case facts: Colombian-line applicants; Genova appeal reversed for ignoring appointment-impossibility evidence. **Not** a ruling that Law 74 is unconstitutional; **not** Sezioni Unite.
4. **Venue centralization:** Law **11/2026** + MAECI consular pages — adult JS recognition from abroad moves to **central MAECI from 1 Jan 2029**; through **31 Dec 2028** consular channel remains (Basilea: dichiarazione volontà by that date). Paper-mail deposit for central channel described in secondary commentary of L. 11 — treat operational mail details as **soft** until MAECI implementing notice.
