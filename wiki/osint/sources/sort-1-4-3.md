## Narrative

Short paper on using a multi-agent system to construct and evaluate crypto portfolios. Preview: "practical application of a multi-agent system designed to autonomously construct and evaluate crypto-asset allocations." Motivation is crypto volatility and the dynamic-portfolio management problem.

Honest assessment: 12 pages is concise — likely a methodology + small empirical study rather than deep theory. Paolo Giudici (if it is the Pavia Giudici) has a substantive publication record in statistical fintech, which lifts the prior on quality. The "agentic AI" branding is fashionable; the question is whether the multi-agent structure adds value over a single optimization policy or is mostly framing. The crypto-portfolio-management literature is very noisy — small papers frequently show beating Bitcoin-only buy-and-hold in-sample but fail OOS during bear phases.

Relevance: directly relevant to Cemini's crypto watchlist (BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE, ADA, AVAX, LINK). Worth a deeper read to extract (a) which agent roles they use, (b) what the evaluation window looks like, (c) whether they include bear-market data. These are the diagnostics that separate useful crypto-portfolio papers from the noise.
