## Narrative

The title is a joke about the editor's golf trip. The body is a full prediction-market roundup.

### Louisiana sues almost every operator

Louisiana Attorney General Liz Murrill filed suit in the 21st Judicial District Court, Tangipahoa Parish, against Kalshi, Polymarket, Robinhood, Crypto.com, Underdog, Novig, ProphetX and several others.

Kalshi answered the same day with a preliminary-injunction motion in the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Louisiana. The court granted a temporary restraining order. Per NEXTPredict, the order restrains the defendants from "operating, offering, listing, accepting, clearing, settling, brokering, making a market in, or otherwise facilitating" any event contract for a person in Louisiana. It requires geofencing and bars the defendants from calling the contracts licensed in the state.

Murrill's line: "It's not complicated: if you aren't a licensed gaming operator, you aren't legal."

This is the first state action that names the whole operator set at once. It converts a series of bilateral fights into a single test. See @concepts/dcm-state-preemption.md.

### Kalshi funding

Bloomberg reports Kalshi is in talks to raise at a **$65bn** valuation, weeks after closing a round at **$40bn**. That is a 62.5% step-up in weeks. [TENTATIVE: unnamed sources, one outlet]

### Corporate and political items

- **Donald Trump Jr. is out as a Kalshi strategic adviser.** He was named in January 2025 and received a stake the FT valued at $300,000. Kalshi said the role ended by mutual agreement after his firm invested in a competitor. Bloomberg tied the timing to Democratic promises to scrutinise the Trump family's prediction-market ties.
- **Two CFTC commissioner nominees**: Nathan Anonick (Republican, Senate Agriculture Committee counsel) and Matthew Rowland (CFTC attorney). With Michael Selig, a confirmed pair would give three of five seats.
- **Clearinghouse lawsuits**: CME and Cboe sued the CFTC over single-stock perpetuals. Nasdaq objected without filing. The New York Times reported the suits as rare action by clearinghouses against their own regulator.
- **Texas bill**: Rep. Salman Bhojani pre-filed a bill with a minimum age of 21. The text conflicts with federal rules in several ways, so court challenge is likely.

### Product and market items

- **NVIDIA perpetual futures on Kalshi**: about **$90m** in 24-hour volume, one of the new single-stock perps.
- **Prospect Markets goes live** in the US as a sports-native prediction-market venue.
- **High Roller (NYSE: ROLR) × ProphetX**: a ROLR-branded interface over the ProphetX exchange, with ProphetX holding accounts, funds and clearing.
- **Underdog launches live NFL drive markets.** Context: the CFTC told Novig that first-point-scored contracts are micro contracts and should not be listed. ProphetX's scorigami contracts did not meet the swap definition.
- **DOPE** launched an AI prediction-market assistant over iMessage.
- **Kalshi hiring a GTA VI researcher** at a $100,000 fixed fee plus $15,000 completion.
