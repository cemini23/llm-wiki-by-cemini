## Narrative

A macro calendar for 15-22 November 2026, verified to 14 November.

China: October industrial production, retail sales and fixed-asset investment. September context given is industrial output +5.5% year over year, retail sales +1.1%, fixed-asset investment -7.2% over nine months with property investment -20.6%.

United States: October retail sales (September +0.8% month over month but control group only +0.1%; October CPI +0.4%), then Nvidia fiscal Q3 — last quarter revenue $96.22bn, up 106%, data centre $89bn, up 117%, guidance $108bn at a 74% non-GAAP gross margin excluding China data-centre compute — then the October Fed minutes after a 10-2 hold at 3.75-4.00% with two members preferring a 25bp hike.

The AI-valuation item is Anthropic's IPO: 825 million shares at $70-78, up to $64.35bn offered, plus a $26bn private placement, with Reuters putting fully diluted valuation at $1.4 trillion. Q3 revenue is reported up about 47% sequentially to $16.96bn against roughly $900bn of compute commitments.

The useful framing is the pairing: growth rate against committed infrastructure spend.
