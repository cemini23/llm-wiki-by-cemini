## Narrative

Long-form macro/culture essay (four drafts). Thesis: the economy increasingly rewards ownership over effort, restructuring both the payoff structure and the culture. Key data points:

**Labor vs capital.** Labor share of gross domestic income fell from ~58% (1980) to ~51%; corporate profits' share rose 7% → 11.7% (Greg Ip/WSJ). Tax code migrated the same way: corporate income tax fell from >1/5 of federal revenue (1950s) to <1/10, while payroll tax grew to the second-largest revenue source (Krugman). Worker's-share cost ≈ $8k–$12k/yr.

**Getting rich = nonwage income.** 42% of top-1% nonwage income is pure capital gains; S-corp income is "labor in the economic sense." Median household net worth ~doubled since mid-1990s while incomes grew ~30%; climbing from bottom-quarter to top-quarter wealth took ~20 years a generation ago, now ~40 (Burn-Murdoch/FT).

**Passive-income culture.** >half of Americans (60% of Gen Z adults) say a full-time job won't meet their goals; 1 in 4 have a side hustle; "passive income" search interest +50%/5y; passive-income courses are a grift layer on top of genuine wealth-begets-wealth mechanics.

**Everything is the AI trade.** AI boom = latest installment of asset-holder enrichment; AI wealth effect drives consumption + state data-center revenue + Caterpillar/Cummins trading like tech stocks (Neil Dutta/Bloomberg). Data-center construction ~$70B annualized in June while homes/hospitals outlays fell $100B; $2.5T AI spending by end-2026 projection; WEF: AI may displace 92M jobs by 2030.

**Demographics — Boomers own the economy.** 55+ hold 74% of US wealth (was ~50% in 1989); under-40 fell 11% → 6.6%; 70+ are 12% of population holding 32% of net worth; retirees drive >quarter of consumer spending; typical voter is 52; ~half of campaign donations from over-66. Fernández-Villaverde: aging society's first-order problem is redistributing worker→retiree income via taxes (visible) or capital income (invisible, 401k-driven).

**Prospect theory at generational scale.** 80% Gen Z / 75% millennial drawn to speculative investments because they feel financially behind (Northwestern Mutual); sports betting used as a financial strategy by young traders (Eric Balchunas chart); gambling and investing blur inside the same apps (Robinhood: IRA + event contracts); the "yolofolio" world. South Korea: ~110M active retail trading accounts (~2 per citizen), home ownership out of reach; FSC requiring trading exercises before leveraged single-stock ETFs.

**Illegibility + kludgeocracy.** Teles' kludgeocracy, Lowrey's time tax, Foroohar's shadow work (25% of US healthcare spend); Fed dropping forward guidance (Warsh) = more opacity (Sahm).

**Voice, resignation, exit.** ANES four-box (Hirschman): 2020/2024 "angry voter" plurality (50.6%/49.3%); youth resigned voter 23%+ in 2024 (+6 pts), >60% of under-35 say no say. Young people are the most news-trusting cohort (33%) and most-wanting-to-participate but find no legible path.
