## Narrative

IndicBankBench measures banking-assistant reliability on Indian retail-banking cases. Strict pass3 is lower than at-least-once success. The abstract reports strict reliability from 43.7% to 58.2% and at-least-once success from 60% to 74% across eleven models. The gap means a single success can overstate dependable banking behavior.

This paper is not genealogy. It is not Atto. It is not HTR. No lab steps are recorded.
