## Narrative

A calendar piece, verified as of 21 November 2026. The reusable content is the data series it restates. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-23-wublockchain-weekly-outlook-dell-earnings-spotli.md]

### AI server demand

Dell booked **$60.9 billion** of AI server orders last quarter, recognised **$16.4 billion** of revenue, and ended with a **$95 billion** backlog. The gap between orders and recognised revenue is the delivery constraint, so deliveries, margins and memory costs are the watch items. Workday reports AI agent adoption and subscription growth; HP reports AI PC demand against component costs.

### Macro series

- November flash PMIs due 23 November. The S&P Global manufacturing PMI was 54.9 in October, with factory selling-price inflation at its highest since September 2022.
- October PCE and jobless claims due 25 November. September PCE inflation was 3.6 percent year over year, core 3.1 percent. Nominal consumer spending rose 0.5 percent; real spending was nearly flat.
- Q3 GDP second estimate due 25 November. Q3 growth was 3.4 percent annualised, up from 2.2 percent in Q2, with private domestic final demand up 4.9 percent.
- Fed Beige Book due 25 November. ECB October accounts due 26 November; the deposit rate stayed at 2.50 percent.
- US markets closed 26 November for Thanksgiving and close early on 27 November.

### Read

The useful fact is the Dell backlog ratio: $60.9bn of orders against $16.4bn of revenue and a $95bn backlog. That is the AI-capex pipeline in one line, and it is the kind of number the AI-industry page tracks. The macro series are calendar markers for the inflation-regime page, not new claims.
