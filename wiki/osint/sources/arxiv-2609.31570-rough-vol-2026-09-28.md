## Narrative

A point fit of a rough-volatility model hides the uncertainty left after an implied-volatility surface is observed. The paper learns a posterior for rough Heston parameters and pushes those draws through a neural pricer. This page keeps that claim. It is not a pricing recipe and not a trade.
