## Narrative

The Anthropic IPO opened and then sold off. The author reads it as a clearing event: the "Anthropic Factors" basket sold off into the listing, and ES broke below the 7400 level he had flagged. [Source: substack-rss-capital-flows-2026-11-20-anthropic-ai-flows-and-the-coming-hiking-cycle.md]

His cross-asset observation is the useful part. ES sold off, but ZT rallied only marginally. That is a small safety bid, not a rotation. FX volatility stayed low while VIX rose. Bond volatility rose a little, well below the equity move.

The thesis: a hiking cycle is coming. The Fed will be forced to raise rates, or long-end rates blow out and the currency suffers. If equities enter that cycle from here, the downside is large. Once bond volatility actually blows out, equities can take another leg lower. [Source: substack-rss-capital-flows-2026-11-20-anthropic-ai-flows-and-the-coming-hiking-cycle.md]

This is a single newsletter opinion. The checkable claims are the relative vol moves (VIX up, FX vol flat, bond vol mildly up) and the ES level. Tag the hiking-cycle thesis `[TENTATIVE]`. The cross-asset ordering is the part worth an alert: divergence between equity vol and rate vol is measurable.
