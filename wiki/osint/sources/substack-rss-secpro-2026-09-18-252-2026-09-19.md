## Narrative

SOC work shifts from collecting to **deciding**. Humans still decide. Detection becomes software (Sigma / Hayabusa / Atomic Red Team / CALDERA as examples). Investigation is a data problem, not a single SIEM dashboard. Cyber FILE. **No PoC. No CTF writeups. 0 Integrate GO.**
