### Macro: Heavy Rare Earths (DyTb)

China + Myanmar control >90% of global DyTb production. China restricted Dy/Tb exports in April 2025. US responded with $540M in loans and preferred equity to MP Materials. Humanoid robotics projected as largest single demand driver by 2040 (~70,000 tpa REO). Adamas Intelligence forecasts supply gap by 2040 equivalent to ~116x Lynas Mt. Weld annual production, with 100%+ price increases forecast.
