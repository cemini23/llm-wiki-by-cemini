## Narrative

The note ties a policy signal to an appropriations fact. Rubio's 7 October 2026 Athens speech named drones, weapons, warfare, chips and minerals. The budget carries the trade: the FY2027 defense request is $1.45 trillion, and the missile and munitions pool is about $95 billion. The Patriot interceptor stockpile is down about 65 to 67 percent from a pre-Iran-war base of about 2,330. Iran runs past the November midterms, and a reported European intelligence assessment puts a possible Russian Article 5 test in 2027.

Eight growth names carry the theme, not the mega-cap primes. See the concept page for the list and the trade structure.

The note passed an independent fact-check and a nine-model OpenRouter audit (about $1.27 total). Corrections applied: the munitions percentage, several market caps, an award qualification, a probe description, and loose causality.
