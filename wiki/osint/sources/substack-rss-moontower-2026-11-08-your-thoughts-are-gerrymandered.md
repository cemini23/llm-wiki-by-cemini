## Narrative

This is an essay, not market content. It also runs as the lead section of Moontower #335. The full text is duplicated in that source page, so this page records the argument and its one transferable idea.

### The argument

Abdelmessih writes that he does not follow politics and does not treat that as a virtue. His claim is about reasoning, not about policy.

- **Beliefs come first, coherence comes second.** "Your beliefs are darts that you then draw a bullseye around."
- **Logic is load-bearing only when reality is the judge.** A bridge must stand, so a bridge needs logic. A tax policy does not face that test.
- **Power decides where reality is not the judge.** He supports this with the Göring line from the film *Nuremberg*: "You have your freedom and I am a prisoner because you've won and we lost. Not because you are morally superior!"

### The one transferable idea

The trading point is the useful part:

> "A trader would notice this before a commentator because a trader's greatest battles are with p/l. P/L is an agent of Reality over enough trials."

That is a **falsification gate**. A strategy is judged by profit and loss over enough trials. A narrative is judged by nothing. The same person can hold both standards at once, and the essay says most do.

The practical corollary for this wiki is the one the schema already uses. A claim needs a source. A thesis needs a disconfirming test. If a view cannot lose, it is not a view.
