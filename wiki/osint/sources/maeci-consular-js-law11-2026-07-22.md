## Narrative

### Consular a / a-bis (Prenot@Mi parallel to Interno 26185)

Statute text on consular pages matches art. 3-bis a/a-bis (consolare **or** sindaco). Operational clarifications [CONFIRMED Miami + Montreal]:

| Topic | Consular holding |
|-------|------------------|
| **Submitted** by 27 Mar 2025 23:59 Rome | Counter delivery; mail with verifiable timestamp; mail without stamp if **received** before deadline; Montreal also **Fast-It** before deadline |
| **a-bis appointment** | Email confirmation from **Prenot@Mi** **or** institutional email of the competent consular section, communicated by the cutoff |
| After cutoff | New rules |
| Fee | Adult JS recognition **€600** non-refundable (from 1 Jan 2025; Miami/Montreal) |
| Docs | Still cite Interno **K.28.1** (8 Apr 1991) forms/packet; Miami: incomplete = not processed; originals |
| Minor Issue cite | Miami points to Cass. + Interno Circ. **43347** (3 Oct 2024) |

**Contrast with K188 Circ. 26185:** Interno a-bis = **ufficio comunale** appointment. Consular a-bis = **Prenot@Mi / consular email**. Same statute letter; different evidence artifacts. [CONFIRMED]

### Law 11/2026 — central MAECI venue (primary GU)

New d.lgs. 71/2011 art. 10 [CONFIRMED from GU PDF]:

- Consulates keep: maintenance of already-recognized status; minors who are children of already-recognized citizens; certificates for those.
- **Adult** recognition abroad → presented to a **dirigenziale generale** office in MAECI central admin (not local consulate), judicial + sindaco competences untouched.
- Filing: **original paper** + fee proof, **exclusively postal service** (CAD derogation); shipping/vendor costs on applicant; MAECI may outsource receive/digitize/archive with privacy guard, user-paid.
- Post-filing communications: **electronic** (email on application, including non-PEC).
- Commencement of central presentation rules: **1 Jan of the third calendar year** after the year of entry into force → **1 Jan 2029** (entry into force 2026).
- Until that date: each consulate’s annual adult JS intake ≤ number of adult JS recognitions **concluded** 1 Jan–31 Dec of the year before the provision entered into force (i.e. **2025** outcomes); floor **≥ 100**.
- After central start: for two years, central office annual intake capped vs prior-year 7-bis fee collections (formula in art.).
- Procedure deadline: **36 months**.

Montreal already surfaces the **2028 cap** language citing L. 11 / art. 10 c.6. [CONFIRMED]

### Atto implications

- Keep `DOC_PRENOTAMI_*` + add explicit `DOC_PRENOTAMI_CONFIRM_EMAIL` / institutional consular email as **a-bis evidence candidates** — still never auto-declare grandfathered.
- Soft-flag annual **cap** through 2028 (consulate-specific; floor 100).
- Soft-flag **2029**: paper mail to central MAECI; address/vendor TBD until MAECI implementing notice.
