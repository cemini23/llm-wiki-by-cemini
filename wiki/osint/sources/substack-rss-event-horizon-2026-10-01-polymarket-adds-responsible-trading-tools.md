## Narrative

Polymarket launched user-protection and responsible-trading tools. Named features: voluntary self-exclusion with periods from 30 days to lifetime; deposit limits by day, week, or month; and mental-health resources. Polymarket is expanding its Trust and Safety team, publishing community guidelines and moderation standards, and opening a Trust and Safety Center. It partnered with Birches Health for virtual care across all 50 states.

Other items in the briefing: a CFTC probe of Adam Kinzinger over Kalshi trades; a Kalshi funding round targeting about a $40B valuation, up from $11B in December and $22B in March.

Volumes reported: Kalshi on 29 September did about $2.2B, split 61.1% parlays, 18.3% sports, 16.1% crypto. Trailing seven-day: Kalshi $16.7B, Polymarket US $2.95B, DKeX $608M, CDNA $501M, Novig $440M, Rothera $295M, Underdog $203M, ProphetX $64.5M. Polymarket US 15-minute Bitcoin markets did about $92M in their first full week.
