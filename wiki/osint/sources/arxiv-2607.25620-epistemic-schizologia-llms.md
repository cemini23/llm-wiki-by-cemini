## Narrative

Accepts “Epistemia” (fluency mistaken for warranted knowledge) but rejects agent-vs-agent pipeline comparison. Proposes **epistemic schizologia**: cleavage between linguistically finished signs and socially embedded circuits of evidence / criticism / responsibility. LLM reframed as techno-semiotic machine automating a phase of written semiosis — not a defective human knower. Unit of analysis = full practice (prompt → interpret → verify → contest → consequential use). Design programme: inspectable genealogy, contestability, distributed responsibility.

**Route:** wiki-only (+ light CCC verification culture). No Phase-0.
