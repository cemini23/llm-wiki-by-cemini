## Narrative

Ten-item crypto and macro weekly. Headlines, not levels.

**Ethereum roadmap.** Vitalik Buterin published a piece projecting the 2030 roadmap: few-slot finality, PeerDAS, SNARK verification, and private mempools. He calls next year's upgrade the last "regular" one.

**Regulation.** The SEC chair advanced tokenised-securities rules and an innovation exemption. ESMA proposed amendments and a new DeFi-gateway category. The SEC also proposed a crypto custody framework. The lobbying figure is worth noting: over $13M in the first half, about $8M of it on one bill, which failed a Senate procedural threshold.

**Institutional.** HSBC unveiled a tokenised deposit product. Morgan Stanley opened a digital asset lab. El Salvador launched a national super app on a public chain with a flat cross-border fee.

**One prediction-market line.** **Kalshi is reported in a roughly $1B round at about a $40B valuation.** That is a valuation, not a price level.

**One instrument-level figure.** A corporate bitcoin vehicle's preferred stock carries an annualised dividend yield of roughly 12%. A yield is not a watch price.

Other figures: an on-chain real-world-asset perpetual futures market at about $1.4T annualised in July; a $110M round for a payments firm.
