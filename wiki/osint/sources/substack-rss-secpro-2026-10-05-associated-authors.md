## Narrative

A contributor list. Low news value, but it names the **agentic-security standards people** worth tracking:

- **Idan Habler** (Ph.D., Ben-Gurion). Co-lead of the **OWASP Securing Agentic Applications** project. Architecting **OWASP Agentic TOP 10**, **Agent Name Service (ANS)**, and the **Agent-to-Agent Secure (A2AS)** protocol.
- **Vineeth Sai Narajala** — GenAI security engineer at AWS; AI/ML, cloud, app security.
- **Orhan Yıldırım** — offensive security and agentic-AI security.
- Miriam C. Wiesner (Microsoft) — PowerShell tooling (EventList, JEAnalyzer).
- Joylynn Kirui — cloud/DevSecOps/AI security. Lester Nichols III — JPMorgan Chase security architecture.
