## Narrative

RSS teaser. Shopify adopted React Native in 2020 to speed Android launches. It migrated six apps and reported 95% shared code. It now reverses that decision and says native is the future of mobile.

The stated reason is that AI coding agents now do the Swift and Kotlin implementation, translation, testing, and review. Shared-code savings no longer cover the cost of losing platform access. The Shop app was rebuilt native in 12 weeks.

Named: Shopify, Farhan Thawar, Mustafa Ali, Airbnb, Kotlin Multiplatform.

This is a software-engineering item. It is out of domain for our trading workstreams. The one transferable line: an agent that removes a language barrier can flip a previously settled platform decision. Record the pattern. Do not act.
