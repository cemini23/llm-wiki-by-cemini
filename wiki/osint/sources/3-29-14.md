## Narrative

This paper presents a modular RL-based navigation component for Embodied Semantic Scene Graph Generation, modernising the policy-optimisation method so embodied agents can trade off information gain against navigation cost while constructing semantic scene graphs within a limited action budget. The contribution is an embodied-robotics navigation / computer-vision method. This is **out-of-scope** for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda — embodied navigation and scene-graph generation have no transfer path to financial-market modeling or OSINT workflows.
