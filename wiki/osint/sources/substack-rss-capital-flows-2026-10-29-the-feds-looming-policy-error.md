## Narrative

Capital Flows published this note on 2026-10-29. The file is an RSS teaser. The header says the full post may be paywalled. The teaser stops at "Read more". It points to a video and a Macro Intelligence Pack. Those items are not in the file.

The author says the Fed held rates in a passive stance at the FOMC meeting that day. The author calls the hold explicit confirmation that the Fed is ignoring inflation. The author says positioning underestimates this policy error. The author says market participants will keep downplaying it. This is a single-source claim.

The author says the financial industry is built to keep investors in a 60/40 stock-bond portfolio. The author says industry narratives downplay upside risk to inflation and downside risk to growth. The stated motive is fees. If investors expect higher inflation and lower growth, they move to cash or short bonds, and establishment fees fall. The author also says investors avoid risk work and still want large returns. The author says the investor alone is responsible for returns, through an advisor, a hedge fund, private investments, or a business.

The author says the Fed makes errors, as hedge funds and traders do. The author says a Fed error hits the whole global monetary system. The author calls interest rates the asymmetrical linchpin of the financial system. The author says the money supply is linked directly to interest rates.

The author cites Kevin Warsh. Warsh said they want inflation down to 2%. The author says the Fed paused and made ZERO attempt to guide more hikes into the forward curve. The author says a curve that prices FEWER hikes while inflation rises does the opposite of that goal. The author says easing into rising inflation stimulates inflation and opens credit, so more money flows from financial institutions into the economy. The author says the absence of forward guidance can create as many policy errors as the presence of forward guidance. The author says the pause had no clear reason beyond a wait, and asks why current signals do not merit a hike. The 2% target, the ZERO hike guidance, and the FEWER-hikes path are this author's words. The file has no inflation print, no curve level, and no data table behind those figures.
