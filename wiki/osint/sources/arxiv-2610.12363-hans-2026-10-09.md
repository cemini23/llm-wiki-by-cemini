## Narrative

HANS is a dataset of student handwritten math sheets. Pages mix formulas, words, tables, and strike-through corrections. The paper adds NA-GOT, a two-stage noise filter. It says current parsers struggle on this set.

The PDF licence is only the arXiv non-exclusive distribution licence. The paper says the dataset will be public upon publication. That is not a permissive grant for the genealogy kit. No Atto brief.

The shape (noisy handwriting plus layout) is adjacent to archive scans. The licence blocks a kit brief.
