## Narrative

Public loss figure: the exchange says about **$351.6 million** was affected. Containment claim: cold wallets remain secure. User balances were not rewritten. A user protection fund of more than $464 million can cover the loss. Withdrawals are paused. Deposits and trading remain open. No transfer method is recorded. On-chain analysts observed roughly $180 million in publicly labeled addresses. Two figures currently coexist: $351.6 million is the exchange's internal accounting; roughly $180 million is the on-chain tally.

**Cemini posture:** news claim only. **No level to arm.** Never auto-arm. FILE. **0 product Integrate GO.**
