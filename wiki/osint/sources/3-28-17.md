## Narrative

Standard networking/ML paper proposing **ASMS** (Adaptive Social Metaverse Streaming), a federated multi-agent RL system for adaptive bit-rate (ABR) selection in XR headset streaming over MEC-enabled social metaverse platforms. The core algorithm, **F-MAPPO** (Federated Multi-Agent PPO), combines FedAvg-style federated learning (to keep biometric/behavioral data on-device) with multi-agent PPO (so each headset's local agent adapts bit rate cooperatively under shared bandwidth), adding local differential privacy (Laplacian noise on gradient uploads) to the FL aggregation step. The modeling formalism is a Dec-POMDP with a six-tuple state (target bit rate, received bit rate, MTP latency, jitter, packet loss, NACKs) and a bespoke time-step QoE reward combining scene quality, choppiness penalty, latency penalty, and a stability/disruption term; QoE coefficients were calibrated from an 8-participant MOS study (R² = 0.92).

Experimental setup is real hardware: 2× RTX 3090 rendering servers, an RTX 4090 global-agent server, 3× Meta Quest 2 + 2× HTC Vive + 1× HoloLens 2, Unreal Engine 5.3, WebRTC transport, Clumsy for network-impairment injection, across 6 synthetic network scenarios (5G edge → 4G LTE → congestion → recovery). Against 7 baselines (IPPO, SAC, GreenABR, Ruyi, ARTEMIS, GCC, BBR), F-MAPPO claims ≥14% QoE gain headline — numbers are 0.85–0.97 QoE vs 0.55–0.92 for baselines across scenarios. Communication overhead is ~0.5 MB/round/device, GPU load 74.5% vs 68.2% centralized MAPPO — modest. This is competent engineering-grade RL research, not a breakthrough.

## Key findings

- **F-MAPPO algorithm**: federated + multi-agent PPO for ABR streaming in multi-user XR, with LDP Laplacian noise on gradient uploads and dynamically-weighted FedAvg aggregation every 4 iterations.
- **QoE model**: time-step QoE with 5 terms (scene quality with user-density decay, choppiness, MTP latency, stability, disruption); coefficients (α=1, β=0.4, γ=0.2, δ₁=0.6, δ₂=0.5) fit from 8-participant MOS study at R²=0.92.
- **Empirical result**: ≥14% QoE improvement over 7 baselines (IPPO, SAC, GreenABR, Ruyi, ARTEMIS, GCC, BBR); strongest gains in degraded scenarios (4G LTE, congestion, recovery).
- **Real-hardware testbed**: 2× RTX 3090 rendering servers + RTX 4090 FL coordinator + Meta Quest 2 / HTC Vive / HoloLens 2 clients over WebRTC, Unreal Engine 5.3.
- **Overhead**: 0.5 MB/round/device, 3 MB total per 6-device aggregation, GPU utilization 74.5% (F-MAPPO) vs 68.2% (centralized MAPPO) — FL overhead "negligible" per authors.
- **Crypto content**: none substantive. Blockchain mentioned once in intro as enabling tech; NFTs/Decentraland cited once each. **Cluster assignment misfit.**
