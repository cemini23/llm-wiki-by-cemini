## Narrative

Integrative review: software/systems engineering shifts from artifact authorship → direction, verification, and governance of autonomous systems. Introduces **ACCEL** (Agentic Competencies through Curricula, Collaboration, and Enduring Learning) with five pillars: intent specification, orchestration/delegation, V&V/critical eval, ethical governance, adaptive self-directed learning.
