## Narrative

Podcast episode with **Maggie Appleton**, staff research engineer at **GitHub Next**. Appleton builds prototypes that explore how software engineers might collaborate with AI in new ways. Her background spans design, anthropology, and web development. She was the first designer hired by AI startup Elicit, and lead design engineer at Normally.

The episode is more visual than usual because Appleton brought her notebook, with prototype pages shown on video. The episode title frames the topic as what other disciplines can learn from designers and design engineers.
