## Narrative

Klement summarizes McKinsey's UK-vs-US/Germany operational-management critique, then cites Bloom et al. (2026, LSE): UK firms' ability to forecast staff turnover and UK GDP. Better-managed and higher-productivity firms have **lower forecast error** on both series, know it, report higher confidence, and allocate resources more proactively.

**Concrete claim `[TENTATIVE]`:** management quality correlates with forecast error on GDP and employee turnover (four figures in the post).
