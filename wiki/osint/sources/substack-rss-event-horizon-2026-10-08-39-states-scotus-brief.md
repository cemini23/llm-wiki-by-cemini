## Narrative

### The amicus — 39 states back New Jersey

On Wednesday, **39 states filed an amicus brief** supporting New Jersey's SCOTUS petition to rule that **federal law does not preempt state gambling laws** for sports event contracts. **The NFL also sided with New Jersey.** The states span the political spectrum.

The brief's core argument: prediction markets claim a classic sports bet is a **"swap"** in the CFTC's exclusive jurisdiction, so states "can do nothing to regulate those transactions." The states answer that markets "cannot strip the States of their core sovereign power through **relabeling and window-dressing**." Their certiorari grounds: state sovereignty, the **major-questions doctrine**, citizen protection, and a **circuit split**.

**Ten states did not join** — Texas and Florida are the largest. But **Kentucky, Montana, Tennessee, and Washington** have all taken steps against sports event contracts anyway. **IAGR and NAGRA** (gaming regulators incl. tribal) filed too, arguing sports betting is **Class III gaming under IGRA** and that out-of-compact sales break the terms tribes negotiated.

**The circuit picture:** the **Third Circuit** upheld an injunction for **Kalshi** against New Jersey, but Kalshi **lost in the Ninth and Sixth circuits** — a live split.

### Other hard news

- **Coinbase agreed to halt sports-related event contracts in Michigan** by 2026-10-10, and to close open positions — following a near-identical deal with **Robinhood Derivatives** and an injunction against KalshiEX.
- **Kalshi launched prediction apps with four Native American tribes** (California, Oklahoma), **dividing Indian country**; hundreds of tribes oppose. Tribal casino gaming was **>$46 B in 2025** (NIGC).
- **Bloomberg:** **longshots dominate.** Since 2025, ~half of Kalshi volume is one side chasing ≥**1,000%** returns; recently >**65%**. Average yes-longshots **lost 15% on Kalshi, 27% on Polymarket** before fees (up to 7%).
- **Kalshi bid for a perpetual-style oil contract** to the CFTC; CME shelved its round-the-clock oil contract after pushback.
- **Paid-posting scandal:** Current Revolt reported a media outfit offering cash to post anti-Kalshi content; the space has paid-influencer history on both sides.
