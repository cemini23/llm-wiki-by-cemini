## Narrative

This is a satire post with one underlying study. The author marks it "Warning: Satire".

The study, by Patel et al. (2026), looks at US traffic accidents from 2017 to 2022 against the release days of the ten most-streamed albums in a single day. On those release days, music streaming rose typically by 43%. Traffic fatalities rose too: about 20 more deaths than on the ten days before or after the release. [Source: substack-rss-klement-2026-11-20-taylor-swift-kills-people.md]

The causal story offered is driver distraction from a new album. The satirical proposals are insurance liability, driving bans and streaming blocks in cars.

Relevance to this wiki: none for trading. It is recorded for completeness and as an example of a natural-experiment design: a sharp, dated event, a large exposed population, and a difference against a local baseline window. That design transfers. The result itself is a single study and should be tagged `[TENTATIVE]` if used.
