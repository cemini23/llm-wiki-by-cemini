## Narrative

**Republished content.** WuBlockchain states that the article is republished for information only, and does not constitute investment advice.

The thesis: if the goal is pricing power over an underlying asset, do not challenge Nasdaq where it is strongest. Move the battlefield to perpetuals, 24/7 trading, leverage, on-chain inventory, and decentralized market makers. Use perps to seize price discovery. Use bStocks to absorb inventory and correct mispricing.

The author draws a historical analogy to the Chicago Board of Trade. CBOT standardized grain grades and appointed inspectors (1859), then institutionalized margin requirements and delivery rules (1865). The warehouse behind the futures contract kept futures from becoming gambling detached from physical goods.
