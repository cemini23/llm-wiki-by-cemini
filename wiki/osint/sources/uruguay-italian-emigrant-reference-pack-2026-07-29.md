## Narrative

Uruguay uniquely splits **ciudadanía** and **nacionalidad**. *Carta de ciudadanía legal* (Art. 75) is treated by Corte Electoral / Italian Montevideo practice as a naturalization-like event for exclusive-Italian proof, even though Uruguayan doctrine often says legal citizens never become nationals. Creates administrative blockade + judicial argument path under Law 555/1912.

DGREC: secular from **1879**; digital partidas mostly post-2022 / foreign acts post-2015; historical = manuscript + local Intendencias. Online digital ID (gub.uy / TuID) locks non-residents. Apostille: MRREE Cuareim 1384 — physical presentation for manuscript partidas; remote diaspora usually needs **Carta Poder** + gestor.

Montevideo Embassy: Prenot@Mi; L74 exclusive / 2-year residency; **€600** non-refundable evaluation fee; Traductor Público on consular roster + consular legalization of translation (not second UY apostille).
