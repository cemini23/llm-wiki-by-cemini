## Narrative

ICLR 2026 submission proposing DG-MAPPO, a fully distributed multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) framework that removes the centralized-training-decentralized-execution (CTDE) assumption dominant in MAPPO/HAPPO/MADDPG. Core contribution is a Distributed Graph Attention Network (D-GAT) that infers global state via multi-hop peer-to-peer message passing, combined with decentralized SGD (D-SGD) parameter averaging and a consensus regularization loss.

Methodology is rigorous and cleanly grounded in prior work (GATv2, D-SGD, multi-agent policy gradient theorem from Zhang et al. 2018). Agents optimize local PPO-clipped objectives over augmented observations `[o_i || ô_i]` where ô_i comes from K-hop D-GAT inference, with parameter averaging across neighbors each step.

Evaluated on StarCraft II Multi-Agent Challenge (SMAC), Google Research Football, and Multi-Agent MuJoCo. Results claim CTDE-parity or better on 10 SMAC maps (including 25-agent 25m, Hard+ MMM2, 6h vs 8z). The paper provides explicit communication-cost analysis: O(N) for DG-MAPPO vs O(N^2) for multi-hop CTDE routing, plus physical energy-scaling discussion (path-loss exponent α ∈ [2,4]).

Relevance to infrastructure: demonstrates that mesh-style peer-to-peer coordination can outperform central-server gather/scatter when communication is constrained — directly analogous to distributed-agent trading architectures where a central orchestrator becomes a bottleneck. Relevance to rl-marl is primary — this is a strong methodological artifact for decentralized-agent designs. Honest limit: benchmarks are game/robotics, not trading; mapping to financial multi-agent settings is inferential.

## Key findings

- DG-MAPPO matches/exceeds MAPPO, HAPPO, MAT-Dec on 10 SMAC scenarios (e.g., MMM2 Hard+: 98.9% vs 91.2% best baseline; 6h vs 8z: 95.0% vs 93.8%).
- Near-optimal win rates achievable with only K=1 hop in moderately complex scenarios; N/2 hops match CTDE globally.
- Communication cost scales O(N) for DG-MAPPO vs O(N^2) for multi-hop CTDE under constrained topology (1-D worst-case).
- Consensus regularization (α_consensus=20) most beneficial at 6-hop budget; non-detrimental at 1-hop.
- First distributed MARL approach claiming CTDE-level performance at 25-agent team size (25m SMAC map).
