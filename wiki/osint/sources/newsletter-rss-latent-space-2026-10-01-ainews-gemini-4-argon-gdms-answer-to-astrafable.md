## Narrative

RSS digest. Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Argon, its first larger-than-Flash model since February. The reported targets are coding, enterprise knowledge work, and cyber defense. Access starts limited to government users under a named program, with no open developer access at the time of writing.

Reported figures: first on 13 of 19 benchmarks, output length up to 1M tokens from 64K, $4 and $20 per million tokens with a 50% introductory rate, a 95% cached-input discount, and a 15% hallucination rate against 51% for Astra.

One security item is worth recording separately. The digest reports a hidden-reasoning extraction campaign that OpenAI attributes to individuals linked to Moonshot AI.

This is a model-release headline plus one security item. The benchmark claims are the vendor's own numbers. It does not change `/route`. Do not swap `/route`. No level to arm. FILE only.
