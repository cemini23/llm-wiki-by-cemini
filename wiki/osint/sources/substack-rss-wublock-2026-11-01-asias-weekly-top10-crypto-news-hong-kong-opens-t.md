## Narrative

The newsletter is a ten-item Asia crypto list from Wu Blockchain. The file dates the post to 2026-11-01. Each figure below is a single-source claim from this newsletter.

Hong Kong opened secondary trading for SFC-authorised tokenized funds. Investors can buy and sell units on licensed virtual-asset platforms, on the platform or through a connecting broker. The newsletter says the rule set is similar to the ETF rule set. Primary-market units and secondary-market units can move both ways. The newsletter names four risks: a premium or a discount from supply and demand, thin liquidity outside usual hours, different prices across platforms, and reliance on one market maker. BlackRock Financial Management, BlackRock Japan, SBI Holdings, and Startale Group signed a memorandum of understanding. They will explore tokenized money-market funds linked to the yen stablecoin JPYSC. The newsletter states that BlackRock's tokenized fund BUIDL has assets under management of approximately 2.17 billion US dollars.

Thailand's WORPT launched under the Tourist DigiPay sandbox. Foreign tourists can convert USDT and USDC into Thai baht and pay with QR PromptPay. Waan Exchange and Wipay built the app. The newsletter names Tether, Visa, and The Mall Group as partners. Merchants receive Thai baht through existing payment systems. WORPT plans a physical Visa prepaid card in mid-November 2026 and a virtual card in early 2027. On the first anniversary of JPYC, chief executive Noritaka Okabe said cumulative issuance exceeded 10 billion yen. He said actual circulation stands at around 3 billion yen, one quarter of the internal target. The newsletter cites a 1 million yen cap on one issuance or one redemption. After a September listing on Upbit, the price rose to 2 to 3 yen. Okabe plans to expand circulation by at least tenfold in the second year.

Russia set a knowledge test for crypto investors on regulated platforms. Brokerages may offer the test from the end of November. A pass on one platform counts on other regulated platforms. A non-qualified investor may trade whitelist assets after the test. South Korea may reclassify cryptocurrency exchanges as financial enterprises and levy an education tax after the Digital Asset Basic Act. The newsletter estimates an annual tax of approximately 10.2 billion won for Dunamu, the operator of Upbit, and approximately 3.2 billion won for Bithumb. The estimate uses the financial-industry rate and 2025 revenue estimates. The tax uses operating revenue. KuCoin stopped all products and services for users in Russia and Belarus with immediate effect. The newsletter says MEXC stopped service for those users on October 20, and that Bitget also announced restrictions. CoinDCX set futures to reduce-only mode after developments at a global liquidity provider. Users cannot open a new position or increase a position. They can still change margin and reduce or close a position. The exchange said user funds are safe and that deposits and withdrawals still run.

The FBI attributed a Binance cryptocurrency theft of over 6 billion US dollars to North Korea and named the activity TraderTraitor. The notice says the attackers are converting stolen assets into ETH. It says they may launder funds through DeFi and instant-swap services. The FBI asked node operators, swap platforms, and other virtual-asset service providers to block listed addresses. Chad Barraford, co-founder of THORChain, said node operators hold no non-public information. In a separate item, a British Virgin Islands court appointed joint provisional liquidators for 30 entities controlled by Chen Zhi. The liquidators said a claim process is not yet in place. If formal liquidation proceeds and funds are available, victims of telecom fraud and online gambling from China and other regions may file claims.

The list records venue access and stablecoin size for a quant-finance and macro reader. Tokenized-fund units can trade at a premium or a discount to the primary market. JPYC circulation is around 3 billion yen against cumulative issuance above 10 billion yen, and the 1 million yen cap limits one issuance. Exchange exits and a reduce-only futures mode change who can hold risk. A theft of over 6 billion US dollars, as the newsletter reports the FBI notice, is a large ETH and DeFi flow item.
