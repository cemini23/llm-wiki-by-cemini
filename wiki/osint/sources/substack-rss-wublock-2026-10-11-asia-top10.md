## Narrative

The Asia roundup. The compliance and custody items are the ones to keep.

- **Kyrgyzstan terminates USDKG** — the national gold-backed stablecoin — and **liquidates its issuer EVA** and Coin Nomad Exchange, the country's first state-owned crypto exchange. Launch was Nov 2025; ~50M tokens. **In May 2026 the UK sanctioned the issuer** on suspicion of supporting Russia. Holders may redeem for fiat or USDT.
- **South Korea** opened consultation on the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act decree to **tighten cross-border virtual-asset transfers**: operators must **register in advance**, meet facility and two-professional-staff requirements, and **report transfer records via the Bank of Korea** — shareable with the National Tax Service, Customs, FSS, and FIU.
- **Russia: miners only get Class 4 grid connection** (Resolution №1300) — meaning they are **first in line for power cuts** during shortages.
- **China**: the CPC Central Committee and State Council call for a **national integrated computing power network and national blockchain network** (with "East Data, West Computing", "AI Plus", embodied AI, quantum, BCI).
- **Project Trinity (Japan)**: nine institutions completed **Phase 2 cross-chain DvP atomic settlement** — ST corporate bonds against trust-type stablecoins, verifying a **T+2 cycle**. Participants include SMBC, Daiwa, SBI Securities, Progmat, Datachain.
- **Dubai VARA** clarified reserve audits: VASPs must hold **≥100% of client liabilities at all times**, at **1:1 in the same virtual assets**, with **daily reconciliation**; audits cover hot/warm/cold and third-party wallets, and check for **rehypothecation**.
- **HKMA × Bank Negara Malaysia**: joint working group on **cross-border stablecoins and tokenisation**, plus RTGS/CSD interconnection.
- **Tether × National Bank of Kazakhstan × Alatau City**: MoU on a **tenge-pegged stablecoin**, tokenisation (possibly Tether's Hadron), and an Almaty pilot.
- **Thailand SEC**: 11 crypto-ETF rules effective **Oct 16** — **Bitcoin and Ethereum only** in phase one; listed on SET, passive, ≥80% net exposure, regulated custodians; **no securities-firm financing**.
- **Binance:** a WSJ-reported internal investigation found a network linked to sanctioned Iranian financier **Babak Zanjani** using **21 accounts** (~**$850M** volume); ≥$67M moved to wallets linked to the IRGC. Binance says it blocked and liquidated the accounts.

### Why it matters here

The **VARA reserve standard** and the **Korea transfer regime** are the reusable rules; the Kyrgyzstan shutdown is a **sanction-driven unwind** case. **No xsp scp.**
