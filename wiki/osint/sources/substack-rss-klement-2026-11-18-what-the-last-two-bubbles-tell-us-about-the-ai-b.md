## Narrative

A pointer to Klement's Reuters column. The claim is that Wall Street analysts were overwhelmingly bullish at the peak of the 1990s tech bubble and on the eve of the 2008 crisis, and that the same is true of AI stocks today while bad news accumulates. The open question in the teaser is whether the boom ends with a bang or a slow deflation. [Source: substack-rss-klement-2026-11-18-what-the-last-two-bubbles-tell-us-about-the-ai-b.md]

The only evidence named is analyst recommendation distributions at the two earlier peaks. The charts are referenced but not reproduced in the free text. The full column is behind the Reuters paywall.

Use: a sentiment-regime marker, not a timing tool. Analyst-bullishness concentration is measurable and is a reasonable slow-moving input to a bubble flag. It says nothing about when a top occurs. Tag `[TENTATIVE]`.
