## Narrative

### The study

FactSet's 2023 integration of generative AI is used as a natural experiment. The question is what changes when analysts get an AI assistant inside their data terminal. [Source: substack-rss-klement-2026-11-24-the-upside-of-ai-for-investment-analysis.md]

### Results

- FactSet appears as a cited source in about 25 percent of analyst reports in Q3 2023, rising to about 50 percent by the end of 2024.
- The number of third-party sources cited by FactSet users rose about **26 percent**.
- The number of topics covered per report rose about **24 percent**.
- The number of analyses and tools used per report rose about **21 percent**.
- Reports produced with FactSet were, on average, released faster.

Usage rose most among smaller firms and larger teams. That suggests existing subscribers used the feature more rather than new users switching terminals.

### The caveat

The study did not test for hallucinations. Klement says so plainly, and adds his own practice: check the outputs. As an investor, the stated takeaway is that AI makes analyst research timelier, broader and more quantitative.

### Read

This is the positive half of a two-part pair. It measures output quantity and speed, not accuracy. The natural follow-up is the next post, on model herding, which is the downside. Both are indexed in `concepts/ai-in-investment-research.md`.
