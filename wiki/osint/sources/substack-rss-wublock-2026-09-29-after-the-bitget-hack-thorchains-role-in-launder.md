## Narrative

Headline summary only. On 24 September about $387.5M left Bitget's hot and warm wallets. Bitget says private keys and cold wallets were not compromised, and that attackers breached backend wallet infrastructure and manipulated transaction data so the system's own authorization approved the transfers. Detection was at 18:31 UTC.

Bitget says the method suggests a North Korea-linked cluster. The assets were split and routed through THORChain toward bitcoin. Bitget's CEO asked THORChain to deny service to the addresses. THORChain replied that it is decentralized and permissionless.

Named: Bitget, THORChain, BNB, TRX, XRP, Bybit, Chainflip, NEAR Intents, Wasabi CoinJoin, MistTrack, OKX, RUNE.
