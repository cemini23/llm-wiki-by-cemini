## Narrative

AraMS-28k is positioned as the largest publicly released **line-level** Arabic manuscript dataset: **14 books**, **3043 pages**, **28600 lines**. License is **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** — non-commercial share-alike; do not fold into proprietary Atto training without counsel. Dataset includes **insertion anchors** useful for layout-aware HTR. Pair with RefLAM Confidence-100 annotation discipline. Atto steal: dataset schema + insertion-anchor idea for FIND manuscript surfaces; respect NC/SA before any local fine-tune.
