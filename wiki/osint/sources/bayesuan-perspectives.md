## Narrative

This paper analyzes three landmark medical clinical trials (ECMO, CALGB 49907, I-SPY 2) through a unified Bayesian framework spanning prior specification, sequential adaptation, and decision-theoretic optimization, contributing exact backward induction for two-arm binary trials via Beta-Binomial conjugacy and a Pólya-Gamma augmentation bridge to covariate-adjusted logistic regression. It is a biostatistics / clinical-trial-design paper aimed at the 2026 FDA draft guidance on Bayesian methodology. It is out-of-scope for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda: although it shares Bayesian decision theory and adaptive-design machinery, the application domain (patient-sparing medical trial design) has no path to financial-market modeling or the CeminiSuite roadmap.
