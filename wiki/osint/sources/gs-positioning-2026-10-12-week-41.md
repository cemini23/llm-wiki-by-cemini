## Narrative

**The single clearest number: CTA exposure has collapsed.** GS models CTAs long roughly **$43.5bn** of global equities, **down from $125bn a month ago** and **$67bn last week**. Baseline flow forecasts imply trend followers **sell $3.5bn** over the coming week if prices and vol persist — with **$5.5bn of buying in the US** from SPX and NDX, where modelled trend signals stay positive.

### Positioning table

| Measure | Level | Percentile (1y) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global book gross leverage | 308.7% | 60th | +0.7 pts |
| Global book net leverage | 75.9% | **10th** | −1.5 pts |
| Overall book L/S ratio | 1.652 | **14th** | −1.2% |
| Fundamental L/S gross | 209.5% | 43rd | +3.3 pts |
| Fundamental L/S net | 52.9% | **6th** | −0.2 pts |

**The pattern the desk names: funds are adding exposure while leaning bearish.** Gross up, net down — "no offense being played by the fundamental community", uncomfortable with indices at or near record highs.

### Other observations from the notes

- **Info Tech was by far the most net-sold US sector** (−1.0 SDs 1y) after being the most net bought in 4 of the past 5 weeks — triggered by OpenAI revenue headlines, which the desk reads as a marker of where positioning is fullest.
- **US-listed ETF shorts rose +4.4%** week-on-week (+6.2% month-on-month), led by Large Cap Equity and Tech ETFs, partly offset by covering in Financials.
- **Buybacks are light** — corporates sidelined in the pre-earnings blackout. Roughly **10%** expected in open window by week's end, ~1–2 days after each release.
- **Sentiment bounced to neutral**, with the GS Sentiment Indicator the exception.
- **Marginal buyers exist** if the oil and rates overhangs recede.

### Why it is filed here

Positioning data is **context for interpretation, not a signal**. The value is the CTA number and the gross-up/net-down divergence: mechanical flow support is sharply lower than a month ago while discretionary funds remain under-invested.

**No trade, no scp.**
