## Narrative

Osmani (14+ years Chrome/DevTools/CWV, then AI DX) on the PE podcast. Preingest flagged `https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/what-is-loop-engineering` as LIKELY vs the 2026-07-14 source page. That URL is a **cite inside this episode**, not a re-download of the earlier post. Ingest proceeds.

**Concrete claim `[TENTATIVE]`:** the big risk of AI-assisted development is **cognitive surrender** — erosion of comprehension of the problems being worked on, and of memory of what is going on. Push back by understanding every major decision an LLM makes (reading the entire reasoning trace is no longer practical). Aim for **mutual amplification**: the agent logs decisions and key learnings; the human reviews and internalizes. Accountability cannot be delegated to a model. Worker ≠ verifier. Do not unattended auto-evolve `.cursor/skills/*`.
