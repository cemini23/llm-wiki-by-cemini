## Narrative

The September mining roundup. The single theme: **public miners are pivoting from Bitcoin to AI/HPC**, and mining economics are bad.

### The pivot (the headline)

- CoinShares Q2 2026: **AI compute ≈ $1.5 M/MW annualised profit vs ~$500 k/MW for Bitcoin mining** — a 3× gap. Firms with contracted AI/HPC trade at **12.9× EV/NTM Sales** vs **3.7×** without.
- **IREN's AI Cloud revenue exceeded mining revenue** for the first time; **HPC lease = 71% of TeraWulf revenue**; **Core Scientific paid $41.9 M to cancel ~15 EH/s of miner orders**.
- Miners with AI are **+21% YTD**; miners without are **−8%** (CryptoQuant).
- Publicly listed miners cut active BTC hashrate **~23%**; HPC/AI revenue has surpassed mining revenue, but **conversion spend is ~15× the revenue so far**.

### Mining economics

- CoinShares: weighted-avg **pre-tax cash mining cost ~$75,500/BTC** in Q2 2026, **above** the quarter-end price of ~$58,400. **Hashprice hit a record low $27.7/PH/s/day** in June.
- JPMorgan: BTC briefly rose above its **~$85,000 production-cost** estimate after 280 days below it. Production cost is a "soft floor."
- Network hashrate fell **~19%** from its October 2025 high; difficulty −15%.

### Power and policy

- **Ethiopia cut power to Bitcoin miners ~75%** after El Niño reduced hydropower inflows. Miners had been ~35% of Ethiopian Electric Power revenue and ~1/3 of the country's ~9,730 MW.
- **Sweden:** Skatteverket seeks ~**SEK 540 M (~$58 M)** in back taxes from six crypto firms in Boden; a HIVE subsidiary entered restructuring. HIVE plans to **wind down Swedish mining**.
- Kazakhstan: plan to mine on **flared associated gas**. US House: two crypto tax bills (mining/staking deferral; wash-sale rules).
- **Zcash mining is unusually profitable** — ~2× BTC daily rewards per machine, ~4× per MWh (Grayscale); total Zcash hashrate up >2.5× this year.

### Security / legal notes

- **Ethereum Classic** social-engineering: an unreviewed `core-geth v1.13.0` release was promoted as a "security update." **Same incident reported in the K283 WuBlock** — see @sources/substack-rss-wublock-2026-10-06-september-blockchain-tech.md.
- **Coinmint** shareholder suit: allegations of 448.7 BTC moved to a personal wallet; RICO claims; $570 M cumulative profits alleged.
- **Illicit mining:** Mexico (300 GPUs, hydro theft), Paraguay (35 machines), Malaysia (30 machines) — drug-trafficking groups mining on stolen power (Chainalysis).

### Why it matters here

A **miner-to-AI capex signal** — the strongest in the batch. Feeds the crypto/AI-infrastructure macro read. **No xsp scp.**
