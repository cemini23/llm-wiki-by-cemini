## Narrative

Behavioral anchoring for $299: indie Mac ($29–$99) and genealogy hobby (~$70) are **wrong** anchors; single professional service (~$300 apostille line) and full-service JS ($5k–$15k) are **right** anchors.

**Kit framing:** sell “Complete DIY kit” (software + checklists + guides), not “app.” Avoid arbitrary bloat kits.

**Comparison table:** DIY spreadsheet vs full-service agency vs specialized kit — Goldilocks / extremeness aversion.

**Tier recommendation:** optional decoy Standard (~$189) vs Kit ($299). **Operator deferred full marketing site** → lean launch can keep **single $299 SKU** and still use paradigm comparison + kit language without building a multi-tier commerce UX.

**Language hazard:** “Automated lineage validation safeguards” in comparison table is UPL-adjacent — rephrase to checklist / soft-flag / user-approve only.

**Refund:** prefers **14-day** with license revoke; argues against no-refund-on-download for high-ticket trust + chargeback defense.
