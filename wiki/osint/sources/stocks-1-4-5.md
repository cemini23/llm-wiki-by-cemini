## Narrative

Undergraduate conceptual paper published in the International Journal of Management (a low-impact/pay-to-publish journal; "Journal Impact Factor 9.678" is self-reported via GISI, not a credible metric). The piece argues that blockchain-based tokenization of equities ("digital stocks") could displace or complement traditional stock exchanges by enabling 24/7 peer-to-peer settlement, eliminating brokers/clearing intermediaries, and reducing settlement times from T+3 to near-real-time. It walks through Blockchain 101 (transparency, decentralization, immutability), lists institutional initiatives (NASDAQ, ASX/CHESS replacement, HKEX, LSE–IBM partnership from July 2018), and points at DX Exchange as the first mover in NASDAQ-powered EU-regulated tokenized stock trading.

Methodologically this is a literature review/opinion piece, not original research — no experiments, no model, no data. Evidence is limited to secondary sources (Disruptor Daily, Medium posts, NASDAQ PR) and a single cited Finoa projection of a $24trn tokenized asset market by 2027. The $24trn figure is the kind of number that propagates uncritically through crypto marketing; the source is a Medium post by a crypto custody firm with a commercial interest in the claim.

Who should care: useful only as a circa-2019 snapshot of the "tokenized equities" narrative — DX Exchange subsequently collapsed, ASX eventually abandoned its blockchain CHESS replacement (scrapped November 2022 after ~AU$250M write-down), and the broader "security token" thesis stalled. Value to the Cemini research library is minimal beyond noting the narrative lifecycle.

Honest limits: shallow treatment; many undefined claims (e.g. "blockchain works 24/7" as if that alone solves settlement); no discussion of oracle problems, custody, corporate actions, KYC/AML for on-chain equities, or dividend distribution mechanics. Flag as **low-rigor** / **dated narrative piece**, not a reference.

## Key findings

- Core thesis: tokenize real-world equities (1 token = 1 share) onto blockchains (Ethereum cited) for 24/7 peer-to-peer settlement.
- Institutional signals circa 2018–2019: NASDAQ pursuing blockchain for stock trades; ASX planned blockchain-based CHESS replacement by late 2020/early 2021 (since abandoned); HKEX partnering with ASX; LSE partnered with IBM in July 2018.
- DX Exchange named as first NASDAQ-powered EU-regulated digital stocks exchange.
- Market size claim: $24trn tokenized asset projection by 2027 (sourced from a Finoa Medium post — propagated crypto-industry figure).
- No original data, no model, no empirical test — pure narrative/conceptual paper from undergrads.
