## Narrative

Birth in France ≠ French nationality at birth for product logic. Hard title is the **Certificat de nationalité française (CNF)** via Tribunal judiciaire (Cerfa 16237); passport/CNI are presumption only. Prefer **copie intégrale** of the birth act plus **EU 2016/1191** multilingual standard form for FR→IT packs filed **inside the EU**. CIEC Form A *extrait* usually lacks marginalia Italian seats need. Filing at an Italian consulate **outside the EU** flips authentication to Hague.

**COMEDEC is not a citizen retrieval portal** — never list it as a DIY path. **Double droit du sol** and Algeria **1962/63** (droit commun vs local) are Clock A minefields; deferred acquisition windows (esp. Méhaignerie 1993–98) make acquisition date ≠ DOB.

Portals: service-public R1406 · SCEC Nantes · commune post · Archives départementales (research only).
