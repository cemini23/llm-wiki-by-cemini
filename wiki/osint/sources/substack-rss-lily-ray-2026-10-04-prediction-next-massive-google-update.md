## Narrative

Lily Ray predicts a major Google update is near. Her method: Google edits its documentation and quality-rater guidelines before big updates, so the written clues come first.

The warning signs this time, from the week of October 1 to 4, 2026:

- **AI content guidance rewritten (Oct 1).** Google now says it is "critical to manually factcheck and review all AI-generated content" before publishing. This applies to titles, meta descriptions, structured data, and image alt text.
- **"Main content" defined.** A new section of the helpful-content guide lists four things raters look for: **effort, originality, talent or skill, and accuracy**. Producing large amounts of AI text without manual oversight is named as "little to no effort".
- **Fake authors named as deception.** AI headshots, made-up names, and false credentials are called a low-quality signal.
- **Google staff said it aloud.** At Search Central Live Barcelona, **Gary Illyes** said scaled content is becoming a bigger problem than link spam.

Ray shows precedent. Google shipped **4 spam updates in 2026** against **1 in 2025**, and the gaps keep shrinking. The March 2024 core update ran **45 days** and cut low-quality content by **45%**.

On GEO tactics, her updated research across 100 "best B2B software" queries found AI Overviews cite **38% fewer** self-promotional listicles than in June, leave the publishing brand out **83%** of the time (up from **69%**), and help competitors in **70%** of cases. Paid mentions sell for about **$135 to $250** each.

Workspace relevance: SEO / GEO-AEO. Route to seo-wiki.
