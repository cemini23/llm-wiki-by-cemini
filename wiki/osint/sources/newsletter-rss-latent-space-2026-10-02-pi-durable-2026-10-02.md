## Narrative

An RSS news roundup. Two harness items matter here.

**Pi 1.0** adds a code-mode interface with native tool and image-model support, virtual models, deferred tool loading, cache warming, mid-conversation system messages, and a new terminal UI.

**Pi Durable** is a port that externalises state. Named properties: crash-survival checkpoints, portability across runtimes, concurrency, extensions, background compaction, multiplayer state sync, and hot-swapping.

The durable-state list is the same theme as the coherence paper in this wave. Once state lives outside the process, it survives a crash, it can be inspected, and it can be shared. That is the harness-owns-state rule again, arriving from the tooling side rather than the theory side.

Other headlines in the roundup: a new Gemini model, a new GPT point release with reported per-task pricing of $0.72 against $3.26 for an earlier tier, a small 35B model with a 1M-token context and 262K output, an image model with a temporary 50% API discount through 8 October, and a video-avatar claim of 48% of live participants mistaking it for a human.

These are vendor and release headlines. Do not swap `/route`. No skill install. No level to arm. FILE only.
