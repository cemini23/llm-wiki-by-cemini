## Narrative

Substack Notes essay arguing the hardest part of trading is **reading yourself**, not the market. Author describes **Falcon**, a proprietary agentic market operator that only takes checklist-complete setups with identical sizing. Cited forward-test stats (**92.3% win rate**, **3.14 profit factor**, ~2 weeks / 1 loss) are **single-source / unverified** — do not encode as strategy truth.

Core product-useful claim: agentic execution **amplifies** discipline; it does not manufacture an edge. Retail failure modes (inconsistent size, FOMO, improvisation) stem from not trusting a measured system. Six commandments: consistent size; no FOMO entries; know win rate / PF / expectancy; honor pre-decided stops; journal every trade; separate self-worth from P&L. Compounding thought experiment (20%/week → ~$1.31B) is explicitly **not a promise** — illustrates that one emotional blow-up resets the exponent.

**Route:** xsp brief (discipline / agentic-handoff framing) · wiki-only for Falcon marketing numbers · not GuruWatcher (no price levels).
