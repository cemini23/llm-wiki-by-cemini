## Narrative

Leopold Aschenbrenner's landmark essay on the trajectory of artificial intelligence through the end of the decade. Written from San Francisco with insider perspective (former OpenAI, advised by Ilya Sutskever and other prominent figures), the essay argues that AGI by 2027 is "strikingly plausible" based on straightforward extrapolation of three trendlines: compute scaling (~0.5 OOMs/year), algorithmic efficiencies (~0.5 OOMs/year), and "unhobbling" gains (chatbot → agent → drop-in remote worker).

### Structure (five parts)

**I. From GPT-4 to AGI: Counting the OOMs (p.7-45)**
The core quantitative argument. GPT-2 (2019, ~preschooler) to GPT-4 (2023, ~smart high-schooler) took 4 years. Extrapolating ~100,000x effective compute scaleup over the next 4 years projects another preschooler-to-high-schooler-sized qualitative jump — taking models to PhD/expert level capable of automating AI research itself. The method: trace growth in (1) physical compute, (2) algorithmic efficiency gains convertible to "effective compute," and (3) "unhobbling" — fixing obvious limitations like lack of tool use, internet access, and agentic persistence.

**II. From AGI to Superintelligence: the Intelligence Explosion (p.46-73)**
Once AI systems can automate AI research, feedback loops accelerate. Hundreds of millions of AGIs could compress a decade of algorithmic progress (5+ OOMs) into ~1 year. The transition from human-level to vastly superhuman AI would be rapid and disorienting.

**III. The Challenges (p.74-140)**
Four sub-challenges:
- IIIa. Racing to the Trillion-Dollar Cluster — GPU/datacenter/power buildout; US electricity production growing tens of percent
- IIIb. Lock Down the Labs: Security for AGI — current lab security is "handing the key secrets for AGI to the CCP on a silver platter"
- IIIc. Superalignment — controlling AI systems much smarter than humans is unsolved; "things could very easily go off the rails"
- IIId. The Free World Must Prevail — superintelligence as decisive economic/military advantage; China not out of the race

**IV. The Project (p.141-155)**
By 2027/28, the US government will initiate some form of national AGI project. "No startup can handle superintelligence. Somewhere in a SCIF, the endgame will be on."

**V. Parting Thoughts (p.156-161)**
"What if we're right?"

## Snippets

### Opening thesis
"You can see the future first in San Francisco. Over the past year, the talk of the town has shifted from $10 billion compute clusters to $100 billion clusters to trillion-dollar clusters. Every six months another zero is added to the boardroom plans. Behind the scenes, there's a fierce scramble to secure every power contract still available for the rest of the decade, every voltage transformer that can possibly be procured. American big business is gearing up to pour trillions of dollars into a long-unseen mobilization of American industrial might. By the end of the decade, American electricity production will have grown tens of percent; from the shale fields of Pennsylvania to the solar farms of Nevada, hundreds of millions of GPUs will hum." [Source: situationalawareness.pdf, p.3]

### AGI by 2027
"AGI by 2027 is strikingly plausible. GPT-2 to GPT-4 took us from ~preschooler to ~smart high-schooler abilities in 4 years. Tracing trendlines in compute (~0.5 orders of magnitude or OOMs/year), algorithmic efficiencies (~0.5 OOMs/year), and 'unhobbling' gains (from chatbot to agent), we should expect another preschooler-to-high-schooler-sized qualitative jump by 2027." [Source: situationalawareness.pdf, p.7]

### Counting the OOMs
"GPT-2 to GPT-4 — from models that were impressive for sometimes managing to string together a few coherent sentences, to models that ace high-school exams — was not a one-time gain. We are racing through the OOMs extremely rapidly, and the numbers indicate we should expect another ~100,000x effective compute scaleup — resulting in another GPT-2-to-GPT-4-sized qualitative jump — over four years." [Source: situationalawareness.pdf, p.9]

### Intelligence explosion
"AI progress won't stop at human-level. Hundreds of millions of AGIs could automate AI research, compressing a decade of algorithmic progress (5+ OOMs) into 1 year. We would rapidly go from human-level to vastly superhuman AI systems. The power — and the peril — of superintelligence would be dramatic." [Source: situationalawareness.pdf, p.4]

### On lab security
"The nation's leading AI labs treat security as an afterthought. Currently, they're basically handing the key secrets for AGI to the CCP on a silver platter. Securing the AGI secrets and weights against the state-actor threat will be an immense effort, and we're not on track." [Source: situationalawareness.pdf, p.4]

### The Project
"As the race to AGI intensifies, the national security state will get involved. The USG will wake from its slumber, and by 27/28 we'll get some form of government AGI project. No startup can handle superintelligence. Somewhere in a SCIF, the endgame will be on." [Source: situationalawareness.pdf, p.5]
