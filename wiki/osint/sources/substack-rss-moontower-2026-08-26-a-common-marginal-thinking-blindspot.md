## Narrative

Moontower revisits Jevons (1871) on marginal utility: not all units of a good share equal utility (water’s first quart vs later gallons). Common blindspot is averaging or total-value thinking when decisions need **marginal** analysis — sizing, hedges, last-dollar risk. Useful GW/options hygiene reminder; no trade arm.
