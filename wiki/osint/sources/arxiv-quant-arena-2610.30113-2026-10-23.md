## Narrative

Quant-Arena runs a full research loop. The model builds features and stock-scoring code. It sees feedback for 2022-2023 only. It then freezes one strategy for 2024.

A cash account applies costs, including market impact from a simulated order book.

Nine frontier models entered. GPT-6 Astra scores highest. No model beats the equal-weight market in 2024.

Homepage in the paper: https://lab.einsia.ai/quant-arena

This is one published benchmark on China cash equities, not a Cemini backtest. Do not arm a model-written book from a loop that only saw the sample period. **No trade.**
