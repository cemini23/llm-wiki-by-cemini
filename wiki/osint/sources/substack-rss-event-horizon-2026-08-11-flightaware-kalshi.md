## Narrative

FlightAware sued Kalshi in SDNY (breach of contract + trademark infringement + more), seeking a block on using FlightAware's name/data plus damages. Alleged timeline: Jul 14 Kalshi employee created a free AeroAPI account; Kalshi self-certified with CFTC naming FlightAware "Primary Source Agency" for settlement. Jul 15 FlightAware cancelled the account + sent C&D. Jul 17 Kalshi denied violation, claimed nominative fair use; disclaimer added afterward. FlightAware argues irreparable reputational harm + safety risk (incentivizing bad actors to cause cancellations). Notably, Kalshi's self-cert named DOT BTS On-Time Reporting as an alternative source — i.e. markets don't need FlightAware data.

**Related court/regulatory:** CT federal judge (Vernon D. Oliver) rejected every major Kalshi argument re sports contracts (CEA preemption + swaps-classification) in one of the most comprehensive rulings against Kalshi; appeal likely. Eight states now have authority to ban/restrict Kalshi. CFTC Innovation Advisory Committee inaugural meeting Aug 20 (Mansour, Coplan, Robins listed). Kalshi × Nasdaq Market Surveillance partnership. CFTC proposed rules on affiliated market-maker conflicts of interest (~8 DCMs with affiliated MMs). Volume: Kalshi $1.07B Sunday; sports+parlays 79.2%.
