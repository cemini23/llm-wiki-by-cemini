## Narrative

WuBlockchain's monthly spot-volume table. **September 2026 spot volume across the 14-exchange sample was about $696.7B, up 36.5% from $510.4B in August.** **All 14 venues rose** month over month; **none fell**.

**Concentration fell.** The top three venues took **62.4%**, down from **64.8%** in August. Binance still dominates at **44.2%** of the sample.

| Venue | September volume | Share |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| Binance | $307.8B | 44.2% |
| OKX | $65.8B | 9.5% |
| Coinbase | $61.3B | 8.8% |
| Top 3 combined | — | 62.4% (August: 64.8%) |

**Caveat the publisher states:** the data **may include wash trading or bot activity**. The figures are **preprocessed** — outlier removal, calibration, standardization.

### Why it matters here
Spot volume is a **venue-liquidity and attention proxy**, not a tradeable signal on its own. It pairs with `@concepts/crypto-derivatives-volume-2026.md` (derivatives notional) and `@concepts/crypto-venue-website-traffic.md` (web attention). Three independent venue measures now move together: a **broad September risk-on rebound**. **No trade. No ssh scp.**
