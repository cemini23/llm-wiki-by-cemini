## Narrative

The paper defines a Hybrid Coevolutionary Opinion Game. It mixes cost-minimizing Friedkin–Johnsen agents with language-model agents on a network that rewires. Beliefs start from 5,199 Reddit stance comments on a scale from −1 to +1. A mix parameter sets how many agents are language models.

The paper reports that a language-model update still lets the graph converge, which is what lets the authors read a price of anarchy. On that reading, a population of only language-model agents is about five times less efficient than a population of only analytical agents.
