## Narrative

Xu, Shang, Dong, Ferrara. Coding agents for robot manipulation collide with obstacles in most cases even when the prompt forbids it. SAFEHARNESS adds obstacle-aware route planning and contact execution. Claims 71.9% task success and 87.5% collision avoidance. OOD robotics stub. Wiki-only. **0 Integrate GO.**
