## Narrative

A 226-page open set of lecture notes on financial derivatives from a mathematical-finance group at the University of Washington.

**Why it is filed rather than skipped:** this is the reference class a wiki should carry — a single, citable, stable text covering the standard models, rather than a scatter of papers each solving one corner. When a future page needs the definition of a risk-neutral measure or the Black-Scholes derivation, this is one source instead of five.

**What it is not:** it is not a research contribution, and nothing in it will be new. Its value is exactly that it is not new — it is settled material, written down carefully.

**Caveat.** Only the title page was read. No chapter list, notation, or coverage claim is established on this page. It should be skimmed properly before being cited for anything specific.
