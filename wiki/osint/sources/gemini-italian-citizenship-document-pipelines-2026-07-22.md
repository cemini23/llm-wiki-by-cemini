## Narrative

Operational report for 2026 Italian citizenship acquisition with emphasis on **birth acquisition** vs **ancestral jure sanguinis recognition**, plus apostille/translation/forms pipelines for US diaspora applicants. Frames Atto as checklist + vault software (not a law firm).

**Claimed hard shifts (verify against primary law):** Law 74/2025 (“Tajani Decree”) generational limits on administrative jure sanguinis for foreign-born dual citizens; Interior Circular No. 43347 (“Minor Issue”) treating parental naturalization while child was a minor cohabitant as chain-breaking for administrative filings; grandfathering for applications filed before 27 Mar 2025 23:59 Rome; 2026 Budget Law easing minor birth-registration fee window to three years. Pending: Constitutional Court hearing on Law 74/2025 (cited 9 Jun 2026); Cassazione Sezioni Unite on Minor Issue (spring 2026) — **[NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-07-22]** dates/outcomes.

**Venues compared:** consulate (Prenot@Mi, €600 fee cited), comune residency fast-track (*permesso per attesa cittadinanza*), tribunale (1948 / Minor Issue / Tajani challenges). Document stages 0–6: triage → Italian *copia integrale* → US long-form chain + naturalization/no-record → Hague apostille → venue-specific translation (consular certified vs *traduzione asseverata*) → forms → AIRE / codice fiscale / passport. Pathway IDs + document matrix included for software.

**Citation quality:** mix of consulate/MAECI pages and citizenship-law-firm blogs. Prefer primary statutes/circulars/consulate checklists before shipping product copy; treat wait times and fee amounts as **jurisdiction-variable**.

**Wiki scope:** pathways + document ontology. No private Barone pedigree. No filing advice that misrepresents naturalization dates.
