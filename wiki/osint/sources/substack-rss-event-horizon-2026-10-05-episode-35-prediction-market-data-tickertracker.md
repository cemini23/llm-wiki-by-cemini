## Narrative

Event Horizon episode 35 puts hard numbers on prediction-market volume. Eric Ramsey of TickerTracker explains the data; Daniel O'Boyle of InGame covers the news.

Volume for Saturday, October 3: Kalshi did **$3.45 billion**. Sports markets were **21.1%** of volume, parlays **69.6%**, and crypto markets **8.9%**. Seven-day trailing volume on designated contract markets: Kalshi **$17.76B**, Polymarket US **$2.84B**, DraftKings DKeX **$627M**, Crypto.com/NADEX **$461M**, Novig **$484M**, Rothera **$271M**, Underdog **$241M**, ProphetX **$61M**.

The big story: **Kalshi won in Illinois**. Federal appellate courts are **two to one** in favor of states against Kalshi, so the win matters for morale. The Supreme Court will decide the main issue. Missouri is in "ongoing discussions" with Kalshi, the first of six firms to answer a cease-and-desist letter; the state says the firms run unlicensed sports wagering. In Brazil, **Sen. Flávio Bolsonaro** became the Kalshi favorite over Lula da Silva as the race goes to a runoff; da Silva led as recently as September.

Responsible-trading items appear: the NCPG executive director left, and Polymarket launched responsible trading.

Workspace relevance: this is the volume-data reference for Kalshi and Polymarket. Route to osint-wiki for prediction-market research and to gambling-wiki for retail-wagering context. TickerTracker is a data source worth tracking.
