## Narrative

**MoRe (Mixture of Roles)** argues a **single model** can host multiple role minds and capture much of multi-agent synergy without a full multi-agent system (MAS). Reported **+2.2%** versus single-agent baselines and about **20× token cut** versus MAS. CCC posture: **K322 WATCH** vocabulary only — useful language for role routing inside one harness, not a mandate to replace `/route` or spawn more agents. **GRPO training path: wont_wire.** Steal: role mixture as a prompt/tool partition inside one agent loop; measure token cost against MAS before any architecture change.
