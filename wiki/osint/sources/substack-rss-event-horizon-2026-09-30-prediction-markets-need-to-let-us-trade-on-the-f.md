## Narrative

RSS teaser. The author wants to hedge his own business on whether sports event contracts survive. A Stanford Law School note proposes a Kalshi Sports Legality contract indexed to Supreme Court litigation. Gaming-industry groups attack prediction markets. The CFTC is preparing a crackdown on trading incentives.

One market level is named. The Kalshi market on when Anthropic announces an IPO trades near 54% for an announcement before 1 December.

Volumes reported: Kalshi raised about $1B at about a $40B valuation, after a May round at $22B. Sept 28 volume was about $2.07B, split 61.8% parlays, 22.1% sports, 13.8% crypto. Trailing seven-day: Kalshi $16.3B, Polymarket US $2.87B, DKeX $606M. Bernstein projects $410B in 2026 rising to $10T by 2035.

The 54% IPO-announcement figure is a claim for the ledger, not a trade. Never auto-arm. No pm scp.
