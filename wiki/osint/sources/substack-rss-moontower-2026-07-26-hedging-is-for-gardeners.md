## Narrative

Hedge pedagogy: hedges isolate unwanted risk at a cost (premium, spread, opportunity); "ragrets" both ways; direct vs correlated hedges with basis risk; put/collar/covered-call tradeoffs; short- vs long-dated protection; stock rallies increase unprotected deductible on old puts; rebalance on schedule vs risk bands. SIG lore: "hedging is for gardeners" — only hedge aggressively when the order looks smart (overhedge to synthetic).

**xsp:** Lane B LEAPS/hedge monitor — tolerate intentional residual delta; don't mechanical-hedge every tick; smart-flow overhedge is discretionary, not default bot policy.
