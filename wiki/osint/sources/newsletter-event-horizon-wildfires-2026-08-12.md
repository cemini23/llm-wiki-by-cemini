## Narrative

The wildfire-betting panic (senators' letter to CFTC, CBS/local news) rests on a **false premise**: no exchange currently offers wildfire markets. Kalshi says it hasn't and won't list them; Polymarket's international site isn't offering them either. Polymarket did offer some wildfire markets in 2025. The author argues this is both a fact-check failure and an illustration that prediction markets can list almost anything if they want to.

**Kalshi × NY AG regulatory escalation** (the real story):
- **NY AG Letitia James** filed a complaint in state court (July 31) seeking a TRO prohibiting KalshiEX from offering **all event contracts nationwide** plus **>$36B in damages**.
- **CFTC exercised emergency authority** (KalshiEX notified of a market emergency) ordering the exchange to keep operating under CEA Core Principles — Chairman Selig: "New York intends to make event contract derivatives waste away under its iron curtain of state gaming laws." CFTC is effectively telling Kalshi to keep serving NY even if it means defying a court order.

**FlightAware v Kalshi**: sued Kalshi (Monday) over using its name/data to resolve flight markets; Kalshi changed usage and FlightAware **voluntarily dismissed** (can refile).

**Market data / misc**:
- Wisconsin governor Dem primary: Crowley upset Hong; >$16M traded at Kalshi; prediction markets aligned with polls but missed this upset.
- Kalshi volume Aug 10: **$956M**; sports+parlays 70%, crypto 27%.
- **Kalshi tops $4B annualized revenue, seeking $40B valuation** (The Information).
- **Polymarket World Cup comedown**: combined weekly volume −56% from June high; Kalshi's comedown −25%. Summer lull; rivalry "in danger of fizzling out."
