## Narrative

**A teaser that states a thesis and links out.** The full argument is behind a Reuters paywall and was not read.

The stated thesis: the administration has made **the AI boom a signature economic bet**. A strike on Iran after the midterms could "blow that up".

The teaser's premise is that oil prices are falling rapidly now, and that the market may be underpricing the probability of a strike. The connection drawn is: a strike → higher oil → consequences that damage an economy whose headline story is AI investment.

### Why it is worth a page

It names a specific, checkable linkage that a macro reader can follow: **the AI trade is now exposed to an energy shock.** If AI capex is the growth story, then an oil-price event that raises input costs, tightens financial conditions, and competes for policy attention is a direct risk to it, not a separate concern.

That is a falsifiable framing rather than a forecast — the piece is arguing a risk is mispriced, which can be tested against the oil curve.

**Caveat.** Single source, teaser only, and the author's own position is that the market is wrong. The full reasoning is not available here.
