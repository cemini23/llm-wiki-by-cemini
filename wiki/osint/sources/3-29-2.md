## Narrative

Hetero-Net (IEEE ICC 2026 Workshop) applies multi-agent optimization to energy-efficient resource allocation and 3D gateway placement in heterogeneous LoRa networks, unifying ground-based and underground wireless sensor networks under a single LPWAN connectivity design. The contribution is an IoT/telecom network-planning method. This is **out-of-scope** for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda — IoT network optimization has no transfer path to financial-market modeling or OSINT workflows.
