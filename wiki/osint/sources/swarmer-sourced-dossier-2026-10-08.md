## Narrative

The evidence base behind the Swarmer thesis (@sources/swarmer-long-thesis-2026-10-09.md). Read-only; nothing was posted, bought, or contacted.

### Bottom line

1. **The company is real; revenue is tiny and concentrated.** FY2025 revenue **$309,920**; Q2 2026 **$216,413**. The "SkyKnight" counterparty group (Meta Bureau LLC + Czech Progress TRW) was **~99% of Q2 revenue**. Swarmer agreed to pay that group **$4.9M** against **$3.9M** of licences bought — most treated as a **revenue reduction**. (CONFIRMED, 10-Q.)
2. **"100,000+ combat missions" is company-only.** No Ukrainian government or military body confirms it. The count grew 4,000 (Apr 2024) → 8,000 → 70,000 (Sep 2025) → 100,000 (2026). Independent reporting (WSJ) confirms real use **at small scale: typically 3 drones, up to 8.** (CLAIMED / CONFIRMED split.)
3. **No Ukrainian unit is publicly named as a user.** No MoD codification, no DELTA integration, no Brave1 Market listing. Brave1 membership + a **2M UAH Brave1 grant (2024)** are CONFIRMED.
4. **The Ratel deal is "up to $224M" only at one share price and only if every earnout is hit.** Structure: ~$7.2M cash + 1,064,942 shares at closing, then up to $7.2M cash + 4,422,125 shares in earnouts. At the Oct 8 close of **$13.13**, the maximum package is worth **~$88M** (calc). Ratel separately reports **substantial doubt** about going concern.
5. **"$200M pro forma 2027 revenue" is a Lucid Capital Markets analyst note, not guidance.** Lucid was the sole IPO underwriter **and** the ELOC counterparty — a **conflicted** source, reached through an IR-style syndicated outlet.

### Basics worth keeping

- Delaware corp., Austin TX, CIK **0002092574**. A **traditional firm-commitment IPO** (not a SPAC), priced **Mar 16 2026 at $5.00**, listed Nasdaq Capital Market as SWMR.
- **Erik Prince is non-executive chairman** (since Dec 2025) and holds **no SWMR shares** and no vested options as of Sep 15, 2026.
- Co-founder **Kupriienko resigned as CEO (Global)** Jul 26, 2026; Fink is principal executive officer. Largest holder Kupriienko **28.7%**.
- Price **$13.13** (Oct 8 2026); 52-week range **$11.25–$83.30**; market cap ~**$218M** (calc). The stock rose ~1,000% within days of the $5 IPO, peaked $83.30, and is now down ~84% from the peak.
- Products renamed across filings: Trident OS → Swarmer OS; MINAS → Swarmer AI; STYX → Swarmer UI.

### The bear case (all CONFIRMED unless noted)

**Post-war demand** (a ceasefire cuts "substantially all" of Ratel revenue) · **customer concentration** (~99% one group) · **revenue quality** ($4.9M paid against $3.9M licences; $2.4M of "marketing" services unsubstantiated) · **dilution and the ELOC** (313,996 shares for $13.5M in June; 3,997,762 option shares; Ratel adds up to 5.49M) · **going concern** · **swarm-under-EW skepticism** (Trent Emeneker: "no evidence they can actually do it in an electronic-warfare environment") · **Ukraine's export plan had issued zero permits** by mid-Sep 2026 · **related-party Prince/Vectus** · **key-person and governance** · **export controls/sanctions** (risk factor; no enforcement found).

### Gaps

No named unit or official mission confirmation. SkyKnight/Rakuten/X-Drone/Kara Dag unconfirmed on their own channels. The Lucid note is not public. WSJ/Reuters paywalled. Brave1 Market status unknown. The ~1,000-page POS AM/DEFM14A was searched, not read whole.
