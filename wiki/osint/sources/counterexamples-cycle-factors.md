## Narrative

Pure combinatorics paper. Disproves a conjecture (Christoph et al., FOCS 2025) that the expected number of cycles in a uniformly random cycle-factor of a directed d-regular graph is uniquely maximized by the disjoint union of complete looped digraphs. The conjecture holds for d=2 but fails for every d≥3 and every n=kd≥2d. The counterexample for the general case uses a construction based on the looped bidirected 6-cycle.
