## Narrative

Bond-market playbook teaser. 1988–2020 rates-only-down hardwired the 30y refi book, 60/40, and asset-management industry. Post-2020 that regime is over.

Watchable claims (alert-only):

1. **Covariance lockstep** — largest cycle moves when **SPX + 30y yield + DXY** move together. 30y pushing back toward all-time highs is *where* to watch that alignment.
2. **Bear steepener through a Fed debate** — only ~**42 bp** of hikes priced into the terminal rate through 2027 while the long end bear-steepens → author reads hotter NGDP than consensus.
3. **Inflation retraced; bonds never bid** — crude holding, corn/wheat bid, copper elevated, short-term stagflation impulse → structural, not cyclical.
4. **Financials rallying as rates rise** + **10s30s bear steepening** — not a collapsing-economy signature. "Rates up means everything breaks" is unsupported on this tape.
5. **MMFs at 3% are not a safe harbor** if growth accelerates with inflation flat. Decompose real rates vs inflation expectations.

K236 already flagged tomorrow's stream at 30y 5.21% (highest in a quarter century). This issue is that stream's write-up.
