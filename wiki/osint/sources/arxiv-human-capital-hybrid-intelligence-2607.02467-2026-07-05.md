## Narrative

Pilot (n≈77 analyzed): live **Polymarket** contracts as externally resolved Brier ground truth. Hybrid forecasters split **trimodal** post hoc:

| Style | Behavior | Scaled Brier (×100, lower better) |
|-------|----------|-----------------------------------|
| **Automators** | Defer to model | ~10.4 — beats human-only (~14.7) but worse than AI-only (~5.5) |
| **Validators** | Rubber-stamp prior guess | ~31.7 — **worse than human-only and AI-only** |
| **Cyborgs** | Complementary iteration | ~3.8 — matches/beats market (~3.5) and each solo model |

Raw **g** and fluid reasoning track model benchmark ordering but **do not** predict hybrid success (r≈−0.08 to −0.10). **Perspective-taking**, curiosity, and intellectual humility do (r≈−0.25 to −0.32). Solo humans: all traits predict accuracy strongly.

Implication for Poker Arena multi-axis profile and PM bot design: record **interaction style** and collaborative-trait proxies, not IQ/benchmark proxies alone. `[TENTATIVE]` — small Cyborg/Validator cells; replication pending.
