## Narrative

A podcast with Peter Mattis, Cockroach Labs co-founder and GIMP co-creator. Topics: Gmail's early storage, Colossus erasure coding, CockroachDB's design, why LSM trees fit append-only storage, Pebble, and why consensus needs at least three replicas. He reports about 100,000 lines of database-grade code per year and a 33% storage-overhead cut from Colossus.

On AI, he says agents brought him back to coding and that non-engineers built about 1,000 internal apps in two months. He predicts line-by-line code review will fade.
