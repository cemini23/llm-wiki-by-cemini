## Narrative

Caltech’s Anima Anandkumar discusses why continuous physical systems (weather, fusion, fluids) resist LLM-style scaling. Open physics datasets are small; industrial grids imply hundreds-of-billions context lengths where transformers fail. Her answer: **Neural Operators** and inductive biases that model functions across scales — FourCastNet as a competitive weather model on consumer GPUs. CCC relevance is awareness only: structure and priors still matter when tokens cannot be thrown at the problem. Not a wire target.
