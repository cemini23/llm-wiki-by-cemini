## Narrative

### Polymarket Protocol V2

A **full rebuild of the Gnosis Conditional Tokens Framework (CTF)** used since 2019. V2 unifies position tokens into **one ERC-1155** standard, uses **pUSD exclusively** as collateral, and provides **one unified exchange and router** for all market types. Modular support for binary, atomic neg-risk, incremental neg-risk, and combinatorial markets. Adds an **OracleAggregator** (UMA + Chainlink) and a **native cross-chain bridge**. Mainnet testing **Oct 5–30**; full migration for new markets **Nov 2**, with a **Rust Data API V2**.

### Pyth — the one to note

Q3 2026: **ARR $11.49M (+86.5% QoQ)**, 276 paying customers. **94.1% data share in RWA perpetuals** ($2.09T Q3 volume). **It prices Polymarket and Kalshi** and **partners with Exa to deliver structured market data to AI agents via MCP**. The PYTH DAO adopted the **"100% Rule"** — all external protocol revenue to **open-market PYTH buybacks** (up from one-third).

**Exa + MCP for agent market data is directly relevant to this workspace's tooling.**

### Everything else

- **Robinhood Chain:** daily tx avg **6.2M** (Oct 2–8), **−42%** vs Sep 10–16; DAU ~322k (−31%); spot volume $7.45B (−21% WoW); **DeFi TVL +2% to $1.04B**; fee subsidy extended to Dec 31.
- **Aptos:** proposed cut to staking rewards **5.19% → 2.6%**, gas up 10×, supply cap **2.1B**, **210M APT** permanently locked; unlock volume to drop 60% after Oct 2026.
- **Starknet:** CEO warns on quantum risk; **evaluating becoming an independent L1** rather than waiting on Ethereum L1 post-quantum (target 2029).
- **Orca + Loopscale merge → Formation** (Solana), aiming at AI/energy/robotics/**defense** assets and regulated US capital markets.
- **Aerodrome + Velodrome → Aero** on Oct 22; initial supply ~2.11B.
- **Arbitrum:** native **USDG** (Paxos) launch; proposal for +100M ARB incentives.
- **USDC/Aave:** Circle Mint BTC-backed borrowing — deposit BTC, mint cirBTC, borrow USDC.
- **Abstract shuts down Dec 15, 2026.** Igloo reports tens of millions in losses over two years.

### Why it matters here

**Polymarket Protocol V2** (Nov 2 migration) is a structural change for the prediction-market lane. **Pyth's Exa MCP partnership** is a direct tooling signal. **No xsp scp, no pm scp.**
