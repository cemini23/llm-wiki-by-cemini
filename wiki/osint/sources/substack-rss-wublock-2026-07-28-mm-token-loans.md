## Narrative

Standard altcoin MM structure = **token loan + European call** (1–5% float, 12–24mo, strike +25–100% TGE). Explains persistent non-unlock selling. Disclosure ask: loaned qty, wallets, term, strike, schedules, profit-share. MOVE leak as rare visibility case.

**Steal (TipDrop/crypto OSINT):** when reading tokenomics "MM allocation," model as effective float + option wall — not free liquidity gift.
