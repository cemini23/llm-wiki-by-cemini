## Narrative

Osmani argues coding-agent configuration has a **half-life**: models, harnesses, and codebases change while stale CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md / skill instructions linger. He runs Claude `/doctor` every few weeks, reviews memory separately, and makes each instruction re-earn its place. Personalized skills show inconsistent value. CCC steal: scheduled agent-file audits alongside wiki lint — delete dead rules, keep STE100 and `/route` doctrine. No auto-evolution of `.cursor/skills`.
