## Narrative

Klement reads an NBER working paper by **José Fillat and Stefania Garetto**. The paper's own subject is management quality and multinational expansion. Klement's reading is that the authors' "risk premium" is a **valuation premium**: better-managed multinational firms trade at higher multiples.

He then says the shareholder return should be lower for those firms. In his chart of factor-adjusted US returns, domestic firms and multinationals that do not acquire show higher returns than firms that do acquire. He also says UK firms are a common target for US buyers.

A valuation premium is **not a trade signal**. This page does not size a position from the chart.
