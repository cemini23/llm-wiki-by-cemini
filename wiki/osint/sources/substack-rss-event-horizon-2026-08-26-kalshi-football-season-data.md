## Narrative

Kickoff-relative 30-day comps (2025 vs 2026, same preseason NFL/college counts). Kalshi daily volume ~$25M (Aug 2025) → **>$1B** random weekday now; 3M new customers during World Cup.

Football preseason volume **30×** YoY but only **1.1%** of platform incremental growth — parlays ($15.2B) and crypto dominate. Hall of Fame Game: $21.2M vs <$500k prior year. Cumulative football ~$400M vs $13.8M same point 2025. >80% incremental 2026 volume from markets that didn't exist a year ago (parlays foremost). 100+ individual football markets dilute Game Winner share.
