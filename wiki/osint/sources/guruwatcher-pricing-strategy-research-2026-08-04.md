## Narrative

Internal/strategy research (not a Cemini price decision). Claims:

1. **$100 one-time is a dead zone** — too dear vs $29–49 utilities, too thin vs $149–249 “Author” SKUs.
2. **Near-term:** $79 one-time CLI/self-host floor; **post-packaging** $149 “Author+/lifetime” with templates/installer/SLA.
3. **Moat vs OSS clone:** friction on free tier (no prebuilt binaries/deploy scripts) or gated convenience — not alpha claims.
4. **Frame:** “SaaS replacement infrastructure” (TradingView alert caps + Make/Zapier TCO ~$300–800/yr) — **not** execution bots (Alertatron / 3Commas anti-comps).
5. **Kill criterion:** if GW becomes managed cloud, abandon one-time → MRR.

**GuruWatcher product brief** — no Discord watches, no orders, no prod scp.
