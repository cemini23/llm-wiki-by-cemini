## Narrative

A summary of research by Hang Nguyen. The question: how do corporate income tax rates change firm productivity?

Reported finding, across almost 80,000 European businesses: after a tax rise, more productive domestic firms raise productivity further, while less productive firms lose productivity. Higher tax selects for productive firms and pushes weaker firms toward creative destruction.

The effect does not hold for multinationals. They shift production abroad instead.

The stated takeaway: a manager should fear a tax rise only if the firm is less productive than its competitors.
