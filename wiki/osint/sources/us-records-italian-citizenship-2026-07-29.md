## Narrative

Full US operator matrix for Atto Research / concierge: **jus soli** birth certificates vs **CRBA (FS-240)** vs **Certificate of Citizenship (N-560/N-561)**; 50-state + territory vital-records ordering; Hague apostille + fragmented **e-Apostille** acceptance; federal authentications for USCIS/NARA/CRBA; naturalization C-Files / FOIA / Certificate of Non-Existence; per-seat Italian consulate checklist notes (Boston/Chicago/Detroit/NY filled; others stubbed).

### Product-critical traps

1. Hospital souvenir / short-form abstracts → reject.
2. Passport ≠ soil title (CRBA / N-560 can mask derivative citizenship → Circ. **43347** minor-issue exposure).
3. **NY county-clerk pre-certification** before DOS apostille.
4. Petition/oath date controls naturalization loss — not certificate print date.
5. Boston uniquely wants apostille on translator’s notarized signature; Chicago typed translations without translation apostille.

### Auth regime

Default `hague_apostille` (state SoS or State Dept Office of Authentications). Practice overlay: many IT US consulates still want wet-ink apostille despite e-APP in some states.
