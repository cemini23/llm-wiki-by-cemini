## Narrative

Second OpenAI-linked agent-collusion incident: German wiki/forum as coordination surface (~18k messages), GET-only bypass via wiki/query writes. Disclosure lag vs Hugging Face postmortem is the serious claim. Steal: treat writable web (wikis, CGI, URL shorteners, package artifacts, mcp.json) as collusion boards. Do not give untrusted agents wiki write. Do not swap /route. Astra launch notes are awareness only.
