## Narrative

### Circ. 26185 — art. 3-bis letters (comune admin)

Art. 3-bis is a **preclusion** on automatic acquisition for born-abroad + other citizenship; exceptions do **not** invent a new transmission mechanism and do **not** heal an interrupted line. [CONFIRMED]

| Letter | Operational meaning (circular) |
|--------|--------------------------------|
| **a** | Admin recognition if **domanda + necessaria documentazione** filed by **23:59 27 Mar 2025**; recognition may be later under pre-28 Mar rules |
| **a-bis** | Admin recognition if domanda filed on day of **appuntamento communicated by the competent municipal office** by **23:59 27 Mar 2025** (appointment must have been set **and** communicated by that deadline). Evidence: protocol date/time of domanda; date/time of appointment communication |
| **b** | Judicial domanda by same deadline; comune **takes note of judgment** — no re-litigation of motives |
| **c** | Parent or grandparent exclusive Italian at birth event (or at death if earlier); line must be intact; **party affidavits alone insufficient** — need negatori / non-rinuncia / etc. |
| **d** | Parent/adopter Italian resident in Italy **≥2 continuous years after acquiring citizenship and before** birth/adoption; prove with **certificato storico di residenza** |

**Docs for a/a-bis:** only **formal** gaps may be cured before final determination (e.g. translation; non-citizenship-critical civil act). K28.1 (8 Apr 1991) still cited. Minor birth-transcription domanda by recognized Italian parent by 27 Mar 2025 counts under a/a-bis. [CONFIRMED]

**Beneficio di legge (art. 4):** not iure sanguinis from birth — status from day after conditions met. Parent must be citizen **per nascita** (not naturalization / matrimonio / iuris communicatione / beneficio). €250 contributo per minore. Declarations **in person** before stato civile. [CONFIRMED]

**Riacquisto window:** 1 Jul 2025 – 31 Dec 2027 for losses under L. 555/1912 not after 15 Aug 1992; not for renunciations from 16 Aug 1992. [CONFIRMED]

**Art. 9 residency naturalization:** parent/grandparent citizen-by-birth → legal Italy residency reduced **3→2 years**. [CONFIRMED]

### DAIT 59/2025 — formule

Adapts DM 5 Apr 2002 formulas 79 / 85 / 88 / 193 for beneficio, transitional minor declarations, riacquisto. Same **31 May 2026** transitional deadline as then in force (later extended — see 19951). Declarations formal, in person. [CONFIRMED]

### Circ. 36356 (24 Jul 2025) — lett. d + art. 14

- Lett. **d** transmission: child born abroad + other citizenship is Italian **from birth** if parent’s 2y post-acquisition Italy residency completed **before** birth; else not JS — may fall to art. 4 beneficio / art. 9 later. Parent may have acquired Italian status by any title; clock start depends on title (birth / day after oath / after beneficio declaration / etc.). [CONFIRMED]
- Art. **14** iuris communicatione after **24 May 2025**: for abroad-born child with other citizenship, requires parent’s 2y Italy residency conditions under 3-bis d **and** child’s legal Italy residency/cohabitation rules under art. 14. For parent naturalization, use **giuramento** date (effect day after), not decree notification. Oaths through **22 May 2025** → old rules; from **23 May 2025** → new child-residency rule. [CONFIRMED]

### Circ. 19951 (12 Mar 2026) — proroga

Milleproroghe L. 26/2026 extends art. 1 comma 1-ter declaration deadline from **31 May 2026 → 31 May 2029** for persons who were **minors on 24 May 2025** and children of 3-bis a/a-bis/b citizens-by-birth. Does **not** enlarge the class. If they age out, they file personally by the new deadline. [CONFIRMED]
