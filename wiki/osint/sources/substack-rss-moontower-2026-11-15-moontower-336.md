## Narrative

A round-up issue that bundles two items already covered as their own sources — the "no such thing as a stock market bubble" essay and the back-of-the-envelope hedging exercise — plus one new item.

The new item is a Jane Street open-source `SKILL.md` called Socratic Tutor. It turns a chat model into a strict tutor: an opening diagnostic, a 0-5 score per subskill, one question at a time with the answer withheld, progressively narrower prompts when the learner is wrong, and promotion only after two correct demonstrations, one from cold recall. It also schedules review of older material.

The author maps it to Math Academy's method — generation effects, reinforcement, spaced repetition — and to his own "Socrates 2026" piece.

A second, smaller item: a custom explainer video made from a board-game rulebook PDF, with notes on iterating in segments so the model can be corrected before burning tokens.
