## Narrative

October 2026 spot volume across 14 major exchanges was about $623.6bn. That is down about 9.3% from $687.7bn in September. Five venues rose and nine fell. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-17-october-2026-exchange-spot-volume-report-total-6.md]

| Venue | Volume | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Binance | $279.2bn | 44.8% |
| OKX | $61.9bn | 9.9% |
| Bybit | $50.9bn | 8.2% |

The largest venue held about 44.8%; the top three held about 62.9%.

The publisher states that the data may include wash trading or bot activity, and that figures are preprocessed with outlier removal, calibration and standardisation. That caveat is the important one. The report is a relative concentration measure, not a clean activity measure. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-17-october-2026-exchange-spot-volume-report-total-6.md]

Derivative context from the next day's report: the futures-to-spot ratio was 6.01x. Spot is the smaller side of the market.
