## Narrative

Ninth Circuit 3-0: Kalshi sports event contracts are **likely not "swaps"** under the CEA. The panel upheld dissolution of the injunction that had let Kalshi keep offering sports contracts in Nevada. Judge Ryan Nelson: substance is sports gambling (prop, spread, score, parlay), not a hedge of financial risk.

Effects:

- Opens state-level enforcement in the Ninth Circuit (CA, NV, and other western states).
- Circuit split vs Third Circuit (Kalshi beat New Jersey). Possible Supreme Court path.
- Panel reads 17 C.F.R. § 40.11 as a **shall-not** bar on listing gaming-related contracts on a DCM.
- Geofencing costs are not irreparable harm; CFTC 2025-09-30 letter already told DCMs to plan for state actions.
- Election contracts remanded to the district court.

**Routing:** gambling-wiki awareness. **No pm scp** — no retail-bot steal; no order-path change. Cemini PM bot does not list Kalshi sports. If a future sports lane exists, NV geofence is Kalshi's problem first. Do not treat this opinion as a trading edge.
