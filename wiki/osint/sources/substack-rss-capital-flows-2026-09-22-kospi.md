## Narrative

The essay argues that global macro alpha is underused, and that policy-driven capital flows now matter more than single-name AI risk. The central claim: the **KOSPI is the first flow on the AI trade each day**. Samsung and SK hynix make the memory that goes into every Nvidia GPU. The index prints in the Asia session before the Nasdaq opens. Foreign investors buy Korea as a levered AI-capex expression, Korean retail buys US tech, and SK hynix now trades in the US.

The author lists Korean policy changes in five buckets and maps each to where capital flows. Named items include:

| Bucket | Item |
|--------|------|
| Political mandate | Martial law, 2024-12-03, declared and reversed inside six hours; won to 1,480 |
| Political mandate | Lee elected, 2025-06-03, on a platform with a KOSPI 5000 target |
| Governance / tax | Value-up Program, 2024-02 |
| Governance / tax | Commercial Act amendment, 2025-07-03 — duty of loyalty to shareholders |
| Governance / tax | Second amendment, 2025-08-25 — cumulative voting, separate audit-committee election under the 3% cap |
