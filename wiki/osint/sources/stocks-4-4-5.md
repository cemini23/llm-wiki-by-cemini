## Narrative

It seems to be very unlikely that all relevant information in the stock market could be fully encoded in a geometrical shape. Still…the present paper will reveal the geometry behind the stock market transactions.

Author Racorean (Government of Romania) maps stock-crossing dynamics to the **positive Grassmannian** — the same combinatorial-algebraic structure that underlies cluster algebras and (in physics) on-shell scattering amplitudes.
