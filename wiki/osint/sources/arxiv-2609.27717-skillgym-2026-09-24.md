## Narrative

SkillGym turns human-written agent skills into training tasks. The paper reports 2,756 environments in 12 categories and 8,364 successful trajectories. The mean trajectory has 49 tool calls. The paper says the trajectories can support supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning with outcome rewards.
