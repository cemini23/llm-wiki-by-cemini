## Narrative

Pilot GPS + exit-survey study (n=23 guests, 26 visits across three Southern California theme parks) testing whether informal planner typology — **waders** (aimless), **swimmers** (moderate planners), **divers** (attraction maximizers) — emerges from observed movement clusters.
