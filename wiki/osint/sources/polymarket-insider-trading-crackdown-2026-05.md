## Narrative

Prediction markets hit a regulatory inflection point this week:

- **Insider-trading scrutiny.** A New York Times investigation found **dozens of Polymarket bets showing signs of insider trading**. The flagged archetype: suspiciously timed bets on geopolitical events (the Venezuela/Maduro raid, the Iran war). The most-cited case — a US Army soldier (Gannon Ken Van Dyke) said to have made >$400K on a Polymarket bet on the removal of Venezuela's Maduro.
- **Enforcement is arriving.** CFTC Chair Michael Selig said the agency would aggressively prosecute insider trading on prediction markets; the CFTC began crafting prediction-market regulations in March 2026. Kalshi banned three US congressional candidates for "political insider trading." WIRED reports the US is using **AI to detect** suspicious trading patterns.
- **Volume cooled.** Polymarket trading volume **fell 9% in April 2026 — its first decline since August 2025** — even as combined Polymarket+Kalshi monthly volume sits near **$25B** (up from <$2B a year earlier). Kalshi (CFTC-regulated, ~89% of US volume) continues to grow; Polymarket's founder acknowledged missteps.

**Why it matters here**: this is the regulatory and reputational backdrop for all of the wiki's Polymarket work — the trading research, the microstructure pages, and the Outlier Weekly newsletter. Insider flow is now an enforcement target, not just a microstructure curiosity, and reinforces the `arxiv-polymarket-fill-side` finding that a small set of professional addresses dominates notional.
