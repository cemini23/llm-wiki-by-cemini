## Narrative

**Valuation.** Kalshi is reported to be raising at a $65bn valuation. Robinhood's share price was down after a crypto selloff. The post asks whether Kalshi could be the Robinhood of financial platforms. Supporting fact: Kalshi bought FB Securities, a small New York broker founded in 2024, in July, and plans to launch equity trading within two months. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-17-is-kalshi-worth-as-much-as-robinhood.md]

**Regulation.** Senator Adam Schiff and other Senate Democrats asked CFTC Chair Michael Selig to reverse the agency's ban on geofencing by prediction markets. They argue the ban conflicts with court orders in California, Michigan, Nevada and Washington, including the Blue Lake Rancheria v. Kalshi order. Separately, the CFTC approved Backpack and tZERO for blockchain-based derivatives exchange and clearinghouse status. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-17-is-kalshi-worth-as-much-as-robinhood.md]

**Industry.** Flutter (FanDuel) expects to invest a couple of hundred million dollars in prediction markets in 2026, with similar or more in 2027. Macquarie analysts estimate sports event contracts generated $22.7bn of taker volume in October, up 668% year on year and 27% month on month. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-17-is-kalshi-worth-as-much-as-robinhood.md]

**Product.** Kalshi offers a "Gas prices in the US this month" market. Rideshare and delivery drivers can buy Yes on a price threshold as a fuel-cost hedge.

For the PM/Kalshi retail bot, the important numbers are the venue-share ladder and the parlay share (about 70% of Kalshi volume). Liquidity sits in parlays, not in the niche event markets.
