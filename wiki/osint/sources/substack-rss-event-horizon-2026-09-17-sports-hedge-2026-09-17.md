## Narrative

Kalshi bar-promo "hedge" creates artificial risk. Wants real underlying sports hedges (bet against local team / under win total). Parlays/gambling FILE. **No pm scp.**

## Snippets

> Creating a promotion based on a sporting event and then betting on the other side of it is not hedging an underlying risk; it is creating an artificial one. Real hedge: bet under on the local team's win total. [Source: https://nexteventhorizon.substack.com/p/i-would-love-to-see-real-hedging-on-sports-events (retrieved 2026-09-17)]
