## Narrative

### The interview

Dorothy DeWitt, former director of the CFTC Division of Market Oversight and now founder of Tölt Strategies, discusses event contracts and the CFTC. The free text gives no transcript detail, only the framing. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-23-episode-42-the-cftc-now-and-then-with-tölt-strat.md]

### The legal news that matters

- A federal judge rejected the CFTC's request to temporarily block New York's enforcement case against Kalshi. The judge found the CFTC had not established a likelihood of success on preemption. New York is seeking **$36 billion** in the case, per the newsletter.
- A federal judge previously sent the Kalshi case back to state court.
- New York's temporary restraining order request would, in the CFTC's words, "completely shut down Kalshi's operations nationwide". It was denied.
- Kalshi lost in the Fourth Circuit.
- California: Kalshi gave $2 million to the California Democratic Party and hired the attorney general's former chief of staff.

### Industry and volume data

- Kalshi mention-market volume fell nearly 90 percent from its February peak after the CFTC paused Trump mention markets. Mention markets generated nearly $977 million from January through July.
- Institutional trading on Kalshi reached **$26.3 billion** in October, up from $622.7 million in April, a 4,124 percent rise in six months.
- DraftKings short interest rose 17 percent since the NFL season began, per S3.
- 44 state attorneys general opposed two CFTC rulemakings that would give the CFTC sole authority over sports and election event contracts.
- MGM and BetMGM left the National Council on Problem Gambling after the NCPG admitted Kalshi as a member.
- Seven-day venue volume: Kalshi $27.7bn, DraftKings DKeX $1.26bn, Crypto.com/NADEX $1bn, Underdog $938m, Novig $832.1m, Rothera $407.8m, ProphetX $76.8m.
