## Narrative

**Closed Jul 2, 2026; announced Jul 6, 2026** — classic **close-before-PR** gap. CXW stock **flat on Jul 2** (+0.06% to $31.03) then **faded Jul 6** after premarket pop.

| Facility | Beds | Gross |
|----------|------|-------|
| California City | 2,560 | $732.6M |
| Otay Mesa | 1,994 | $739.2M |
| **Total** | **4,554** | **$1.5B** |

**Net proceeds:** ~**$1.1B** after ~**$0.4B** taxes + expenses. **Blended ~$329k/bed** gross.

**Post-close:** CXW retains ICE **management**; contracts through Aug 2027 (Cal City) and Dec 2029 + option (Otay). Terms may be modified to reflect ownership change. **Additional facility sales** under discussion — **no assurance**.

**Use of proceeds:** Substantial portion to repay credit facility + 2027 notes; balance general corporate purposes including debt reduction and **possible** repurchases subject to leverage covenants.

Validates WaPo **two CoreCivic CA sites** on turnkey shortlist `[CONFIRMED]`. GEO leg **still open**.
