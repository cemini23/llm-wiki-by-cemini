## Narrative

Klement extends his SF Fed ChatGPT inflation-forecast critique: models look strong inside training windows then decay out-of-sample as training-data leakage inflates backtests. A newer team paper shows the same **performance decay** pattern for LLM trading strategies. GW alert: treat LLM signal claims as HITL research, not auto-arm inputs. Pairs with Cemini “never optimize pass criteria” and backtest hygiene doctrine.
