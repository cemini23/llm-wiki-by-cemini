## Narrative

Structured generation for LLM tool use highlights the value of compact DSL intermediate representations (IRs) that can be emitted directly and parsed deterministically. This paper introducesaxial grammar: linear token sequences that recover multi-dimensional structure from the placement of rank-specific separator tokens.
