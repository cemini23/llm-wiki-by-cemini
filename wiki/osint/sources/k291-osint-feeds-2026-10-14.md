### 2. CFTC internal handling of suspicious oil trades (Event Horizon, citing Bloomberg)

Bloomberg reported that CFTC **Division of Market Oversight** analysts filed an insider-trading referral over suspiciously timed oil trades placed before a Trump announcement on Iran. A senior adviser to the Trump-appointed chairman, **Mel Gunewardena**, told a supervisor to **retract the referral**. The agency says the decision was to pursue a **broader** investigation and that Enforcement continues to probe.

**Why it is filed:** it is not a prediction-market story, but it is a story about **the prediction-market regulator**. The commentary's point is that regulator credibility is load-bearing for a nascent industry. Gunewardena's appointment was announced 2 March, three weeks before the trades in question. ### 3. Agency controls in financial agents (Latent Space, AI Engineer NY)

OpenAI's **Joseph Kim** (Head of ChatGPT for Financial Services) described a **"threshold of usefulness"** — the point where a model is capable and reliable enough to save time. He predicted that by **2027** agents will handle **"investment idea generation and trading"**, and that finance will rely less on Excel and PowerPoint.

**The design claim that matters:** safe autonomy depends on **where controls and human judgment sit**, not on how many restrictions exist. Kim framed human-in-the-loop as a **product design decision** — what the user still controls, reviews, or approves — rather than a policy setting. He also stressed **traceability**: checking where a number came from, as a natural part of the interface.
