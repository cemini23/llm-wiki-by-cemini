## Narrative

The author accepts that news outlets cite prediction-market probabilities. He questions a narrower thing: sponsorship deals that put a venue's own market data inside editorial coverage.

The trigger is a **Kalshi and CBS News partnership**. The comparison drawn is to sportsbook and media integration, where a betting company sponsors coverage in which the newsroom repeatedly uses that sponsor's product as a journalistic source.

Volumes reported, Kalshi on 30 September: about **$2.065B**, split 59.5% parlays, 19.2% sports, 16.5% crypto. Trailing seven-day: Kalshi $17.0B, Polymarket US $2.95B, DraftKings DKeX $613M, CDNA $499M, Novig $445M, Rothera $294M, Underdog Exchange $215M, ProphetX $63.4M.

One survey number is worth recording. A consumer-finance site reported that **79% of prediction-market users lost money** in the past year, 27% lost more than $500, and 44% use the products for extra income.

Other items: a DOJ filing on the swap definition in an insider-trading case, tribal concerns in Maine, and a hot market in 15-minute Bitcoin contracts.
