## Narrative

**Multiplicative Kantian equilibrium (MKE)** is not invariant under monotone strategy-space reparameterizations. A **Kantian player** publicly selects a smooth increasing **scale** before interacting with a Nash player — payoffs unchanged, but Kantian FOC altered via endogenous elasticity weights. Scale = **commitment device**.

| Strategic structure | Scaling role |
|-------------------|--------------|
| **Substitutes** | Defensive — approach Nash payoff, eliminate naive Kantian loss; cannot exceed player-2 Nash benchmark |
| **Complements** | Offensive — can implement **Stackelberg leader** outcome, payoff strictly above Nash |
