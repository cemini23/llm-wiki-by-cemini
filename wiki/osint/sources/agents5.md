## Narrative

Proposes a framework in which an LLM acts as an online adaptive controller for SIMP topology optimization, replacing fixed-schedule continuation with real-time, state-conditioned parameter decisions (penalization exponent, projection sharpness, filter radius, move limit). Benchmarked on 2-D and 3-D structural problems (cantilever, MBB beam, L-bracket), the LLM agent achieves the lowest final compliance, −5.7% to −18.1% versus a fixed baseline. Out-of-scope for Cemini: this is structural/mechanical engineering topology optimization, with no application path to the trading-stack research agenda.
