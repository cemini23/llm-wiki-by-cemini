## Narrative

An opinion piece with a heavy news roundup. The headline argument: prediction markets keep insisting they are *not* gambling, and religious leaders are the newest group to say they are.

### The opinion

LDS President D. Todd Christofferson, Cardinal Blase Cupich, Albert Mohler (Southern Baptist), and theologian Dan Doriani all frame prediction markets as gambling. Author Dustin Gouker (himself from the gambling industry) argues the industry loses credibility by denying it. **Opinion — file as context, not fact.**

### The news (the useful part)

**Ohio.** The state regulator sent **cease-and-desist letters to 10 prediction platforms** — Coinbase, Gemini Titan, MooMoo Financial, Novig, Plus500US, Polymarket, Prophet X, Robinhood Derivatives, Underdog Predict, WeBull Financial — giving them 14 days to respond and shutter. Sent one week after the Sixth Circuit let Ohio enforce its gambling rules against Kalshi.

**CFTC perpetuals.** The CFTC's Division of Market Oversight issued a **no-action letter** letting DCMs convert perpetual-*style* broad-based security index futures into **true perpetual futures** (no expiry), subject to customer-protection conditions. The no-action position **expires 2026-10-20**. Separately, Kalshi got approval for **S&P 500 perpetual futures** — putting it against CME and Cboe. (CME had already sued the CFTC over crypto perpetuals, arguing they are swaps.)

**Volume.** Kalshi posted a **record $3.68 B** on Sunday 2026-10-04. Parlays 68.7%, sports 21.8%, crypto 8.67%. Across all platforms, Kalshi took ~76% of the weekend's $9.37 B.

**7-day exchange volume:** Kalshi $18.4 B · Polymarket US $2.71 B · DKeX $649 M · Novig $532 M · CDNA $463 M · Underdog $315 M · Rothera $276 M · ProphetX $60.5 M.

**Other.** A House bill would ban federal candidates from trading markets on their own elections ($10k fine or 3× gain). Kalshi argues in the Second Circuit that the Commodity Exchange Act pre-empted state law in a 1981 "Potato War" case. ARK bought ~126,594 Kalshi shares across three funds. DraftKings upgraded to Buy on its prediction-market position. A new platform, **Verifact**, centers markets on *disputed facts that already happened*.

### Why it matters here

The Ohio action and the CFTC perpetuals letter are the hard news. **No pm scp.** This is awareness for the prediction-market lane and the gambling wiki.
