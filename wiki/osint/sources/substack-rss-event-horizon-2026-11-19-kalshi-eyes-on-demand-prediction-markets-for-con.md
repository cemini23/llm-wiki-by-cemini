## Narrative

**On-request markets.** Kalshi posted documentation for "on-request" markets. A user could request a market that does not exist, then solicit pricing from market makers. Documented examples are weather outcomes at a specific location and time, and whether an individual concert is postponed or cancelled. Documentation for single-flight markets was live on Wednesday and then returned a 404. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-19-kalshi-eyes-on-demand-prediction-markets-for-con.md]

This is a market-discovery change, not a trading change. It moves the long tail of event risk from "Kalshi decides what to list" to "a user asks and a maker quotes". For a bot, the relevant question becomes how to discover and price bespoke series, and whether request-for-quote flow is visible.

**Competition.** Novig announced partnerships with seven NHL teams for the 2026-27 season, extending from MLB and the New York Mets. Hockey is a small share of prediction-market volume, so this is brand building more than liquidity.

**Regulation.** The comment window closed for the CFTC's two swap-definition rulemakings, including the one that would exclude casino-style gambling products. A bipartisan coalition of 43 state attorneys general plus DC, co-led by Ohio's Andy Wilson, urged the CFTC to withdraw the rule that would classify sports event contracts as swaps. Separately, Kalshi lost its appeal in Maryland; the Fourth Circuit affirmed. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-19-kalshi-eyes-on-demand-prediction-markets-for-con.md]

**Industry.** Coinbase plans in-house event contracts in the first half of 2027; it currently offers prediction markets through Kalshi. Kalshi is working with Compute Desk on futures tied to AI compute cost, after pulling earlier compute markets under CFTC scrutiny. Kalshi added 36 more single-stock perpetuals, including TSMC and SK Hynix. Polymarket US is adding short-duration oil and natural-gas markets after its crypto up-or-down contracts passed $1bn in six weeks. OmenX launched a 30-second Bitcoin prediction product. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-19-kalshi-eyes-on-demand-prediction-markets-for-con.md]

**Volume, Tuesday 17 November.** Kalshi $3.61bn: 68.7% parlays, 14.1% sports, 14% crypto. Seven-day: Kalshi $28.4bn, Polymarket US $3.73bn.
