## Narrative

The post records a joint selloff in one company's equity and debt, and the spillover into AI infrastructure names.

**The October 2026 move.** In mid-October, yields on some CoreWeave unsecured bonds rose to about 15%. One bond maturing in 2031 briefly yielded more than 16%. The shares fell nearly 25% in the week ended 16 October. Peers fell too: Nebius declined, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost more than 11% that week.

**The business.** CoreWeave began as an Ethereum miner. It was founded in 2017, launched its cloud platform in 2020, and listed on Nasdaq in 2025. It now supplies GPU clusters and cloud services to Microsoft, OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic. Microsoft accounted for approximately 67% of 2025 revenue, which is a single-customer concentration risk.

**The backlog.** Remaining performance obligations were $103.7bn at the end of June 2026. About 41% of that was expected to be recognised as revenue over the following 24 months. The separately disclosed "revenue backlog" was about $104bn. Both figures depend on delivery and service availability before revenue can be recognised.

**The financials for Q2 2026.** Revenue was $2.575bn, up about 112% year over year. Net loss was $626m. Net interest expense was $640m. At the end of June the company reported $35.6bn of total debt and about $16.3bn of operating lease liabilities.

The interest expense figure is the one to hold on to. Net interest expense of $640m exceeded the net loss of $626m. That means operating results were positive before financing costs.

**The dispute over the outlook.** Jensen Huang said CoreWeave's financial strength was improving and its cost of capital was declining. Goldman Sachs upgraded the stock to Buy on 19 October, expecting continued access to financing. Gartner forecast that cost pressures and limited differentiation would leave more than 80% of neocloud providers no longer operating independently by 2030.

The article is a summary piece. Every figure is attributed to company disclosure or to the named analyst sources, and none is independently verified here.
