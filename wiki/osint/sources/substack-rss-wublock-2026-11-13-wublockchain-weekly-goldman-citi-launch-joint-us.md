## Narrative

### The liquidity signal

CryptoQuant's 10 November report has three numbers that point the same way:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| USDT market-cap decline over 60 days | $6.6bn |
| Bitcoin apparent demand, 30 days | −284,000 coins |
| Coinbase premium index | Negative for 30 consecutive days |

The USDT contraction exceeds the redemption wave after the FTX collapse in December 2022. CryptoQuant treats stablecoin supply as purchasing power; a sustained fall means less capital available to buy Bitcoin. The demand contraction is the largest since March 2025.

CryptoQuant notes that similar contractions appeared near earlier Bitcoin bottoms. It also notes that stablecoin liquidity is still shrinking and US spot buying has not recovered, so the bottom signal is not complete.

### Who sold

Fourteen listed companies sold about **108,247 BTC** worth roughly **$8.08bn** in 2026:

| Company | BTC sold |

| MARA | 40,581 |

MARA and Riot together are about 56.2% of the total. The figures include estimated fund flows and option-related settlements.

### The bank stablecoin

Twenty-one financial institutions are building **Project Faro**. Goldman Sachs, Citigroup, Santander and Deutsche Bank have committed about **$270m** to fund five years of operations. Other participants include Wells Fargo, Fidelity, UBS and Lloyds.

Plan: a US stablecoin issuer, a dollar token in the first half of 2027, then other currencies including the euro. The project has no chief executive and no final corporate name.

Context for the competitive threat: USDT and USDC together are roughly **85%** of the global stablecoin market. A bank consortium enters against that duopoly with a distribution advantage and a compliance advantage, not a product advantage.

### Regulation and structure

- **Coinbase** received final OCC approval for a national trust bank charter. It can expand custody and payments, and may issue its own stablecoin or administer issuance for others. A trust charter cannot take general deposits or make loans. Circle and Ripple already hold final clearance.
- **Canada** will bar federally regulated lenders from issuing stablecoins directly from 2027. Issuance must sit in a non-financial subsidiary regulated under the Stablecoin Act. Foreign institutions outside domestic prudential supervision will also be captured.
- **Senator Blumenthal** sent records demands to Binance, Tether, World Liberty Financial and Fight Fight Fight LLC, with a 4 January 2027 deadline. The Binance and Tether probes concern unlawful crypto use and Trump-administration communications.
- **IRS Letter 6174** volume is up sharply; Donnelly estimates about 10,000 letters a month, unconfirmed. The letter is a voluntary-compliance reminder since 2019, not an audit.

### Markets and infrastructure

- **SEC amicus brief** backs the CFTC on single-stock perpetual futures. The Capital Markets Alliance, which includes Cboe and Citadel Securities, sued to vacate the approval. Coinbase, Kalshi and Payward filed supportive briefs. The three firms' applications were deemed approved on 2 November.
- **OKX–ICE joint venture** opens a tokenized-securities venue on 23 November under a five-year SEC innovation exemption. Seventy assets are confirmed, including 59 ETFs. Meta, Tesla and Netflix are among 67 companies that opted out.
- **Ethereum**: Ethlabs proposed the "pqai27" roadmap to reach post-quantum and AI-threat resistance in 2027, a year earlier than planned. It withdrew support for Quick Slots to move resources to post-quantum work.
- **Kalshi** is negotiating a round at a **$65bn** valuation. See @entities/platforms/kalshi.md.

### Why it is filed here

The USDT drain and the negative Coinbase premium are the two inputs that a crypto-adjacent prediction-market bot must respect. Kalshi crypto contracts were 14.7% to 15.9% of volume on consecutive days; a liquidity drain changes the venue's inventory and the retail flow mix.

The Project Faro and Coinbase charter items change the stablecoin map that Circle and Tether sit on. See @entities/companies/circle.md and @entities/companies/tether.md.
