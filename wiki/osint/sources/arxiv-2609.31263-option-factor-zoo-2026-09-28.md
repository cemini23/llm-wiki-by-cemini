## Narrative

The paper builds 137 option-implied characteristics for optionable U.S. stocks from 2004 to 2023, screens them to 20 long-short factors, and tests them against 160 equity factors. In sample, equity factors account for about 91 percent of the option-factor variance. Adding the option factors does not raise the maximum Sharpe ratio. Eight of the twenty option factors, mainly jump tails, kurtosis, and implied-volatility convexity, still have a non-zero SDF loading. [TENTATIVE]

This is not advice. Do not paste those loadings into CeminiDFS or a parlay model.
