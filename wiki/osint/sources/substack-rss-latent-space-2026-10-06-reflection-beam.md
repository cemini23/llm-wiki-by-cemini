## Narrative

**Reflection** ended its long stealth period and launched **Beam**, a **501B-parameter (A23B active) American-trained open-weight model**. It is trained from scratch in the US — the first such launch in this wave.

### Position in the field

Reflection compares Beam to Inkling, Nemotron, and GLM 5.2. The newsletter is blunt that the SOTA open models — **GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, Qwen 3.8 Max, and DeepSeek 4.1 Flash** — are generally ahead. The value of Beam is **geography, not benchmark leadership**: a US-trained open model for buyers who want one.

The AI Twitter recap across the 10/03–10/05 window reports "a wave of open-weight releases" led by Beam.
