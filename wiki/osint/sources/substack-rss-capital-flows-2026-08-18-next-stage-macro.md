## Narrative

Livestream playbook. **Thirty-year rates printed a cycle high**; bonds made new lows. Position already on sides — move stops to breakeven, do not chase. Order book is pattern recognition (urgent vs passive); a live gold example: ride a capitulating bid, fade when it finishes, same player on the offer later (~150 ticks). Charts cannot show the 8,000-lot offer above a long.

**Three greatest equity risks right now `[TENTATIVE]`:** (1) interest-rate vol from higher inflation; (2) carry-trade unwind against system leverage; (3) vol complacency at the highs (1-week vol below 1-month; leveraged ETF flows into MU/SanDisk bought the dip). Path dependence: if the curve prices fewer hikes / more cuts, easier financial conditions force the long end higher. 2y decomposition: real rates doing the work.
