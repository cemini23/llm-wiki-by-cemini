## Narrative

SecPro essay on cybersecurity shift from **defending static systems** to **governing autonomous enterprises**. Three converging trends: AI-assisted dev, autonomous SOC, identity-as-perimeter. Security engineers become **governors** — policy for which AI-generated merges auto-land; supervise autonomous triage/response. **Non-human identity explosion:** dev agents, SOC agents, procurement agents each need authN/authZ, audit trail, revocation. **Disposable software:** ephemeral apps → secure the **factory** (pipeline, IaC, policy-as-code) not just long-lived apps. **Adaptive controls** + explainability requirement. **Metrics shift** from patch counts to governance quality (agent boundaries, reproducibility of autonomous decisions). **cybersec brief** handoff — no David/TipDrop adopt.
