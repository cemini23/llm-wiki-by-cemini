## Narrative

Belgium NBB study on EU SFDR: announcement/implementation boosted Art. 8/9 ESG fund flows; later stricter rules raised compliance cost without much new AUM. Managers greened products and charged higher fees. Moderation thesis: some regulation helps labeling/trust; overshoot raises cost without flow benefit. Wiki-only — no prod hook.
