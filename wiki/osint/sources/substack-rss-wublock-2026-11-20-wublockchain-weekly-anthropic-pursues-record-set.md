## Narrative

Ten items plus a fundraising list.

**Quantum risk.** IOSCO published a 63-page report on quantum threats to public-key cryptography. It estimates a machine able to break current cryptography between 2030 and 2035. Post-quantum signatures such as ML-DSA-44 are about 2,420 bytes, 35 to 40 times the 64-byte current signatures. Migrating Ethereum, Solana and TRON needs hard forks, and decentralised networks cannot be forced to move fast. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-20-wublockchain-weekly-anthropic-pursues-record-set.md]

**Tokenised deposits.** The HKMA is working with Swift, Bank of China (Hong Kong), Standard Chartered Hong Kong and HSBC on interoperability between Swift Ledger and Hong Kong's Ensemble settlement layer.

**Stablecoin rules.** Brazil's central bank says fiat-to-stablecoin swaps may qualify as money laundering; Resolution 561/2026 took effect on 1 October. The World Bank will work with Citi and Circle on USD stablecoin disbursement rails and on FundsChain, which covers 96 countries. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-20-wublockchain-weekly-anthropic-pursues-record-set.md]

**Ethereum.** Vitalik Buterin described EIP-8037, which raises gas costs for new storage slots, code and accounts to hold state growth in check. A tenfold gas-cap increase with other Glamsterdam proposals could raise annual state growth from about 110 GiB to 1,100 GiB. Glamsterdam has been postponed.

**Anthropic IPO.** Anthropic (ANT) opened at $111.50, about 34.3% above the $83 IPO price. The Nasdaq fell to its intraday low as it began trading. SBF's 2021 $500m investment, sold in 2024 for about $1.3bn, would have been worth about $37bn at the expected valuation. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-20-wublockchain-weekly-anthropic-pursues-record-set.md]

**Hyperliquid.** Hong Kong's SFC added Hyperliquid and Hyperliquid Hong Kong to its alert list of suspicious virtual asset trading platforms on 18 November. The regulator says they are unlicensed and suspected of promoting services to the Hong Kong public.

**CoinGecko.** The Solana Foundation holds a majority equity interest in CoinGecko; terms are undisclosed. CoinGecko will expand on-chain asset, RWA and derivatives coverage, market intelligence and trading-adjacent business.

**Strategy.** Strategy spent about $2.7bn repurchasing STRC preferred shares since July, about 52% of it above $99. From 9 to 15 November it repurchased $228.1m of STRC at about $99.93 and bought only 485 BTC for $33.4m. It has used about 89% of its $3bn preferred repurchase quota. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-20-wublockchain-weekly-anthropic-pursues-record-set.md]

**Bitcoin versus equities.** A DWF Labs report says Bitcoin's correlation with the Nasdaq is about 0.40 since 2020. When the Nasdaq falls more than 1% in a day, Bitcoin falls with it about three-quarters of the time. Bitcoin annualised volatility fell to 44%, below the semiconductor sector's 52%, for the first time in a decade. Since March, after removing broad Nasdaq effects, Bitcoin is positively correlated with software (+0.20) and negatively with chips (-0.30). Its 90-day correlation with gold hit a ten-year high of 0.59 in September and was about 0.49 at publication. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-20-wublockchain-weekly-anthropic-pursues-record-set.md]

**Fundraising.** Tether invested in SCRYPT; Securitize acquired River One; Codex raised $30m at $130m; Variational raised $110m at $1.5bn; Tether and Further put about $10m into MetaComp; Null raised $1.7m pre-seed.
