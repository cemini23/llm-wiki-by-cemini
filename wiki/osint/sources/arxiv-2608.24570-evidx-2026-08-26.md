## Narrative

EviDx treats diagnosis as active evidence-seeking rather than static case→answer prediction. **E-Synthesis** builds interactive environments from raw cases; a clinical diagnostic scaffold organizes role-specialized agents, evidence tools, and evolving evidence states; an observer-guided harness regulates runtime exposure.

**Clinical domain wont_wire.** OSINT keeps a thin stub for harness vocabulary (scaffolded evidence acquisition, observer harness) — no product install, no clinical runtime.
