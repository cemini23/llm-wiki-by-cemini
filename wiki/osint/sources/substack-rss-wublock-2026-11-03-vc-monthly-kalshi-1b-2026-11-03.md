## Narrative

### The numbers

| Measure | October 2026 | vs September | vs October 2025 |
|---------|--------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Disclosed deals | **67** | +8.1% (62) | **−40.2%** (112) |
| Disclosed funding | **$2.099bn** | +65.4% ($1.269bn) | **−65.3%** ($6.046bn) |

By sector: DeFi 26.9%, CeFi 17.9%, AI 14.9%, RWA/DePIN 10.4%, Tool/Wallet 4.5%, L1/L2 4.5%, Privacy 1.5%, other 19.4%.

Deals with undisclosed amounts count in the deal total but not the dollar total.

### The tension in the two lines

Deal count is down 40% year over year while dollars are down 65%. So both fewer deals and smaller deals, with the dollar decline steeper — the average cheque shrank too. Month over month the reverse: deals up 8% and dollars up 65%, which means one or two rounds carried the month.

That round is **Kalshi**.

### Kalshi

**$1 billion raised at a $40 billion valuation.** The previous round closed in **May at $22 billion**. Co-led by **Sequoia Capital and Wellington Management**.

So the valuation roughly **doubled in about five months**, on a CFTC-regulated event-contract exchange.

That is the same $40bn figure Event Horizon reported from its show notes — see @sources/substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-02-episode-39-election-markets-with-sam-henry-lazar.md — and here it has a named data source and the prior round for comparison.

### Whop

**$200 million Series D at a $5.09 billion valuation**, co-led by Sequoia Capital and Shopify Ventures. Payments infrastructure for individual entrepreneurs and small merchants; revenue mostly a cut of platform transactions at a blended average fee of about **6%**.

### Why the Kalshi figure is the one to keep

A regulated prediction market doubling its valuation in five months, in a month when total crypto VC is down 65% year over year, is a specific claim about where capital thinks the growth is. It is also a claim that can be wrong — the regulatory picture is contested, as the Utah litigation shows.

**Caveat.** RootData-sourced, single newsletter, and figures may be revised.
