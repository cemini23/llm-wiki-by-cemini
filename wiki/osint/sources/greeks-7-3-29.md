## Narrative

Academic paper on the execution and hedging of share repurchase programs (corporate buybacks executed on behalf of issuers by investment banks). Preview identifies the setup: company instructs bank to buy back shares rapidly; when the bank is permitted to hedge its exposure, it faces product-specific challenges. The paper combines "optimal strategy" (presumably stochastic-control / optimal-execution theory) with "deep hedging" (neural-network-based hedging, the Buehler/Lütkebohmert line).

Honest assessment: 28-page scope, clearly in the applied-quant-finance / optimal-execution literature. "Deep hedging" specifically is a well-defined technique since Buehler et al. 2019. The intersection with share-repurchase programs is a narrow corporate-finance application — practical for sell-side banks executing buybacks, less directly applicable to a retail / long-only systematic setup.

Relevance to Cemini: low direct applicability. The Cemini watchlist trades equities and crypto for own-account, not as an executing agent for corporate issuers. Useful as background on deep-hedging technique, which generalizes to other optimal-execution contexts (e.g., portfolio rebalancing under transaction costs). Home cluster (portfolio-strategy) is a loose fit — this is more properly market-microstructure / optimal-execution.
