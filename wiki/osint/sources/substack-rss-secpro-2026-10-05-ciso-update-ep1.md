## Narrative

Episode 1 of a new CISO-facing roundup. Five items:

- **NetScaler:** patching and recovery are **separate decisions** — a fix does not undo a compromise. CVE-2026-76504 advisory with fixed releases and temporary protection.
- **Cisco SD-WAN:** confirmed exploitation of an SD-WAN management flaw.
- **Warlock:** research on how an intrusion becomes widespread ransomware.
- **Microsoft annual report:** brings **AI access into the security review** — AI usage is now a governance surface.
- **US audit (GAO-26-108937):** persistent **connected-device inventory gaps**.

### Why it matters here

CISO-level awareness. Two threads worth noting: (1) **patch ≠ recover** as a defensive doctrine, and (2) **AI access as a security-review item** — the same agent-governance theme the wiki tracks. Route to Cybersecurity wiki awareness; no action brief.
