## Narrative

Kris Abdelmessih uses Kindle highlights from Geoffrey West's *Scale* as seed for a Claude project curriculum. Naive highlighting is self-deception; the option payoff is turning excerpts into **practice**, not recitation. Kindle export caps (~10%) force a mid-book dump-and-reset. Motivation: a Jane Street interview miss traced to not internalizing that variance is E[X²] − (E[X])².

**Concrete steal `[TENTATIVE]`:** export highlights as a curriculum seed, then force retrieval practice — reviewing notes is cram, not invention. Wiki-only learning method. No price/level. **No xsp scp.**
