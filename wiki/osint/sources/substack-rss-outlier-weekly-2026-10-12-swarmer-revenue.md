## Narrative

**The published form of the Swarmer thesis** (@sources/swarmer-long-thesis-2026-10-09.md). It follows the Oct 8-9 desk work and reflects the corrections applied after the multi-model audit. **Observational note, not advice** (CFTC Rule 4.41).

### The thesis, as published

**Swarmer is a software company.** It builds the **coordination layer** for many drones under jamming — not the drones. **Erik Prince** became non-executive chairman in Dec 2025. In combat since **April 2024** by company account; the company counts **>100,000 missions**. **The real swarms are small — three drones typically, up to eight**; the US CEO said the largest fielded was about **25**.

### Where the money comes from — three parts

1. **Licenses.** Per-drone, **$750 to $30,000** a unit. Gross margin **84.8%** in Q2 — but read carefully: **cost of revenue was ~$33,000 because the hardware cost sat in an advance and no drone had been deployed yet.** The margin reflects timing as much as the license business.
2. **One arrangement — nearly the whole top line.** ~**99% of Q2 revenue** and ~90% of H1, from the **SkyKnight** group. Swarmer licensed software to them **and agreed to pay them ~$4.9M** ($2.5M for 82 drones + $2.4M for integration/OS/marketing) against their ~$3.9M consideration. Where a company pays a customer more than the customer pays it, **part of the payment reduces revenue**. The filing says it **could not substantiate the fair value of the $2.4M of services.** By end-June ~$0.4M had reduced revenue. **The drones were valued ~$1.8M and none had been deployed.**
3. **An acquisition that has not closed.** **Ratel Robotics** — audited **2025 net sales $18.6M** (from $1.5M). Price up to **$224M**, but the proxy marks it nearer **$189M** at the September price, mostly stock and earnouts. Earnout targets: **$77M revenue / $8.47M operating income (2026)**, then $130M/$14.3M and $187M/$20.57M. **H1 2026 delivered $9.6M of Ratel revenue.** Ratel shows **$86.6M of customer advances** — a **contract liability, not cash** (unrestricted cash $2.3M, restricted $22.6M), and failure to deliver can mean refunds or penalties. **Ratel separately reports substantial doubt about going concern.**

### The Baltics connection

The letter opens with the **Oct 5-6 Latvia incursion** (armed men, damaged electricity cabinet, two border cameras taken — the border chief called it organised crime), **Lithuania's ~100 troops on its Kaliningrad border** two days later, and **Russia's 48 missiles + 130 drones on Ukraine (≥28 killed)**. **The read: this is the 2027 window testing the door a year early.** If the front widens toward the Baltics, the systems that work in Ukraine become the ones NATO reaches for. **Poland ~4.7% and Lithuania ~5.3% of GDP on defence** (NATO 2026 estimates).

**The bet is not this quarter's revenue** — it is whether the **autonomy layer becomes the layer every drone and ground robot runs on.**

### What to watch

The **Ratel shareholder vote 2 Nov 2026**; the deal's **outside date 7 Jan 2027**; whether H2 2026 turns advances into **delivered, accepted** robots (the revenue half — the **operating-income half is harder**); any **US or NATO contract**; and **Baltic news**.
