## Narrative

Unified VLM HTR. Page–Region–Line. Four tasks: full transcript, physical-line coverage, localization, localized recognition. Sparse MoE with always-on shared branch + Sparsegen. Seven handwriting benchmarks. Atto FILE methodology. **Do not replace Transkribus as product HTR.** No Barone PII. Human-gated `truth.ged`.
