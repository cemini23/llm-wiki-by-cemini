## Narrative

RSS teaser. The argument: the offshore defense does not protect a prediction market. The CFTC has acted before, against Polymarket in 2012 and 2022, and Polymarket is now a CFTC-designated contract market.

Volumes reported: Polymarket launched 15-minute Bitcoin up-and-down markets on its US app on 22 September 2026 at 12:00 ET. Kalshi has offered up-and-down markets since December with lifetime volume above $20B. Kalshi set a record 27 September volume of about $3.04B. Trailing seven-day volumes: Kalshi $15.8B, Polymarket US $2.83B, DKeX $607M.

Named: CFTC, DOJ, Polymarket, Kalshi, DraftKings DKeX, Crypto.com, Novig, Robinhood, Bernstein, Sixth Circuit.
