## Narrative

A **macro thesis** on free-floating currencies and sovereign debt. The part after "Read more" is paywalled.

### The argument

**Free-floating currencies are the release valve** for economic warfare and financial imbalances. The market prices a currency — and a currency is the gateway to a country's goods and services, so **the market sets the premium for access**. This, the author argues, has driven every credit-cycle melt-up and crisis of the last 40 years, and it is the most misunderstood.

**The target of the piece:** the "devaluation solves all problems" narrative — the claim that governments can print out of any crisis and that devaluation means risk assets rise forever. The author calls it a **seductive lie** headed for a reckoning.

**The concrete case — France.** French **yields are blowing out vs German Bunds**, and Euro swaps are pricing the risk. The **CAC 40 is down considerably**. Europe's specific problem: it "allowed China to hollow out its industrial base," so the government must spend more than it makes. **The author's read: there is no turning back for Europe, only limiting damage.**

### The second argument

**Politics and regulation are the largest drivers of rates and FX on any horizon beyond a few days** — so keeping politics out of markets is a mistake. The author ties this to **quant alpha decay**: everyone has become a short-term momentum trader.

### Why it matters here

A **macro regime read** for the GuruWatcher and macro lanes: the **France-vs-Bund spread** and Euro swaps as the stress signal, and the devaluation-narrative critique as a falsifier frame. **Awareness only. No xsp scp. No positions.**
