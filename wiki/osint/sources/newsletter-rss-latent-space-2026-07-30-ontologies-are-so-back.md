## Narrative

Frank Coyle (UC Berkeley / AIEWF): agents need ontology "logical guardrails" beside probabilistic LLMs — neurosymbolic stack. Neo4j Eifrem: business ontology + technical metadata ontology + execution-trace ontology → thin agents on shared semantic layer. Idehen: RDF agent memory. Maintenance remains hard; agents that update ontologies change the character of the problem.
