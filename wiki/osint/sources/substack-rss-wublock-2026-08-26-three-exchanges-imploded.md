## Narrative

Podcast with former ops employee (Dougao) at **WebSea**, **JuCoin**, **CoinUP** — three controversial mid-tier exchanges. Pattern: platform-token narratives, high-yield packaging, opaque withdrawal reviews, insider preferential releases, agent hierarchies. WebSea 2024 withdrawal freeze during BTC drawdown; employees could withdraw while users frozen.

Counterparty/venue risk awareness for crypto-perp research — not CeminiSuite integration. Flag "principal protection / high returns / locked mining" as red flags. **No trading on these venues from prod stack.**
