## Narrative

**[RETRACTED]** Prior framing that tied **Libro de Transcripción** mainly to Ley **169-14 Group A**. Correction: **Group A** = original Acta validated = nationality **at birth**; **Group B** = never in ordinary books → naturalization → **Libro de Transcripción** (**Resolución 13-2024**) = **not** birthright → Clock A break risk. **Libro de Extranjería** still confers no nationality.

**Acta Única (Jul 2025)** may **mask** inscripción vs transcripción on its face — soft-require QR metadata or *copia in extensa* `[OPEN QUESTION 2026-07-29]`. JCE online **hard-requires cédula**; no-cédula paths = POA / DR consulate abroad (digitized only) / gestor under *their* cédula.

Apostille: Acta Única QR → MIREX `servicios360.mirex.gob.do` (**RD$620**, ~**24** business hours). Translations: **PGR** signature legalization **then** MIREX apostille. Naturalization/status search: **MIP** Ventanilla Única (**RD$1,000**, 5–10 days). Santo Domingo Italian checklists remain **silent** on Extranjería / Transcripción — silence ≠ trap absence. L.74 cutoff **27 Mar 2025 23:59 Rome**.
