## Narrative

Lovable CTO Fabian Hedin describes SaaS shifting from human-opened apps toward **agent-accessible capabilities**. Published apps expose selected functions through a **hosted MCP server**, yielding one product with dual interfaces: human UI and agent tools usable from ChatGPT, Claude, and other MCP clients. Hedin frames a “digital brain” with max context plus reusable capabilities, including async resume for deployments and recurring checks. Growth claims cited in the piece: ~$500M ARR run rate, $13.3B Series C valuation. CCC steal: treat internal tools as MCP capabilities behind one agent entry point — pattern reference only, no prod wire.
