## Narrative

### The design

The **Eurozone Consumer Expectation Survey**: **2,000 households** each in Germany, France, Italy, and Spain, plus **1,000** each in the Netherlands and Belgium. Respondents are asked what they would do with a lottery win of **€5,000 to €50,000**.

The windfall framing is deliberate. Two established behavioural findings should push investment up: windfall money sits in a different mental account and is treated more aggressively, and people with more money invest more in stocks because they can afford to lose more.

### The result

Both effects appear, and both are small.

| Measure | Finding |
|---------|---------|
| Participation of **non-stockholders** after a €50,000 win | rises **8.4 to 12.8 percentage points** |
| Share of money in stocks for **existing** investors | **hardly changes** |
| Households that would still hold **no stocks at all** after the windfall | **more than 60%** |

### What that means

The second and third rows are the ones to carry.

The intervention moves **participation marginally** and moves **allocation not at all**. People who already invest do not become more aggressive; people who never invest mostly keep not investing.

Klement's conclusion is that it takes a lot more than money to change this. The data supports a narrower reading: **the barrier is not affordability**. A windfall removes the budget constraint and the behaviour barely moves, which points at something structural — habit, trust, product access, or a cultural prior about equities — rather than at liquidity.

### Why a 12-point move is the wrong number to quote

An 8.4–12.8 point rise in participation sounds like a finding. Against a base where more than 60% *still* hold nothing after a €50,000 windfall, it is a small shift within a large non-participation pool. Both numbers are in the article and only one is the story.

**Caveat.** Single source, summary of a study not read here. Stated intentions in a survey are not observed investment decisions.

## Dead Ends

- **Quoting the participation rise without the base rate.** Over 60% still hold nothing after the win.
- **Reading stated intentions as behaviour.** It is a survey of what people say they would do.
