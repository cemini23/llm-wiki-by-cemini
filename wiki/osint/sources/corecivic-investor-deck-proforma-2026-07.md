## Narrative

Slides from CoreCivic Jul 2026 investor materials (post **California City + Otay Mesa** sale). Headline claims: reduce indebtedness, return capital, flexibility to grow/diversify, execute long-term plan.

**Footnotes (slide):**
- (1) **$30.38** share price **2026-06-30**; **101.284M** FD shares.

**Debt maturity (pro forma):** **2028** $108M term loans · **2029** $500M senior notes · **2031–2040** $133M mortgage.

### Leverage box vs honest math

| Metric | Pre | Post (slide) |
|--------|-----|--------------|
| Net debt / Adj EBITDA | **2.8x** (Q1'26 TTM) | **0.0x** PF |

**Reconciliation:** Pro forma **net debt $123M** ÷ FY26 guided Adj EBITDA **~$454–462M** ≈ **0.27x** — **not** 0.0x. Slide **0.0x** likely **marketing rounding**, **secured-leverage** presentation, or assumes **further cash sweep** not static in the table. Footnote (4) warns EBITDA may **fall** post-sale (mgmt-fee-only economics) → true leverage **higher** than slide.

**Implied TTM EBITDA at 2.8x:** $1,196M ÷ 2.8 ≈ **$427M**.

### Enterprise value bridge — key fade metric

**EV falls ~$1,073M** ($4,273M → $3,200M) despite **$1.5B gross** asset sale. Market read: **cash + delever ≠ replacement of sold earning assets**; equity mcap flat at **$3,077M** while EV compresses.

### Owned beds by agency (pre vs post transaction)

| Agency | Pre | Post |
|--------|-----|------|
| State governments | 43% | **46%** |
| USMS | 20% | **21%** |
| **ICE** | **25%** | **20%** |
| **Idle facility** | **10%** | **11%** |
| BOP | 1% | 2% |
| Local governments | 1% | 1% |

**Read-through:**
- Sold sites were **premium ICE** beds (Otay + Cal City — **active**, overcrowded), not the **idle** bucket.
- **Idle share rises** (10% → 11%) because **denominator shrinks** after removing ~4,554 sold beds; idle absolute beds **unchanged** (~7k per Q1 commentary).
- Portfolio **more state-DOC weighted** post-sale; ICE **owned** exposure down **5pp** while CXW **retains ICE management** on sold sites.

### Why slide didn't save the stock (Jul 6)

Credit metrics improved; **EV down ~$1B**; **no committed buyback $** on slide; Jul 2 **flat** leak tell; stock **premarket +8% → close −2%**. See @concepts/corrections-reit-asset-sale-event-study.md.
