## Narrative

Maps Canadian citizenship history (pre-1947 British subject → 1947 Act → 1977 dual citizenship → 2009 first-generation limit → Bill C-71 / Dec 2025 1,095-day rule), why long-form provincial birth certs prove *jus soli* while wallet citizenship certificates do **not**, provincial/territorial vital-stats portals, and the **post-11-Jan-2024 Hague** apostille split:

- **GAC** for federal docs + MB/NB/NL/NS/PEI/territories
- **Provincial competent authorities** for AB/BC/ON/QC/SK (GAC rejects those provincial docs)

Toronto consulate: legalization of Canadian public docs ended; apostille required. Translation path often needs **two** apostilles (cert + notarized translation) unless translate in Italy.

### Product traps

Short-form trap; citizenship-certificate ambiguity; GAC fallacy; stale pre-2024 legalization blogs; Quebec maiden-name retention; British-subject-era naturalization still counts as voluntary foreign acquisition under Italian 1912 law.
