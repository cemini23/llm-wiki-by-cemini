## Narrative

Livestream thesis: next macro shock likely comes from **interest rate volatility repricing off historically low levels**. NFP print set a short-term top in both bonds and equities; the FOMC level in bonds is now the line defining interest-rate risk into this week. System structurally short rates (mortgages, corporate debt, leverage) → a rate-vol repricing hits more portfolios than expected. MOVE at lows while growth runs in excess of leverage compresses the spring; a money impulse or crude supply shock blows rates out.

**Yield curve regime indicator:** downward bond moves driven by **bear steepening**; long end says the Fed is not restrictive enough. Pine code for the regime indicator is shared in the Substack chat. Government outlays accelerating (absolute + share of GDP) → higher long-end premium; trade-war mechanic: foreign savings flood in → someone runs a matching deficit; post-2008 deleveraged household → US government is the borrower, while China suppresses its currency to hold the imbalance.

**Cross-asset:** S&P and Russell highs/lows directly connected to bonds; trading ES without watching bonds misses half the picture. Tomorrow's stream: Inflation Risk In The Global Economy (commodities, wages, government spending).
