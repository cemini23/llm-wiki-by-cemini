## Narrative

MARL paper on coordinating multiple robots in narrow/confined environments where congestion and interference limit collective performance. Preview: inspired by insect colonies that coordinate via stigmergy (modifying and interpreting environmental traces — e.g., pheromone trails), proposes a "Stigmergic Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning" method. The stigmergy concept is well-established in swarm robotics; the novelty is combining it with deep-RL.

Honest assessment: stigmergy + DRL is a legitimate research direction — the key question is whether the "environmental trace" is learned or hand-coded, and whether the method scales. 11 pages is on the short side for a full empirical evaluation. The robotics-focused experiments will not directly transfer to trading, but the conceptual architecture (agents leaving state-changing traces that other agents read) maps onto a shared-state trading architecture where agents communicate through a persistent data store (e.g., shared Redis Streams — see 3-19-redis-streams-research) rather than via direct messaging.

Relevance to Cemini: indirect but interesting. The stigmergic pattern is one of two plausible coordination paradigms for decentralized trading agents (the other being explicit message-passing as in DG-MAPPO / 3-29-10). A Redis-Streams-based architecture is literally stigmergic: agents write to streams, other agents read and respond to the persistent trace. So this paper provides theoretical grounding for a pattern already emergent in the Cemini infrastructure.
