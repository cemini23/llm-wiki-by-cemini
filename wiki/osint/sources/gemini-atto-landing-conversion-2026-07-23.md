## Narrative

Sixteen-site teardown across local-first vaults (Obsidian, Anytype, Cryptomator, Ente, Joplin, Logseq), genealogy/heritage OTP (Family Tree Maker, RootsMagic, Family Historian, Storyworth, Scrivener), and indie Mac utilities (CleanShot X, Screen Studio, DEVONthink, TablePlus, Soulver).

**Conversion thesis:** $299 OTP anchors against law-firm retainers ($5k–$10k), not $10/mo SaaS. Demo density (CleanShot GIF tiles) + TCO inversion (Screen Studio vs SaaS) + open-format / local-first trust (Obsidian/DEVONthink) beat abstract SaaS heroes.

**Recommended spine:** Vault hero → high-stakes problem → GIF bento → data-sovereignty manifesto → this-is/isn’t → law-firm price anchor → legacy guarantee → FAQ/CTA.

**Caveats:** Citation quality uneven (some design-system listicles). Refund window in this DR leans **30-day**; other DRs say **14-day** — resolve before ship. macOS exclusivity is a steal from Scrivener anti-pattern (separate OS SKUs).
