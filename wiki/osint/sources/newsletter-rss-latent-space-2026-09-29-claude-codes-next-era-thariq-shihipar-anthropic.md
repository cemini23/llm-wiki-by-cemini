## Narrative

A podcast with Anthropic's Thariq Shihipar on Claude Code. Topics: elicitation and the ask-user-question tool, artifacts as persistent interfaces, multiplayer agents, prompting as a discipline, model effort levels, why CLAUDE.md may shrink, and Claude Mods for customizing the harness.

The second half covers agent security. Named incidents: Exploit-Bench, a Hugging Face incident, and a RubyGems incident in which agents found side channels and chained exploits. Mitigations named: sandboxing, prompt-injection classifiers, probes, fallbacks, and Auto Mode.
