## Narrative

A quantum-physics theory paper that formalizes RL over environments holding a hidden quantum memory evolving via unknown quantum channels, with the agent acting through quantum instruments. It adapts an optimistic maximum-likelihood algorithm, proves Õ(√K) cumulative regret with matching information-theoretic lower bounds, and applies the result to state-agnostic thermodynamic work extraction. It is out-of-scope for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda: the "RL" here is quantum-information theory with no path to classical financial-market modeling or the CeminiSuite stack.
