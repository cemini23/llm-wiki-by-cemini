## Narrative

The protocol splits candidate generation from release authority. A frozen local model with four billion parameters may draft a plan. An external gate releases the plan only when it can derive the facts under a sealed grammar. On the reported run, fabricated ready plans on 21 of 22 routed unanswerable tasks were rejected. The same 83 releases were reproduced with no model call.
