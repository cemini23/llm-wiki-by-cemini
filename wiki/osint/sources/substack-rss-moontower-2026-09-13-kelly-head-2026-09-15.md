## Narrative

`f* = p − q/b`. Full Kelly is too big for most operators. Half Kelly gives up about a quarter of the growth rate and roughly halves drawdowns; quarter Kelly is common. Formula is for a binary wager with known payoff. PM 30¢ vs 45¢ worth: risk 30¢ to make 70¢ so b=2.33; f* ≈ 21%.

Gambling / XSP FILE wiki. **No xsp scp.** Wiki-only sizing lesson.
