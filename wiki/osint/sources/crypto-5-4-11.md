## Narrative

A Cisco Research position paper proposing a two-dimensional coordinate framework (cryptographic resilience vs. computational capability) to map the migration from classical to quantum-resilient cryptographic infrastructure, arguing the "Quantum Gap" between CRQC arrival and quantum-safe adoption is the highest systemic risk. This is post-quantum-cryptography migration strategy / infrastructure policy, not finance, with no path to Cemini's trading-stack agenda; reclassified out-of-scope.
