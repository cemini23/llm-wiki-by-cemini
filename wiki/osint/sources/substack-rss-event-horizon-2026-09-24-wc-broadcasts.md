## Narrative

| Brand | Logos in the teaser | Share or span |
|-------|---------------------|---------------|
| ADI PredictStreet | 7,384 | 78.9% of prediction-market logos; every match |
| Kalshi | 1,582 | 41 matches; almost all knockout rounds |
| Fanatics Markets | 394 | 39 matches |
