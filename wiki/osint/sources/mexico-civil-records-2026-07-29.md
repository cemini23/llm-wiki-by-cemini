## Narrative

Mexico is strict *ius soli*; mid-century *Carta* practice required Italian renunciation; dual for *por nacimiento* from **20 Mar 1998** — do not back-apply. Ambassciata **CDMX rejects SIDEA extracts** — need **copia fiel del libro** + **state** apostille (Secretaría General). Federal SEGOB DICOPPU covers SRE nationality docs, not state civil actas.

**e-Apostille (from Oct 2025) = federal criminal records only** — **not** civil vital records. CURP ≠ nationality. Llave MX / SIDEA are locator/research aids; proxy/gestor is the diaspora default. Prenot@Mi Amb CDMX contention windows (Tue/Wed/Thu 19:00 CDMX). Embassy *perito* list for translation; Amb legalizes translator — that is **not** a Mexican apostille on the translation.
