## Narrative

### The dominant fact: the fee share is gone

Bitcoin transaction fees were **0.31%** of total block rewards on 11 October. That is the second-lowest daily reading in a dataset that starts in July 2016; only 15 February 2026 was lower at 0.30%. The ratio fell **50.4%** in 30 days and stayed below 1% for **51 consecutive days**. Eight of the ten lowest daily readings in the dataset occurred in 2026.

CryptoQuant analyst Axel Adler Jr. puts the 30-day average fee share at **0.64%** of miner revenue. Fees have stayed below 1% since mid-August 2025.

Miner revenue is therefore almost entirely block subsidy. That makes the halving schedule and the hashprice the whole business model.

### The hashprice squeeze

Bitcoin hashprice fell to about **$33 per PH/s per day**, the lowest since August.

At that rate, a rig with **25 J/TH** efficiency earns about **$32.68 per PH/s per day** after a 2% pool fee. At **$0.06/kWh** the same rig spends about **$36 per day** on electricity. That is a negative gross margin before hosting, maintenance or any other cost.

Per-coin costs sharpen the point:

| Rig | Electricity cost per BTC at $0.05/kWh |
|---|---|
| Antminer S19 | about $86,700 |
| Antminer S21 | about $44,300 |

The S19 figure is above spot. The S21 still has a cushion.

### Production: two cohorts, two directions

| Cohort | Output |
|---|---|
| IREN, Core Scientific, Bitfarms, TeraWulf, Cipher | 7,075 BTC YTD, down about 47% from 13,263 |
| MARA, CleanSpark, Riot, HIVE, American Bitcoin | Up 16% year on year |

IREN, Core Scientific and TeraWulf each fell more than 50%. Bitfarms fell 49% and Cipher 18%.

The split matters more than the aggregate. The declining cohort is the one that pivoted capacity toward AI and HPC hosting. That is a business-model change, not an operating failure.

### Zcash

ZEC is up about **3,000%** since mid-2025. Cypherpunk Technologies, backed by the Winklevoss twins, controls about **20%** of network hashrate. Fortitude Mining, backed by Barry Silbert, plans to reach about 20%. Grayscale launched a Zcash ETF in August; a Winklevoss application followed in October.

Hashrate concentration near 40% across two friendly parties is a governance risk, not just a market fact. [TENTATIVE: Bloomberg reporting via WuBlockchain]

The NU7 upgrade activates at block height 3,543,405. It cuts block time from 75 seconds to **25 seconds**, removes **60%** of each block's transaction fees from circulation until about February 2031, and shuts down the legacy Sprout pool.

### The cost structure comparison

Vitalik Buterin's chart puts Bitcoin mining at about **40%** capital expenditure and **45.9%** electricity. AI inference is about **76%** capex and **4.8%** electricity. The two industries compete for the same power and the same capital goods, but their cost curves respond to different inputs. That is the economic basis of the miner-to-AI pivot recorded above.
