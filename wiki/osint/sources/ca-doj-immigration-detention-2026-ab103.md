## Narrative

175-page fifth AB 103 report covering **seven** California ICE detention facilities inspected in **2025**. Findings: conditions largely **worsened** vs 2023 visits; mass-deportation surge drove **overcrowding** and strained medical/food/hygiene resources; **six detainee deaths** Sep 2025–Mar 2026 (four Adelanto, two Imperial) — highest since AB 103 reviews began (2017).

**Facilities inspected:** Adelanto IPC + Desert View Annex (GEO), Golden State Annex + Mesa Verde (GEO), Imperial (MTC), Otay Mesa (CoreCivic), California City (CoreCivic). **Central Valley Annex** (McFarland, GEO, 700 beds) began receiving ICE detainees **Apr 2026** while report was finalizing — **not inspected**; future AB 103 cycle.

**Population snapshot (Table 2, site-visit dates):** statewide **6,028** detainees at inspection time vs **~2,300** in 2023 (+162%). Key YoY facility deltas from prior Cal DOJ visit:

| Facility | Operator | ICE contract beds | Count (2023 visit) | Count (2025 visit) | Δ |
|----------|----------|-------------------|--------------------|--------------------|---|
| Adelanto | GEO | 1,940 | 7 (Nov 2023) | 1,570 (Jul 8, 2025) | surge |
| Desert View | GEO | 750 | 417 | 517 | +24% |
| Golden State Annex | GEO | 700 | 159 (May 2023) | 569 (May 2025) | **~258%** |
| Mesa Verde | GEO | 400 | 41 (May 2023) | 370 (May 2025) | **~802%** |
| Imperial | MTC | 704 | 492 | 627 | +28% |
| Otay Mesa | CXW | 1,142 | 1,187 (Sep 2023) | **1,433** (Oct 6, 2025) | +21%; **only CA site over ICE contract bed cap** |
| California City | CXW | 2,560 | N/A (not operating) | 942 (Nov 19, 2025) | new facility |

**Otay Mesa nuance:** facility staff reported **1,970** total beds (ICE + USMS); ICE allocation **1,142**; guaranteed minimum **750**. Oct 2025 ICE count **1,433** exceeded contract allocation by **291** (~25%); total facility count including USMS exceeded contract cap by **448** (~39%). Overcrowding via cots/"boats"; insufficient toilets per PBNDS.

**California City:** opened Aug 2025; NDS (not PBNDS) applies; "crisis-level" medical understaffing at Nov 2025 visit (~1 physician for ~942 detainees); intakes sometimes in vacant housing pod.

**Thesis relevance:** Primary source for **CA overcrowding weak-signal** posts (e.g. trader social citing Otay 1,433/1,142, Mesa Verde 802%, Golden State 258%). **Not** evidence of federal turnkey purchase or greenfield expansion plans — report documents **in-facility** surges, not adjacent vacant land. Cross-check occupancy vs ICE biweekly ADP monitor (@meta/ice-detention-stats-cadence.md): DOJ counts are **point-in-time at 2025 inspections**; ICE excel reports **FY26 YTD ADP** vs **guaranteed minimum** (different metric and date).

**Secondary coverage (same primary):** KPBS, CalMatters, Capital & Main, CNN, SF Chronicle, NBC 7, Artivist.Media — all May 15, 2026.
