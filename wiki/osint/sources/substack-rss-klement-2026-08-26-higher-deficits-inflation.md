## Narrative

Klement summarizes Hazell & Hobler (2025): 2020 Georgia Senate runoff as natural experiment — Democrats winning both seats implied ~$900bn+ extra stimulus. Bond markets, inflation swaps, and dividend futures adjusted **in real time** during the runoff. Estimated: +1pp budget deficit → +0.19pp on 2-year-ahead price level expectations; deficit shock accounted for ~**one third** of 2021–2022 inflation (before supply-chain/energy shocks).
