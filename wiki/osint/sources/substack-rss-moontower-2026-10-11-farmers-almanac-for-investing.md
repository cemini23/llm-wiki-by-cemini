## Narrative

A review of **Exponential Wealth: Centuries of Stock and Bond Returns** — the 50th-anniversary rebuild of Ibbotson & Sinquefield's **SBBI**, spanning **1926–2025**.

### The century in numbers

- **$1 in US large caps (1926) → $14,751** by end-2025 (**$814 real**). Long Treasuries: **$117 nominal / under $8 real**. T-bills: **$25 / under $2**.
- **Real compound returns: stocks 6.9% · long Treasuries 1.9% · bills 0.3%.**
- **About a third of the century sat below a prior real high** (1929–WWII, 1966–82, 2000–2012).
- A 50% three-year run-up was followed by **another 50% run-up ~3× as often as a full reversal** (144 vs 50 of 394 episodes, 1792–2024).
- **Top 25 companies ≈ 50% of US market cap** — a level last seen in the 1930s. Tech ≈ 40% of cap.
- **Investors held the least cash when expected returns were highest.**
- Micro cap: **16.5% arithmetic / 11.1% geometric, σ = 37%**; mid cap matches the compound return at σ = 24%.
- **October 1987 was a 1-in-10¹²⁸ event under lognormal i.i.d.** — the chapter links fat tails to the persistent **apparent overpricing of OTM S&P puts**.

### The forecast disagreement (the useful part)

**Ibbotson / SBBI, 2026–2050:** **8.0% nominal, 5.6% real**, an equity risk premium of **~4.7% over cash**. (Raw US history would say 9.5% / 7.0%; they pull toward global history.)

**Elm Wealth, implied from current yields and valuations (Sep 30):** **US stocks 2.92% real / 5.30% nominal**; non-US stocks 5.78% real; 10-year TIPS **2.91% real**; **US equity risk premium over TIPS ≈ 0.01%**.

**The tension:** history says ~4.7% over cash; today's valuations say ~0 over TIPS. Both sides agree the premium is a **poor near-term predictor**, and that a forecast is "the center of a wide distribution, not a guarantee." Also worth keeping: **vol is more predictable than return**, so vol is the sounder basis for "know-nothing" sizing.

### Why it matters here

A **reference dataset and a forecast tension** for the macro lane — the equity-risk-premium gap is the number to carry. **Wiki-only; no scp.** Note: Moontower **#331** republishes this piece (@sources/substack-rss-moontower-2026-10-11-moontower-331.md).
