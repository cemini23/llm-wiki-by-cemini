## Narrative

Livestream teaser on interest-rate mechanics. Takeaways relevant to GuruWatcher watches:

1. **Rates and FX are the same trade in two markets** — Fed policy mistakes show in the long end; issuance shifts show in the currency. Watch both or miss half the picture.
2. **Yen divergence** explained by quantity of money: MoF funding deficits with bills while BoJ suppresses the short end devalues the exchange rate even with rate differentials flat.
3. **Impossible trinity** as the constraint map: fixed FX / free capital movement / independent monetary policy — pick two. China (closed account) vs US (free movement + independent policy) asymmetry shapes global flows.
4. **2y yield drop came from the inflation-swap side while real rates stayed restrictive**; the long end barely moved → the Fed can stay restrictive longer without credit stress — a high pain threshold, not dovishness.
5. **Bonds won't bid until the AI CapEx cycle ends or its credit collapses**; front-run via the value chain: electricity permits, zoning approvals, grid buildouts precede data-center press releases.
6. **Buying breakouts beats buying dips in a trending devaluation.**
