## Narrative

**Thesis:** Kalshi learned **velocity + volatility → volume**. Sports still ~70–80% notional, but **crypto ~20%+ daily**; **Bitcoin 15-minute markets ~$1.1B** of **$1.5B** crypto notional (7d, Ticker Tracker). Kalshi expanded **15-min gold/silver** + hourly temperature; Polymarket Intl has **5-min crypto**.

**Implications for CeminiSuite:** Short-horizon contracts increase feed churn and MM surface — relevant to pm-canary / cross-venue monitoring, not immediate prod wire. FanDuel Q2: moving sports contracts to **Crypto.com** exchange; **~$50M market-making revenue** expected 2026.
