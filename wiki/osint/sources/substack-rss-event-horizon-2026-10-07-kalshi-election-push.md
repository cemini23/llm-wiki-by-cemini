## Narrative

### The Kalshi election push (the opinion + the numbers)

Kalshi ran a **full-court press on election-market accuracy** ahead of the midterms, publishing transparency features and a paper. Claims:

- Election trading volume is **higher in 2026 than in 2024** (a presidential year); election volume grew **6× faster than football** volume in 2026 vs 2025.
- **Low-volume markets (<$10,000) show Brier scores on par with top forecasters** (~0.1).
- Viewers outnumber traders **5:1** on midterm markets.

The author's corrective, worth keeping: election volume is still a **rounding error** — **$1.27 B YTD, ~0.5% of Kalshi's notional**, less than a week of football. And: markets *pricing* an outcome better than polls is not the same as markets *calling* an outcome.

### The regulation news

- **CFTC proposes crypto rules (CTX and CAM).** Chairman Selig's WSJ op-ed: after the Senate failed to advance the CLARITY Act, the CFTC proposes **Regulation Crypto Asset Transactions (CTX)** and **Regulation Crypto Asset Markets (CAM)**. Crucially, registration is **optional** — the CFTC cannot compel registration without Congress — but registered venues could offer **retail margin/leverage**, which state-licensed venues cannot. An **ANPRM** opened; comments due within 60 days.
- **Connecticut crackdown:** **ProphetX, Gemini, and Webull withdrew** from Connecticut; the DCP is in contact with six more (Polymarket, Coinbase, Crypto.com, Robinhood, Novig, Underdog).
- **UK:** Polymarket took **$77,507** of positions on whether HSBC and Lloyds fail by year-end. The **FCA** says it is engaging international regulators.
- **Kalshi US 500 index perpetual futures** launched (funding-rate perps).
- **Kalshi offered to cap its affiliate desk at 5% of quarterly volume** during the CFTC conflict-of-interest comment period.
- **Polymarket Protocol V2** — a smart-contract rebuild from the position token up (repo linked).
- **EDGE Markets** announced **programmatic capital allocation + agentic access** for market makers: pre-authenticated accounts with capital limits so agents execute within parameters.
- **Coinbase** launched 15-minute and hourly crypto price markets.
- **FanDuel** secured a standalone FCM after moving sports contracts off CME.
- **ProphetX** filed markets on **other exchanges' volume** (a meta-market).

### Why it matters here

The **CFTC CTX/CAM** proposal and the **Connecticut withdrawals** are the hard news. **EDGE Markets' agentic capital-allocation** pattern is the one directly relevant to the bot lanes — pre-authorised agents within capital ceilings. **No pm scp.**
