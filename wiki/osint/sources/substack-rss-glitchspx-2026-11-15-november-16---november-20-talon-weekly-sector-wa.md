## Narrative

A weekly options watchlist produced by a scanner, published with unusually explicit disclaimers about what its own labels mean. The most reusable content is the vocabulary section, which draws distinctions the scanner output would otherwise blur.

Scanner pulse, 15 November: 265 strict-qualified maps, 254 bullish against 11 bearish, a 95.5% bullish score-weight, average snapshot move across qualified setups -0.54%, and no sector-rotation signal detected. The author states plainly that these are setup classifications, not probabilities, and that score-weight is a scanner mix, not a confidence measure.

Directional content is mixed by design. SPXW and QQQ are labelled bullish, SPY and IWM bearish at their rally references, SMH bullish against a bearish SOXX, and NDX/NDXP returned no qualifying setup at all — an unqualified result, explicitly not a bearish signal.

Level semantics worth keeping: OTE is a quoted pullback reference, called a "reclaim reference" when it sits above spot. Structural invalidation is a reassessment boundary, not an order. GEX and VEX lists are separate exposure structures and do not form one sequential path. Expired anchors must be refreshed and a farther target does not extend a broken structure.
