## Narrative

This short post points to a longer Klement column on Reuters. It carries one published number and one argument.

The published number: Klement's year-end 2027 S&P 500 target is 5,000 points. He states that this is down 35% from the level at the time of writing.

The argument: the bull case for equities rests on very high earnings forecasts. History shows that forecasts fail most spectacularly exactly when the market is priced for exceptional future profit growth. Klement states that the market is now in that condition.

The post also records the pushback. Goldman published a strategy note the next day. That note argued that on future earnings growth, capex and valuations do not look bad. Klement's response is the title of the piece.

Two points of caution. First, the Reuters article itself is paywalled, so the evidence behind the 5,000 target is not in this source. Second, the 5,000 target and the 35% decline are Klement's own projections. They are not forecasts this wiki endorses.
