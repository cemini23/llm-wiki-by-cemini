## Narrative

Local San Diego recap of CoreCivic's Jul 6 announcement. **No new financial terms** vs 8-K. Adds **local political frame**: Otay "under the spotlight" after San Diego County supervisors were **denied full access** for a public-health investigation → **lawsuit vs DHS, ICE, CoreCivic**.

Quotes **Ryan Gustin** (CoreCivic PA): facility sales to government partners are not uncommon; operating government-owned facilities is an established business model.

Sale completed **Thursday** (Jul 2, 2026). CXW retains management under existing ICE contracts; workforce unchanged per article paraphrase; press release disclaims assurance on future management or contract terms.
