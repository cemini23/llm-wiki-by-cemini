## Narrative

Mathematical validity aspires to be anti-auratic (a theorem does not care who proved it), but attribution and *lived understanding* still carry aura. Drawing on Benjamin and Yehuda Rav (proofs as bearers of knowledge), Enayat argues AI extends technical participation from inscription/verification into **conjecture, proof construction, and explanation**. Proofs may become more abundant while loosening their link to human surveyability, provenance, and responsibility.

**Concrete claim `[TENTATIVE]`:** distinguish three registers — (1) anti-auratic validity, (2) genealogical aura of attribution, (3) lived understanding — and do not collapse "the machine emitted a proof" into "we understand it." CCC steal: Lean/agent formalization still needs a human surveyability gate; verifier output is not understanding.
