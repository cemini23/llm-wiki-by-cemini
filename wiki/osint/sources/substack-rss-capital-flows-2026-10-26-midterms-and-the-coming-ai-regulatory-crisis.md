## Narrative

The argument is that three catalysts land in one month and each one changes the flow regime. The three are the US midterms in early November, the Anthropic IPO in mid to late November, and a Fed decision in December that the market prices as a hike.

The base claim is about deregulation. Deregulation under the current administration has driven the liquidity cycle and credit issuance since early 2025. The author argues it is the least visible input because it never gets credited. The article cites Ken Griffin on the shift from the previous regulatory environment.

The AI trade is the concentration risk. Half of all index returns come from AI names. The trade rests on how fast capex produces a return, so any rule change that slows the pace of investment reprices the whole index.

The main stated risk is a Democratic sweep of both houses. A sweep returns the government to gridlock, and gridlock ends the deregulatory flow. The article says the AI regulation basket already moves in lockstep with the odds of a Democratic sweep.

The Anthropic IPO is treated as an information event, not only a financing event. It will show how much demand exists for neoclouds and hyperscalers. The Fed is described as falling behind the curve on inflationary pressure and needing to catch up.

The author's conclusion is that the bar for trades rises into 2027 across all three axes.
