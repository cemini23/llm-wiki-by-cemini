## Narrative

WuBlockchain lists the macro events to watch for the week of October 5 to 11, 2026 (ET). Rates and energy dominate.

- **Mon, Oct 5, 10:00 ET — U.S. September ISM Services PMI.** August rose to **55.4**, with new orders at **60.9** and prices at **72.6**. Employment stayed in contraction at **47.8**. A reading above 50 would signal services growth supporting hiring.
- **Wed, Oct 7, 10:30 ET — EIA petroleum inventories, week ended Oct 2.** Middle East supply risks pushed **Brent above $100** before the G7 announced the release of **100 million barrels** of emergency oil and fuel reserves. More draws support oil prices and inflation expectations; a build shows the releases are easing supply.
- **Wed, Oct 7, 14:00 ET — Minutes of the September Fed meeting.** The Fed voted **12–0** to raise rates by **25 basis points**, to a **3.75%–4.00%** target range. The minutes show the support threshold for another move.
- **Thu, Oct 8, 7:30 ET — Minutes of the September ECB meeting.** The ECB raised its deposit rate by **25 basis points to 2.50%** and revised its **2027 and 2028** inflation forecasts higher.
- **Fri, Oct 9, 10:00 ET — Preliminary October U.S. consumer sentiment.** September fell to **48.1**; one-year and long-run inflation expectations rose to **4.6%** and **3.4%**.

The bottom line: the minutes plus ISM, EIA, and Michigan data shape expectations for further tightening, Treasury yields, and tech valuations.
