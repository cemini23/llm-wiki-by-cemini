## Narrative

Hidden constraints live outside the tree. A **person** draws zones, not the agent (left alone, the agent starts in the scariest file). Zone verbs: green = tight loop; yellow = characterization tests then change; red = human pairing on every step or the work does not happen. Zones move only when earned.

Write down what the code cannot say. For yellow/red, keep a durable comprehension memo; claims cite a file, issue, ownership record, or dashboard. Instructions = unusual repo facts. Skills = reusable procedures. Harness = context, tools, permissions, tests, logs, recovery. Worktrees isolate changes, not behavior. Track lead time / review minutes / interventions / escaped defects — not lines generated.
