## Narrative

GTM pack for shipping Atto as a **local-first macOS utility on Gumroad** (not Mac App Store-first). Frames the product as risk mitigation for DIY jure sanguinis applicants facing non-refundable consular/comune fees and multi-thousand-dollar document stacks — not as Ancestry-class entertainment.

**Personas (Deliverable A):** (1) Jure sanguinis DIY applicant — checklist + document status anxiety; (2) privacy-conscious genealogist — anti auto-merge, perpetual license; (3) independent citizenship consultant — multi-vault / commercial use.

**Pricing thesis (Deliverable B):** two-tier lifetime licenses — Standard ~$49, Pro/Consultant ~$89 — anchored under MacFamilyTree (~$70) and beside MacWhisper-class local AI utilities; reject MRR for this class. Pack: notarized Universal `.dmg`, Setup/Workflow PDF, Cloudflare Zero-Trust family-share primer, Pro-only `atto-cli` wheel.

**Copy + demo (C/D):** headline “local-first command center”; Approve-only tree updates; citizenship checklists; optional Cloudflare Access share. 90s demo script: chaos tabs → drag URL → Approve → checklist → share toggle.

**Risk (E):** UPL — never “you qualify”; AI — suggest-only phrasing; Gatekeeper notarization as chargeback prevention; 14-day refund.

**Citation quality:** mix of citizenship-firm blogs, indie Mac utility comps, Tauri/Apple notarization docs. Fee amounts and 2025 Budget Law claims need primary-source lock before marketing copy ships. [NEEDS VERIFICATION 2026-07-22]

**Wiki scope:** GTM/pricing/listing. No Barone PII. No legal advice.
