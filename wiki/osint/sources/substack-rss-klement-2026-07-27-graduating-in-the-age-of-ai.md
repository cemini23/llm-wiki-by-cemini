## Narrative

Lee Tucker (Census) analysis: ages 22–24 employment down in high AI-exposure industries (IT, finance, professional services) since ChatGPT era; older cohorts in same industries less hit. Caveat: trends predate AI / COVID acceleration. Complements Jul 15 Jevons piece — net jobs ≠ even age/industry incidence.
