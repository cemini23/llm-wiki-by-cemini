## Narrative

European **Synthetic Risk Transfers (SRT)** let banks offload first-loss tranches while loans stay on balance sheet — frees Tier-1 capital. ECB concerns: banks sell **riskier** loans via SRT, reduce monitoring post-transfer, and **fund buyers** via correlated lending (~29% circular volume). 2008 CLO parallel. Macro risk monitor for regime playbook — cross-route gambling-wiki for bankroll/counterparty hygiene metaphor only.
