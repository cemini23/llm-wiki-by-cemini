## Narrative

Internal-linking playbook driven by Search Console data. **SEO wiki topic, not Atto product, not Jev.** Second-highest impressions of the wave at **400,800**.

**Sample and its limit.** The author analysed the internal links in **11 public Mailchimp articles**. All 11 linked to other educational pages, 7 linked to product pages in the article body, and all 11 linked to products through the footer. He states plainly that this shows how one site connects its pages and that **he did not test ranking changes**. Do not cite it as a ranking study.

**The eight-step playbook.**

1. **Check Search Console** — Search results performance, last three months, Web search, one country, one device filter. Export page performance with clicks, impressions, average position. Open a page's Queries tab and export. Compare the latest 28 days with the previous 28 to spot pages losing visibility.
2. **Group topics** — give the agent page titles and content plus the export; group pages by the question they answer and what the reader wants to do. Topic groups beat keyword matching ("authenticating a sending domain" is not the same group as "welcome email template").
3. **Pick pillars** — one existing broad guide per topic; link it to its support posts and back.
4. **Pick money pages** — where a visitor can buy, book a call, or start a trial. Assign one relevant money page per topic group and find the passages where that next step fits.
5. **Pick support posts** — narrower question pages. Link support posts to each other, up to the pillar, and to the money page where the product fits the task.
6. **Boost pages stuck on page 2** — start with pages averaging **positions 11 to 20** for a relevant query. Find source pages that already discuss the same task and have no contextual link. Draft the exact sentence and anchor text.
7. **Add money and pillar pages as footer links** — consistent site-wide route to main products and guides. Short labels. Verify each link reaches the current page. Inspect desktop and mobile.
8. **Vary link text** — write the link text for the sentence it appears in. The same guide can be linked as "welcome email sequence", "plan the emails that follow", "set up your welcome sequence", or "welcome sequence examples". Ask the agent to show the full sentence with each suggestion.

**The link structure used by the author.**

```
support posts ↔ support posts → money pages / pillar content
support posts → pillar content
money pages ↔ money pages
pillar content ↔ pillar content
pillar content → money pages
```

**Working-list schema.** Source page, destination page, link text, and the sentence that will contain the link. Whether the agent can apply edits or only prepare them depends on the CMS connection.
