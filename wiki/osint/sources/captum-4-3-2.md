## Narrative

Captum is a comprehensive library for model explainability in PyTorch, offering a range of methods from the interpretability literature to enhance users’ understanding of PyTorch mod- els. In this paper, we introduce new features in Captum 1 that are specifically designed to analyze the behavior of generative language models.
