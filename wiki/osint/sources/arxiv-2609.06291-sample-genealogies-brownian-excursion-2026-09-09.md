## Narrative

Bocharov and Harris: joint law of minima on a Brownian excursion conditioned to exceed level 1, sampled at k i.i.d. level-a intersections. Maps to coalescent times in Aldous CRT of height 1. Mathematical genealogy, not Italian civil-record product work. Atto skip (no HTR/GEDCOM/vault hook). No Barone PII.
