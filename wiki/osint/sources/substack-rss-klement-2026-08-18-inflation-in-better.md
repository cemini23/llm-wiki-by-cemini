## Narrative

Martin & Nagy Mohácsi (LSE, 2026) compare the Visegrad 4 around the 2022 inflation spike. Slovakia is in the euro (2009); PL/HU/CZ have independent policy and hiked faster. Despite that autonomy, **inflation was higher outside the euro** than in Slovakia. Deficits and GDP trends similar (Hungary worse). Resolution: the ECB is de facto Europe's central bank — balance-sheet gravity forces open neighbors to adjust. Inflation expectations stayed better anchored in the euro / Slovakia.
