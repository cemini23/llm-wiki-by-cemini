## Narrative

Galaxy: Q1 2026 crypto VC **~$4B / 355 deals**, about **−50% QoQ** funding and −16% deal count; new funds at the lowest since Q3 2020. Capital concentrated in later-stage firms with customers/revenue (57% of dollars). Trading/exchanges/lending still the largest bucket (~$2.6B). Stablecoin **payments** are the sector still printing successive sizable rounds (Rain, OpenFX, RedotPay, Mesh, Conduit) across cards, FX, wallets, bank connectivity. Fed: stablecoin mcap ~$317B (6 Apr 2026). Visa/Artemis: ~$10.2T trailing-12m volume, but ~36% of 2025 adjusted volume was CEX deposit/withdraw — **not** goods-and-services payments.
