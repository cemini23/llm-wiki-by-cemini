## Narrative

A MARL paper that proposes a robust traffic-signal-control framework validated in the PTV Vissim simulator, combining turning-ratio randomization, an exponential phase-duration action space, and a MAPPO/CTDE neighbor-based observation scheme, cutting average waiting time by over 10%. It is out-of-scope for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda: the domain is urban traffic-signal optimization, and although it uses CTDE/MAPPO (techniques that recur in financial MARL), the framework, action space, and simulator are traffic-specific with no direct trading-stack transfer.
