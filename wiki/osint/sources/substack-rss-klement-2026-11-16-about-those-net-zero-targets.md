## Narrative

Klement summarises a 2026 study by Simon Dietz and Nikolaus Hastreiter on whether corporate net-zero pledges move emissions.

Adoption rose sharply. Of roughly 2,000 global large-cap companies, only about 70 had a long-term net-zero target at the end of 2019. By the end of 2024 more than 1,200 had one, covering about two-thirds of the sample's combined market capitalisation. Most targets sit between 2040 and 2050.

The measured effect is directional but not significant. Three years after a company adopted a target, absolute emissions fell by about 15% on average and emissions intensity by nearly 10%. The paper's own conclusion, as reported here, is that this is not strong enough to be statistically significant.

This is a secondary summary of a primary study. The figures come from two charts in the post and were not re-derived here. Treat the numbers as a reading of Dietz and Hastreiter, not as an independent measurement.
