## Narrative

This is a short trade note, not an article. The author says he is taking a small punt on gold upside before the end of the year, and points to a thread for the detail.

The reasoning comes from a quoted post. The setup has three parts. First, GLD may have bottomed that morning. Second, the "bank hacking" narrative may have legs. Third, and this is the quantitative part, metals volatility screens cheap across the cross-section, and call skew sits at a low percentile.

The post names the two axes of the screen: the x axis is a "buy vol" algorithmic score, and the y axis is skew percentile. Cheap vol plus low skew percentile is the condition being bought.

The trade is explicitly small. The author calls it a punt. The position size, strikes, and expiry are not in the retrieved text.

Treat the screen readings as the author's own at the time of writing. They describe one moment and are not a standing signal.
