## Narrative

October 2026 derivatives volume across 11 major exchanges was about $3.75tn, up about 3.7% from $3.62tn in September. Six venues rose and five fell. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-18-october-2026-exchange-derivatives-volume-report.md]

| Venue | Volume | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Binance | $1.79tn | 47.8% |
| OKX | $652.3bn | 17.4% |
| Bybit | $381.6bn | 10.2% |

The top venue held 47.8%; the top three held 75.4%.

**Structural shift.** Deribit traded about $192.1bn, up roughly 507% month on month. Coinbase International Exchange merged into Deribit on 9 September, and the integration was declared complete around 1 October, with a large slate of USDC-settled perpetuals across crypto, equities, ETFs and commodities. Per Deribit's own statistics, USDC perps and futures rose from about $7.5bn to about $149.8bn and drove most of the increase. BTC coin-margined perps roughly doubled. Options did not surge in tandem. Official venue-wide turnover rose about 136%; this dataset is more futures-sensitive, so it prints a larger rise. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-18-october-2026-exchange-derivatives-volume-report.md]

**Ratio.** Futures-to-spot volume was 6.01x, up about 14.4% from 5.26x in September.

The same wash-trading caveat applies. The Deribit change is a reporting and product effect more than an organic volume effect, and that distinction matters when reading the 507%.
