## Narrative

This is a new feed in the inbox and it is off-domain for OSINT. The post is about search engine optimisation and AI search visibility. It has no financial, quant or prediction-market content. Routing: SEO wiki, not OSINT.

The thesis, recorded here briefly: loopholes in search are not a strategy, because they expire. Examples given are exact-match domains, article directories, parasite SEO, guest posting at scale, private blog networks, programmatic pages, and now scaled AI content and "inauthentic mentions" in the generative-engine-optimisation era. The durable investment is real, identifiable experts at the centre of the marketing. [Source: substack-rss-lily-ray-2026-11-19-why-building-trust-through-real-people-stands-th.md]

The post traces Google's guidance back to the 2003 Webmaster Guidelines, which already told site owners to make pages for users and warned against cookie-cutter content. It maps old spam policies onto current AI-search tactics.

Why it is in this wiki at all: it bears on how a public wiki or product page is surfaced by AI search, which is adjacent to the AI-industry trend of answer engines replacing link lists. That is the only OSINT link. No numbers here are usable for trading.
