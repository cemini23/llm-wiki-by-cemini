## Narrative

**Jun 26, 2026** — both CXW and GEO received bullish PT raises ahead of Jul 2 CXW close:

| Firm | Analyst | Ticker | Action | PT | Prior |
|------|---------|--------|--------|-----|-------|
| Benchmark | Bill Sutherland | CXW | Buy | **$36** | $28 |
| Northland | Greg Gibas | GEO | Outperform | **$40** | $30 |
| Northland | Greg Gibas | CXW | Outperform | **$40** | $32 |
| Zacks SCR | M. Marin | CXW | Valuation raise | **$36** | $31 |

**Benchmark (CXW):** Post–management call; cites ICE enforcement resumption, DHS turnkey pivot after warehouse retreat, and valuation framework for facility sales. Prior modeling: **80% probability × 2 facilities ≈ $680M after-tax** at 8.5× FY2027 EBITDA (sites not named in public coverage) `[CONFIRMED media / TENTATIVE model]`.

**Northland (GEO):** Post–sector meetings; **H2 2026 active**; government acquisition initiative **on track** with prior expectations `[CONFIRMED media]`.

**Northland (CXW):** PT **$32 → $40** same week `[CONFIRMED media]`.

**Jul 6 outcome:** CXW closed **~$30.42** despite cluster of **$36–40** PTs — market had **pre-priced** two-facility sale by Jun 26; Jul 2 flat close confirmed leak; Jul 6 was **sell-the-news** despite vindication.

### Jul 7, 2026 — Benchmark PT raise + buyback unlock

| Firm | Analyst | Ticker | Action | PT | Prior |
|------|---------|--------|--------|-----|-------|
| Benchmark | (media) | CXW | Buy (maintained) | **$41** | $36 |

- **PT bridge:** prior **$36** embedded **80% × 2-site ≈ $700M net**; revised **$41** adds the **~$400M realized upside** now that the sale actually closed above the modeled proceeds `[CONFIRMED media]`.
- **Buyback unlock (material):** CXW plans **~$600M of proceeds → debt reduction**, cutting leverage **under 2:1** and **enabling unrestricted share repurchases**; company now at **zero net debt**; mgmt reportedly **aggressively buying back shares** `[CONFIRMED media / TENTATIVE on pace]`. This is the **repurchase-over-debt** trigger the Campbell REIT event-study literature associates with **positive** abnormal returns — the catalyst absent on Jul 6.
- **DHS pipeline:** committed to **≥10 turnkey facilities**, retreating from other capacity-expansion strategies; CXW **in active discussions** for further ICE sites, assuming detention rates recover **H2 2026** `[CONFIRMED media]`.
- **Comp anchor:** $329,380/bed blended vs **~$250k national new-build** cost `[CONFIRMED media]`.
- **Tape:** CXW **~$31.20 (+0.79%)** intraday Jul 7 — modest drift up off the Jul 6 $30.42 close (early, weak "convergence" signal, not a rip).
