## Narrative

Kalshi offers Week 1 NFL **player availability** markets — economic impact on ticket resale, sponsors, media rights when stars sit. Individual sports (Alcaraz/Sinner US Open injury risk) show hedging utility sportsbooks cannot offer under state gaming law.

CFTC proposed rules flagged **player injury contracts** as likely contrary to public interest (perverse harm incentives; medical-diagnosis settlement). Kalshi markets are semantically "will play" not "will be injured" — regulatory gray zone. Leagues press PM venues to avoid injury-adjacent listings.
