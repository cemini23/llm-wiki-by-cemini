## Narrative

European electricity LTTRs historically **underpriced vs forwards** (limited arbitrage signal). Option-pricing logic: LTTR holders short importing-market forwards / long exporting-market forwards to lock rents. German EEX panel **2018–2025** shows post-auction forward price effects — systematic rents = inefficient regulatory transfer **consumer → LTTR holder**.

Wiki-only awareness (quant/energy). **Not** an active Cemini project brief.
