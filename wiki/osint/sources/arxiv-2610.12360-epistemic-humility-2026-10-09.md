## Narrative

The paper scores epistemic humility on three steps: identify a conflict, solve it, and escalate when it stays open. It uses cases where parametric memory fights the retrieved text, and cases where two sources disagree.

Higher task accuracy does not mean higher humility. Agents often see the conflict early and then drop it. Model-level changes can raise humility and cut accuracy. The result depends on the model, the harness, and the test. No rule change.

The PDF metadata also lists arXiv id 2000.01234 inside a figure. That id is a figure label, not this paper.
