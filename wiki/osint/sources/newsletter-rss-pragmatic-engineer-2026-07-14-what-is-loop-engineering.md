## Narrative

Defines “loop engineering” as designing systems that prompt agents (Boris Cherny / Steinberger / Addy Osmani), tracing Ralph Wiggum bash loops → `/goal` in major harnesses → cron/event loops. Flags **tokenmaxxing** cost, agent drift, and the argument that loops were a temporary hack until single-prompt harness features shipped. Context engineering may matter more for most app engineers.
