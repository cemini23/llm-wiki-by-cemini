## Narrative

Australia abolished unrestricted *jus soli* effective **20 August 1986**. Pre-cutoff: full BDM birth certificate usually proves citizenship at birth. Post-cutoff: birth cert alone is **insufficient** — need parent citizen/PR evidence or Home Affairs Form **119** Evidence of Australian citizenship (acquisition date must match DOB for at-birth status; 10-year residence rule acquires on 10th birthday).

Apostille: **DFAT only** (federal), paper original only, **no e-apostille** (e-Register verifies physical seals). Fee ~AUD 105/doc (2026). Translation: **NAATI** preferred; apostille itself need not be translated; translation need not be re-apostilled.

Historical naturalizations: NAA series A1 / A435 / A446. Pre-1949 naturalization = British subject — still voluntary foreign nationality under Italian Law 555/1912.

Melbourne consulate publishes L74/2025 posture; Law 11/2026 seat notices still `[OPEN QUESTION 2026-07-29]`.
