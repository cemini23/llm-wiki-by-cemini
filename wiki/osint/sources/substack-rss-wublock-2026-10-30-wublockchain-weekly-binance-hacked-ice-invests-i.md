## Narrative

This file is the Wu Blockchain weekly published 2026-10-30. The body is a ten-item news list. Every figure on this page is a claim from that one file.

The lead item reports a Binance security update on 30 October 2026. The item headline says Tether and Circle froze over $2.3 billion in related stablecoins. Binance estimates that the incident involves approximately 6.3 billion US dollars in assets. The body says around 3 billion US dollars of on-chain assets are frozen with partners. A later paragraph says about 3 billion US dollars of related assets are frozen. Binance says early checks point to a compromised DevOps laptop. Binance then says later checks found more intrusion paths. Binance says it has found no private-key leak so far. The first paragraph says the platform suspended deposits and withdrawals. A later paragraph says deposit services remain unaffected and withdrawals should resume in roughly 45 hours. Binance says the SAFU fund and corporate capital will cover all losses. Binance says it will update Proof of Reserves once remediation is complete and will release a detailed technical post-mortem.

ICE, parent of the New York Stock Exchange, plans a U.S. perpetual futures market with Trade.xyz. The market would use Hyperliquid infrastructure and would have its own clearing, KYC, and AML. The contracts would track stocks, stock indices, commodities, and foreign exchange. The file says the business still awaits regulatory approval. ICE will take an equity stake. The file states no stake size. The file says Elon Musk reposted Stan Druckenmiller and added the comment “Interesting”. Druckenmiller said he invested in Trade.xyz.

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said more than 90% of stablecoins peg to the U.S. dollar and hold U.S. Treasury securities as reserves. He said broader stablecoin use can lift demand for the dollar and for Treasuries. He said the Clarity Act failed in Congress. The file says the Treasury will coordinate the SEC and the CFTC by administrative rule. CFTC Chairman Mike Selig discussed autonomous trading agents and a possible Know Your Agent framework similar to Know Your Customer. The CFTC will host a round-the-clock trading roundtable on 15 December. The file cites Reuters and two people familiar with the matter. It says the CFTC is reviewing whether Polymarket breached a 2022 settlement by failing to block U.S. users on an offshore platform. The file says the CFTC and Polymarket declined to comment. It says no violation has been determined to date. It says a similar 2025 review ended with no charges.

Robinhood reported Q3 2026 revenue of $1.424 billion, up 12% year over year, roughly in line with a consensus of $1.42 billion. Net income was $476 million, a 14% year-over-year decline. Diluted EPS was $0.51, below a forecast of $0.56. The file assigns about $0.07 per share to an unrealized loss on Robinhood Ventures Fund I. Adjusted EBITDA was $808 million, up 9% year over year. Event-contract revenue rose more than sixfold year over year to $173 million. Crypto trading revenue fell 55% year over year to $120 million. Quarterly crypto notional volume fell 38% year over year to $50 billion. Event-contract volume rose more than sevenfold year over year to 16.4 billion contracts. Robinhood Chain recorded over $80 billion of DEX volume in its first quarter after mainnet launch and more than $50 million of on-chain fees. Guidance for 2026 adjusted operating expenses and equity-based compensation narrowed from $2.675 billion–$2.775 billion to $2.675 billion–$2.75 billion.

The same file says public objections to the OKX and ICE tokenized-stock plan, named OKXICE, rose from 26 companies to 38 companies. OKXICE had planned round-the-clock trading of more than 60 U.S. stocks under an SEC innovation exemption. Strive bought 6,182 bitcoin at an average price of approximately $82,732, including commissions and fees, between 17 and 23 October. Strive held 43,000 bitcoin as of 23 October, cash of $504.4 million (an increase of $186.9 million from 16 October), and 505,000 Strategy STRC preferred shares with a fair value of roughly $50.18 million. Strive plans at-the-market sales of up to $1 billion of Series A perpetual preferred stock (Nasdaq: SATS). Kalshi closed a $1 billion Series G at a $40 billion valuation and has discussed an IPO. The fundraising list also states approximately $129 million of fixed-rate financing for HIVE unit BUZZ HPC and an equity deal of up to $80.7 million for Evernorth. Bithumb, called South Korea’s second-largest crypto exchange, reported unauthorized access and a personal-information leak and is still checking member-data exposure. Daishin Securities said a breach stole personal and credit data of 38,490 customers. The file says police will check a link to an earlier breach at 16 financial firms, including KB Kookmin Bank, which exposed roughly 100,000 personal records. Latvia plans real-world-asset tokenization rules in the first half of 2027. The file says a legal review due on 1 October is still open, so the timetable is uncertain.

These claims matter to a quant-finance and macro reader because they sit on venue risk, dollar funding, and listed-product volume. The Binance lines are a large exchange-incident claim and a stablecoin-freeze claim. The ICE plan is a proposed U.S. perpetual market for equities, indices, commodities, and foreign exchange. The Bessent lines link stablecoin reserves to Treasury demand. The Robinhood lines separate event-contract revenue from crypto trading revenue. The Polymarket item is an access and enforcement claim. The Strive, Kalshi, and OKXICE items are treasury-flow, valuation, and tokenized-equity claims. Each number has only this newsletter behind it on this page.
