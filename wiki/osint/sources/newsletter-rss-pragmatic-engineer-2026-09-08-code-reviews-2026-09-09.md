## Narrative

GitHub: PRs up 5x; late-2025 doubling. Common pattern: humans review AI reviews; triage by blast radius; review plan/tests/schema not every line. Noise is the failure mode. HITL scope decisions stay. CCC FILE. Do not drop human review on money paths.
