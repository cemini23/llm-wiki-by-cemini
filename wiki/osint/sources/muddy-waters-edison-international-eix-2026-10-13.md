## Narrative

Muddy Waters published a short report on **Edison International (NYSE: EIX)**, the parent of **Southern California Edison (SCE)**, on **13 October 2026**. Block delivered it on stage at the Sohn San Francisco conference in the 10:30 AM PT "Best Ideas" slot.

**The thesis.** SCE **"consciously disregarded public safety"** when it failed to consider de-energizing four **220 kV transmission circuits** in **Eaton Canyon** on **7 January 2025** — the corridor tied to the **Eaton Fire**. Fire investigators have formally concluded those circuits started the fire.

**The numbers.** A **willful-disregard** finding by the **California Wildfire Fund Administrator** could trigger roughly **$20 billion** in reimbursement, described as **~$20 per share discounted, post-tax**. A lesser **imprudence** finding means about **$4.3 billion**, or **~$6.50 per share discounted, post-tax**.

**The reaction was weak.** EIX traded about **$53.83, down 3.25%** (prior close $55.64), near its **52-week low of $51.10** (52-week high $81.62). This is a small day-one move for an MW report — compare DLocal (−50%, 2022), AppLovin (−20%, 2025), and e.l.f. Beauty (−8%, 2024).

**Read: largely priced and stale.** The cause of the Eaton Fire — SCE equipment — was already public and already in the price. The stock already sits near 52-week lows on that liability. The report adds a **legal theory** (the Wildfire Fund willful-disregard channel), not new causation. There is no fresh edge for a puts trade here.

## Dead Ends

**The pre-reveal prediction attempt failed.** Before the reveal, the working thesis was an AI-infrastructure short. Block's 2026 interviews pointed that way: the Economist "How to short AI with Carson Block" episode (30 Sep 2026), a stated plan to short "AI pretenders and wannabes," and a refusal to short mega-caps. Grok, Codex, and TypeSafe Jev were all fed that framing, and all converged on AI names (APLD, SMCI, CRWV). All were wrong.

**Why it failed.** The framing was anchored on Block's *macro commentary*, and the candidate list inherited that anchor. Two stronger signals were underweighted: the **San Francisco venue** (a California-liability story fits) and **"many months of investigative work"** (the Eaton Fire predates the report by about 21 months).

**What worked.** The report URL pattern on Muddy Waters' own site — `muddywatersresearch.com/research/<year>/<slug>/` — resolved the target once guessed. Check the publisher's own site before reasoning from interviews.

**Lesson for the wiki.** A short-seller's public commentary is not a reliable predictor of the next target. Treat it as context, not a prior.
