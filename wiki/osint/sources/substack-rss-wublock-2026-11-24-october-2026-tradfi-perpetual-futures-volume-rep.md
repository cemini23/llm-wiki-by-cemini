## Narrative

### Headline

Stock, commodity and index perpetual futures on crypto exchanges totalled **$758.1 billion** in October 2026, up **21.9 percent** from September's $621.8 billion. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-24-october-2026-tradfi-perpetual-futures-volume-rep.md]

By sector: stocks $541.8bn, up 33.2 percent; commodities $202.5bn, down 1.4 percent; indices $13.9bn, up 41.8 percent. Within commodities, metals were $133.3bn and energy $69.1bn. Gold $92.0bn, down 4.5 percent; silver $39.9bn, up 10.1 percent.

### Venue share

Binance $446.7bn, 58.9 percent; OKX $83.1bn, 11.0 percent; Hyperliquid $70.6bn, 9.3 percent. The top three are 79.2 percent of the total. Bitget grew 68.5 percent month over month; MEXC fell 23.2 percent and lost 3.1 percentage points of share.

### Single names

Top listed-stock perpetuals: Sandisk $81.4bn, SK Hynix $46.3bn, Micron $25.2bn, Strategy $11.7bn, Intel $10.6bn. The top five are 32.4 percent of the stock sector.

ETFs and leveraged funds were $179.8bn, 33.2 percent of the stock sector, up 81.9 percent. SOXL alone was $84.9bn, KORU $27.5bn, SKHY $19.2bn.

Pre-IPO names were $48.6bn, 9.0 percent of the stock sector, up 23.9 percent. SpaceX $42.3bn, Unitree $2.93bn, ChangXin Memory $1.32bn, OpenAI $1.17bn, Anthropic $0.851bn.

### Time-of-day and funding

US cash-session volume, 9:00 to 16:00 ET, was 46.7 percent of the total across all venues. Weekend volume was $44.8bn, 5.9 percent of the month, up 18.0 percent. Index contracts had the highest weekend share at 10.7 percent.

Cumulative funding, positive means longs pay shorts: CSOPSKHYNIX2L on Crypto.com at 22.60 percent, KIOXIA at 18.71 percent, OKLO at 12.97 percent. Lowest were Unitree on HTX at −18.05 percent and XCU on Kraken at −7.16 percent. Median cumulative funding for major names: gold +1.08 percent, NVDA +0.31 percent, SpaceX +0.96 percent, SK Hynix +1.37 percent.

### Read

This is a new data series, not previously in the wiki: stock and commodity perps traded on crypto venues. The numbers show that equity-linked perps are now a large market and that the open interest concentrates in semiconductor names. The funding dispersion across venues for the same underlying is a carry signal worth tracking. The pre-IPO book, led by SpaceX, is a new kind of exposure.
