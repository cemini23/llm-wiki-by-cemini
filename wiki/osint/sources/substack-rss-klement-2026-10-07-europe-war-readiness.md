## Narrative

A short macro-geopolitics note. Klement highlights **D'Artis Kancs** (Latvian National Armed Forces) and his **readiness-enabling index** for European countries.

### The four dimensions

1. **Macroeconomic capacity** — can a country finance and absorb defence expansion?
2. **Sectoral resilience** — can it keep producing under disruption?
3. **Defence-industrial capability** — can it turn higher demand into higher output, or do capacity/supply-chain constraints bind?
4. **Logistics and sustainment** — can it sustain and replace capacity if damaged?

Countries are grouped into blocks: **Nordics**, **Baltic-Polish Flank**, **Industrial Core** (incl. UK, France, Germany), **South-West Med**, **South-East Europe**.

The point that interested Klement: the method identifies each region's **binding constraint** — "where the chain breaks first." Relevant to the **defence-industrial equity theme**.
