## Narrative

### The finding

The New York City Council released preliminary findings from its inquiry into prediction-market marketing. The findings allege deceptive marketing aimed at young users and disproportionate net losses for young and individual accounts. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-25-making-sports-event-contracts-21-feels-like-a-no.md]

Numbers the Council published, attributed to Kalshi's own disclosures:

- About **484,000** individual Kalshi accounts with New York City addresses as of 1 November 2026.
- About **51,000** of those accounts, 10.5 percent, belonged to users aged 18 to 20. A further 78,600 belonged to users aged 21 to 24.
- Those 18-to-20 users lost about **$8.9 million** net from January to November 2026, about 17 percent of the $52.5 million lost by all individual New York City users.
- They traded about 1.3 million contracts per day, against about 30 million per day for users 21 and older.

### The recommendation

The newsletter argues for a 21-and-over rule on sports event contracts. The reasons: young adults are more vulnerable to gambling harm, legal sports betting is generally 21-and-over, and the industry already advertises the product as sports betting. Novig already applies a 21-and-over rule. Kalshi's counsel said it would consider the idea.

A joint Council oversight hearing is set for 14 December, with first-in-the-nation marketing legislation under consideration. Kalshi, Polymarket, Coinbase, Gemini Titan and Novig were asked to testify.

### Legal roundup

- Utah moved to dismiss its 14 criminal charges against Kalshi after the company geofenced the state. The dismissal is without prejudice, so charges return if Kalshi returns. Utah's constitution bars all gambling.
- Florida's attorney general moved to intervene in the Seminole Tribe's suit against DraftKings and to shut down its prediction product in the state.
- A Michigan judge said she was very concerned about targeted sports-wagering advertising that continued after an order barring Kalshi from offering online sports wagering there.

### Volume and product

Monday 23 November Kalshi volume: **$3.32 billion**. Parlays 66.2 percent, sports 14.2 percent, crypto 16.1 percent. Seven-day: Kalshi $27.6bn, Polymarket US $3.66bn.

Perpetual futures on Kalshi reached $3.4 billion of notional on 20 November, when Anthropic stock debuted, above the roughly $3 billion on the prediction markets that day. That is the first reported day when the perps book topped the event book.

### Read for the venue

Under-21 access and marketing are now the live regulatory pressure point, ahead of the preemption fight. For a retail bot this is an access and compliance question; for the venue it is a user-base question, because a 21-plus rule removes about a tenth of New York accounts.
