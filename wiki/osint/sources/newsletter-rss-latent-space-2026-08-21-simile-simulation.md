## Narrative

K244 leftover (Simile pod missed that wave). Folded with the next-morning AINews grid that treats Simile as “Stage 7: the human subject.”

**Simile (Joon Sung Park, $2B Series B, GreenOaks/Index; Fei-Fei Li / Karpathy backers).** Lineage: Smallville generative agents (2023) → *Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People* (2024). Digital twins from two-hour interviews reproduced source humans **85% as accurately as the humans reproduced themselves** two weeks later. Frontier models trained to be rational agent-models are **bad** human simulators (gen-pop ~50–60%; niche 20–30%). Simile post-trains on interviews, transactions, and registered RCTs from the Open Science Framework to recover bias, inconsistency, and causal texture. Early scaling-law glimpse for simulation quality. Market-research TAM $100B is the *starting* wedge; stated ambition is decision-making about humans. CVS / Gallup / Wealthfront named. Politics: partnership with Gallup, not yet a product surface — they want guardrails first.

**AINews grid (teaser, 2026-08-22).** Eight flips from human-made → model-made: (1) reward/judge 2022, (2) training data 2023, (3) teacher 2023, (4) curriculum 2024, (5) researcher 2026 (Karpathy autoresearch; AlphaEvolve; AI Scientist), (6) environment 2026 (z.ai GLM-5.3 synthetic gyms; Ornith-1.5), (7) human subject 2025–26 (Simile / SimGym / Tencent billion-persona), (8) physical world still experiment-bound (Poolside reverse-execuhire; CZ Biohub virtual cell). Recurring pattern: each flip happened when a **verification** mechanism made the synthetic version trustworthy — not when generation got prettier.

Same-day AINews also notes Ox Alpha mystery model / GLM-family speculation, DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Vision-Exp, OpenAI Sol >20% price cut, Codex 20M users. CCC awareness; **do not swap `/route`**.

**OSINT steal:** simulation ≠ prediction (shape the path, not the point forecast). Do not treat 85% twin-accuracy as a Cemini eval number. No Simile clone. No Atto/Barone persona twins.
