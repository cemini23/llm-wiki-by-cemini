## Narrative

Swiss births do **not** confer a soil title usable as Italian Clock A foreign nationality by place alone. The cut document is the **Einbürgerungsbeschluss** (naturalization decree) and/or SEM **Negativbescheinigung** (negative citizenship certificate), not a birth extract.

Civil extracts for Italy-bound packs should request **CIEC Convention 16** multilanguage forms explicitly. Switzerland ratified CIEC **34** (2022); **Italy has not** — INFOSTAR NG defaulting to Form 34 risks Italian reject; free replacement per EAZW Weisung when forced. **EU 2016/1191 does not apply** (EFTA ≠ EU). Nationality negatives and judgments still need **Hague** apostille via cantonal Staatskanzlei / Bundeskanzlei. **Familienschein** is not a consular citizenship pack substitute.

Product hooks: soft-flag `ciec_16_34` with **force_16**; never auto-tag `DOC_FOREIGN_IUS_SOLI_TITLE` for CH births; cantonal Zivilstandsamt post/email (~30 CHF) + SEM negative + FAST-IT AIRE for routine civil.
