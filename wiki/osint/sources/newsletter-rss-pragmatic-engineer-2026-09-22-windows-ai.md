## Narrative

Part 2 of a series on how AI changes operating systems. The article interviews Microsoft staff about the Windows plan for agentic tools. It follows an earlier article on Ubuntu.

The Windows team vision includes:

- **Agent identity and discovery** — native support for agent identification, plus a **centralized registry of locally available MCP servers** to ship with the OS.
- **Agentic primitives** — new building blocks for agent tooling at the OS level.
- Reversal of some prior decisions that pushed developers away from the system.

The article also discusses developer OS share. macOS appears far more popular at startups than Windows, and Linux may overtake Windows in developer popularity.
