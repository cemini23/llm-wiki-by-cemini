## Narrative

**CFTC American-odds letter:** DMO + MPD letter reminds CFTC-regulated entities that "American" odds format (casino bookmaker style) is likely misleading; display should indicate an event contract on a regulated exchange, not a bookmaking product. Kalshi said it will comply by deadline. **ProphetX** ep 27 with co-founder Jake Benzaquen; ProphetX × Pikkit partnership to bring CFTC-regulated sports PMs to Pikkit's community.

**Also:** Drug-trial betting criticized ("ghastly", NPR All Things Considered; patients on the other side of bets). Kalshi volume Sat $1.27B; sports+parlays 83.7% (46% of all volume was parlays); Mansour NYT interview: sports ~2/3 of volume vs 95% last year. Polymarket sued for $170K over Trump "Khamenei" bet. BadCredit survey: 51% of PM users borrowed money to fund bets; 79% lost money in past year. Lockerbie jury instructions to warn about prediction markets.

**Route:** pm/gambling awareness only (no prod scp).
