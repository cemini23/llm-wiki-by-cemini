## Narrative

**Hearing.** The Senate Agriculture Committee held a nomination hearing for two CFTC commissioners, Nathan Anonick (Republican) and Matthew Rowland (Democrat). Both declined to say whether a sports event contract is a sports bet, citing conflicting federal court decisions. Rowland said he would look at the public-interest balancing in the special rule, and expects a Supreme Court decision within a term or two. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-18-i-want-my-money-back-after-watching-the-senate-h.md]

Senator Schiff reopened his dispute with Chair Selig, who he says promised to be a "blank slate" and then directed courts rather than deferring to them. Senators also pressed on CFTC staffing: a reported loss of all enforcement attorneys in the Chicago office, about a quarter of staff overall, and reduced enforcement in traditional commodity markets.

**Courts.** The Fourth Circuit became the third appellate court to side with a state against Kalshi, affirming Maryland 2-1. The two judges in the majority gave different reasons; all three opinions differed. The 3rd Circuit had earlier agreed with Kalshi. Kalshi's statement stresses the splintered decision. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-18-i-want-my-money-back-after-watching-the-senate-h.md]

**CFTC action.** Kalshi paused several Trump "mention" markets at the CFTC's request. The CFTC's concern is manipulation by a single speaker. Commentators note the asymmetry: other individuals' mention markets stayed open.

**Market integrity.** Kalshi's Market Integrity Committee reported after a months-long review: 500 million historical trades, nearly 900,000 surveillance alerts, more than 44 billion order-book updates, 288 million recent trades worth $48bn, and 5,585 market series across 421 subcategories. It found that markets representing about 98% of volume carry lower risk of material non-public information. It recommended subcategory-level risk ratings and calibrated alert thresholds. Kalshi disclosed more than 50 referrals to law enforcement in Q2 and Q3. [Source: substack-rss-event-horizon-2026-11-18-i-want-my-money-back-after-watching-the-senate-h.md]

**Volume, Monday 16 November.** Kalshi $3.5bn: 67.7% parlays, 14.8% sports, 14.5% crypto. Seven-day: Kalshi $27.9bn, Polymarket US $3.68bn, DKeX $1.38bn.

**Also.** Utah's Third District Court requires the Kalshi co-founders to appear in person for arraignment on 13 felony counts on 4 December. Anchorage Digital will let institutions post Bitcoin and stablecoins held in custody as margin on Kalshi Prime (HODL Margining). Polymarket is live in the Pump.fun app for permitted jurisdictions.

For a bot operator, the venue-availability risk is now legal, not technical: an arraignment, a state injunction or a federal pause can change the tradable list with little notice.
