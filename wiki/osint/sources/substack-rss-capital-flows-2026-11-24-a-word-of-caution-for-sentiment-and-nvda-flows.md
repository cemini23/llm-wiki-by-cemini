## Narrative

### The claim

Twitter sentiment on NVDA reached its highest level in history. Call and put open interest and volume blew out to historic extremes while the stock traded in massive size. [Source: substack-rss-capital-flows-2026-11-24-a-word-of-caution-for-sentiment-and-nvda-flows.md]

### The argument

The author draws a line between attention and edge. When a name carries this much focus, you need a very high degree of informational edge to monetise it. The flow around Jensen Huang, the board and insiders is carefully staged, so public speculation has little true signal. Binary coin-flip bets that the whole market is watching are not where large trades come from.

The second point is scale. NVDA matters for the AI supply chain, but the macro backdrop and factor flows are larger than one chief executive.

### Read

This is a sentiment-crowding caution, not a price call. The specific numbers are in the paywalled charts and are not in the free text. The usable statement is narrow: record-high attention on one name is a reason to demand more edge, not less. That is consistent with the crowding framing already in `concepts/ai-industry-trends-2026.md`.

No new numbers. Do not treat "highest sentiment in history" as verified; the source gives no series.
