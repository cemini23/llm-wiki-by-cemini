## Narrative

UK National Living Wage (21+) launched 1 Apr 2016 at £7.20 and is now £12.21. OBR originally estimated −60k jobs / +0.2 pp unemployment. Albagli et al. (LSE, 2026) tenth-anniversary study: full-time employment in low-pay industries **rose** 0.3–1.0 pp; underemployment declined. Firms adapted via contract/responsibility redesign, not mass firing. Klement: effects small and opposite the classic prediction.
