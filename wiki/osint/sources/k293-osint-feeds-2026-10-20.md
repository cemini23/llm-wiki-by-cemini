### 2. Do not amplify a thin print (Event Horizon)

Polymarket removed a market on whether any of seven Cornell defendants will be charged with a felony sex crime, after criticism of the market and of athlete endorsers.

Kalshi posted that there was an **87%** chance Tucker Carlson would be charged with a federal crime this year. Contracts did trade as high as **88 cents**, but the move was a burst on the morning of 16 Oct in an illiquid book. The contract later showed about **7%** on less than **$13,000** traded. The newsletter's point: a fleeting spike is not a consensus, and the venue amplified it.

### 6. FortiBleed is credential reuse, not a new bug (SecPro #256)

The FBI and US Secret Service advisory of **6 Oct** describes credential compromise and access brokering against exposed FortiGate firewalls and SSL VPN gateways. Fortinet's assessment: FortiBleed is **not a newly disclosed vulnerability**. A password can still work after an upgrade. An extra administrator account can survive a reset of the original account. Updating the device and recovering control of it are separate checks. Appearance in a scanning dataset is a prompt to investigate, not proof that a named network was compromised. **No procedure in this page.**

### 7. Data centres and new US power (Klement, 19 Oct)

A Swiss study of US data-centre buildout from 2022 through 2025, as summarised by Klement. New sites cluster where power is cheap (Texas, Virginia, Ohio, Nevada). In counties that gained a data centre, renewable and battery buildout accelerated more than in counties that did not. Gas plants also accelerated, but less than in counties without a data centre.
