## Narrative

Sparton is a fused Triton GPU kernel that eliminates the memory bottleneck in the LM-head of Learned Sparse Retrieval models (e.g. Splade), where materializing the large vocabulary logit matrix throttles throughput. The contribution is a GPU systems / kernel-optimization method. This is **out-of-scope** for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda — Triton-kernel performance engineering is GPU systems infrastructure with no path to financial-market modeling, and Cemini runs no GPU inference of its own.
