## Narrative

ChatGPT fan-out queries (the background searches RAG fires) are shifting. **Retrieved** (pages fetched) is rising; **cited** (unique domains in the visible answer) is falling. ChatGPT 5.6 (Sol vs Luna; >90% of weekly users on free/Luna) uses `site:` as a quality prior — spam-reduction, E-E-A-T-shaped.

Industry numbers `[TENTATIVE]`:
- Peec AI (Konitzny, 5.6 default day): single-fan-out prompts 94.0% → 43.5%; avg retrieved ~12 → 24; `site:` in fan-outs ~0.3% → ~23%.
- Nectiv (Long, ~4k Sol prompts vs 2025): fan-outs/prompt 2.17 → 7.61; `site:` in 64% of API queries.
- Resoneo: unique domains cited/response 19 → 15 after 5.3.
- Suganthan (57 conv / 3,554 pages): 21/27 initial queries injected brand names the user never typed; brands named in the fan-out cited 68.9% vs 2.1% for fetch-only; only 3.1% of retrieved pages made the answer.

Opinions → Reddit/subreddits; YMYL → .gov; specs/pricing → brand `site:`. Product pages 16.39% of retrievals, ahead of listicles (the GEO-spammed format).

**OSINT awareness / SEO steal:** retrieved ≠ cited. Being the brand in the *first* fan-out is the game. Official pages + `site:` priors beat listicle spam.
