## Narrative

Second Gemini genealogy drop of 2026-07-09. Unlike K153 (generic computational stack), this report is a **regional playbook**: mid-1500s geography for Abruzzo Citeriore / Calabria Ulteriore / Prince-Bishopric of Trento; how to request Italian *copie integrali* via PEC/email; US long-form NYC births + Steve Morse ED Finder; Trentino **Nati in Trentino** index; Catasto Onciario / Liste di Leva / Cassa Sacra as parish-gap bridges; a ~25-row free/low-cost tool matrix; red-flag false matches (ubiquitous Rossi, Sellia vs Sellia Marina, “Austrian” Trentini on US manifests).

**Wiki scope:** archive contacts, tool URLs, and methodological red flags only. Private pedigree, DNA matches, and living persons are **out of wiki** (operator laptop research folder).

**Citation quality:** mixed. Prefer primary portals (NatiTrentino, comuni, Archivio di Stato, Torricella Onciario transcription, FamilySearch wiki) over secondary synthesis footnotes (some Williamsburg/DEC PDFs and Grokipedia hits are noise). Treat feudal “Lords of Rallo” Odorizzi origin claims as **theoretical** until parish chains prove them — the report itself flags this.
