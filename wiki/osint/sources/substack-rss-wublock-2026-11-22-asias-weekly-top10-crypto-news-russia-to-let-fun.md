## Narrative

**Russia.** The Bank of Russia plans to revise rules in 2027 so investment funds for qualified investors can buy and hold crypto directly. Previously only cash-settled crypto derivatives were allowed. A Duma deputy also proposed adding legally confiscated crypto to national reserves, with caps and custody rules. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-22-asias-weekly-top10-crypto-news-russia-to-let-fun.md]

**Tokenised deposits.** MAS says a token is a bank deposit only if its legal and economic substance is a deposit. Bearer tokens that grant repayment purely by possession are generally not deposits. Banks must disclose deposit-insurance applicability, reversal mechanisms and technical risk. The HKMA will test Swift Ledger interoperability with its Ensemble settlement layer, with Bank of China (Hong Kong), Standard Chartered Hong Kong and HSBC.

**Korea.** The Financial Services Commission expects the Digital Asset Basic Act draft this month. A major-shareholder cap of 15% to 20% is expected, with exceptions up to 34%. That could affect Naver Financial's acquisition of Dunamu, the Upbit operator. KRW stablecoin issuers may need at least 100% safe-asset reserves, with priority for bank-majority consortia.

**Enforcement.** The UK High Court approved a settlement under which Su Haijin and Xu Haika surrender about £55m; the NCA has seized about £75m from the group in 12 months. Singapore's case involved more than SGD 3bn, including more than SGD 38m of crypto. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-22-asias-weekly-top10-crypto-news-russia-to-let-fun.md]

**Hardware-wallet tampering.** Two users reported thefts from Ledger devices bought at Virgin Megastore in Dubai Mall. One lost about 1,300 SOL after a teardown showed an extra circuit board and antenna in a Ledger Flex. A second reported 507,000 USDT lost from a Ledger Stax. The implants can capture seed phrases from the screen and exfiltrate over cellular; the secure chip stays genuine, so device verification passes. Ledger has not confirmed linked cases outside Southeast Asia. Independent verification is pending, so tag `[TENTATIVE]`. [Source: substack-rss-wublock-2026-11-22-asias-weekly-top10-crypto-news-russia-to-let-fun.md]

**Agentic trading.** Daiwa completed a test where multiple AI agents screen DEX options from natural-language instructions, the user confirms, and test stablecoins swap against security tokens. The test also checked permissions, conditional limits and post-trade audit logging.

**Tokenised bonds.** Maybank completed Malaysia's first tokenised government-securities pilot with Etiqa, settling via tokenised deposits on a private chain with on-chain delivery versus payment.

**Binance.** Binance plans to enter five Asian markets next year, focused on South and Southeast Asia. It disclosed a $6.3bn crypto-asset theft last month.

The custody item is the operationally important one: a supply-chain attack defeats device verification because the secure element is untouched. Buy hardware wallets only through verified channels.
