## Narrative

Klement shows how a false analyst price-target story can stick to stock A even after correction that the upgrade was for company B — especially when a causal narrative accompanies the headline. Humans are storytelling animals; markets price stories longer than facts alone. GW/wiki: flag narrative stickiness in thesis reviews; no auto-arm.
