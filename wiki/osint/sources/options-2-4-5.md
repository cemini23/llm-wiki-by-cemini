## Narrative

Academic finance paper on options market microstructure — specifically how informed traders affect option bid-ask spreads. Preview: develops and tests a model for option spreads that explicitly captures strategic trading by informed investors. Classic microstructure-theory-meets-options-markets genre (Easley/O'Hara lineage).

Honest assessment: 34 pages is substantive. Gautam Kaul is a published academic financial economist at Michigan — credible. The "develop and test a model" framing is standard for top-finance-journal-style empirical work. The paper is not 2026-new (no publication date captured; likely older working-paper or a reprint). Informed-trading-and-spread models have well-known limitations (assumes stable informational structure, requires ex-post identification of "informed" trades) — but the framework remains a citation staple in options microstructure.
