## Narrative

Sponsored Zacks SCR note published **2026-07-06** (same day as CXW 8-K press release) raising **valuation to $36** from **$31** using **13.3×** 2026 normalized FFO/share ($2.67), up from ~12×. Report price reference **$31.03 (7/2/26)** — the **close date** of the transaction, not announcement day.

**Bull framing:** Sales **de-risk** ICE-owned real estate exposure (ICE-owned beds 25% → 20% of portfolio); strengthen balance sheet; minimal EBITDA/cash-flow impact post-sale with retained management; CA political risk reduction; tuck-in M&A (CSP pharmacy) + buybacks.

**Cash waterfall (matches 8-K):** ~$716M to revolver + term loans + Oct 2027 4.75% notes; remainder general corporate purposes including tuck-ins and repurchases.

**$/bed:** **~$323k** average on closed deal. Naive **57.8k owned beds × $323k ≈ $18B** vs **~$3.2B** pro forma EV — author explicitly states shares **should not** trade at full private-market portfolio value.

**Next sale hint:** Prairie Correctional Facility (MN, 1,600 beds) + Saint Paul ICE sole-source notice — reactivation narrative, not a second CA tranche.

**Disclosure:** Zacks SCR receives **$10k–$50k/yr** from issuer for non-IB services including report preparation `[CONFIRMED in PDF]`.

**Market read (Jul 6, 2026):** CXW **premarket ~+8% → close ~$30.42 (−2%)** despite PT raise — tape treated note as confirmation, not catalyst. See @concepts/corrections-reit-asset-sale-event-study.md.
