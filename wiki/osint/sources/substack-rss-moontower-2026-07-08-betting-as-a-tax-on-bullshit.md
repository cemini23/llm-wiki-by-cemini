## Narrative

Moontower recap of Patrick McKenzie × Aaron Brown (*Wrong Number*) podcast. Core frame: **"betting is a tax on bullshit"** (Marginal Revolution / Nate Silver lineage) — forcing public falsifiability where experts resist scorekeeping. Tetlock: domain experts often **worse than random**; prominence correlates with overconfidence. Trader discipline: define **stop / falsifier** before thesis ("gold to $1k but stop at $6k"). Applies to **PM/Kalshi** calibration (require market-implied loss function) and **XSP** narrative trades (macro hot takes without exit). Brief → `briefs/xsp-*` + pm exploratory.
