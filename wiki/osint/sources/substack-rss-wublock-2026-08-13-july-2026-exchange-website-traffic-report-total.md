## Narrative

WuBlockchain-compiled July 2026 website traffic across **12 exchanges: ~134.31M visits, −2.35% MoM** from June's 137.54M (5 rose, 7 fell).

Top by visits: **Binance 36.04M (26.8% share)**, **OKX 25.62M (19.1%)**, **Coinbase 20.60M (15.3%)**; top-3 combined **61.2%**. Strongest MoM: Bitfinex 0.56M (+3.7%), KuCoin 15.58M (+3.2%), Bybit 11.11M (+2.8%). Largest declines: Deribit 0.63M (−44.7%), HTX 1.64M (−23.0%), Upbit 4.16M (−16.0%).

Geography: Binance top visitor origin **India**, OKX **Japan**, Coinbase **USA**.

Relevance: (1) retail-traffic contraction is broad but venue-uneven — Deribit's −44.7% mirrors its −44.4% derivatives-volume drop (options-venue contraction, not just traffic noise); (2) top-3 concentration 61.2% (website) vs 74.9% (derivatives notional) — same venue-concentration story; (3) OKX strong in Japan, Coinbase in USA, Binance in India = geographic venue-split for crypto-retail flow context.
