## Narrative

A condensed-matter physics paper that uses deep RL to discover dynamic magnetic-field-temperature paths for deterministically creating thermally stable magnetic skyrmions in Fe3GeTe2 monolayers, optimizing a cost function combining topological charge and dissipated work. It is out-of-scope for Cemini's trading-stack research agenda: the work is spintronics device physics, with the RL applied to micromagnetic simulation control and no transfer to financial-market modeling or the CeminiSuite stack.
