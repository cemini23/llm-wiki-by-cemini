## Narrative

Needed Atto paper. GZSL handwriting over large vocabularies is biased toward seen classes. Two-stage statistical debias (black-box GZSL plus Monte Carlo bias-correctors) yields over 20 percent relative gain on unseen words. Steal for lexicon/HTR eval only. Transkribus remains sole product HTR. No Barone PII. FILE.
