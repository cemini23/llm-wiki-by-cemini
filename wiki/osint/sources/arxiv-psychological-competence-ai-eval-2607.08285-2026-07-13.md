## Narrative

Conceptual paper: technical AI eval (accuracy, robustness, reasoning, compliance) is **necessary but insufficient** for human-facing systems (advisors, coaches, tutors, companions). Unit of evaluation = **human–AI interaction**, not model alone.

Defines **psychological competence** — capacity to support user cognition, emotional interpretation, and behavioral decision-making appropriately to user/context/purpose (framing, tone, perceived authority, uncertainty handling, conversational guidance). Assessment via scenario-based probes, structured human eval, model-assisted methods — **no new named benchmark shipped**.
