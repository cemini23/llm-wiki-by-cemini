## Narrative

K159 **family-tree** methodology ingest for inland **Sellia** (Catanzaro), not coastal **Sellia Marina** (post-1956; homonym trap). Archival fracture:

| Era | Repository | Access |
|-----|------------|--------|
| 1809–1865 Restoration | Antenati / ASC Catanzaro | ~91 Sellia packs; **46 processetti** matrimoniali |
| 1866–1910 | FamilySearch **2043789** (Tribunale Catanzaro) | Image-only browse — no comprehensive index |
| 1867–1890 morti | FamilySearch **726599** | Death registers for Gen5/6 mortality |
| Post-1910 / 1930s | Comune di Sellia | *Copia integrale* via PEC — not on Antenati browse |

**Critical path:** simultaneous Comune PEC for 20th-c integral act + human browse Nati 1895–1910 on DGS 2043789 for Gen4 births. **Liste di Leva** (ASC Catanzaro PEC `as-cz@pec.cultura.gov.it`) backup if civil nati unreadable. **Processetti** unlock Gen6–8 once Gen5 marriage window (1830–1865) identified. Spillover comuni: Cicala, Simeri Crichi, Soveria Simeri, Zagarise, Magisano, Albi, Sersale.

Surname notes: **Costantino** patronymic cluster in Presila; **Passante** endemic in modern Sellia council records. No DNA or living-person vitals on wiki.
