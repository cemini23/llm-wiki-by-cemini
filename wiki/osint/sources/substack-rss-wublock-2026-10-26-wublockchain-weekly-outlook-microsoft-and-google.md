## Narrative

This is a calendar post for the week of 26 October to 1 November 2026, verified as of 24 October 2026.

The week has three rate decisions and three large earnings reports. The Fed, the ECB, and the BOJ all announce. Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon all report.

The Fed decision is Wednesday 28 October at 2:00 p.m. ET, with the press conference at 2:30 p.m. The Fed raised rates by 25 basis points in September on a unanimous vote, which set the target range at 3.75%–4.00%. Markets broadly expect no change this week.

The stated focus points for the Fed: Chair Kevin Warsh on energy inflation, on employment, and on consumer spending, plus any signal about a further hike in December. There is no new dot plot and no updated economic projections this meeting, so the statement and the press conference are the only policy signals.

The earnings relevance is the AI capex question. The post frames the question as whether cloud revenue growth can support AI capital spending, and whether inflation and economic data justify more tightening. Alphabet's Q3 call and Microsoft's fiscal Q1 call are both on the same Wednesday evening.

The ECB and BOJ decisions are scheduled but the retrieved text does not give their times or the expected outcomes.

Use this page as a dated calendar record, not as a forecast. The rate expectations are the market's, as the post reports them.
