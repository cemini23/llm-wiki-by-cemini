## Narrative

UPL/FTC-framed claim taxonomy (safe / risky / forbidden), competitor disclaimer quotes (BecomeItaliani, ICA, ITAMCAP, 23andMe), privacy trust via architecture not “bank-grade,” this-is/isn’t columns, 15 FAQ drafts, perpetual-fallback license language, and Meta/Google ad review checklist.

**Core rule:** locus of judgment stays with user/counsel — software is passive organizer + approve-only suggest.

**Ad risks:** Google “Government documents and official services”; Meta personal attributes (“Are you Italian?” / “your heritage”); no passport/outcome guarantees in creatives.

**Conflicts to resolve before ship:**
- FAQ #14 claims **macOS and Windows** — product canon is **macOS-first**; do not publish Windows until real.
- Refund: this DR = **14-day**; landing DR = **30-day**.
- License copy mentions “one full year of updates” — confirm product policy before FAQ.
