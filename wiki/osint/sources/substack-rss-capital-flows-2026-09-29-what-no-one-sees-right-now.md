## Narrative

RSS teaser. The piece argues that pattern recognition is treated as a virtue but prevents seeing what the world can become. When real risk is on, traders narrow on the information tied to their pain or reward.

The examples given: reverse-engineering the social algorithm, copying a reverse-engineered playbook, and hedging the last regime's risk. It notes deflation hedges built after 2008 and 2020, and that 2022 caught stocks and bonds selling off together.

Named: Josh Kushner, Thrive Capital, Invest Like the Best, Rick Rubin.
