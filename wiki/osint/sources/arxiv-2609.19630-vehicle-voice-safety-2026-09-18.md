## Narrative

Afroze, Zhang, Tu, Hei. 202-scenario vehicle voice authorization benchmark. Seven-class taxonomy (execute / refuse / clarify / confirm / defer / emergency / no-tool). Alignment 40.1% (Llama 3.2 3B) to 89.1% (Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview). API models 83.2–89.1%. Even the best still produce 2–3 False Executes among 161 non-execution scenarios. Structured LLM decisions are not a standalone safety layer. Cyber FILE. **No PoC. 0 Integrate GO.**
