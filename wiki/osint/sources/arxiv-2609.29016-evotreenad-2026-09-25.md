## Narrative

EvoTreeNAD grows a tree of neural architectures. The root is empty. Each node is one complete architecture. The search uses the top percentile of a node and its descendants to pick a lineage. A language model then reads that design history. The paper supplies no seed architecture and no hand-built search space.

The word genealogy in the title is an architecture tree. It is **not** civil-record genealogy. It is **not** HTR. It is **not** Atto.
