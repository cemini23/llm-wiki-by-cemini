## Narrative

EH argues 2024 Republicans treated prediction markets as truth-telling after Trump won. Into 2026 midterms, Kalshi-style odds (as of this teaser) have Democrats favored for the House and the Senate near a coin flip. The comms risk: if GOP remains the House underdog, they may tell audiences not to listen to prediction markets; if they win as underdogs, they may call the markets fake the way they have called polls fake. Scale is new — election markets as national consciousness, not just a betting niche.

Same briefing: Cantor opens Kalshi block trades to ~3,000 institutional clients; Susquehanna prices/liquidity; Kalshi filed perpetual futures on financial indices; Kalshi volume Aug 18 **$1.18B** (sports+parlays 76.5%, crypto 17.9%). Institutional positioning as a hedge against sports/novelty legal headwinds.
