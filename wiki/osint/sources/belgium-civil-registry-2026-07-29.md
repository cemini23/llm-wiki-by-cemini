## Narrative

**BAEC/DABS** works well for residents; **Just-on-web** needs eID/itsme — diaspora default = commune/proxy. Demand **copie/afschrift**, not **extrait/uittreksel**. **Art. 11** third-generation rules can silently attribute Belgian nationality at birth (parent also BE-born + residence history) → Italian continuity cut risk if unexamined. Dual-nationality liberalization **28 Apr 2008** (IT pairing relevant) is **not retroactive**.

Default auth: `eu_2016_1191` and/or **CIEC 16** multilingual; apostille only out-of-scope cases (eLegalisation €25 from Aug 2026). Birth ≠ Belgian — never auto soil-tag. Seats: Amb Brussels + Charleroi (Wallonia) split; FAST-IT routine civil; Prenot@Mi for JS.
