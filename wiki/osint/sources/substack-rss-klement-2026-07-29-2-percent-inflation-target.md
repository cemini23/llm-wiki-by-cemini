## Narrative

Klement’s structural case for ~3%: aging/labor +0.5pp; end of globalization gains +0.5pp. Empirically 0–4% fine for businesses/equities; pain above ~4–5%. Kara (Cardiff): UK microdata — once competitor price rises exceed ~**1.9%**, firms treat as persistent and follow → spiral risk.
