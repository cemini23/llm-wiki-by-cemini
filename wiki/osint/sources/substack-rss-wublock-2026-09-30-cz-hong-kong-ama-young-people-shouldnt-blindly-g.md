## Narrative

A summary of an offline Binance founder CZ AMA in Hong Kong on 28 August. The main theme is risk management over expected return. He warns young people not to borrow for concentrated bets. He says his own early Bitcoin concentration was safe because a zero outcome would not have hurt his family.

He is bullish on real-world assets and tokenization, and lists a sequence: stocks on chain, then stablecoins and foreign exchange, then real estate and intellectual property. He discusses Hong Kong's regulatory pace and Giggle Academy's growth.

One allocation rule is stated: if young, keep 80% to 90% of a crypto allocation in major assets, and commit only 10% to 20% of monthly income. That is a sizing rule, not a price level.

The named figures are a rule of thumb, not a signal. This is not investment advice for this workspace. No level to arm. FILE only.
