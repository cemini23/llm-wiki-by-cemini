## Narrative

A short pointer to Klement's Reuters column, built from Anthropic's IPO prospectus (via Reuters).

### The numbers

- **Anthropic 2025 revenue: $4.6 B** (up nearly 12× year over year).
- **Compute and infrastructure spend: $7.3 B.**
- **Operating loss widened: $2.98 B (2024) → $8.06 B (2025).**

The framing: "AI has a math problem" — revenue grows fast, but compute cost grows faster. The column argues the sector's path to profit is the open question, not the revenue trajectory.

### Why it matters here

A **concrete, sourced data point for the AI-capex economics debate** — useful against the pragmatic-engineer cost discussion (@sources/substack-rss-pragmatic-engineer-2026-10-06-state-of-tech-2026.md). Macro/equity context; **wiki-only, no scp.** The full column is paywalled at Reuters.
