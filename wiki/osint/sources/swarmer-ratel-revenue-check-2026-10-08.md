## Narrative

A primary-source check of **Ratel Robotics** — the Ukrainian UGV maker Swarmer is acquiring — against the **earnout** that drives the Swarmer thesis.

### The correction that matters

**The FY2024 and FY2025 statements are AUDITED** (US GAAS, unmodified opinion with a going-concern paragraph, report dated 2026-09-11; auditor "SAX LLP", Parsippany NJ). Only the H1 2026 / H1 2025 interims are unaudited — and those were **reviewed** (AU-C 930). **The desk note's "unaudited" framing is wrong** (@sources/swarmer-long-thesis-2026-10-09.md). Swarmer converted Ratel's Ukrainian statutory (PSBO) accounts to US GAAP.

### Confirmed financials (DEFM14A)

| Metric | FY2024 | FY2025 | H1 2025 | H1 2026 |
|--------|--------|--------|---------|---------|
| Net sales | $1,507,324 | $18,635,667 | $4,199,667 | $9,602,328 |
| Net income (loss) | $(310,333) | $1,965,911 | $(893,000) | $640,780 |
| Operating income (loss) | $(304,065) | $1,919,123 | $(858,774) | $759,412 |

**Total assets $99,987,284 (30 Jun 2026)** vs $8,895,952 (YE2025) vs $2,593,161 (YE2024). **Contract liabilities (deferred revenue) $86,632,403 (30 Jun 2026)** vs $4,690,029 (YE2025) vs $896,061 (YE2024). Government customers were **93% (FY2025) / 86% (H1 2026)** of net sales.

### The earnout arithmetic

Targets: **$77.0M revenue / $8.47M operating income (FY2026)**, then $130.0M/$14.3M (FY2027), $187.0M/$20.57M (FY2028), bonus $249.0M/$27.39M. First-earnout payout = **$7.2M cash + 1,064,942 shares**. Payout = revenue-achievement % × operating-income-achievement %, capped at 100%; **no partial payment below 58% of revenue target** ($44.66M).

**The 2026 test, on confirmed numbers:** H1 2026 revenue $9.60M = **12.5% of target**. Required H2 = **$67.4M** — **7.0× H1**, **4.7× H2 2025**, **3.6× all of FY2025 in six months**. Run rate ~$1.6M/month in H1 vs ~$11.2M/month needed.

**The only path:** convert the **$86.6M of advances** already received into delivered, accepted product. Management "expects to recognize substantially all of this amount as revenue during 2026." Converting 78% of it hits $77M. Supporting: **$44.0M prepaid to suppliers**, **$16.7M inventories**, total assets up to $99.99M.

**Bottom line:** the first earnout is **not achievable on Ratel's demonstrated delivery rate**. It is achievable only with a **~7× step-up in half-year deliveries** — i.e. most of the advances converting in H2 2026. The proxy's own risks: delivery "concentrated in the second half of 2026"; failure could force "refund advances, pay contractual penalties or forgo the related revenue"; going-concern doubt; UAH depreciation.

### The "$86 million contracts" claim

The claim lives **only in the 10 Sep press release** (EX-99.1): "secured contracts totaling $86 million." It appears **nowhere in the PREM14A or DEFM14A** (text search: 0 hits); the proxy has no backlog or order-book disclosure. The closest filed number is the **$86.6M of contract liabilities** — *advances received*, a different concept. Secondary coverage drifted from "signed" to "secured". **Treat as CLAIMED and unfiled.**

### Disagreements flagged

Deal value: release "up to $224M" is the dollar figure from a **28 Jun draft LOI**; at the 8 Sep $31.85 close the proxy values max earnout at $148.0M and total consideration at **$189.4M**. The $224M works only near the $43.00 LOI reference price. Also: statutory Ukrainian aggregators **disagree on FY2024 profit** (UAH −31,800 vs +3,452,000), though revenue lines broadly reconcile.
