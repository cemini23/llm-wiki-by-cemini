## Narrative

A short macro note. The thesis: nationalisation does not work, and it is not even a vote winner.

### The study

Gonzalez and Prem (2026) rebuilt the financials of **71 large Chilean firms, 1967–1973**, of which **32 were nationalised** under Allende.

- Nationalised firms saw **return on assets fall 11–13%** after nationalisation.
- **Sales over assets fell 20%.**
- In the **1973 municipal elections**, the governing coalition lost **1–4% of vote share** in municipalities exposed to nationalised factories and firms — with no compensating gains elsewhere.

### Why it matters here

Macro-political context for the equity lane. The interesting angle is the *political* result: expropriation is punished at the ballot, not rewarded. Relevant to any country-risk or policy-regime read. No action item; wiki-only.
