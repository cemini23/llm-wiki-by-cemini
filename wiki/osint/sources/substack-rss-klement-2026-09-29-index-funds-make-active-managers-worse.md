## Narrative

A summary of research by Hannah Unterberg at UC Irvine. The question: did the shift to passive investing hurt active fund performance? Theory said less competition should make outperformance easier. The data says the opposite.

Reported finding: the four-factor alpha of active funds, gross and net, shows a regime shift and a decline starting around 2010.

The mechanism named is flow pressure. When an active fund faces redemptions it must sell. Passive funds then re-buy at benchmark weights. The pressure runs against the fund's active tilt. Funds with higher active share and more concentrated books are hit hardest.

Reported context: at end-2024, passive held 20% of US market cap against active at 12%. Passive passed active in AUM in 2020.

No level to arm. This is an academic summary, not a signal. FILE only.
