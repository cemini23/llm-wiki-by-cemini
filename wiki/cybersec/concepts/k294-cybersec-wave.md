## Narrative

The wave has one rule. **English safety does not transfer.**

Conditioned on a safe English answer, mean translation-bypass rates run from **21.9%** to **44.4%**. Nine of ten models change rank when the conditioning scheme changes and the answers do not.

Removing the refusal direction does not remove a script gap about Taiwan. An abliterated checkpoint still answers the same claim at a different rate in each script.

A binary allow-or-refuse gate is the wrong shape for mixed-sensitivity context. The paper that measures this asks for a graduated level of detail instead.
