## Summary

At **$7.21**, Critical Metals (NASDAQ: CRML) is a **$1.06 billion** company with four employees, no mining revenue, and a Greenland rare-earth licence. The classified resource is **44.87 million tonnes at 0.38% TREO**. Booked exploration on Tanbreez is **$36.1 million**. First concentrate, on current management speech, is 2028–29. Thirteen months ago they said commercial production in **2026**.

The tape is paying for Greenland geopolitics and the chance Washington writes a cheque. It is not paying for a built mine.

The funding plan is an all-stock takeover of European Lithium (ASX: EUR) — the parent that still holds **45.5 million** CRML shares, chaired by the same man who runs CRML. That deal issues more CRML paper. It is not a reverse split. At today’s price the exchange ratio is indicated at the **maximum** 0.045 CRML per EUR share.

I would not short the stock. The name is already **22.3 million** shares short, and it printed two +20% sessions in late August (25 Aug ran on USA Rare Earth headlines; neither day printed a CRML contract). The listed **2 October 2026 $7 put** (CRML261002P00007000) is the cleaner way to hold it. Last **$0.78**, open interest **42**, volume **3** on 1 Sep. Bids on this chain are often **0** — treat last as a print, not a live market. That expiry covers the **15 September** Western Australia court hearing and the start of the deal’s VWAP window. It dies before the mid-October shareholder meetings and before implementation. If the bet is Australian holders dumping Nasdaq paper after the deal closes, you need a later expiry.

## Body

### 1. The company

Critical Metals is a British Virgin Islands Nasdaq name, file 001-41973, CIK 0001951089. It is the 2024 de-SPAC of Sizzle Acquisition. The office that matters is 32 Harrogate Street, West Leederville — the same Perth address as European Lithium.

Flagship: **Tanbreez** (Killavaat Alannguat), south Greenland, near Qaqortoq. Licensee on the government document is **Tanbreez Mining Greenland A/S**, not the Nasdaq ticker. CRML owns the A/S (claimed **92.5%** after April 2026; EUR keeps **7.5%** until the scheme). Second story: Wolfsberg lithium in Austria, still not “fully permitted” on the Austrian ministry’s telling.

The local record is Greenland’s mineral authority plus a Kujalleq municipality impact-benefit agreement signed with the licence in 2020.

Tony Sage is executive chairman and CEO of CRML and executive chairman of EUR. Gregory Barnes’s Rimbal Pty Ltd was paid in CRML paper for the rest of Tanbreez (14.5 million shares in April 2026, on top of an earlier 8.4 million). That is the related-party loop. It is also why the EUR scheme exists: fold the parent in, cancel the cross-holding, and bring EUR’s cash across.

### 2. Why the stock is the short

Tanbreez has a classified indicated-plus-inferred resource, a 30-year exclusive licence, Greenland approval of the 50.5% transfer, a narrow binding offtake, and some cash. That is the asset.

The equity is four staff, a $1 billion tape, a 4.7-billion-tonne slogan, production guidance that walked three years, offtakes sold as if they were cash, and a conflicted all-stock merger whose floating ratio is already indicated at 0.045 — at today’s price the dilution dial is pegged, and only a rally above $8 in the VWAP window turns it down.

USA Rare Earth’s federal package in August moved CRML **+21%** on 25 Aug. CRML still has no equivalent funded US cheque. That is lottery-ticket beta, and it is why a crowded short gets run.

### 3. Resource versus slogan

Agricola SK-1300 / JORC (SEC exhibit):

| Class | Million tonnes | TREO |
|---|---|---|
| Indicated | 25.42 | 0.37% |
| Inferred | 19.45 | 0.39% |
| **Total** | **44.87** | **0.38%** |

No mineral reserves. Licence start rate in the technical report: **0.5 million tonnes a year** of eudialyte run-of-mine, not an open-ended mega-mine.

The **4.7 billion tonne** figure is the conceptual mass of the kakortokite host. The same report says it does not indicate certainty of hosting mineralization and investors should not place undue reliance on it.

Eudialyte is the host mineral. There is no commercial eudialyte rare-earth mine at scale. Recovery — silica-gel in the hydrometallurgy — is the binding constraint, not the press-release tonnage. The FY2025 20-F/A still says outcomes hinge on confirming metallurgical performance and completing a definitive feasibility study.

Book value of capitalised Tanbreez exploration and evaluation: **US$36.1 million** at 30 June 2025. Third-party notes put mine-plus-downstream capex near **$500 million to more than $1 billion**. That is not a company table.

### 5. Offtakes are not cash

- **EXIM:** non-binding letter of interest, up to **$120 million** (16 Jun 2025). Not drawn.
- **Ucore:** non-binding LOI. Headlines like to say “DoD-funded processor.” The 20-F says there is no guarantee of a binding agreement.
- **REalloys (May 2026 binding):** **15% of Phase 1** monthly concentrate, about **2,250 tonnes a year** if Phase 1 is 15,000 tonnes nameplate. Term is **15 years from first delivery**. Either side may walk after **five years** if first delivery never happens. October 2025’s LOI talked **up to 6.75 million tonnes**. Two different objects.
- **Saudi TQB (15 Jan 2026):** non-binding 50/50 term sheet, plant “up to $1.5 billion,” **25%** life-of-mine offtake. Not cash.
- Reuters “75% pre-sold” is management speech. Those percentages have different scopes (Phase 1 vs life-of-mine vs non-binding). They can sit on a slide together. They cannot be added to prove 100% **binding** coverage.

GEM’s old $125 million equity line went to a dispute, then a **$40 million** equity settlement. That is more paper, not a project loan.

### 6. The EUR scheme

October is an all-stock takeover, not a reverse split. No CRML reverse split is filed or announced.

Two Western Australia schemes: CRML acquires EUR. Draft booklet lodged with ASIC **26 Aug 2026**. **First court: 15 Sep 2026, 09:15 AWST.** Meetings expected **mid-October**. Implementation October / early November if it closes.

**Ratio (6-K, 21 Aug 2026).** Was 0.035 fixed. Now floating:

- Scheme VWAP ≤ **$8** → **0.045** CRML per EUR (maximum shares)
- Scheme VWAP ≥ **$16** → **0.025** (minimum shares)
- Straight line between. VWAP is 20 Nasdaq sessions ending two trading days before the share-scheme meeting.

Spot **$7.21** is a **current indication** of 0.045, not the locked print. Further declines **cannot** push the ratio above 0.045. A rally in the measurement window **above $8** cuts dilution. “Merger arbs must short more as the stock falls” only works between $8 and $16. At the floor it is false. Below the floor, EUR holders eat further CRML downside, so **vote or break risk can rise**.

**Cash test.** The scheme’s minimum-cash condition is **A$330 million** at 8:00am on the second court date: EUR net cash and cash equivalents **plus** permitted Velta loans (about US$26–31 million). **Why this contract.** It is listed. Loss is capped. You do not locate 22 million shares of borrow. You do not sit through a +40% Washington headline with unlimited stock risk. The next hard date **inside** this life is the **15 September** first court hearing. The deal’s 20-day VWAP window also starts to matter in this window.

**Calendar.** Scheme meetings are **mid-October**. Implementation is later. This put expires before those. If the thesis is Australian holders dumping Nasdaq paper after implementation, 2 Oct is the wrong expiry. The 16 Oct monthly and November dates cover more calendar and cost more time.

**Why $7, not $10.** At $7.21 spot the $7 weekly sits next to the stock. The $10 puts are already in the money and priced more like stock. Illiquid. Use a limit. Do not market-order a 0-bid chain. Getting out of a winner on a 0/0 chain is just as hard as getting in — plan to hold toward expiry or use exercise mechanics, not a market sell. Size so a total loss of premium is boring.

**What would make me not use it.** No two-sided market when you go to fill. Borrow already exploding *and* you were about to short stock instead — still prefer the put, but the premium will not be $0.78. A committed EXIM or US government cheque **before** 2 Oct — that is a thesis-ender; do not pick up the put into that headline.

Pairing CRML risk against a funded rare-earth name (MP, or USAR after the federal package) is a separate construction. It strips “Washington bids rare earths” and leaves the funding gap. It is not required to buy the $7 puts.

### 11. Kill criteria — what breaks the short

- A **committed** financing package: EXIM drawn, or a US government equity / forward deal on USA Rare Earth-style terms. This is the thesis-ender.
- A feasibility study with credible eudialyte recoveries and refinery costs **inside** the NPV, not a PEA that books refined revenue and skips the plant.
- Independent expert report that marks Tanbreez so ~$1 billion looks boring rather than promotional.
- Clean confirmation the licence security / plan conditions were met or formally extended.
- Wolfsberg or other non-core sold at a price that actually funds Greenland without more equity.
- Production in 2026/27 (would falsify the timeline pillar).
- Scheme breaks and the tape re-rates *up* on “no more dilution” — possible; it is not automatic.
