5. Story vs audited company

FY2025 (year ended 30 Jun 2025) is still a shell on the books. Continuing revenue $61,000. Net loss $12.8 million (three-year cumulative about $83.8 million). Cash at 30 Jun 2025 was $60,000 (Yahoo now shows about $7.1 million after later PIPEs). Equity flipped from −$40.5 million to +$20.1 million mostly by booking a $60.5 million placement as a receivable. Auditor going-concern language three years running. FY2026 ended 30 Jun 2026; that annual report is not out yet.

What they sell (deck / press)

  • Powered-land landlord for sovereign and hyperscaler AI
  • Norway Mo i Rana ~42 MW, cheap hydro power
  • Pro forma ~$31 million hosting revenue claimed on that site
  • “358 MW secured” = 42 Norway + 291 Finland (28% stake) + 25 UAE intent
  • Tembo e-LV SPAC: ticker TEMB reserved, $838 million pre-money claimed
  • 2029 vision: 2 GW / $1 billion revenue (a deck, not a forecast)

What is actually filed

  • Only controlled operating megawatts: Norway ~42. Finland is 28%. UAE is intent.
  • No signed hyperscaler lease in the public record reviewed (May: shortlist only)
  • 29 Jul: $50 million PIPE, converts at $7.50, 6% PIK prefs + warrants. Lead: Blue Sky Capital
  • 3 Aug: $28.8 million founder-loan principal retired. Accrued interest still due
  • Tembo listing still needs SEC F-4 and a SPAC vote. Residual % unknown

8. What has to be true / what breaks it

Bull path

  • Next annual report shows Norway hosting in the tens of millions, not $61k
  • Named AI or sovereign tenant on a 15–25 year lease
  • TEMB lists; VIVO keeps a real stub
  • No more emergency equity
  • Float stays tight

Break points

  • Another story pivot (energy → crypto → data center already happened in 18 months)
  • Finland 28% never becomes cash
  • PIPE prefs + warrants grind the common
  • Tembo SPAC fails
  • Nasdaq listing tests if cash burns again
